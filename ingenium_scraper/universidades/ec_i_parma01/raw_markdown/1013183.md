# ENERGY SYSTEM AB

- Credits: 12 credits
- Language: course unit in ITALIAN
- Semester info: Annual

## Learning objectives

The integrated course provides knowledge and skills to understand the operating principles, real characteristics, and performance of major energy systems and prime movers (gas turbines, internal combustion engines, steam plants). By the end of the course, students will be able to: - Understand the fundamentals of energy conversion and the types of fluid machines (drivers and driven). - Analyze real thermodynamic transformations and calculate performance parameters (efficiency, specific fuel consumption). - Assess energy system performance under varying operating conditions. - Critically compare different plant configurations and select fluid machines for specific applications. - Present evaluations and comparisons effectively using graphical and numerical representations.

## Prerequisites

Basic knowledge of: - Calculus - Physics - Thermodynamics - Fluid Mechanics - Applied Thermodynamics

## Course unit content

- Conventional and renewable energy sources. - Energy systems and fluid machines: types, classification, and components. - Steam plants, gas turbine plants, reciprocating internal combustion engines. - Combined-cycle and cogeneration systems. - Ideal, limiting, and real thermodynamic cycles (Rankine, Joule, Otto, Diesel, Sabathé). - Analysis of real thermodynamic transformations and performance calculations. - Fluid flow in ducts, compression and expansion processes, sonic conditions. - Fluid machines: pumps, compressors, fans, turbines. - Renewable energy conversion: hydroelectric, photovoltaic, bioenergy systems. - Overview of energy cost and utilization factor.

## Full programme

- Energy demand, sources, and conversion processes. - Thermodynamic fundamentals: mass and energy balances, ideal gas transformations, property calculations. - Hydrocarbon combustion: stoichiometric air calculation, calorific values. - Thermodynamic cycles: ideal, limiting, and real; work and heat exchange calculations. - Gas turbine plants: components, cycle variants, Joule cycle optimization. - Steam plants: ideal and real cycles, regeneration, reheating, boilers, condensers, cooling towers. - Internal combustion engines: ideal and real cycles, losses, supercharging, performance curves. - Combined-cycle and cogeneration systems: performance indices (IRE, PES). - Renewable energy conversion: hydroelectric systems, photovoltaic systems, bioenergy. - Fluid machines: classification, similarity laws, characteristic curves, cavitation, compressors, fans. - Quantitative analysis of performance and thermodynamic parameters.

## Teaching methods

- Lectures supported by slides and schematic diagrams. - Numerical exercises to apply theoretical concepts and develop calculation skills. - Presentation of quantitative examples and calculation procedures. - Teaching materials available on Elly platform.

## Assessment methods and criteria

- **Written exam**: one numerical exercise, five structured questions, and two open questions (exercise must be correct and minimum score required on structured questions). - **Oral exam**: two questions on theory and practical applications (each question must score at least 18/30). Final grade is the average of the two oral questions. - Evaluation focuses on critical thinking, clarity of presentation, ability to correlate topics, and numerical problem-solving.
