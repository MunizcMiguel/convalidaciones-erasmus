# MACHINE DESIGN AB

- Credits: 12 credits
- Language: course unit in ITALIAN
- Semester info: Annual

## Learning objectives

Knowledge and understanding: At the end of this course the student should know the basics of machine design. He should gain understanding of the many factors affecting the design of mechanical parts with special regards to safety and reliability. He should be familiar with design schemes and checks required to analyze machine parts. Applying knowledge and understanding: He should be able to design a mechanical part on the basis of suitable material and process selection, load analysis and functional requirements. He should be able to select from catalogues the main mechanical components found in machines. He should be able to draw and dimension the part for production. Making judgments: By the end of the course the student should be able to critically analyze machines and mechanical components and to their functions and requisites. Communication skills: The student should be able to clearly present the functioning of mechanical devices and the design issues involved.

## Prerequisites

Physics 1: Forces and moments in space, their vector representation and vector sum/difference Machine drawing: rules for drawing components and parts, dimensioning, understanding the drawing of parts and assemblies Strength of materials/Solid mechanics: beam theory, calculating constraint reactions and internal forces, geometry of masses, stress calculations, resistance criteria Applied physiscs: kinematics and dynamics of rigid bodies Metallurgy: naming steels and light alloys (symbols and meaning), material selection criteria in relation to the manufacturing process Mechanical technology: manufacturing process in relation to the type and shape of the mechanical component to be manufactured

## Course unit content

The course covers the principles of mechanical design applied to machine components, starting with the strength characteristics of commonly used materials and the concept of safety. It then introduces methods and models for the schematization of acting loads and the calculation and sizing of components. A key aspect is the introduction to strength testing under fatigue loads. The course will cover the main mechanical components.

## Full programme

Introduction to Construction Design, Safety, Factor of Safety with Statistical Reliability Concepts, Technical Standards, Mechanical Testing of Materials, Tensile, Hardness, Impact, Fatigue, Fracture Testing, Metallic Materials (Steel, Cast Iron, Aluminum, Magnesium, Titanium Alloys), Non-Metallic Materials (Polymers and Ceramics), Manufacturing Technologies, Selection Criteria. Load Analysis, Equilibrium, Application of the Beam Model in Mechanics, Normal Force, Bending, Shear, and Torsion. Stresses and Strains. Mohr Circles. Principal Stresses, Strength Criteria (Tresca, Von Mises, Rankine), Ductility and Brittleness. Compressive Buckling and Eulerian Loading. Stiffness. Highly Curvatured Beams. Notched Geometries, Nominal and Local Stresses, Stress Concentration Factor Kt. Conformal and Non-Conformal Contact, Specific Contact Pressure, Hertzian Theory, Stresses in Contact between Elementary Bodies. Thermal Expansion. Fatigue of Metallic Materials, Characteristics of the Phenomenon, Fatigue Testing, Stress Limit fatigue, S/N curves, fatigue parameter estimates, influence factors, mean stress effect, fatigue damage accumulation, multiaxial stress effect, notch sensitivity, Notch effect factor Kf. Contact fatigue, pitting, fretting. Mechanical power transmission: axles and shafts, materials, construction details, sizing and testing, shaft-hub connections, gear wheels, pulleys; rolling bearings: types (rollers, balls) and load capacity, assembly, choosing bearings from the catalog; plain bearings: examples, materials, testing. Screws: examples and threads, testing. Loose connections: screws, bolts, rivets, and nails, standards for threaded parts, load transmission by shear or friction, bolted joints, preload, stiffness of parts and load distribution, testing in the presence of fatiguing loads, anti-loosening devices; pins. Fixed connections: welding: technical standards, sizing of welded joints, types of seams, strength testing criteria; notes on sizing of glued joints. Springs: fields of application, types, stiffness. Pressure vessels under membrane conditions: standards, stress state in thin and thick vessels, sizing.

## Teaching methods

Course topics are presented through lectures and numerical exercises carried out in the classroom. Problems are proposed to be carried out at home with subsequent discussion in the classroom.

## Assessment methods and criteria

The exam for this module consists of a written test and an oral test. During the written test, students are allowed to consult tables, slides, and use software or online applications to support the definition and selection of components to be sized or verified. To access the oral test, students must achieve a passing grade (18/30) in the written test. The grade for the exam is a partial result and will count equally with the partial result o fModule I to determine the final grade for Machine Design AB.
