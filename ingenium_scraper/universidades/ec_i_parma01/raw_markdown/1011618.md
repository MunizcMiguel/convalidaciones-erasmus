# INFORMATICS FUNDAMENTALS

- Discipline: Sistemi di elaborazione delle informazioni (ING-INF/05)
- Hours: 48 hours of face-to-face activities
- Credits: 6 credits
- Language: course unit in ITALIAN
- Semester info: Second semester

## Learning objectives

The aim of the course is to provide students with an understanding of the principles of computer science and programming.

## Course unit content

Introduction to computer science: * Programs and algorithms * Boolean algebra and logic * Data representation * Computer architecture * Operating systems Programming in Python: * Structured programming * Data structures and collections * Functions * Objects * File reading and writing

## Teaching methods

Lectures and Exercises.

## Assessment methods and criteria

Written Exam. There might be an oral exam if deemed necessary by the teacher.

## Bibliography

Michele Tomaiuolo e Alberto Ferrari, Introduzione all'Informatica con Python, McGraw-Hill, 2024
