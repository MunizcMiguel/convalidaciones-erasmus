# MECHANICS OF MACHINERY AB

- Credits: 12 credits
- Language: course unit in ITALIAN
- Semester info: Annual

## Learning objectives

On the basis of a deep knowledge of kinematics, statics and dynamics of mechanical system, the course provides the practical and theorical knowledge required for a proper functional design of machines and their devices

## Prerequisites

Meccanica razionale

## Course unit content

Mechanisms assembly Forces acting on mechanisms Efficiency Fundamental concepts of tribology: friction, wearing, lubrication Joints, brakes, clutches Concepts of rigid body kinematics and applications to mechanisms Articulated systems Cam-follower devices Analysis and synthesis of mechanisms Motion transmission between shafts Belt and chain transmissions Gears Fundamentals of dynamics. Mechanism dynamics. Vibrations of one degree-of-freedom systems. Introduction to the experimental investigation. Rotor dynamics.

## Full programme

Historical notes and terminology Degrees of freedom, Gruebler and Kutzbach formulas Friction Sliding and static friction Cases of friction in prismatic pairs, revolute pairs, thrust bearings, inclined planes, screw and nut Rolling friction Cases of idler wheels, driving wheels, driven wheels Examples for vehicles Efficiency, machines in series and parallel, retrograde motion Lubrication Newtonian fluids, conservation of mass, constitutive laws, viscosity and units of measurement Reynolds equation in 2D and 3D, notes on elastohydrodynamics Hydrodynamic lubrication: applications—fixed and tilting pad bearings, axial and radial bearings, bushings, Sommerfeld number Hydrostatic lubrication: cases of radial and axial pairs Kinematics Galileo’s theorem, instantaneous center of rotation Trajectories, primitives and polars Euler–Savary theorem, conjugate profiles, circle of inflection points, Rivals theorem, applications Linkage systems Analysis and synthesis of four-bar and six-bar linkages, crank mechanisms Roberts theorem Oldham coupling, Cardan joint Notes on other joints (Birfield, tripod, Rzeppa) Cam mechanisms and motion laws Gears Friction wheels and motivation for gears Involute and its properties Pinion and rack Cylindrical involute gears: definitions, properties, corrections Helical gears, bevel gears, Tredgold’s method, worm–helical gear pair Ordinary and epicyclic gear trains Willis formula, combinatorial gear trains, differentials, automotive gearboxes Flexible elements Pulleys, hoists, belt drives, band brakes Rigid body dynamics review Systems with constraints and equations of motion Lagrange equations Dynamics of crank mechanisms Equivalent masses, balancing of engines with 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 cylinders Machines with periodic operation Periodic irregularity, flywheel calculation Single-degree-of-freedom vibrations Free, damped, forced vibrations Frequency response, transmissibility, suspensions Rotor balancing Numerical methods for integration of motion equations Torque curves, longitudinal dynamics of the vehicle

Lessons and exercises with applied examples of topics which are discussed at the lessons.
