# MECHANICS OF MACHINERY A

- Discipline: Meccanica applicata alle macchine (ING-IND/13)
- Hours: 48 hours of face-to-face activities
- Credits: 6 credits
- Language: course unit in ITALIAN
- Semester info: First semester

## Learning objectives

On the basis of a deep knowledge of kinematics, statics and dynamics of mechanical system, the course provides the practical and theorical knowledge required for a proper functional design of machines and their devices.

## Prerequisites

Meccanica razionale

## Course unit content

Mechanisms assembly. Forces acting on mechanisms. Fundamental concepts of tribology: friction, wearing, lubrication Joints, brakes, clutches. Concepts of kinematics and dynamics and applications to mechanisms. Gears. Transmissions.

## Full programme

Historical notes and terminology. Degrees of freedom, Gruebler's and Kutzbach's formulas. Friction: sliding and static friction; cases of friction in prismatic, revolute, thrust, inclined plane, screw-nut pairs; brakes and clutches; rolling friction; cases of idler, driving, and driven wheels; vehicle examples; efficiency, machines in series and in parallel, retrograde motion. Lubrication: Newtonian fluids, mass conservation, constitutive laws, viscosity and units of measurement, Reynolds equation in 2D and 3D, introduction to elastohydrodynamics; hydrodynamic lubrication: applications – fixed and tilting-pad bearings (axial and radial), bushings, Sommerfeld number; hydrostatic lubrication. Kinematics: Galileo’s theorem, instantaneous center of rotation, trajectories, centroids and polodes, Euler-Savary theorem, conjugate profiles, inflection circle, Rivals’ theorem, applications. Linkage systems: analysis and synthesis of four-bar and six-bar linkages, slider-crank mechanisms, Roberts’ theorem, Oldham coupling, Cardan joint, brief notes on other joints (Birfield, tripod, Rzeppa). Cam mechanisms and motion laws. Gears: friction wheels and motivation for toothed gears, involute curve and its properties, pinion and rack, involute spur gears. Chain and belt transmissions. Dynamics of slider-crank mechanisms, substitution masses, balancing of engines with 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, and 12 cylinders.

## Teaching methods

Lessons and exercises with applied examples of topics which are discussed at the lessons.

## Assessment methods and criteria

Written exam.
