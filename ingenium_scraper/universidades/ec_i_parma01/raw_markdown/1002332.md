# FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING + INTRODUCTION TO ELECTRONICS

- Credits: 9 credits
- Language: course unit in ITALIAN
- Semester info: Second semester

## Learning objectives

The module of PPLICAZIONI INDUSTRIALI ELETTRICHE aims at providing basic knowledge and methods for the analysis and synthesis of linear circuits in DC, AC and transient conditions. Part of the contents will be preparatory for later Electronics courses. The aim of the Electronics Elements module is to provide students with a basic knowledge of: - electronic components and circuits - analysis techniques of first and second order linear systems in the frequency domain - transfer functions and harmonic response functions - behavior and characteristics of feedback systems - techniques for evaluating the stability of linear systems 2) Ability to apply knowledge and understanding The skills that the student will be able to acquire with this teaching are: - analyze the behavior of linear systems, with particular reference to first and second order systems, through analysis in the frequency domain - analyze behavior and stability of feedback systems

## Prerequisites

The student must demonstrate familiarity with the basic teachings taught in the first years of the degree course. (Mathematical Analysis 1, Mathematical Analysis 2, General Physics 1, General Physics 2).

## Course unit content

The course aims to provide students with the general criteria for analyzing the behavior of basic electric and electronic circuits. Lectures will cover the following topics: Analysis and synthesis on linear electric circuits in direct current, alternating current, and transient operation. Energy analysis. Frequency analysis. Mathematical models of linear electronic devices and systems. Analysis of linear systems in the frequency domain in the frequency domain. Stability of feedback systems.

## Full programme

AIE: 1) Steady-state electrodynamics (6 hours). Voltage and currents. Resistor, voltage and current independent generators, Ohm's laws, energy. Voltage and current limits, rated values. Thermal sizing. Elements of Electronics: - Introduction to Electronics - Semiconductor Devices - BJT Linear Amplifiers - Laplace Transform and Transfer Function - Bode Plots - Passive Filters - Feedback Systems - Stability of Linear Systems - Operational Amplifier - Circuits with Operational Amplifiers - Overview of Power Converters - Exercises

## Teaching methods

The ELECTRICAL INDUSTRIAL APPLICATIONS module includes lectures. In-class exercises will be completed by the instructor. The Electronics Elements module includes 3 training credits, which correspond to 24 hours of lessons. Teaching activities will be conducted by privileging frontal lessons alternating with classroom exercises. During the frontal lessons, the course topics will be addressed from a theoretical-design point of view, in order to promote a deep understanding of the topics and to bring out any prior knowledge on the topics in question by the trainees. The projected notes and any notes used to support the lessons will be uploaded to the Elly platform. To access this material, registration for the online course is required. Non-attending students are reminded to check the available teaching material and the instructions provided by the teacher via the Elly platform, the only communication tool used for direct teacher/student contact. The topics covered in class are indicated on this platform, which will then form the index of contents in view of preparing for the final exam.

## Assessment methods and criteria

The exam consists of a written test. The written test is unique for the two integrated modules of "Industrial Electrical Applications" and "Elements of Electronics". During the test, for the part of AIE, the student will have to demonstrate knowledge of circuit analysis and synthesis techniques in direct current, alternating current and transient operation, frequency analysis. For the part of Elements of Electronics, the student must demonstrate that he/she knows and can apply the techniques of analysis of linear systems covered in the course, and that he/she can analyze the behavior of simple circuits. Importance is given to the ability to solve exercises and answer simple theoretical questions.
