# ENERGETIC SYSTEM A

- Discipline: Macchine a fluido (ING-IND/08)
- Hours: 48 hours of face-to-face activities
- Credits: 6 credits
- Language: course unit in ITALIAN
- Semester info: First semester

## Learning objectives

At the end of the course the student will be able - to know the basics of energy conversion in energy systems; - to know the types and performances of the fluid machines; - to evaluate the performance of energy systems by according to the operating parameters; - to choose fluid machines according to the specific application.

## Prerequisites

Applied thermodynamics.

## Course unit content

Conventional and renewable energy sources. Thermodynamics of thermal power plants: steam plants, gas turbines and internal combustion engines. Combined power plant. Cogeneration. Architecture, principles of operation and performance of fluid machines. Renewable power plants.

## Teaching methods

Lectures.

## Assessment methods and criteria

The written examination is composed of - an exercise; - five tests; - two open questions. The exam is passed if the exercise is correct and the threshold of 4 points in the tests is overcomed.
