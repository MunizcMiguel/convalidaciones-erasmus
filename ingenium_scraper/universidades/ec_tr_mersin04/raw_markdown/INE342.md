# INE342 — COST ANALYSIS IN ENGINEERING

ECTS: 3 · Year 3 · Semester 6 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Objectives of the course

This course aims to provide sound and comprehensive coverage of the basic concepts of cost analysis in engineering and also address the practical concerns of cost analysis in engineering.

## Course content

The course covers the following topics: Management Accounting and Financial Accounting, Financial Statements-Transaction Recording, Inventories-Depreciation, Cost Terms and Objectives, Determining How Costs Behave, Cost-Volume-Profit Analysis, Master Budget, Flexible Budgets and Variances, Allocation of Support Department Costs, Joint Products and By-Products, Job Costing, Activity-Based Costing, Process Costing, Pricing Decisions.

## Recommended or required reading

- Horngren/Datar/Foste, (2006) Cost Accounting (12 th Ed.), Pearson Education
- William G. Sullivan, Elin M. Wicks, C. Patrick Koelling, (2017) Engineering Economy (17 th Ed.), Pearson

## Learning outcomes

1. Performs basic accounting transactions and records
2. Explains the formation of inventory cost
3. Knows the concepts of cost types
4. Explains the content of the budget
5. Knows the variables that affect/are used in costing
6. Explains the different principles used in costing
