# INE323 — ENGINEERING ECONOMICS

ECTS: 4 · Year 3 · Semester 5 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Objectives of the course

This course aims to provide sound and comprehensive coverage of the basic concepts of engineering economics and also address the practical concerns of engineering economics.

## Course content

The course covers cost types, basic economic calculations, the concept of price, economic environment and indexes. Afterwards, simple and compound interest, time value of money, single and multiple economic evaluation of an engineering activity, depreciation and exchange rate effects are discussed.

## Recommended or required reading

- William G. Sullivan, Elin M. Wicks, C. Patrick Koelling, (2017) Engineering Economy (17 th Ed.), Pearson
- Leland Blank, Anthony Tarquin, (2005) Engineering Economy (7 th Ed.), McGrawHill

## Learning outcomes

1. Explains cost and its types
2. Makes basic economic calculations
3. Uses economic indexes
4. Knows the types of interest and makes their calculations
5. Makes time value of money calculations
6. Analyzes a single engineering project economically
7. Can compare multiple engineering projects economically
8. Makes depreciation calculation
9. Uses exchange rate calculation in the analyses
