# TUR101 — TURKISH LANGUAGE I

ECTS: 2 · Year 1 · Semester 1 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Prerequisites

There is no prerequisite or co-requisite for this course.

## Objectives of the course

It aims to use the Turkish language according to the rules, to be able to express himself correctly both orally and in writing, to have knowledge about important sources belonging to various periods of
Turkish.

## Course content

This course covers the definition and properties of language, language-culture relationship, the place of
Turkish among the world languages, the historical development of Turkish language, the Turkish Language Association and its studies, the phonology of Turkish, the sound events in Turkish, the properties of Turkish words and spelling rules.

## Learning outcomes

1. Defines language and knows its properties.
2. Can define culture and explain its relationship with language.
3. Classify the world languages and determine the place of Turkish in these languages.
4. Can analyse the historical periods of Turkish language.
5. Recognise and explain the historical texts of Turkish language.
6. Have knowledge about the Turkish Language Association and its studies.
7. Knows the phonological properties of Turkish.
8. Knows and can apply the rules of Turkish spelling and punctuation.
