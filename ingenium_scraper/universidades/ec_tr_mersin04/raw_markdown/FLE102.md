# FLE102 — ENGLISH II

ECTS: 3 · Year 1 · Semester 2 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Objectives of the course

This course intends to develop students’ language skills through academic writing, reading and critical thinking techniques.

## Course content

This course is a 3 hour course aims to empower students with language and academic skills which they need to carry out their academic goals. To this end the courses provide the students with background in major concepts and ample opportunities for students to build awareness and practice the language in academic environment. The courses will provide opportunities to practice language students need for work in their profession and academic life.

## Recommended optional programme components

Students should follow weekly videos and assignments and consult to their instructor during progress.

## Recommended or required reading

Weekly materials provided by the instructor / source: San José State University Writing Center www.sjsu.edu/writingcenter Written by Karen Liou

## Learning outcomes

1. Developing academic speaking, listening, writing, and reading skills
2. Identifying related terminology
3. Developing personal strategies for reviewing new related vocabulary
4. Using vocabulary in a variety of academic speaking, listening, writing, and reading activities
5. Discussing a variety of topics needed for work in the students’ professions
6. Evaluating a variety of texts
