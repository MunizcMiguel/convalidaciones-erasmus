# ISG202 — OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH AND SAFETY II

ECTS: 2 · Year 2 · Semester 4 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Objectives of the course

To give information on the occupational health and safety procedures and practices in industry

## Course content

Occupational health and safety procedures and practices in construction

## Learning outcomes

1. Knows structural work concept
2. Knows personal protective equipments
3. Knows Safety measures in mould and wharfs
4. Knows safety measures of working on high place
5. Knows safety measures in destruction works
6. Knows safety guidelines
