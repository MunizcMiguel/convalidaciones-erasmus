# TUR102 — TURKISH LANGUAGE II

ECTS: 2 · Year 1 · Semester 2 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Prerequisites

None

## Objectives of the course

The aim of the course is to teach students the rules of the Turkish language and to enable them to express themselves clearly and understandably.

## Course content

It is aimed to introduce presentations, novels, stories, essays, poems, compositions, magazines, newspaper reviews and applications, spelling and punctuation marks, composition information, literary, official and scientific written expression types, opinion pieces, oral expression types and prepared-improvised speaking activities within the scope of oral and written expression studies by analyzing Turkish texts and evaluating them in terms of form, content and functions and to be able to analyze examples related to these. Awareness of different text types will be increased by developing the Turkish skills required for written and oral translation.

## Learning outcomes

1. He/she can express himself/herself clearly and understandably by paying attention to speech disorders.
2. He/she knows the sound characteristics of his/her language and can express his/her language accordingly.
3. He/she knows the composition formation information and can create a written composition accordingly.
4. He/she can examine the written expression types and write written types such as essay, petition.
5. He/she knows the beautiful and effective speaking methods and can speak accordingly.
6. He/she knows the oral expression types such as conference, panel, panel discussion, symposium.
7. He/she can make seminar presentations in his/her field.
