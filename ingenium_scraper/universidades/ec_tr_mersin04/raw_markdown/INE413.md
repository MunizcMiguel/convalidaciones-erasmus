# INE413 — MANAGEMENT SUPPORT SYSTEMS

ECTS: 4 · Year 4 · Semester 7 · optativa · content year id 47

## Objectives of the course

Course objectives include developing an understanding of the purpose, functions, components, and applications of transaction processing systems and management information systems in private and public organizations; and describing and evaluating policies for information resource management.

## Course content

The components, types, benefits, importance, creation and start-up methods, and economic evaluation of management support systems are covered in the course. In addition, big data and cloud computing are discussed, and the ethical issues that come to the fore when using these systems and the safety and security dimensions that should be considered are covered in the course. Finally, digital transformation and possible future changes are discussed.

## Recommended or required reading

- Kenneth C. Laudon, (2014), Management Information Systems Managing The Digital Firm (13 th Ed.) Prentice Hall.
- James Taylor, (2011), Decision Management Systems: A Practical Guide to Using Business Rules and Predictive Analytics, IBM Press

## Learning outcomes

1. Knows the components of MSS
2. Explains the types of MSS
3. Knows the stages of MSS implementation
4. Can economically define the tagit and intagit benefits of MSS.
5. Explains cloud computing and its types
6. Knows and explains the features of big data.
7. Explains MSS safety and security risks
8. Explains the stages of digital transformation
9. Knows possible future systems related to MSS.
