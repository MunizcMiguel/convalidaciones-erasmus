# KRY101 — CAREER PLANNING

ECTS: 2 · Year 1 · Semester 1 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Prerequisites

None

## Objectives of the course

The aim of this course is to provide information and skills on recognizing individuals in line with their interests, talents and values ??during the development process, planning their careers according to career development theories and what can be done in career planning after formal education.

## Course content

The concept of career Career planning The relationship between career planning and vocational counseling Individual career development CV preparation and types of CVs Job interview Career planning process Evaluation of the Turkish education system in line with career planning Applicability of career counseling in schools Lifelong career planning Career planning in retirement

## Recommended optional programme components

None

## Recommended or required reading

Kuzgun, Y. (2003). Introduction to Career Guidance and Counseling. Ankara: Nobel Erdoğmuş, N. (2003). Career Development. Ankara, Nobel, Kulaksızoğlu, A. (2005). Adolescence Psychology. İstanbul Remzi. Kuzgun, Y. (2003). Career Counseling Theories and Applications. Ankara: Nobel.

## Internship status

None

## Learning outcomes

1. Students express the principles of career planning.
2. Students use the methods and techniques of career planning and discuss the effects of character traits on career choice.
3. Students explain career development.
4. Students plan career development process and services according to educational rating.
5. It helps individuals in career planning throughout their lives.
