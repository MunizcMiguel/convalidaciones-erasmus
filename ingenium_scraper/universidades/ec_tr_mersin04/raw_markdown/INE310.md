# INE310 — OPERATIONS RESEARCH III

ECTS: 5 · Year 3 · Semester 6 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Prerequisites

None

## Objectives of the course

This course aims to provide students with the skills to model, analyze, and solve complex systems by introducing probabilistic systems analysis and modeling techniques such as Markov chains, network models, and queuing theory.

## Course content

Covers topics such as Markov chains and their applications, queuing systems and performance analysis, Jackson networks, network models and optimization, and deterministic and probabilistic inventory models.

## Recommended optional programme components

None

Lecture notes and additional resources.

## Internship status

None

## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the basic principles and application areas of Markov chains.
2. Be able to model and analyze systems with Markov chains.
3. Be able to recognize the basic concepts and types of queuing theory.
4. Be able to analyze the performance criteria of queuing systems.
5. Be able to analyze queuing network models such as open and closed Jackson networks.
6. Be able to recognize, analyze and use network models in optimization problems.
7. Be able to understand deterministic and probabilistic inventory models and apply them to inventory management problems.
