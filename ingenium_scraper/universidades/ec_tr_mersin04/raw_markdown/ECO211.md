# ECO211 — MICROECONOMICS

ECTS: 4 · Year 2 · Semester 3 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Prerequisites

None

## Objectives of the course

The aim of this course is to define the fundamental concepts of microeconomics and develop the ability to interpret the core economic problem. It seeks to cultivate a way of thinking about scarcity, utility, and choices in economics. Students will analyze market demand and supply, understand the impact of economic decision-making units on the market, and examine market equilibrium. Additionally, they will be able to interpret the concepts of demand and supply elasticity. Furthermore, this course aims to provide foundational knowledge on basic production theory and cost structures.

## Course content

Definition of economics, scope of economics, economic concepts, factors of production, opportunity cost, scarcity, utility and choice, market supply and demand curves, price, elasticity, market equilibrium, production theory, and production costs.

## Learning outcomes

1. Defines the fundamental problem and concepts of microeconomics
2. Applies economic thinking to real-life phenomena.
3. Identifies basic market equilibrium through supply and demand analysis.
4. Interprets the concepts of price and elasticity.
5. Analyzes market equilibrium and the impact of changing factors.
6. Gains knowledge of production theory and costs.
