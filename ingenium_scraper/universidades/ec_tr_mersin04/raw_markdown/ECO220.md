# ECO220 — MACROECONOMICS

ECTS: 4 · Year 2 · Semester 4 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Prerequisites

None

## Objectives of the course

The course aims to teach fundamental macroeconomic concepts. As an introductory macroeconomics course, it also prepares students for advanced economics courses. It covers key macroeconomic variables, including national income, employment and unemployment, inflation, and the foreign exchange market, emphasizing how these economic indicators influence public and private sector policies. The course focuses on the role of government policy in improving economic outcomes. Additionally, it provides insights into interpreting macroeconomic indicators through examples from the Turkish economy.

## Course content

The emergence of macroeconomics, national income accounting, unemployment, inflation, economic growth, Keynesian approach, money, monetary policy, foreign exchange markets.

## Learning outcomes

1. Defines fundamental macroeconomic variables.
2. Understands the components of economic growth and national income.
3. Interprets key macroeconomic indicators.
4. Follows and analyzes developments in the Turkish and global economies.
