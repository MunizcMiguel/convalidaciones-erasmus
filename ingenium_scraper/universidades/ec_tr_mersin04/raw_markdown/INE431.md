# INE431 — QUALITY ENGINEERING

ECTS: 4 · Year 4 · Semester 7 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Prerequisites

None

## Objectives of the course

This course aims to introduce the fundamental concepts of quality management and statistical process control tools, enabling students to develop the knowledge and skills necessary to monitor and improve processes.

## Course content

It covers fundamental concepts of quality management and the DMAIC process, statistical terminology, process variability and control charts, process capability analysis (PCA), advanced control charts (CUSUM, EWMA), and acceptance sampling techniques.

None

## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the basic concepts of quality management and the DMAIC process.
2. Use statistical terminology and basic statistical concepts.
3. Analyze variability in processes and interpret control charts.
4. Apply control charts for variables and attributes to determine whether processes are under control.
5. Perform process capability analysis (PCA) and evaluate process performance.
6. Develop process improvement suggestions using advanced control charts (CUSUM, EWMA).
7. Explain acceptance sampling techniques and apply them in quality assurance processes.
