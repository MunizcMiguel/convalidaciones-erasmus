# MAT201 — LINEAR ALGEBRA

ECTS: 4 · Year 2 · Semester 3 · obligatoria · content year id 48

## Prerequisites

It is recommended that students acquire the fundamental learning outcomes for Mathematics I and II.

## Objectives of the course

This course aims to introduce students to the fundamental concepts of linear algebra; to teach the mathematical foundations of basic structures such as vectors, matrices, systems of linear equations, vector spaces, and linear transformations; and to demonstrate how these concepts are used in modeling and solving engineering problems. The course aims to develop students' analytical thinking, abstraction, and problem-solving skills. Furthermore, it aims to equip students with a solid algebraic foundation necessary for advanced engineering and mathematics courses.

## Course content

Vectors and basic vector operations. Systems of linear equations and matrix representation. Gauss and Gauss–Jordan elimination methods. Matrix operations, inverse matrix, and determinant concept. Vector spaces, subspaces, and linear independence. Concept of basis and dimension. Linear transformations and matrix representations. Eigenvalues and eigenvectors, diagonalization. Orthogonal vectors, inner product, Gram-Schmidt orthonormalization process. Fundamental applications of linear algebra in engineering and applied sciences.

## Recommended optional programme components

To effectively follow the course, students are advised to attend class regularly, complete individual studies parallel to the weekly topics, and review the example problems solved in class.

## Recommended or required reading

Textbook: D. C. Lay, S. R. Lay, J. J. McDonald, Linear Algebra and Its Applications, Pearson

Reference Books: (1) H. Anton, C. Rorres, A. Kaul, Elementary Linear Algebra with Applications, Wiley; (2) G. Strang, Introduction to Linear Algebra, Wellesley-Cambridge Press

## Internship status

There is no mandatory or optional internship requirement for this course.

## Learning outcomes

1. Defines fundamental concepts related to vectors, matrices, and linear equation systems, and explains the relationships between these structures.
2. Solves linear equation systems using Gaussian and Gaussian–Jordan elimination methods and interprets the solutions.
3. Solves algebraic problems using matrix operations, determinant, and inverse matrix concepts.
4. Analyzes vector spaces, subspaces, linear independence, basis, and dimension concepts and applies them to examples.
5. Relates linear transformations to matrix representations and applies eigenvalue and eigenvector concepts to engineering problems.
6. Effectively uses linear algebraic methods in modeling and solving problems encountered in engineering and applied sciences.
