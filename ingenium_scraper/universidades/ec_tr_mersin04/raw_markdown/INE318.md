# INE318 — SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT

ECTS: 4 · Year 3 · Semester 6 · optativa · content year id 47

## Prerequisites

-

## Objectives of the course

To provide students with a holistic understanding of the background functioning of a production process they are involved in from an engineer's perspective, to provide them with the perspective to evaluate the duties and responsibilities they will undertake in this regard, and to teach them basic knowledge and methods.

## Course content

The course covers basic concepts, components of the supply chain, stages in the management process, interactions of components and basic analysis methods.

## Learning outcomes

1. Knows the basic concepts of the supply chain
2. Knows the components, actors, processes of the SC, explains their interactions.
3. Interprets the flow and shares within the SC.
4. Interprets the SC design factors.
5. Explains the effects of the end customer for the SC.
6. Explains the SC performance criteria.
7. Performs basic analyses within the SC.
