# HIS101 — ATATURK'S PRINCIPLES & HISTORY OF TURKISH REFORMS I

ECTS: 2 · Year 1 · Semester 1 · obligatoria · content year id 48

## Objectives of the course

This course aims to develop students' language skills in their field. Communicative method is used.

## Course content

This is a theoretical course with 3 hours per week. It will be Face-to-Face. The students can study and work on the course topics through coursebook, videos, related materials. Fall term grade will be the total of midterm exam (%30), presentation (%10) and final exam (%60).

## Recommended or required reading

Psychology, Timothy Gilliland, PsyD Jenny Dooley, Express Publishing, 2017

## Learning outcomes

1. Developing personal strategies for reviewing new vocabulary
2. Using words in various academic speaking, listening, writing and reading exercises
3. Discuss the various topics that students need in their area of expertise.
4. Developing academic speaking, listening, writing and reading skills
5. Evaluating various reading texts
6. Distinguishing related terminology
