# ISG201 — OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH AND SAFETY I

ECTS: 2 · Year 2 · Semester 3 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Prerequisites

non

## Objectives of the course

The aim of this course is to introduce the basics of occupational health and safety (OHS) concepts, OHS management systems and risk management techniques, to develop students' safety culture awareness and to provide knowledge and skills for the prevention of occupational accidents by recognizing different risk factors.

## Course content

Introduction to occupational health and safety, OHS practices in Turkey and the world, risk management and assessment processes, physical, chemical, biological and psychosocial risk factors, ergonomics, causes of occupational accidents and concepts of safety culture.

## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the concepts of occupational health and safety (OSH), its historical development and legal framework in Turkey and the world.
2. Recognize and apply OHS management systems and risk assessment processes.
3. Determine and analyze physical, chemical, biological and psychosocial risk factors in the work environment
4. Develop suggestions for improving the work environment in workplaces using ergonomics principles.
5. Will be able to develop prevention strategies by analyzing the causes of work accidents.
6. Will gain professional and ethical responsibility awareness by adopting a safety culture.
