# INE457 — LABOUR LAW

ECTS: 4 · Year 4 · Semester 7 · optativa · content year id 48

## Objectives of the course

Investigation of scope and concepts of employment law

## Course content

Scope of employment law; Laws and regulations in civil engineering; Employer and labour responsibilities and rights; Concepts of execute and nullify of agreement

## Learning outcomes

1. Knows employment law
2. Knows laws and regulations of law employment
3. Knows employer and loborer responsibilities
4. Knows principles of agreement
5. Knows execute and nullify of agreement
