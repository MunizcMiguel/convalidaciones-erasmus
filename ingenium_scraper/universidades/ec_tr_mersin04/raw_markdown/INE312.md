# INE312 — MATERIAL AND STOCK MANAGEMENT

ECTS: 3 · Year 3 · Semester 6 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Objectives of the course

The objective of the course is to make the students understand the concepts of material and stock management of an industrial. This course deals with managing/decision-making related to material and stock management processes so that the resulting goods or service is produced according to specification, in the amount and by the schedule demanded, and at minimum cost.

## Course content

The course covers the concept of material, handling of material in production planning, capacity management, purchasing, estimating material needs, the concept of ordering, distribution and operations.

## Recommended or required reading

- Stephen N. Chapman, J. R. Tony Arnold, Ann K. Gatewood, Lloyd M. Clive, Introduction to Materials Management, (2017), 8th Edition, Pearson
- Max Muller, Essentials of Inventory Management, (2011), 2nd E., American Management Association

## Learning outcomes

1. Knows the classification of the material in the production process.
2. Explains the steps of material planning.
3. Explains the functional operations performed on the material (purchasing, capacity management, etc.)
4. Makes material estimation and calculation.
5. Explains the physical processes of the material.
6. Explains the operations performed on the material.
