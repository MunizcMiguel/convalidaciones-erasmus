# INE401 — SYSTEM SIMULATION

ECTS: 4 · Year 4 · Semester 7 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Objectives of the course

This course provides a basic treatment of simulation (especially discrete-event simulation), including the proper collection and analysis of data, the use of analytic techniques, verification and validation of models, and designing simulation experiments.

## Course content

The steps of modeling from a system perspective and then the stages of simulation are processed in a way that complements each other. How manual simulation can be applied is reinforced with examples. The basic concepts of simulation are examined from the simulation perspective of each. In particular, the concept of discrete events and queues are studied on basic examples. The features of the simulation software to be used are examined. The use of statistical and probability methods is emphasized. Finally, basic simple applications are made in a sample software.

## Recommended or required reading

- Jerry Banks , John S. Carson , Barry L Nelson , David M. Nicol , (2004), Discrete-Event System Simulation, (4 th Ed.) Prentice Hall.
- Andrei Borshchev, (2013), The Big Book of Simulation Modeling (Multimethod Modeling with Anylogic 6), Any Logic North America.

## Learning outcomes

1. Knows the concept of system.
2. Defines model types and modeling steps.
3. Knows the basic concepts in simulation.
4. Explains the stages of simulation.
5. Explains discrete processes with the queuing theorem.
6. Performs manual simulation analysis.
7. Knows the features of simulation software.
8. Explains the role and use of statistics and probability in simulation.
9. Can simulate a process with a simulation software
