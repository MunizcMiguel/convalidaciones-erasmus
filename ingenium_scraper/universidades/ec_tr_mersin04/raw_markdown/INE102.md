# INE102 — MATERIALS SCIENCE

ECTS: 3 · Year 1 · Semester 2 · obligatoria · content year id 47

## Prerequisites

None

## Objectives of the course

The aim of this course is to introduce the structural and physical properties of various types of materials and to equip students with the necessary knowledge and skills for selecting appropriate materials in engineering applications. Students will learn to analyze the mechanical, thermal, electrical, and magnetic properties of materials.

## Course content

Introduction to materials science, atomic and crystal structures, mechanical and thermal properties, phase diagrams, polymers and composites, ceramics, metal alloys, material failures, and material selection in engineering.

None

## Learning outcomes

1. Classify materials and explain their fundamental structures.
2. Analyze the effects of atomic structure and interatomic bonds on material properties.
3. Identify crystal structures and crystal defects.
4. Explain the mechanical, thermal, electrical, and magnetic properties of materials.
5. Interpret diffusion and phase diagrams to analyze material transformations.
6. Discuss the structural and physical properties of polymers, ceramics, and composite materials.
7. Explain how heat treatments affect the properties of metal alloys.
8. Use material selection criteria to choose the most suitable materials for engineering problems.
9. Analyze material failures (fatigue, corrosion, fracture) and propose preventive methods.
