## Contents

Floating point arithmetic.  
  
Algorithms and stability.  
  
Conditioning of a problem.  
  
Direct numerical methods for algebraic linear systems (e.g. Gauss elimination method).  
Iterative numerical methods and algorithms for solving nonlinear equations.  
  
Polynomial interpolation and least-squares problems. Numerical methods.  
  
Composite quadrature rules.  
  
The MATLAB environment.

## Prerequisites

Fundamentals of linear algebra and calculus.

## Teaching methods

1) Lessons carried out both using slides (for most lessons) and on the blackboard.  
2) Exercises in the computer room using the Matlab software for the implementation and discussion of algorithms.  
  
There are 36 hours of lectures and 18 hours of exercises in the computer laboratory (6 lessons of 3 hours).  
During the laboratory activity, exercises are first proposed to be carried out independently using Matlab. Then the implementation of the proposed exercises is discussed, and the results commented on in collective mode.

## Assessment methods

For each of the seven annual exams, the learning assessment includes a written test that contains theory questions, exercises and the "pen and paper" implementation of simple Matlab codes. Each question is assigned a score, for a total of 32 out of 30 (scores above 30/30 correspond to the 30L grade). The allotted time is two hours. During the test, the use of a calculator is permitted, while it is not permitted to consult books, handouts or notes. Examples of exams can be downloaded from the teaching moodle page. ## Extended programme

Algorithms. Floating point arithmetic. Finite precision. Norms of matrices and vectors. Conditioning of a problem. Stability of an algorithm. Direct methods for linear systems: Gauss method and pivoting strategies. Jacobi and Gauss-Siedel iterative methods for linear systems. Iterative methods for finding the roots of a nonlinear equation: Bisection, Newton and Secant methods; corresponding algorithms. Polynomial interpolation, spline interpolation. Least-squares problems. MATLAB: Handling matrices, operations between matrices. Function and script files. If, while and for commands. 2D e 3D plots. Built-in functions for linear systems, nonlinear equations, basic fitting.
