## Learning objectives

To acquire techniques and numerical tools for the analysis and control of uncertain and/or nonlinear systems.

## Contents

Optimal control of linear time invariant systems: the linear quadratic regulator.  
Robust control of linear time invariant systems: the loopshaping technique.  
Dynamical analysis of classical nonlinear feedback control systems: absolute stability criteria, periodic oscillations computation via the harmonic balance technique.

## Prerequisites

## Extended programme

1) OPTIMAL CONTROL OF LINEAR TIME INVARIANT SYSTEMS  
- Background on state-space representations. Observability and controllability. Regulator review.  
- Optimal control and dynamic programming. Linear Quadratic (LQ) regulator on a finite control horizon for discrete-time systems. Infinite-horizon LQ regulators.  
3) ROBUST CONTROL OF LINEAR TIME INVARIANT SYSTEMS  
- Signals norm (L-2) and systems norms (H₂ and H∞).System gains and input-output stability of feedback systems (small gain). Examples.  
-Nominal performance of feedback control systems in terms of weighted H∞ norm of sensitivity function S.  
- Models with (un)structured uncertainty for uncertain plants. Robust stability of feedback control systems in terms of weighted H∞ norm of closed loop function W. Examples.  
- Robust stability and robust performance in terms of weighted H∞ norm of S and W. Controller design via loopshaping for minimum phase nominal plants and extension to plants with unstable poles-zeroes. Examples.  
3) ANALYSIS OF CLASSICAL NONLINEAR FEEDBACK CONTROL SYSTEMS  
- Absolute stability problem of Lur’e control systems: circle and Popov criteria. Examples. Input-output stability and relation between circle criterion and small gain theorem.  
- Periodic solutions in nonlinear feedback control systems: harmonic balance and describing functions. Periodic solutions computation via describing function method, accuracy and stability prediction. Examples.
