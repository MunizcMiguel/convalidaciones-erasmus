## Learning objectives

With reference to the Dublin Descriptors.  
KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING  
1.1 Knowledge and understanding of the principles of operation of modern high-frequency analog front-end architectures.  
1.2 Knowledge of the parameters characterizing high frequency communication systems.  
1.3 Knowledge of CAD-assisted analysis methods of high frequency active electronic circuits.  
1.4 Knowledge of circuit topologies of functional blocks in high frequency systems.  
1.5 Knowledge of the design methods of functional blocks in use in high frequency systems..

2.2 ability to compare different technologies and architectures in relation to application requirements;  
2.3 ability to use CAD systems for the design of high frequency circuits.  
AUTONOMY OF JUDGMENT  
3.1 ability to understand the development of the layer of art and the generations of new technologies and architectures for high frequency applications;  
COMMUNICATION SKILLS  
4.1 Ability to logically expose the principles of operation of high frequency electronic subsystems:  
4.2 Ability to logically expose the correct application of the techniques used for the development of high frequency systems for use in telecommunications;  
ABILITY TO LEARN  
5.1 Ability to independently investigate operating principles and innovative properties of new systems and subsystems for high frequency applications;

## Contents

Analysis of electronic systems and subsystems for radio frequency, and microwave communications applications.  
Ability to design active radio frequency circuits and their experimental implementations.

## Prerequisites

Have acquired the basics contents of circuit theory, electronics fundamentals of electromagnetism.

## Teaching methods

Lectures on the topics of the Course, held at the blackboard, with the support of notes previously provided.  
Numerical exercises on the topics of the course, carried out on the blackboard, with the support of notes previously provided.  
Weekly laboratory exercise starting from the third week of the Course, aimed at the development of a microwave circuit using the techniques learned in the theoretical part of the course. This is followed by the phase of prototyping and performance verification at the laboratories of the Department of Information Engineering.

## Extended programme

Introduction to radio frequency systems: evolution of electronics and wireless systems;  
Basic concepts: noise, sensitivity and nonlinearity concepts in radio systems.  
Theory of high frequency networks and concepts related to power gain and adaptations;  
Methods of analysis of non-linear networks and in the envelope domain;  
Modulators and demodulators: fundamental configurations for balanced mixers and their use in modulators and demodulators;  
Oscillators and phase coupling rings: fundamental configurations for oscillators and principle of operation of the PLL;  
Power amplifiers: discussion of the various classes of operation and design techniques;  
8.Digital techniques in radios  
systems for the transmission of digitally modulated signals, characteristic aspects and their impact on the subsystems used;  
9.Exercises in CAD Laboratory  
design of a class AB amplifier and possible implementation at the Department’s “RF Microwave and Electromagnetism” Laboratory.
