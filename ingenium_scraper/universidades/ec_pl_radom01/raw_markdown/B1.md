## Learning outcomes, curriculum content
LEARNING OUTCOMES
C1 - Introduction of students to basic problems of environmental
protection, causes and after-effects of changes in
environmental as an effect of economic and civilisation
### Learning Objective: development.
C2 - Presentation of possibilities and ways of environment
degradation in the light of sustainable development.
LECTURES (15 h)
Legal conditions of environmental protection. General ecology.
(3 h);
