LEARNING OUTCOMES
The goal is to provide students with knowledge and develop
appropriate skills in the basic issues related to the operation

technical object. The subject of maintenance theory. Vehicle
maintenance principles. Maintenance strategies. Operation of

machine parts. Methodology for implementing a technical
maintenance system.

mechanical engine assemblies. Testing of operating fluids in
machines. Computer-aided support in machine diagnostics and
their working processes. Environmental aspects of machine
operation. Maintenance and repair of selected mechanical
assemblies.
