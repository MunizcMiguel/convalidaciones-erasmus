## Learning outcomes, curriculum content
LEARNING OUTCOMES
C1 – To acquaint students with issues related to optimization and
### Learning Objective:
types of optimization
C2 – Mastering the skills associated with defining the
optimization task
C3 – The use of software designed to optimize the structure
Content of lectures
Introduction to optimal design. Specifying the types of
optimization. Presentation of mathematical foundations in
structural optimization. Examples of the use of optimization in
issues not related to the optimization of the structure. Presentation
of the method of optimal construction design using strength
criteria. Definition of optimization task: objective function,
limitations. Solving optimization tasks in numerical calculation
software..
The content of laboratory exercises
### Curriculum Content: The use of software for numerical calculations in optimization. A
stand-alone solution for a single and multidimensional project.
Designing a structure/frame that meets the limitations imposed
during the classes, manual optimization of the designed structure,
optimization with using the software to find the optimal geometry,
a summary of results.
Topological optimization, a definition of optimization areas and
areas separated from optimization. Definition of new geometry
after topological optimization. With use of the dedicated software.
Manufacturability on an example of the geometry from the
topological optimization.
