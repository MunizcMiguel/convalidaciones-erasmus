## Learning outcomes, curriculum content
LEARNING OUTCOMES
The aim of the course is to acquire the ability to design
technological processes of machine parts together with the
### Learning Objective:
development of a project of a technological process of a selected
structural element.
The content of the classes is related to the conducted scientific
research.
Lecture. Production and technological processes and their
division. Input data for the design of the technological process.
Technological construction. Standardization and unification of
parts and assemblies. Types of semi-finished products, their
selection, preparation for processing. Factors affecting the choice
of blank. Rational selection of materials. Technological
documentation. Machining accuracy, product quality. Typization
of technological processes. Designing the technological process
### Curriculum Content:

and tools for a specific technological process; heat and surface
treatment as well as quality control and technical acceptance.
