## Learning outcomes, curriculum content
LEARNING OUTCOMES
This course provides an in-depth exploration of the legal and
economic frameworks that govern entrepreneurship. It examines
the essential legal principles that affect business formation,
operation, and dissolution, as well as the economic theories and
### Learning Objective:
practices that influence entrepreneurial ventures. Students will
gain a comprehensive understanding of the interplay between
legal structures and economic strategies in fostering successful
business initiatives.
Definition and importance of entrepreneurship
Overview of the entrepreneurial process
Sole proprietorships, partnerships, corporations, and LLCs
Choosing the right business entity
Types of intellectual property: patents, trademarks, copyrights
Protecting ideas and innovations
Basics of contract law
### Curriculum Content: Essential contracts for entrepreneurs (e.g., partnership agreements,
service contracts)
Understanding federal, state, and local regulations
Licensing, permits, and compliance issues
Employee vs. independent contractor distinctions
Labor laws and workplace regulations
Legal and economic aspects of global entrepreneurship
Cross-border regulations and trade agreements
