7.1 The general The main objective of the discipline "Design of digital systems" aims to deepen
objective of the the design and implementation of digital systems to obtain high-performance
subject digital systems.
7.2 Specific In order to achieve the main objective, the following specific objectives are
objectives pursued:
• To use manual or automated tools, to analyze or predict the performance of
digital systems in different operating conditions;
• To implement, simulate and test in Xilinx certain numerical systems;
• To identify, design and implement types of numerical systems.
