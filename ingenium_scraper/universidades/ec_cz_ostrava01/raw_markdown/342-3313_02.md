# Road Vehicles Design

## Type of Course Unit

Choice-compulsory

## Level of Course Unit

First Cycle

## Semester when the Course Unit is delivered

Winter Semester

## Mode of Delivery

Face-to-face

## Language of Instruction

English

## Prerequisites and Co-Requisites

Course succeeds to compulsory courses of previous semester

## Summary

The graduate will be able to describe the division and concept of the powertrain of road vehicles, including hybrid vehicles and electric vehicles. He will be able to calculate the driving dynamics in the longitudinal direction, driving resistance and braking parameters of the vehicle and describe the design of clutches, gearboxes, transfer cases and differential. He will be able to design and assess the design of the frame, body, axles, suspension, steering and spring systems of a road vehicle and defend the completed semester project.

## Learning Outcomes of the Course Unit

The course is focused on the basic design of the cars and subassemblies. The student will be able to understand the basic standardized nomenclatures and categories of vehicles, will gain basic knowledge about the dynamics of cars and will acquire a basic design overview of vehicles, and will be able to perform basic calculations of structural parts.
