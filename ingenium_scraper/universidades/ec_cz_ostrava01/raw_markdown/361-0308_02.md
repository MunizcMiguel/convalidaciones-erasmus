# Economics and Management in Energy Enginnering

## Type of Course Unit

Choice-compulsory

## Level of Course Unit

First Cycle

## Semester when the Course Unit is delivered

Summer Semester

## Mode of Delivery

Face-to-face

## Language of Instruction

English

## Prerequisites and Co-Requisites

Course succeeds to compulsory courses of previous semester

## Summary

The lectures will be given to the special economic and manager task by energy utilisation. A part of the lecture will be given to the municipal energy systems and supplying of energy at whole.

Students will acquire basic knowledge of business economics, the distribution of industrial processes, their specifics, ways of reducing harmful impact on the environment

Creative thinking is further developed in a theoretical manner calculations of possible measures.

Emphasis is placed on the economic and environmental aspects of traffic of energy equipment.

During the lesson will be the energy managemnt discussed

## Learning Outcomes of the Course Unit

The course focuses on the efficient management of resources in the field of economics and management in energetics

the subject requires independent critical thinking and the ability to project independently.

## Course Contents

Economics and management in the energy sector

- Managing the consumption side

- Control on the production side

- Consumption measurement

- Energy sources and efficient handling
