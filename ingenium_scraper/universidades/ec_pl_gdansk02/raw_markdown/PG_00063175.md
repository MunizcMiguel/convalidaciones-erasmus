## Subject objectives

Development and consolidation of German language command, including reading, speaking, writing and
translation in a technical environment.

## Subject contents

• Talking about learning foreign languages; discussing health and happiness; describing processes
• Future forms;
• Conditionals; debating; the language of emotion; talking about quantity and amount
• Accepting and rejecting ideas; predicting; justifying opinions; relative clauses
• Describing charts and graphs; writing reports; describing visual information; compound nouns
• Language of advertisements; modals (future); for and against arguments; describing uses
• Collocations; discussing safety; compound adjectives; agent nouns
• Comparison and contrast; modals (past); emphatic structures; shapes, measurements and descriptions
of objects
• Introduction to presentations; persuading; discussing hypothetical ideas; descriptions of objects
• Writing a formal letter; describing information; talking about differences and similarities; reading complex
texts; giving definitions
• Business terms and expressions; expressing opinions; discussing technological advances; explaining
safety procedures
• Past forms and narrative tenses; word sets; articles and the use of the passive; describing emergency
procedures
• Talking about success; idioms; asking about the way and giving directions
• Making offers and bargaining; talking about needs and expectations; conceding points; obtaining
information asking questions
Negotiating a contract; writing e-mails and leaflets; improving listening and dictionary skills; talking about
properties of materials

## Prerequisites and co-requisites

Before joining a language group at a particular level, the student must first attain the preceding level, i.e. A1
and co-requisites before joining an A2 group, A2 before joining B1, B1 before joining B2, B2 before joining C1 and C1 before
joining C2.
