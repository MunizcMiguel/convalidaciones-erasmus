## Subject objectives

Acquiring fundamental knowledge regarding the protection of intellectual and industrial property. The
intellectual property law course aims to introduce the definitions of copyright and legal protection
mechanisms. It compares the copyright system under common law with that of continental Europe. The
course covers the basics of copyright, patent, and trademark law in Europe.

## Subject contents

Course content – lecture
Definitions of protected categories: copyright and works; patents for inventions; protection rights for utility
models. National procedure (UPRP): patenting inventions and utility model protection, industrial design
registration, trademark applications (names and logos); Patent Office Bulletin and key legal acts.
International procedures. European Patent Office. Preparing and filing a European patent application.
Subject matter not considered an invention. UPRP databases.
