## Subject objectives

The objective of the course is to familiarize students with the basic laws of electrical engineering and the
basics of electrical and electromechanical energy conversion

## Subject contents

Principles and laws of electrical engineering. Measurements of electrical and non-electrical quantities.
Electric drives. Production and distribution of electricity in the power system. Basics of electronics and power
electronics. Rules for safe work with electrical devices

## Prerequisites and co-requisites

Knowledge of basic laws of physics. Ability to use tools of analytical mathematics
