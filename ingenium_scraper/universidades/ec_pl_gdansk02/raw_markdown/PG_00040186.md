## Subject contents

Definitions of ergonomics, their purposes and application area. Description of man - machine - environment
configuration. Conception of balanced development. Environmental management system. Model of man and
it"s characteristics. Man capabilities versus industrial processes. Environment of working man - circle
conditions. Designs principles of environment of working man. Safety and reliable man - machine -
environment configuration. Information acquisition of machines.
