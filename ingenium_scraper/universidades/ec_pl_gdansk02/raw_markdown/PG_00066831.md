## Subject contents

Course content – exercises
Lexis: verbs 'to have', 'to be' in the present tense, numerals up to 1000, polite phrases, greetings, farewells,
simple questions (How much does it cost? Who / What is this?), names of days of the week, drinks and
groceries, asking for directions, shopping, getting travel information, booking accommodation etc. Christmas
customs in Poland (Christmas or/and Easter). Grammar: present tense of some verbs, A subject form of
nouns . Language skills: Understanding politeness and simple questions. Active participation in social
situations. Attempts to use language in everyday life. Adressing academic teachers. Formal and informal
language.
