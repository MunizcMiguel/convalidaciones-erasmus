## Subject contents

1. Short history of sociology. Main traditions: functionalism, symbolic interactionism, conflict theory.
2. Basic sociological concepts: social control, institiutions, social roles.
3. Methodology of sociology: quantitive and qualitative methods, social network analysis.
4. Sociology of scientific knowledge: from positivism to the strong programme.
5. Science and technology studies: actor-network theory
6. Economic sociology: sociological tools in economic analysis.
7. Sociology of culture: socialization, mass media, gender.
