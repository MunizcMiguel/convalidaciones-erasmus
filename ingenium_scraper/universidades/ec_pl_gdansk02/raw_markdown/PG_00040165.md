## Subject contents

Electricity: Electric charge and electric field, Gauss' law, electric field potential, capacitance; current and
resistance. Magnetic field, magnetic induction; magnetic field from current-carrying wires. Electromagnetic
waves: propagation of waves, Poyntings vector, spectrum of electromagnetic waves.
