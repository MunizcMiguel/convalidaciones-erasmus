- Subject name and code Physical Education I, PG_00040162

## Subject objectives

Approving of healthy style of life by means of physical activity.
Developing a correct body posture and building up physical fitness.
Getting to know basic information about sports disciplines.
Using the acquired knowledge in order to choose the proper physical activity.
