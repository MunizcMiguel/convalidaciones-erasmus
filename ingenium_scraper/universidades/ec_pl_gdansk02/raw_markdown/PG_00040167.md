## Subject objectives

The aim of the classes is to learn the principles of technical drawing of machine parts and connections used
in machine building. Preparation of working and assembly drawings.

## Subject contents

Rules of assembly draing.
Permanent joints (wealding, soldering)
Non permanent joints (threads)
Normalized parts on drawing (bearings, gears, clutch, axies)
Sealing and flexible parts.
Electircal diagrams
Pneumatic and hydraulic diagram.
