## Subject contents

Course content – lecture
Classification of material groups. Material properties. Key issues related to design process. Methods of
materials selection in practical applications by using limited line/-es, guideline/-es on Ashby's diagrams.
Basic issues connected with materials selection taking into account the shape of elements. Fundamental
issues related to manufacturing process in the context of materials selection.
