### Objectives of the Course

To teach the fundamental principles of metrology and measurement methods used in engineering.

### Course Content

Importance and aim of metrology, Engineering quality control concepts, Statistical quality control, Tolerance and adjustment systems, Metrological characteristics of height and depth calipers, Angle measurement and angle metrology, Length reference standards, Mass Metrology, Force metrology, Temperature Metrology, Reporting of measurement results
