### Objectives of the Course

In order to analyse vehicle ride and handling characteristics, using pneumatic tires dynamic characteristics, determinin response of vehicle to the road inputs by modelling its plane motions.

### Course Content

Vehicle ride and handling characteristiks, pneumatic tire cornering force characteristics, plane motions of vehicle, stability analysis, four wheel steering systems, roll center approach, ride characteristics of vehicles, ride modelling of vehicles, description of road inputs, response of vehicles to road inputs, ride quality, optimization of passive suspension parameters.
