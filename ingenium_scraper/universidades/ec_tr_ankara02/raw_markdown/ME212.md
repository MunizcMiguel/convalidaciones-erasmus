### Objectives of the Course

1- Analyze the stresses in load carrying members subject to direct axial or shear force.

2- Understand the strain concept.

3- Determine the displacement of the long members subject to axial load.

4- Calculate the stresses and angle of twist in the shafts subject to torque.

5- Calculate the bending stresses in the beams.

6- Calculate the shear stresses in the beams subject to transverse shear force.

7- Analyze the stress state of the members subject to combined load.

8- Understand the concept of principal stresses and max in-plane shear stress by using the stress transformation equations and Mohr?s circle.

### Course Content

Introduction. Stress and Strain. Axial Load. Torsion. Bending. Transverse Loading. Combined Loading. Stress Transformation
