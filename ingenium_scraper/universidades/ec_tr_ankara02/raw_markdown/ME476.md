### Objectives of the Course

Students, will know the thermodynamic properties of air and can calculate heating, cooling and humidifying processes.

can calculate the cooling load of a room and design an air conditioning unit.

can design the air ducts carrying conditioned air.

have the knowledge of preparing an air conditioning project.

### Course Content

Introduction, Fundamentals of air conditioning, Psychrometric propeties and Psychrometric chart, Psychrometric chart (heaating and cooling), Psychrometric chart (humidifying and drying), Winter air conditioning, Summer air conditioning, Introduction to project, Cooling load calculations, Air duct calculations, Project calculations, Mass transfer, Mass transfer applications, Cooling tower, Cooling tower calculations and applications.
