### Objectives of the Course

Students,

1. understand refrigeration systems, main and auxilary components.

2. can analyze the vapour compression and absorption refrigeration systems.

3. know the parameters effecting refrigeration system performance.

4. can design a cold storage.

### Course Content

Fundamentals of Refrigeration, Refrigeration Systems, Vapour Compression Refrigeration Cycle, Single and double system; thermodynamic analysis, Single and double system; thermodynamic analysis, Vapor Compression Refrigeration Cycle; applications and auxiliary components, Alternative refrigeration systems, Cryogenic refrigeration and liquefaction systems, Heat Pump, Absorption refrigeration systems and cycle analysis, Refrigerants, Cold storage and cooling load calculations and Project
