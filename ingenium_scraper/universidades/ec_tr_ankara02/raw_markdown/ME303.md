### Objectives of the Course

The aim of the course is to provide the students with the ability to calculate basic machine elements and select the correct element type for any mechanical application, analytical calculation, design and analysis of how it works in a mechanical system.

### Course Content

1. Machinery, Machine elements and Engineering Design.

2. Load and Stress Analysis.

3. Bending and Stiffness.

4. Failure in Static Loading.

5. Fatigue Failure in Variable Loading.

6. Shafts, Axles and Shaft-Hub Connections.

7. Screws, Threaded Fasteners and Power Screws, Rivets.

8. Non-Detachable Joints; Welded, Brazed, Bonded Joints.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Ability to define loading types on machine elements. |
| 2 | Determine stresses and deformations under static and cyclic loadings |
| 3 | Understands shafts and axles, designs shafts and axles based on static and fatigue design criteria and performs strength checks. |
| 4 | Defines threaded fasteners (bolts-nuts) and power screws. Designs and performs strength calculations of bolt-nut and riveted joints and power screws. |
| 5 | Defines shaft-hub connections and performs strength calculations. Performs strength calculations of press fits and determines their tolerances. |
| 6 | Defines permanent joints (welded, brazed and bonded). Determines the strength of welded joints and designs weld attributes. |
