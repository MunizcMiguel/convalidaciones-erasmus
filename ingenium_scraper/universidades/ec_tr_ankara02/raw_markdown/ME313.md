### Objectives of the Course

To introduce the fundamental numerical methods used for the solution of engineering problems.

### Course Content

Finite difference, interpolation and extrapolation, solution of nonlinear equations, numerical integration and derivative, systems of linear equations and matrices, least squares method, solutions of ordinary differential equations, introduction to boundary value problems.
