### Objectives of the Course

Examining of basic mechanical events in nature with experimental and also study with fundamental information. Obtaining and learning of the experimental data and using this data

### Course Content

Introduction of laboratory and laboratory equipment, Physical measurement and error, Export to Experimental Data to the Table, Graph drawing and using graphs, writing reports, Velocity, Acceleration, Two dimensional motion, Newton?s Laws of Motion in the inclined Plane, Collisions, Simple Harmonic Motion
