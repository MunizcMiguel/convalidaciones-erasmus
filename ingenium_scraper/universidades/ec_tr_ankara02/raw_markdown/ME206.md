### Objectives of the Course

This course provides the student with an introduction to industrial manufacturing processes with limitations, and applications of different machine tools and engineering materials for product manufacturing. The overall aim is to establish the technical knowledge for selection, design, and planning of manufacturing processes and systems for a product.

### Course Content

Basics of manufacturing processes, fundamentals of casting, metal forming, welding, and metal machining processes.
