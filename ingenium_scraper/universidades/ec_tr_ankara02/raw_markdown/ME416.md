### Objectives of the Course

Within the scope of this course, students should recognize energy sources and have knowledge about energy conversion systems.

### Course Content

Energy Economics and global warming, Fuels and Combustion, Rankine Cycle analysis, Brayton Cycle Analysis, Combined power generation, Cogeneration systems, Solar Thermal, Hydroelectric and Wind, Geothermal, Energy Storage, Unconventional energy systems
