### Objectives of the Course

1.Understanding of technical, economical, and environmental properties of natural gas combustion systems and installations, their design and application

2.Applications of engineering analyses, design and optimization methods to research, system development, design, operation and optimization of natural gas

3.Natural gas supplies exterior and interior design and optimization

4.Gas-fired plants safety measures and security systems

5.Calculating the parameters of emission of gas plants

### Course Content

Scope and importance of natural gas engineering. Natural gas and other technical gaseous fuels. Production, processing, transportation, storage, distribution and utilization of naturtal gas. Design calculations of exterial and interial natural gas. Installations. Physical, combustion and emission properties of natural gas. Combustion of natural gas and combustion calculations. Natural gas burners and boilers. Fundamentals of natural gas boilers conversion and conversion problems of natural gas.
