### Objectives of the Course

The course is designed to give fourth year mechanical engineering students the fundamental physics of boiling, condensation and mass transfer. Students are instructed in the analysis and solution of basic two-phase heat transfer, heat exchangers and mass transfer problems, as supplemented by practical charts and tables as well as empirical correlations.

### Course Content

Basic laws of heat transfer. Boiling; pool boiling and correlations, forced convection boiling. Condensation; laminar and turbulent film condensation, dropwise condensation. Heat exchangers; heat exchanger analysis; the LMTD and Effectiveness-NTU method, heat exchanger calculation. Multimode heat and mass transfer. Diffusion mass transfer; rate equations, conservations of species, mass diffusion with and without homogeneous chemical reactions, transient diffusion.
