### Objectives of the Course

Within the scope of this course, students should be able to design and analyze mechanical systems with computers.

### Course Content

2D Design, Creating details on solid bodies, Creation and selection of working planes and applications, Advanced solid modeling, Assembly, Standard parts and their usages.
