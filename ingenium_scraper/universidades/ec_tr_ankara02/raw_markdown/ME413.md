### Objectives of the Course

The course aims to give engineering students a perspective on problems.

Students are aimed to learn the main steps of analysis by modeling and solving in computer environment.

It is aimed to teach the students how to write problems in an analytical form using numerical analysis methods.

### Course Content

Minimum total potential energy formulation. Weighted residual formulations. Solution of linear system of equations by developing element and general stiffness matrices and introducing the boundary conditions. Definitions of the one and two dimensional mesh elements. Numerical modellings of the truss and beam structures. Modellings of the plane elasticity problems. Learning of 1D, 2D and 3D modelling samples by ANSYS.
