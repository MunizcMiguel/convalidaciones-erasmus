### Objectives of the Course

Facilitate the students with basic principles of robotics,

Provide necessary mathematical background for kinematic and dynamic analysis, and control of robot manipulators.

### Course Content

Basic principles of robotics and mathematical background, rotation, transformation and Jacobian matrices, forward and inverse kinematic analysis of robot manipulators, velocity and force analysis, trajectory planning, and dynamics.
