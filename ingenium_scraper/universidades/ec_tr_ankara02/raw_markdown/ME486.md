### Objectives of the Course

To gain the knowledge about the types of material failures.

To gain the knowledge about the mechanisms of material failures.

To gain the knowledge to get an ability of solving failure problems.

To get knowledge about non metallurgical failures.

### Course Content

Introduction, Mechanical behavior of materials, Plane stress-plane strain conditions, stress concentration, types of fracture, crack propagation, ductile and brittle fracture, Weld defects, wrong heat treatment, residual stresses, hot and cold tearing, Micro fracture mechanisms
