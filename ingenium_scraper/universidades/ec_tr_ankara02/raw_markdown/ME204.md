### Objectives of the Course

Students should be able to analyze the power and cooling cycles, determine the thermodynamic properties, solve engineering problems related to the processes of psychrometrics applications and chemical and phase balance in the combustion processes of thermodynamic laws.

### Course Content

Thermodynamic relations, fundamental relations for simple compressible system, mixtures of pure substances, some power refrigeration cycles, vapor power cycles, air-standard power cycles, air standard refrigeration cycles, chemical reactions, combustion process, enthalpy of formation, enthalpy of combustion, higher and lower heating values of fuels, theoretical reaction temperature, adiabatic flame temperature, chemical equilibrium, equilibrium constant, one-dimensional flow of compressible fluids.
