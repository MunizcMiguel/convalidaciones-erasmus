### Objectives of the Course

1.To gain knowledge about engineering materials

2. To understand relation between microstructure and mechanical properties of engineering materials

3. To analysis the general criteria about the material selection in product design

### Course Content

1. Classification of engineering materials

2. Atom and crystal structure, crystal defects, microstructural examination of materials

3. Diffusion in metals

4. Phase diagrams and Iron-Carbon phase diagram

5. Material test methods and mechanical properties

6. Heat treatments

7. Strength hardening mechanisms
