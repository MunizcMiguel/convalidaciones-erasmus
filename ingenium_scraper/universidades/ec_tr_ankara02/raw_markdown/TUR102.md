### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course; To provide students with the basic skills of telling and expressing, to reinforce their correct and beautiful

use of Turkish, to provide them with knowledge about the academic writing process, to present a project or to enable them to

comprehend speaking methods and techniques that will be useful in making a prepared and impromptu speech, to help them

apply for a job in daily life, to work To provide the acquisition of speaking and writing skills that will be required in professional

practices such as interviewing

.

### Course Content

Composition Information: Plot, Plan, Main Idea, Supporting Idea

.

Paragraph Knowledge and Ways to Develop Paragraph Thinking

.

Text Creation and Composition Writing Practices

.

Types of Written Expression I: Thought Writings

Types of Written Expression II: Art Writings

Types of Written Expression III: Official Correspondence

Academic Writing I: The Academic Writing Process. Academic Text Types

.

Academic Writing II: Citation, Citation, Bibliography, Plagiarism, Copyright, etc. Ethical Principles

.

Reading: Types of Reading and Reading Comprehension Strategies

Listening: Types of Listening and the Relationship between Listening and Reading

Oral Expression I: Speech Disorders and Diction

.

Oral Lecture II: Types of Oral Expression

(Speech) (imprepared and prepared speeches

)

Oral Expression III: Correct, Beautiful and Effective Speech. Basic Principles of Oral Presentation

.
