### Objectives of the Course

Teach the basic working principles of internal combustion engines and the main elements and functions of engines. To gain the knowledge and skills necessary for the analysis of how engine performance is affected by design and operating parameters.

### Course Content

Thermodynamic cycles, analysis of ideal cycles in combustion engines, deviations from ideal processes, fuels, knock. Establishment of air fuel mixture, fuel injection, manifold and mixture distribution, valves and valve mechanisms, ignition systems, combustion chambers. Engine performance, piston and engine mechanics, balancing.
