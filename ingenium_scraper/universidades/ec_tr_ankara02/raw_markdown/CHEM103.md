### Objectives of the Course

### Course Content

Properties of the Matter and Its Measurements; Atoms, Atomic

Theory and Some Basic Concepts; Electronic Structure and Periodic

Properties of Atoms; Chemical Stoichiometry and Reaction Types;

Chemical Bonds; Gas Laws; Thermodynamics; Intermolecular

Forces, Liquids and Solids; Solutions and Its Properties; Chemical

Kinetics; Chemical Equilibrium; Acid-Base Chemistry and

Equilibrium of Aqueous Solutions; Electrochemistry.
