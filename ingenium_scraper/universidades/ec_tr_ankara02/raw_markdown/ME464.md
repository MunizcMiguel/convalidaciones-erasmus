### Type of Course Unit

### Course Delivery Method

### Objectives of the Course

To gain ability of engineering welding technology.

To understand welding methods and applications.

### Course Content

Introduction, welding metallurgy, welding methods, testing and inspection of welds, electrode selection, weld design and weld defects, robotic welding and automation in welding

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Ability to define metallurgical structures of welds |
| 2 | Understanding of gas metal reactions, effects of weld penetration and metal transfer ways |
| 3 | Classify welding methods, define and understanding of general working principles |
| 4 | Understanding of testing and inspection of welds |
| 5 | Ability to select proper electrode to welding |
| 6 | Ability to design weld joint and understanding of welding errors |
| 7 | Understanding of robotic welding and automation |
| 8 | Ability to apply common welding techniques practically |
