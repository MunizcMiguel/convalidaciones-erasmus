### Objectives of the Course

This course aims to teach students how to conduct literature review, project planning and project management, having knowledge of professional basic engineering and effective report writing in accordance with the rules.

### Course Content

Literature survey, project management, risk management, change management, professional basic engineering knowledge, effective report writing.
