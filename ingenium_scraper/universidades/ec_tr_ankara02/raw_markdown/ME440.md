### Objectives of the Course

1- Analyze the stress state of the members subject to combined load.

2- Learning the application of the failure theories.

3- Learning the design of the beams and shafts.

4- Learning the deflection of the beams.

5- Learning the buckling of columns.

### Course Content

Combined Loading. Stress Transformation. Theories of Failure. Design of Beams and Shafts. Deflection of Beams and Shafts. Buckling of Columns.
