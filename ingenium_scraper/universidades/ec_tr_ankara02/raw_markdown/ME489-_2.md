### Objectives of the Course

To review the principles of classical control. To teach basic concepts in intelligent and robust control approaches. To explain the fundamentals of neural networks and fuzzy logic. To explain robust control approaches including norm-based methods, sliding mode control, and backstepping control. To give information about adaptive control. To demonstrate intelligent and robust control applications.

### Course Content

Intelligent control. Neural networks. Fuzzy logic. Norm-based robust control. Sliding mode control. Backstepping control. Adaptive control. Machine learning. Intelligent control applications. Robust control applications.
