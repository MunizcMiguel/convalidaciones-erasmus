### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to provide students with the necessary knowledge and skills for the application of dimensional and geometric tolerances and surface finish marks in technical drawings, drawing of weldments, drawing of assembly and detail drawings of assemblies involving machine elements and effective use of CAD software in these activities.

### Course Content

Dimensional tolerances and geometric (shape and position) tolerances. Surface finish marks. Working drawings (detail and assembly) of screws, bolts-nuts, washers, studs, nuts, keys, pins, linkage pins, springs, welded joints, sliding-contact and rolling-contact bearings, gears, etc. and CAD applications.
