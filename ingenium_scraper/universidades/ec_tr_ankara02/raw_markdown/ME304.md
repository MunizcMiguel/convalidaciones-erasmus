### Objectives of the Course

The aim of the course is to provide the students with the ability to calculate basic machine elements design procedure and select the correct element type for any mechanical application, analytical calculation, design and analysis of how it works in a mechanical system.

### Course Content

1. Mechanical Springs.

2. Rolling-Contact Bearings.

3. Lubrication and Journal Bearings.

4. Gears-Basic Concepts, Spur, Helical, Bevel and Worm Gears.

5. Couplings, Clutches, Brakes and Flywheels.

6. Flexible Mechanical Elements.
