### Objectives of the Course

Within the scope of this course, students should be able to design heating, cooling, ventilation and air conditioning systems, learn and apply the principles of project design.

### Course Content

Design process and morphology in the design of Heating, Cooling, Ventilation and Air Conditioning installations. Problem solving and decision making. Modeling and simulation. Use of computers in design. Project engineering, planning and management. Design optimization. Economic decision-making cost and reliability. Human and ecological factors in design. Case studies. Term project assignment.
