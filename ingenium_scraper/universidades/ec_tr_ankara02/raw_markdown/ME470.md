### Objectives of the Course

To learn the production methods such as rolling, forging, extrusion using the knowledge of plastic deformation behavior of the materials.

### Course Content

Principals of metal working, Forging, wire drawing, extrusion, Basic die elements, Design of piercing-blanking dies, Plastics and metal pressure casting dies, Die materials and their heat treatments.
