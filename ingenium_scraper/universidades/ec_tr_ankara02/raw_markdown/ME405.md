### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to teach students general information about lifting and conveying machines, to improve their system design skills by solving engineering applications, and to teach analysis and design of projects of transport systems.

### Course Content

Industrial transport techniques and grouping of transported products. General information about transport machines. Design principles of lifting and transport machinery.
