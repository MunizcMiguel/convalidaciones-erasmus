### Objectives of the Course

To investigate methods and systems for determining and preventing air pollution. To examine emission measurement principles, calculation methods and legislation and practices on emission control.

### Course Content

The causes, contents, measurement and control methods of industrial air pollution. Investigation of sources of air pollution caused by process and combustion. Sectoral emission parameters, Methods of gas and particle emission measurement, Air quality and protection, regulations and laws to prevent air pollution.
