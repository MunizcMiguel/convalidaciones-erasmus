### Objectives of the Course

Understanding the concepts, theory and practice related to solar energy. To learn the applications and calculation methods of different types of solar collectors.

### Course Content

Fundamental concepts, radiation processes and properties, Solar energy, technology of solar energy and applications, Calculation of solar radiation on horizontal surfaces, Performance and theory of flat solar collectors.
