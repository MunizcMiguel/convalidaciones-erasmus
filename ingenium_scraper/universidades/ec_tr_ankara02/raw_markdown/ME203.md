### Objectives of the Course

To teach basic principles of classical thermodynamics. To train students to identify, formulate and solve engineering problems in thermodynamics. To teach the aplication of second law analysis methods for thermodynamic systems.

### Course Content

System and its surroundings, properties of a substance, Thermodynamic equilibrium, ideal gas equation of state, energy. Transfer of energy between system and its surrounding, change state and thermodynamic process, reversible and irreversible processes, simple system, enthalpy, constant-volume and constant-pressure specific heats, pure substance thermodynamic surfaces. The first law of thermodynamics for a control volume, the second law of thermodynamics, entropy, numerical value of entropy. The second law of thermodynamics for a control volume, efficiency, Thermodynamical analysis of processes, the third law of thermodynamics.
