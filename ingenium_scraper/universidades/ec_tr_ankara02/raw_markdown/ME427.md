### Objectives of the Course

This course aims to introduce students to the basic concepts and basic terminology of automotive engineering, to present the fundamentals of the performance of land vehicles, and to provide them with a qualitative and scientific computational understanding of the performance of land vehicles.

### Course Content

Basics of vehicle configurations, basic design considerations. Fundamentals of tire mechanics. Resistances to motion. Vehicle performance calculations, acceleration, velocities, gradeability. Power trains, basic power train requirements, determination of gear ratios. Basic break requirements, braking force distribution.
