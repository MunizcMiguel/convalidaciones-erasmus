### Objectives of the Course

To understand the fundamentals of fracture mechanics.

To be able to make fracture analysis for the engineering structures.

### Course Content

Introduction. Basic concepts. Energy method. Stress intensity approach. Griffith energy Equation. Stress analysis around the crack. J-Integral. Fatigue crack.
