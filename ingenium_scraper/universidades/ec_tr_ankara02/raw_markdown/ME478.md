### Objectives of the Course

The main aim of the course is to teach students the principles of operation and use of directional control, flow control, pressure control valves, hydraulic pumps and motors used in hydraulic circuits, to improve their ability to use pumps and motors, filters and hydraulic fluids efficiently, to recognize, to read and to design hydraulic and pneumatic circuits. to gain skills.

### Course Content

Hydraulic system components, properties and application areas. Pumps and their properties. Hydraulic valves and flow characteristics. Directional, flow and pressure control valves. Reservoirs, accumulators, drive components. Filtering, design of hydraulic circuits. Closed loop control forms. Electrohydraulic control elements.
