### Objectives of the Course

Investigation of mechanical properties of human structural elements such as bones, muscles, cartilages, neurons, blood etc.

### Course Content

General anatomic information. Mechanical properties of biological materials such as bones, cartilages, muscles, tendons, neurons and soft tissues. Classification of biological structures: arm, foot, skeleton and knee. Dynamics of musculoskeletal system. Strength of biological materials. Stress-strain analysis.
