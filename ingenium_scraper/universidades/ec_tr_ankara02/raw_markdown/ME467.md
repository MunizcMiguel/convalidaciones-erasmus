### Objectives of the Course

To teach the machining theory and applications on machine tools.

### Course Content

Cutting mechanics, Friction in metal cutting, Forms of wear in metal cutting, Economics of metal cutting, Surface roughness, Temperatures in metal cutting, Turning machines, Hole making and sizing operations, Straight and contour cutting, Surface finish operations.
