### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to teach the principles of technical drawing and to impart technical drawing knowledge and skills through comprehensive assignments and drawing exercises in a CAD environment.

### Course Content

Basic geometric drawings. Drawing the principal views of objects from pictorial drawings. Understanding objects from principal views and drawing other views. Principles of dimensioning. Principles of sectioning methods. Principles of three dimensional solid modelling in a CAD environment.
