### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course; To ensure that students have knowledge about the importance of language in culture and social life, to

teach the necessary information about the use of language by comprehending the power of expression of Turkish, to gain basic

skills about comprehension and written expression, and to reinforce the skills of using Turkish correctly and beautifully

.

### Course Content

Definition of language, basic features of Turkish language, language-culture relationship and cultural carrier feature of language. Differences between spoken and written language.

The place and characteristics of Turkish among the world languages, historical periods and important works of the Turkish language.

Use and importance of punctuation marks.

Writing rules: Spelling of some suffixes and prepositions. Spelling of proper names, numbers, borrowed words. Where uppercase and lowercase letters are used.

Official correspondence: Petition, minutes. Application studies related to these species.

Official correspondence. Report, business letter, resume. Application studies related to these species.

Current expression disorders at word level.

Expression disorders at the sentence level.

Paragraph creation.

Paragraph analysis.

Creating text related to the field.

Criticism and review writing.

Creating critiques and reviews.
