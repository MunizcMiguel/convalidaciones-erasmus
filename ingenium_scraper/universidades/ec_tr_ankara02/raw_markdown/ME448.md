### Objectives of the Course

Understanding of technical, economical, and environmental properties of combustion systems. In the economic sector of combustion systems to determine the most appropriate applications. To teach the fundamental properties, importance and application of fuels, combustion and combustion systems in engineering. Learing the applications of related analyses, design and optimization methods for research, system development, design, operation and optimization.

### Course Content

Scope and importance of natural gas engineering. Natural gas and other technical gaseous fuels. Production, processing, transportation, storage, distribution and utilization of natural gas. Design calculations of external and internal natural gas. Installations. Physical, combustion and emission properties of natural gas. Combustion of natural gas and combustion calculations. Natural gas, burners and boilers. Fundamentals of natural gas boilers conversion and conversion problems of natural gas.
