### Objectives of the Course

To teach the structure, capabilities and programming of CNC machine tools.

### Course Content

Coordinate systems and motions, Types of Numerical Control (NC) machine tools, MCU (machine control unit) and other components of the NC systems, NC part programming, NC part programming, Fundamentals of NC, DNC and AC systems, NC feed drive systems, Computer-Machine interfacing
