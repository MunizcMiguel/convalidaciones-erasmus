### Objectives of the Course

Students are expected to form all systems necessary to process selected products starting from the products? drawings and market research and prepare reports.

### Course Content

Introduction to manufacturing plant design and planning. Necessary data for plant design and their collection. Market research and determination of sales quantities. Part and product design drawing evaluation. Manufacturing methodologies selection. Machine selection and workload calculation. Material handling systems selection. Necessary equipment design or purchase. Plant layout. Preparation of technical and economical feasibility reports.
