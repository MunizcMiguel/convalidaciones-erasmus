### Objectives of the Course

1- Learning the Force and Moment concepts.

2- Learning the static equilibrium concept.

3- Gaining the ability to make structural analysis.

4- Learning the internal loadings concept.

5- Learning the friction concept.

6- Learning the centroid and moment of inertia concepts.

### Course Content

Basic Definitions. Force Vectors. Equilibrium of A Particle. Moment. Equilibrium of a Rigid Body. Structural Analysis. Frames and Machines. Internal Forces. Friction. Centroid. Moment of Inertia.
