### Objectives of the Course

Teach derivation and application of basic equations in differential form governing the fluid motion, solution of differential equations to find velocity distribution, calculation of forces exerted by flows on bodies, introduce the dimensional analysis, similitude and the boundary layer concept.

### Course Content
