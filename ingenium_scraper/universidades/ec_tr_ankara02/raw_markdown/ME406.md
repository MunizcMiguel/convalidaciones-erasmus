### Objectives of the Course

Within the scope of this course, students should be able to design hydraulic machines and learn and understand their applications.

### Course Content

Pumps, axial flow and centrifugal pumps, Design of centrifugal pumps, performance characteristics, Operations applications and design analysis of pumps, Water turbines, Pelton turbines, special type action and reaction turbines, Francis, Kaplan and propeller turbines, Recent developments in turbines, Turbines operations, Design applications, Applications of hydraulic machinery
