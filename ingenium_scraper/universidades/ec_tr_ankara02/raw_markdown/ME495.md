### Objectives of the Course

To teach basic concepts in mechatronics. To explain the mechatronic design philosopy. To introduce essential actuators and sensors utilized in mechatronics. To teach the modeling and control of mechatronic systems. To introduce with the noteable softwares used in the modeling and control of mechatronic systems. To demonstrate sample mechatronic systems. To give information about the mechatronic applications in defence industries.

### Course Content

Mechatronic systems. Electromechanical, hydraulic, and pneumatic actuators. Mechatronic sensors. Modeling and control of mechatronic systems. Softwares used in mechatronic modeling and control. Mechatronic system applications. Mechatronic systems in civil and military areas.
