### Objectives of the Course

The students are expected to develop computer programs to solve mechanical engineering problems by writing codes in MATLAB

### Course Content

Introduction to basics of computer systems, computer organization, introduction to algorithms, flowcharts and programming, MATLAB programming language and data structure
