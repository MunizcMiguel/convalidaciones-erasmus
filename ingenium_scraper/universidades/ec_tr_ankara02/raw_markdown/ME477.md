### Objectives of the Course

In this course, it is aimed to improve the students ` application and measurement skills with the help of stress-strain relations and strain gauges in engineering mechanics.

### Course Content

Basic principles of engineering mechanics, learning of stress-strain relations, learning of strain gauges and realization of experimental measurement methods, gaining experimental application ability.
