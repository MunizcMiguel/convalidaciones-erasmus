### Objectives of the Course

To give information about the detailed structure and design of plasma systems.

### Course Content

Investigation of the use of plasma technology in today's application areas and engineering branches. Use of plasma technology in different application areas. The obstacle in front of the technology. Use in energy production and waste disposal. Establishing design capability by understanding system elements.
