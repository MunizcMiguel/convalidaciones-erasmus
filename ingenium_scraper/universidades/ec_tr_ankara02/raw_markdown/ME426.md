### Objectives of the Course

The main objective of this course is to model the dynamic systems by using their energetic behaviors and obtaining the dynamic equations based on the linear graph theory. An additional objective of the course is to get linearization of nonlinear equations and the time and frequency responses of dynamic systems.

### Course Content

Modeling of physical systems. Energy ports. One-port and two-port elements. Mechanical, electrical, fluid and thermal system elements. Linear graph. Derivation of dynamic equations. Modeling of impure elements. State variables and obtaining state equations in standard A-matrix form. Linearization. Time response. Frequency response.
