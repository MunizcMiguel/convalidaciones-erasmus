### Objectives of the Course

This course aims to model dynamic systems and analyze and design dynamic systems by using control systems.

### Course Content

Modeling of physical systems and obtaining dynamic equations. Transfer functions and block diagrams. Basic concepts of automatic control. Control actions. Time response. Stability and Routh-Hurwitz criterion. Analysis and design of control systems by root locus method. Analysis and design of control systems by frequency response method.

| Week | Topics | Study Materials | Materials |
