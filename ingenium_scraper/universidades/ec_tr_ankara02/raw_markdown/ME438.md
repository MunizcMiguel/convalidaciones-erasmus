### Objectives of the Course

Combustion phenomenon, which is used as the most common method in energy conversion, is a subject that is being studied intensely and its application areas are becoming widespread. Since combustion can be used in many processes of the industry as well as in transportation vehicles, it is aimed to educate students about an important subject to examine the scientific and practical aspects of combustion with 0, 1, and multidimensional models.

### Course Content

Combustion and flame types, Combustion and thermo-chemistry, Introduction to mass transfer, Chemical kinetics, Conservation equations for reacting flows, Laminar premixed flames, Laminar diffusion flames, Droplet evaporation and burning, Burning of solids
