### Objectives of the Course

The aims of this course are;

to improve the ability of interpreting historical events, to learn the importance of having an independent state and to comprehend the value of the homeland, to understand current social and individual problems in Turkey, to learn Ataturk's principles and reforms with the founding philosophy of the Republic of Turkey, to appreciate national unity and the country's territorial integrity and to reach the level of developed countries.

### Course Content

Basic Concepts of History of Revolution History, World and Ottoman Empire at the beginning of the 20th Century, The Last Period of the Ottoman State, The First World War, The Preparation Period of the National Struggle.
