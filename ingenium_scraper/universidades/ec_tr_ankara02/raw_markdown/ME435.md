### Objectives of the Course

To inform students about the definition, importance, purpose and places of use of heat exchangers, to inform them about the classification of heat exchangers, to be able to design various heat exchangers and to make thermal calculations, to have knowledge about design parameters effective in heat exchangers, to be able to design and plan various types of heat exchangers according to their usage areas. to gain skills.

### Course Content

Classification of Heat Exchangers, Basic Design Methods of Heat Exchangers, Heat Exchanger Pressure drop and Pumping Power, Shell and tube heat exchangers, Compact Heat Exchangers
