### Objectives of the Course

To understand the role of mechanical engineers in industry.

### Course Content

Get information about the organization, factory layout and operation in the possible job sites after they start working life. Examine the organizational structure of the Company and the units in the organization during their internship. They will also examine the duties and responsibilities of engineers in the Company and the hierarchy of employees (manager, engineer, technician, technician, worker, etc.).
