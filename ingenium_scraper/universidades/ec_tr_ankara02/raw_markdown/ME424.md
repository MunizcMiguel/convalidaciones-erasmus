### Objectives of the Course

This course aims to provide students with the competence to design a machine or a mechanical system that performs specific tasks using realistic constraints and using the information they have learned from the courses they have taken during their Mechanical Engineering education.

### Course Content

The design process. Problem solving and decision making. Modeling and simulation. Use of computers in engineering design and CAD. Project engineering, planning, and management. Design optimization. Economic decision making: cost and reliability. Human and ecological factors in design. Case studies.
