# Design and Economics of Power Facilities

- Code: E151115
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

General principles of design work in investment activities in the energy sector are
discussed in this course. Attention is focused on mastering the methodology for evaluating
the economic efficiency of investment projects and economic optimization of technical
solutions.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Basic knowledge of energy systems (power plant, heating plant, Renewable Energy Sources
systems).

## Syllabus of lectures

1) Basic economic principles (time value of money, updates, depreciation, cash flow
calculation)
2) Breakdown of profit and production costs in the energy sector
3) Technical and economic effectiveness of design options
4) Criteria of technical and economic efficiency (NPV, IRR, return) and their application
in the evaluation of projects
5) Selection of the optimal solution, sensitivity and risk analysis
6) Basic questions of designing energy resources (localization, the optimal amount
of power)
7) Parameter optimization of conventional and nuclear power plants
8) Externalities of Energy sources

1) Basic economic principles (time value of money, updates, depreciation, cash flow

2) Criteria of technical and economic efficiency (NPV, IRR, return) and their application
