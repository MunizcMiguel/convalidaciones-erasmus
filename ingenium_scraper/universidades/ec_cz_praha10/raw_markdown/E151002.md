# Nuclear Power Principles

- Code: E151002
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Physical fundamentals of nuclear energy. Development and heat removal from core. Basic
materials for nuclear reactors. Basic types of nuclear reactors. Review of advanced
types of nuclear reactors. Fuel cycle. Reactor radiation, detection and quantification,
determination of radiation doses. Problems of nuclear safety and technical provisions.
