# Technology II. Seminary

- Code: E346007
- ECTS: 2.0

## Synopsis

This seminar is intended to exercise and deepen student?s knowledge from the course
Technology II. In this course students will have a chance to practice the numerical
exercises from cutting conditions calculation, machinability and Taylor's equation.
In this course the consultations of the semestral project from Technology II will
be presented.

## Requirements / prerequisites

(nothing)

## Syllabus of tutorials

1. Metrology tasks - length measurement.
2. Metrology tasks - shape and geometrical constrains evaluation.
3. Theory of cutting - cutting forces.
4. Theory of cutting - cutting temperature.
5. Theory of cutting - tool wear, Taylors equation, machinability and tool quality.
6. Technolgical codtions and their calculations.
7. Cutting condtions selection for turning.
8. Cutting condtition selection for mililng, drilling.
9. Machining volume calculation for different technologies.
10. Manufacturing process division.
11. Example of process plan for a shaft-type part.
12. Example of process plan for disc shaped part.
13. Example of process plan for non-rotational part.

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Goal is to complete the knowledge from the course Technology II.
