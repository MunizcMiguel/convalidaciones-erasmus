# Mathematics Repetitory

- Code: E013044
- ECTS: 2.0

## Synopsis

Lessons are intended for students who expect problems at exams from mathematics. Lessons
have a form of seminary with a short introduction to theory and a variety
of exercises ranging sometimes even from level of grammar school and aimed mainly
to Mathematics I and III.
