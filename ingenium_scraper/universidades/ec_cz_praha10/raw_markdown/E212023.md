# Project and 3D CAD

- Code: E212023
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

Basic modeling knowledge in CATIA V5 (solid and surface modeling, assembly creation,
drafting, FEM analysis). Design and calculations of road vehicle suspension is the
product of individual student's work.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Working with PC (Windows), descriptive geometry, technology, mechanical design, mechanics
of solid and deformable structures.
Information on the organisation of the course, special conditions for the award of
ungraded assessment, graded assessment or examination and study materials can be found
on the study material server https://studium.fs.cvut.cz/studium/u12120/E212023_PRO .

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Student should be able to work with CATIA V5 system. He is able to combine his knowledge
from different subject in solving a mechanical structure.
