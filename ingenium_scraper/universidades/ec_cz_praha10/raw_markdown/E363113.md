# Project III.

- Code: E363113
- ECTS: 10.0

## Synopsis

The project for precision mechanics and optics, including presentation. It develops
the student´s capabilities to go through all stages of the engineering problem solution:
in particular the formulation of the problem under research, conceptual design of
the solution, its optimization and bringing to design solutions. Projects will be
awarded on specified topics from industry or research projects.

## Syllabus of lectures

1. Project formulation.
2. Discussion of the research related to the topic of the project.
3. Assessment of the first proposals for the problem solution.
4. Specification necessary experimental verificatoins of the problem solution.
5. Discussions of the various options and choose the most appropriate method of solution.
6. The distribution schedule of activities for the implementation of the selected
solution.
7. Development of experimental parts of the project.
8. Forum of calculations and numerical models of solutions.
9. Modifications solutions in relation to the calculations and functions of the individual
units.
10. Preparation of drawings.
11. Discussion over the drawings.
12. Finalizing solutions.
13. Presentation of the proposed solutions.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Solution task, conceptual design, optimization, presentation

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

The aim of the course is to teach mastering solution to a complex problem alone. Based
on experience with the team solve the problem, the student will have to deal with
the individual project task in relation to the limited time and possibilities of realization
to the topic under research. The course prooves engineer capabilities of the student.
