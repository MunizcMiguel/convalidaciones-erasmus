# Project I.

- Code: E153051
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

The course deals with design of selected thermal cycles of power plants, heating plants,
decentralized power supply systems, or one selected components from cycles (eg. boilers,
heat exchangers, turbines, fuel cell, steam generators, compressors, pumps, etc.).

## Requirements / prerequisites

According to the task of the Head of student project.

## Syllabus of lectures

Course has not prescribed lectures only exercise.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Course aims to guide in the form of consultation in the range of 5 hours per week.
