# Engineering Simulation of Controlled Processes

- Code: E371085
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Operator and Engineering Simulators - basic types, use. Software support for industrial
process modelling - examples from power plants and engines.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Knowledge of terminology and fundamental principles of simulator development and control
algorithm design within the range of the topics presented in the lectures.

## Syllabus of lectures

Operator and engineering simulators are modern tools for ensuring readiness of operating
workers for an unexpected occurrence of a failure, for providing support in innovation,
proposed algorithm changes, eventually in forecasting future development.
Software support for industrial process modelling -
examples from power plants and engines.

## Syllabus of tutorials

The course is proposed for a small group of students in a computer laboratory, where
the lecture and exercise part are mixed, i.e. each topic is followed by a computer
simulation performed by students themselves.
