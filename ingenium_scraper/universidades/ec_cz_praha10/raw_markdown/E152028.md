# Energy Audit and Legislation

- Code: E152028
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

The course deals with the methodology and the importance of an energy audit. Shows
the application of the law on energy management on rational energy use in the municipal
sector and industry, environmental laws, including the basic laws related to energy
and the environment influenced by energy sources, including European legislation.
The course also deals with the analysis of sustainable development from the perspective
of energy in relation to the environment and the working environment. The subject
also includes processing, evaluation and presentation of a simple energy audit.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Basic knowledge of Excel Word Power Point, basic knowledge of energy sources and equipment
(definition of efficiency, fuel consumption, etc.), basic thermodynamics.

## Syllabus of lectures

Syllabus of lectures:
The methodology and the importance of the energy audit.
Application of the Law on energy management, rational energy use in the municipal
sector and in industry.
Environmental Law.
Basic legislation related to energy and environmental protection of the affected energy
resources.
Sustainable development in terms of energy. Living and working environment.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Training course curriculum will focus on the design, processing, evaluation and presentation
of a simple energy audit for selected energy source or consumer.
