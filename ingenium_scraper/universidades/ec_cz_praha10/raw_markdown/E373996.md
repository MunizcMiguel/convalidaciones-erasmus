# Diploma Thesis

- Code: E373996
- ECTS: 20.0

## Synopsis

Each student will solve his individual theme under guiding of his individual supervising
department specialist. Result is his/her diploma thesis. This Diploma Thesis is intended
for students, who work on more complex projects, or project, which is part of department
research (including publication), or in colaboration with industry. Containing more
then 500 hours of home preparation or individual work.
