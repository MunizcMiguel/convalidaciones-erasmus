# Selected Items of Strenght of Materials

- Code: E111506
- ECTS: 4.0

## Syllabus of lectures

Variational principles in elasticity and strength: Force and deformation variant of
the solution of bar systems,
The principle of virtual displacements and virtual forces.
The principle of the minimum of total potential energy and the principle of the minimum
of complementary energy,
Approximate solutions of bar systems and beams.
Theory of bending of thin plates of general shape: Equilibrium equations (definition
of internal force effects).
Kirchhoff's theory. An example solved both in vector and scalar.
Anisotropic materials, composites: History of composite materials, technology of production
of composite materials,
Unidirectional composite materials, strength criteria,
Classical lamination theory, composites with random reinforcement,
Sandwiches, thin-walled composite pressure vessels - cylinder,
Sphere, various shapes (hemispherical, ellipsoid, geodetic isotenzoid,)
Statistics in theories of strength and fatigue: Statistical methods of data evaluation,
Construction and materials - inspiration by nature, myths and reality about composites,
fatigue of composites
