# Master Thesis Project

- Code: E183000
- ECTS: 16.0

## Synopsis

Master thesis project is an independent activity of the student working on his/her
final thesis. The project tests students ability to comprehensively apply theoretical
knowledge and obtained skills such as logical and technical thinking, deep understanding
specific issue or using technical and research literature.
