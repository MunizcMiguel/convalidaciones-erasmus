# Radiant and Industrial Heating

- Code: E161102
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Student will be informed about the basics of radiant and other industrial heating
systems

## Requirements / prerequisites

Following requirements are demanded:
Heat conduction in rod. Heat conduction in plate with cylindrical heat sources. Thermal
radiation. Large-space radiant heating. Heating with embedded pipes - Floor, Electric
floor, Wall and Ceiling heating. Heating with suspended radiant strips. Heating with
plague and tube gas radiant heaters. Combination of radiant heaters and heat recovery.

## Syllabus of lectures

Heat conduction in rod. Heat conduction in plate with cylindrical heat sources. Thermal
radiation. Large-space radiant heating. Heating with embedded pipes - Floor, Electric
floor, Wall and Ceiling heating. Heating with suspended radiant strips. Heating with
plague and tube gas radiant heaters. Combination of radiant heaters and heat recovery.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Practise: Heat conduction in rod. Heat conduction in plate with cylindrical heat sources.
Thermal radiation. Large-space radiant heating. Heating with embedded pipes - Floor,
Electric floor, Wall and Ceiling heating. Heating with suspended radiant strips. Heating
with plague and tube gas radiant heaters. Combination of radiant heaters and heat
recovery.

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Student should be fully informed about the basics of radiant and other industrial
heating systems
