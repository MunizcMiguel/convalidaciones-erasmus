# Energy conversion of biomass

- Code: E151703
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

The course comprises of following topics:
biomass in context of energy mix in the Czech Republic and Europe
biomass characteristics, specifications, general properties, formation of biomass
biomass as a fuel
thermochemical conversions: combustion
thermochemical conversions: gasification
thermochemical conversions: pyrolysis
thermochemical conversions: hydrothermal conversion
production of liquid biofuels: vegetable oils
production of liquid biofuels: ethanol
biogas
