# Introduction to Transport Technology

- Code: E131061
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

1. Introduction, introduction of the institute, aim of the course, repetition of basic
knowledge in the field of design.
2. Means of conveyor technology (chains, belts, roller conveyors,)
3. Conveyor drive units (motors, gears, clutches)
4. Sensors and control of conveyor lines
5. Principles of construction of machines for agriculture incl. related legislation
and standards
6. Principles of construction of machines for construction incl. related legislation
and standards
7. Principles of construction of machines for forestry incl. related legislation and
standards
8. Principles of machine design for the automotive industry incl. related legislation
and standards
9. Principles of construction of crane and elevator technology machines, incl. related
legislation and standards
10. Experiment as a starting point for design activities
11. Principles of keeping technical documentation
12. Means of intellectual property protection
13. Possibilities, use and pitfalls of application of new technologies
