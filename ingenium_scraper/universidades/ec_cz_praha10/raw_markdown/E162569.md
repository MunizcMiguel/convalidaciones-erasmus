- Code: E162569
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Develop knowledge and facility of measuring method in environmental engineering

## Syllabus of lectures

Application knowledge and facility of measure method of variables in environmental
engineering on complex measure exercises. Basses of measure methods of variables in
