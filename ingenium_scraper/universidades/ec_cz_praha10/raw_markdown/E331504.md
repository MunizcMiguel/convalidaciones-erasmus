# Welding Technology

- Code: E331504
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Division of individual welding methods and description of their basic principles,
used equipment, applications and method of use. Flame welding and oxygen cutting,
electric arc, welding sources for arc welding, manual welding with a coated electrode
(manual metal arc), submerged arc welding, welding in protective atmospheres, resistance
welding. Division and description of individual soldering methods. Basics of industrial
adhesive bonding. Basics of quality control of the welding process (qualification
of welding personnel and welding procedures). Basics of inspection of welded joints
(non-destructive and destructive methods). Basics of weldability of metallic materials.

## Syllabus of lectures

1) Introductory lecture - basic concepts, division of welding methods and their designation
2) Oxy- fuel welding and related technologies
3) Fundamentals of electric arc
4) Sources for arc welding
5) Manual electric arc welding with a coated electrode
6) Introduction to welding in protective atmospheres
7) GMAW (Gas Metal Arc Welding)
8) GTAW (Gas Tungsten Arc Welding)
9) Welding by SAW (Submerged Arc Welding) and ESW (Electroslag welding) method
10) Resistance welding
11) Basics of soldering and brazing
12) Basics of adhesive bonding
13) Basic of thermal cutting
