# Continuum Mechanics

- Code: E111083
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Continuum kinematic, finite strain, rotation and displacement. The Law of Conservation
of Mass. Lagrangian and Almansi Strain Tensor. First and second Piola-Kirchhoff Stress
Tensor, Biot stress. Constitutive Equations, Newtonian fluid.
