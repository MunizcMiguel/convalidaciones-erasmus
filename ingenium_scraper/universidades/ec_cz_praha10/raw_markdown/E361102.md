# Instrument Design II

- Code: E361102
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Principles of the R&amp;D of instruments, overview on usable mechanical and
optical elements. Influence of these elements and its space-organisation
on the instrument error. Optimisation of the designer team-work,
participation of R&amp;D to the quality of product. Some physical phenomena
and its exploatation in the observation and measurement. Image information
and its evaluation. Spectrometer, spectrometry, instrumental profile.
Devices for basic research and its design. Instruments-setting,
suppression of the influence of precinct. Devices for measurement of
distances and coordinate, position and contact sensors. Measuring
instrument as an interface of computer. Prototypes for seriál production,
rapid prototyping.
