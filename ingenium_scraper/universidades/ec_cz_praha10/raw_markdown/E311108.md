# Mechanics III.

- Code: E311108
- ECTS: 6.0

## Synopsis

Modeling. Dynamics of systems of particles. Dynamics of body. Mass distribution in
a body. Inertia tensor. D'Alembert principle. Inertial effects of motion. Balancing
of rotating bodies. Free body diagram method. Newton-Euler equations. Dynamics of
multibody systems. Vibrations of systems with 1 DOF. Free oscillations. Forced oscillations
excited by harmonic force and rotating unbalanced mass. Kinematic excitation. Oscillation
of systems with two DOFs, torsional oscillation. Hertz theory of impact.
