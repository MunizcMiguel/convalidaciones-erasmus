# Nuclear power Energy

- Code: E151095
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

The subject builds on and expands knowledge
1. Fundamentals of nuclear physics I
2. Fundamentals of nuclear physics II
3. Development and heat removal from the reactor
4. Operation and control of the reactor
5. Core materials I
6. Core materials II
7. Basic types of power reactors I
8. Basic types of power reactors II
9. Prospective types of power reactors
10. Fuel cycle
11. Reactor radioactive waste
12. Nuclear reactor safety
13. Nuclear energy and the environment
