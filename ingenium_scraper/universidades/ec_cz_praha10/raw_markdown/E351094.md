# Production Technology

- Code: E351094
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Manufacturing machines and equipment contains three basic parts. These are forming
machines, machine tools and industrial robots and manipulators. Characteristics of
machines and equipment for realization of discrete technological processes will be
explained, technical parameters, basics of construction of production machines and
equipment, OS, TS design, automation of production machines and equipment, industrial
manipulators and robots, their applications, single-purpose and modular machines,
production lines. Examples of applications of production machines and equipment.

## Syllabus of lectures

Handling equipment - introductory terms and definitions
Industrial robots and manipulators (PRaM) and working heads PRaM
Automated technological and machining centers
Automated workplaces for forming and welding
Importance, division and basic classification of forming machines
Stroking and forcing forming machines
Energy forming machines, Die casting machines
Plastic processing machines
Technological classification of machine tools
Sorting machine tools according to the degree of automation
Supporting systems
Machining centers and multifunction machines

## Syllabus of tutorials

Two-button control of automatic cycle
Hydraulic circuits and its identification
Service, safety and construction of forming machines
Parameters of bending forming operation
Geometric accuracy of SU 32 lathe
Static rigidity of OS-headstock FA3V

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Students will learn the basics of machine tools, forming machines, industrial robots
and manipulators.
