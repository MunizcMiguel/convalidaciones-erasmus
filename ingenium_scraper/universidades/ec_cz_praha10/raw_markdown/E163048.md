# Low-Energy Cooling of Buildings

- Code: E163048
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

The basic informations about low-energy and passive cooling methods for buildings.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Final report. Knowledge of subject themes.

## Syllabus of lectures

Heat gains, thermal comfort, thermal mass, night cooling, evaporative cooling, ground
cooling, slab cooling a displacement ventilation.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Heat gains, thermal comfort, thermal mass, night cooling, evaporative cooling, ground
cooling, slab cooling a displacement ventilation.

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

The basic informations about low-energy and passive cooling methods for buildings.

## Study materials (bibliography)
