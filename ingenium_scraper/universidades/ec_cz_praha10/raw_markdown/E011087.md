# Mathematical Modeling in Technical Applicatiions

- Code: E011087
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

Introduction to mathematic models for basic engineering problems. Basic principles
of solution by means of mathematical modeling, numerical mathematics and current computers.
This course will introduce finite difference, finite volume and finite element methods
including different topology of domain discretization (meshes). Numerical simulations
will be aimed at the solution of engineering problems of continuum mechanics.
