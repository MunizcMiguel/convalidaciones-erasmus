# Engineering Design IV.

- Code: E133014
- ECTS: 2.0

## Synopsis

Information about general principles of a new technical product design, stages of
development of a new product, the designer fundamental assignment is to propose a
rivalrous product including.
Designing of a dribling jig. A drilling jig is a device by means of which holes on
many duplicate parts may be drilled exactly alike.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Basic rules of representation and dimensions of parts (Engineering Design I). Information
about tolerancing, geometrical tolerance, surface texture (Engineering Design II).

## Syllabus of lectures

Drawing of workpiece, freehand sketches of 3 variants (drilling jig), evaluate table,
proposal drawing, working drawings, assembly drawing, part list, technical report

## Syllabus of tutorials

Drawing of workpiece, freehand sketches of 3 variants (drilling jig), evaluate table,
proposal drawing, working drawings, assembly drawing, part list, technical report

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

The target of this study subject Designing 3 is to force students in advanced knowledge
and skills from the field of constructional design of modular technical system. Subject
is fully suported by 3D software.
