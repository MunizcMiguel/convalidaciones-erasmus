# Vehicle and Engine Electrical Equipment

- Code: E141125
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

An overview of electrical systems of road vehicles with an explanation of the basic
principles for understanding their function, applications and diagnostics. Perspective
alternative electric vehicle drives. Systems for increasing passenger safety and vehicle
emissions reduction.

## Syllabus of lectures

Car electric power source systems, alternator control, DC / DC converters.
Lead acid accumulators, NiMH, Lithium-Ion.Supercapacitors. Fuel cells.
Cranking systems, seriál DC motors, alternator/ starter.
Spark ignition engine ignition systems.
Engine value sensing and processing in car motormanagement systems.
Electromagnetic and piezolectric actuators vehicle aggregates
Vehicle accessiries electric drives, DC brushless motors, stepper motors.
Permanent magnets synchronous motors, reluctans motors.
Power electronics circuits for supply electric motors, inverter control methods
Control systems for petrol and diesel engines
Chassis management systems. Car restraint systems
Vehicle lighting system, light sources, adaptive lighting systems.
Diagnostics of vehicle systems, CAN, LIN communication lines, on board diagnostics
