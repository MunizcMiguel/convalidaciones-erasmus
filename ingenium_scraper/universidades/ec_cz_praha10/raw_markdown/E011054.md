# Mathematics in Mechanics

- Code: E011054
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Lecture consists of selective parts of mathematics - functional anlysis, variational
calculus, tensors, functions of complex variable

## Syllabus of lectures

Basic terms of functional analysis, tensor calculus including tensor operations (orthogonal),
basic terms of variational calculus, theory and numerical solution of basic problems,
short course of functions of complex variable usable for Basic evaluation techniques
and for theory of harmonic functions

## Syllabus of tutorials

Basic terms of functional analysis, tensor calculus including tensor operations (orthogonal),
basic terms of variational calculus, theory and numerical solution of basic problems,
short course of functions of complex variable usable for Basic evaluation techniques
and for theory of harmonic functions
