# Measurement and Experiment

- Code: E362700
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Students will held 12 laboratory tasks in field of fine mechanics and optics. Every
student work at its own workplace. Students became familiar to many laboratory equipments
and measurement methods.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Laboratory tasks participation. To write output reports.

## Syllabus of lectures

Laboratory exercise to practice and deepen knowledge of geometric and wave optics.
Measurement of a wedge angle of a prism
Refractometry
Measurement of lens focal length by Cornu method, resolving power of lens
Measurement of lens focal length by Bessel method and form two object positions
Measurement of Abbe number
Illumination system
Grating measurement
Measurement of transmission and reflection
Cole mine interferometer
Rayleigh interferometer
Measurement of mirror flatness
Diffraction at a pinhole and slit, coherence length
Polarization and Brewsters angle, retardation plates

## Syllabus of tutorials

12 laboratory tasks in field of fine mechanics and optics
