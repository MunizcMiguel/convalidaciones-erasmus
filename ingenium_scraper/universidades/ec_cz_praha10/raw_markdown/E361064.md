# Instrumental Technology

- Code: E361064
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

The students get knowledge on specific technological procedures used in instrumentation
and their connection with design of instruments. In practical laboratory exercises,
students will try out some of the specific technologies - small parts manufacturing,
production of thin films, holographic production methods, and glass cutting technology
used in production of instruments

## Syllabus of lectures

Fine parts production techniques the watch lathe, automatic Swiss type lathe
Statistical production of balls and the production and assembly of bearings.
Production of precision planar, spherical and aspherical surfaces.
Production and processing of glass and optical fibers.
Production of single crystals and its properties.
Thin films production technologies.
Production of gratings and scales.
Production of springs and spring elements for instruments.
Special technologies for production instrument components - EDM, laser, fluid beam.
Special technologies for production instrument components plasma, ultrasound, rapid
prototyping, micromachining.
Production technology of teeth wheels.
Techniques surveying and production of holes, technology of gluing and production
of composites.
