# Metallic Materials

- Code: E321072
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Metallic materials. Distribution of metallic materials; Steel. Low carbon welded steel
with increased strength. High-strength steels. Stainless steel. Austenitic stainless
steels, ferritic stainless steel. Tool steels. Non-ferrous metal - basic classification.
Copper and its alloys. Aluminium and its alloys. Titanium and its alloys. Heat processing
metals.

## Syllabus of lectures

Low carbon steels with high yield strength
High-strength steels - Heat treatment, mechanical-heat treatment, maraging steel,
TRIP
Stainless steel
Heat resistant and refractory materials. The principle of heat resistance. Creep fracture.
Tool materials.
Copper alloys.
Aluminum and its alloys.
Titanium and its alloys.
Materials for Nuclear Energy - Overview, requirements, properties
