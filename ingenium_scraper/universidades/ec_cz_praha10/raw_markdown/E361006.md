# Design of optomechanical instruments

- Code: E361006
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Instruments and techniques based on optical principle, used in medicine and in the
industry. Medical: instruments for eye inspection and surgery. Industrial: spectrometers
and fibre sensors.

## Syllabus of lectures

Eye and vision
Microscope
Methods and instruments for eye inspection
Optical coherence tomography
Eye aberrations corrections - laser surgery
lasers for eye surgery
Eye aberrations corrections - intraocular implants
spectral instruments - principles, applications
fibres and fibre sensors

## Syllabus of tutorials

problems connected to lectures, analysis of a design and principle of an instrument
