# Electric Accessories of ICE

- Code: E141120
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

Basic engine management system, design and select the components for an ignition system
and implement a strategy for on-board diagnostics. In car network and embedded processor
system for real time application with use of sophisticated peripheral devices.

## Requirements / prerequisites

To get credit the student must participate in contact teaching and must fulfil all
tasks in the laboratory.) If student misses more than 20%, he must fill additional
requirements announced by teachers. The exam is in written form (test) and next in
oral form (2 questions by lot). 60 % is minimal number for success of test.

## Syllabus of lectures

1 Electrical Power Supply in Vehicle Electrical Systems.
2 Starter Motors and Circuits. Integrated Starter Generators.
3 Semiconductor Devices and Power Electronic Circuits.
4 Distributorless and Electronic Ignition Systems.
5 Microcomputer Instrumentation and Control.
6 Micro-actuators and microsensors, micromotors, accelerometers and pressure sensors.
7 Magnetoelectric and Piezoelectric Actuators.
8 Electronic Fuel Injection Systems.
9 Diesel-Engine Management, Systems and Components.
10 Emissions Control Systems. Advance Diagnostic Systems.
11 Adaptive Operating and Prediction Strategy of the ECM.
12 Vehicle Networking Systems.
13 Future Automotive Electronic Systems.

## Syllabus of tutorials

1 Electrical power sources in vehicle -alternator and accumulator
2 Starter motors and voltage controllers.
3 Distributorless and electronic ignition systems.
4 Magnetoelectric and piezoelectric actuators.
5 Emission control systems.
6 KTS diagnostic system.
