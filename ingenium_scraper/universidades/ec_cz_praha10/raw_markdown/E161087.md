# Automatic Control in Environmental Engineering

- Code: E161087
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Application of basic approaches to automatic control of HVAC systems and equipment.
Automatic control sequences of air conditioning and sources of heat.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Following requirements are demanded: Types of control components. Hydraulic loops
and controllability. Stability of the HVAC systems control. Principles of heat sources
control. Controllers for HVAC equipments. Principles of air conditioning control.
Static and dynamic characteristics of typical HVAC systems components. Communication
networks for building automation systems.

## Syllabus of lectures

Types of control components. Hydraulic loops and controllability. Stability of the
HVAC systems control. Principles of heat sources control. Controllers for HVAC equipments.
Principles of air conditioning control. Static and dynamic characteristics of typical
HVAC systems components. Communication networks for building automation systems.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Practise: Types of control components. Hydraulic loops and controllability. Stability
of the HVAC systems control. Principles of heat sources control. Controllers for HVAC
equipments. Principles of air conditioning control. Static and dynamic characteristics
of typical HVAC systems components. Communication networks for building automation
systems.

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Needed knowledge and skills for engineering practice in area application of basic
approaches to automatic control of HVAC systems and equipments, automatic control
sequences of air conditioning and heat sources of setting academic goals.
