# Industrial Comunication Systems

- Code: E142012
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

The specialized knowledge that helps developed practical solutions for the design
and implementation network topology of communication systems and the transmitted
data ensure, both against interference and against unauthorized access.

## Syllabus of lectures

Signal transfer, introduction to digital communication, ISO/OSI model
Means of electromagnetic compability
Physical layer, transmission media, types of electrical signals
Optical data communications, transmitters, receivers, transmission cables and connectors
Link Layer. Synchronization, Modulation, Encoding
Data transmission security, error detection, correction, encryption
Higher layer of model, features, services
Overview of industrial buses, examples of their solutions, network topology
Industrial ASi bus concept, integration of security
DeviceBus concept
FieldBus concept
