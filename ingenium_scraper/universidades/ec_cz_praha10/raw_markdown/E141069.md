# Regulated electrical drive

- Code: E141069
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Equation of motion and mechanical properties of electrical drive, losses and dimensioning
of electrical drive, general properties and control of DC drives, general properties
and control of drives with asynchronous and synchronous motors, using of semiconductor
converters in electrical drives, choppers, inverters, frequency converters, thyristor
rectifiers, feedback control of electrical drive, EMC of electrical drive

## Requirements / prerequisites

1. Mechanical properties and losses in electrical drive
2. General properties and control of DC drives
3. General properties and mathematical model of asynchronous motor
4. Control of drives with asynchronous motors
5. Introduction to power semiconductor converters
6. Choppers
7. Inverters
8. Frequency converters
9. Thyristor rectifiers
10. Feedback control of DC drives
11. Feedback control of drives with asynchronous motors
12. Drives with synchronous motors - general properties
13. Feedback control of synchronous motor
14. Dimensioning of drive, EMC
