# Heat treatment

- Code: E321088
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Theoretical fundamentals of heat treatment, basic processes of heat and chemical-heat
treatment of ferrous and non-ferrous metals, excursion focused on the given topic

## Syllabus of lectures

Introduction to heat treatment
Heating and cooling
Deformation during heat treatment, environment for heating and cooling
Austenitization and austenitic transformations
Annealing
Quenching
Tempering, surface quenching
Chemical-heat treatment
Excursions
Heat treatment of steels and cast irons
Heat treatment of non-ferrous metals
Unconventional methods of heat treatment
Evaluation of the performed heat treatment
