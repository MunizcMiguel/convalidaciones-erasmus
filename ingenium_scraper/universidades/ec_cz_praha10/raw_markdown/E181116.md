# Chemical Production Lines

- Code: E181116
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Thermodynamics of industrial chemistry. Chemical lines and processes. Basic inorganic
and organic substances. Electrochemistry. Crude oil treatment and petrochemistry.
Plastic and rubber. Glass, ceramics and house - building materials. Cellulose and
paper. Surfactants. Biotechnology. Industrial chemical plants. Auxiliary equipment.

## Syllabus of lectures

Thermodynamics of industrial chemistry.
Basic inorganic substances.
Electrochemistry.
Basic organic substances.
Crude oil treatment and petrochemistry. Plastic and rubber.
Surfactants.
Basis of pharmaceutical production.
Ceramics and house-building materials.
Glass.
Cellulose and paper.
Biotechnology.
Industrial chemical plants. Auxiliary equipment.
