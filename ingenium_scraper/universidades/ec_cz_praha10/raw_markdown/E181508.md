# Heat Transfer Equipment

- Code: E181508
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Fundamentals of thermodynamics and conductive and convective heat transfer. Enthalpy
balancing. Technical thermodynamics and basic thermodynamic cycles. Functional description,
mechanical design, thermal and hydraulic design of a heat exchangers, evaporators
and dryers.
