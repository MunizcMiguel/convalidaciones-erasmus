# Branch Project II.

- Code: E352122
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

The course is focused on elaboration of individually work which student solves in
close cooperation with the head of the assigned topic. The student will be acquainted
with the problems of machine tools to the equipment, respectively its parts, according
to the orientation of his work and during regular weekly consultations with the head
of the assigned topic, he works in a professional solution of the problem. At the
end of the semester student presents his work on small oral presentation concerning
the performed work, its coherence and meaning.
