# Electrical Transfer of Signals

- Code: E141506
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

The transmission of signals in measure systems. Electromagnetic compatibility.
Electronics measurements circuits and a conversion of signal for the transmission.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Graduation exercises which posluhač draw, animate and measures the electronic circuits.
Record of the measurements and their evaluation of the workbook
Oral examination according to the basic topics of lectures

## Syllabus of lectures

1. Lab work, tools and equipment for electronics. Measurement of line parameters,
coaxial, pair, twisted, Zo, attenuation, reflections. Determining the interfering
signal transmission components
2. Assembly and testing of the pulse generator.
3. Measurement of static and dynamic characteristics of the optocoupler.
4. Measurements on Fibre-optics and its end converters, transmission, impact deformation
5. Assembly and testing of the current converter
6. Assembly and testing of the reference or power source.

## Syllabus of tutorials

1. Lab work, tools and equipment for electronics. Measurement of line parameters,

signal transmission components
2. Assembly and testing of the pulse generator.

4. Measurements on Fibre-optics and its end converters, transmission, impact deformation
5. Assembly and testing of the current converter

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Knowledge on the transmission of signals over metalic and fiber-optic lines.
Practical design of electronic circuits for measurement.
Diagnosis of rotating machinery from measurements of angular velocity.
