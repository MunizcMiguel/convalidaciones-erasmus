# Perspectice Production in Engineering

- Code: E333040
- ECTS: 2.0

## Synopsis

The subject focuses on the teaching of advanced engineering technology, modern trends
in production management, development of new advanced materials, testing of materials,
energy production and presentation of the virtual factory. Classes will be a combination
of practical nature of work in laboratories FS, instructional videos, and tours to
the high-tech material-working.

## Syllabus of lectures

- Advanced Manufacturing Technology
- Modern trends in production management
- Laboratory technique in materials research
- Development of new materials and their applications
- Virtual Factory
- Technological and environmental testing of products
- Energy production (cogeneration, heat pumps)
- Product life cycle and its impact on the environment
- Materials science in the analysis of accidents

## Syllabus of tutorials

- Advanced Manufacturing Technology
- Modern trends in production management
- Laboratory technique in materials research
- Development of new materials and their applications
- Virtual Factory
- Technological and environmental testing of products
- Energy production (cogeneration, heat pumps)
- Product life cycle and its impact on the environment
- Materials science in the analysis of accidents
- Excursion to the production and testing sites in the automotive manufacturing
- Excursion to the heat treatment of materials

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

The aim of the subject is to acquaint students with the importance of manufacturing
technology, materials, economics and engineering constraints to production.
