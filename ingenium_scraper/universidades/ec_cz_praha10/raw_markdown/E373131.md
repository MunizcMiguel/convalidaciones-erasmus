# Project I

- Code: E373131
- ECTS: 6.0

## Synopsis

Project oriented class the students choose a topic. Under the supervision of the
department teachers, they study and solve the given problems. Regular meetings are
done during the semester with the supervisor. The students present the work done and
get new tasks. The students prepare a written report and presentation as instructed
by the supervisor. The subject warrant organizes at least 2 meetings per semester.
One on the beginning of the semester where the students are instructed and a final
one, where the students presents the results.
