# German Upper Intermediate

- Code: E046080
- ECTS: 2.0

## Synopsis

Understanding standard speech about familiar matters that a student meets at work,
at school, during free time, and talking about these topics. Ability to describe experoences
and events, briefly explain one´s opinions and plans.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Mastering the material that has been taught, 2 tests

## Syllabus of lectures

Grammar: shortening sentences with dass and damit, passive, present participle, subjunctive
of preterite, periphrastic form with wuerde, compound adjunct. Conversational topics:
free time, Prague, study stay abroad, travelling in CR

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Understanding standard speech about familiar matters. Ability to describe experiences
and events, briefly explain one´s opinions and plans.
