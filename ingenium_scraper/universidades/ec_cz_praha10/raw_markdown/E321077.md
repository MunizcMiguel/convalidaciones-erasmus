# Surfaces and Interfaces

- Code: E321077
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

The course deals with regularities of relationships between the mechanical, physical
and chemical properties of solids and the characteristics of their surfaces and interfaces.

## Syllabus of lectures

?The structure of interfaces in solids
?The interface and its application
?Thin layers
?Diagnostic of interfaces and layers
