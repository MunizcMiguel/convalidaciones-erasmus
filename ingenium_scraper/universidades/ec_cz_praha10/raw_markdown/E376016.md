# Robot Control Introduction

- Code: E376016
- ECTS: 2.0

## Synopsis

The class introduces basic concepts and principles of robotics. Students will use
construction kit for design, assemble and programme the robot. This subject is recommended
for students of the second year of the bachelor study. There is no prerequisite for
this subject.

## Syllabus of lectures

1) Use of the robots.
2) The basic principles of the control of the robots.
3) The basic mechanical principles of robots.
4) Control of robot movement.
5) Robotic perception (sensors).
6) General principles of the control.
7) Advanced principles of the robot control.
8) Robotic communication.
9-12) Project - Team development of robot.
13) Robot competition.
14) Assessment.
