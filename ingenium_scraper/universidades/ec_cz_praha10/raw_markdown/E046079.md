# German Lower Intermediate

- Code: E046079
- ECTS: 2.0

## Synopsis

Mapped to the level of Common European Framework of Reference A2
Aim: Understanding clearly spoken language about everyday situations which a student
meets either at school or in his/her free time and speaking about them. Writing in
a simple way about familiar topics. reading and comprehesion of simple texts. Improvement
of professional language.

## Syllabus of lectures

Perfect tenses, strong and mixed verbs, infinitive, time clauses.
Working day, holiday, sport, travelling in Germany.
Operating simple instruments, measurements, mathematical expressions, computer.
