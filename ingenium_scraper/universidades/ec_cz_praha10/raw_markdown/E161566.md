# Heating

- Code: E161566
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Knowledge improvement from the field of heating of residential and industrial buildings.
Designing of convective and radiant heating systems.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Following requirements are demanded:
Computation of total building heat-losses by EN, heat and fuel requirement. Designing
and computation of parallel and counter flow two-pipe heating systems or single-pipe
heating systems. Hydraulics of heating systems with respect to varied regulation of
pumps. Small heat sources. Chimneys. Electric heating.

## Syllabus of lectures

Computation of total building heat-losses by EN, heat and fuel requirement. Designing
and computation of parallel and counter flow two-pipe heating systems or single-pipe
heating systems. Hydraulics of heating systems with respect to varied regulation of
pumps. Small heat sources. Electric heating.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Practise: Computation of total building heat-losses by EN, heat and fuel requirement.
Designing and computation parallel and counter flow two-pipe heating systems or single-pipe
heating systems. Hydraulics of heating systems with respect to varied regulation of
pumps. Small heat sources. Electric heating.

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Needed knowledge and skills for engineering practice in the area of heating are matter
of setting academic goals.
