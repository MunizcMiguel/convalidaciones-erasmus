# Fundamentals of Technology I.

- Code: E333018
- ECTS: 1.0

## Synopsis

The study of manufacturing processes forms a core subject area for a majority of mechanical
enginnering stdents. It contains basic concept of three manufacturing technologies
such as casting, forming and welding, including basic terms, methods and materials.

## Syllabus of lectures

1.Production of steel, cast iron.
2.Casting I
3.Casting II
4.Forming I
5. Forming II
6.Welding I
7.Welding II
