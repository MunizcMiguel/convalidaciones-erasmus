# Polymer Composites

- Code: E324002
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

The subject provides a comprehensive overview of the fundamental properties, processing
technologies, and applications of polymer-based composites. Key phenomena at the matrix-filler
interface are analysed alongside their impact on the final performance of the composite
material. It details the different kinds of synthetic and sustainable bio-based composites
for various kinds of applications. Special attention is dedicated to modern fabrication
processes, microstructural characterisation, and evaluation of mechanical behaviour.
Furthermore, the course considers environmental criteria, material sustainability,
and mechanical recycling, preparing students to design eco-friendly engineering solutions.
