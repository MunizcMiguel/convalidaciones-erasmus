# Bachelor´s Thesis

- Code: E133992
- ECTS: 8.0

## Synopsis

The bachelor thesis is the final student's work in the bachelor's study program. The
aim of this task is to verify student's ability of individual and self-dependent work
in the branch. Students solve chosen topic under mentoring of their supervisors. Usually,
the bachelor thesis follows up to the Branch project course. The credit is obtained
after the thesis submission.
