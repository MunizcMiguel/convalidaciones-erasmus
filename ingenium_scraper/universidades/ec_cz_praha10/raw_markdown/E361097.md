# Instrument Design

- Code: E361097
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Basics of instruments design.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Knowledge of lectires matter, Presence on practices.

## Syllabus of lectures

Materials, joint features, guidelines, rotation guides, rolling guides, gears, junctions,
micrometric screws, aretation systems, backstops, breaks, absorbers, springs, modular
parts, mounting of optical systems.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Solution of examples and a review of design parts.

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Lectures give knowledge about basics of instruments design in order student would
be able to design different kinds of mechanical and optomechanical instruments. Lectures
highlight differences between design of standard mechanical engineering systems and
measurements instruments.

## Study materials (bibliography)

Trylinski, W., Fine mechnanisms and precision instruments, principles of design, Pergamin
Press, Warshaw, 1971, Yoder, P., R., Jr., Opto-Mechanical system design, SpiePress,
Washington, 2005
