# Experimental Methods and Measurements

- Code: E211137
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

The course deals with the application of basic principles, instruments and data acquisition
systems used in the experimental R&amp;D in the field of internal combustion engines.
Classes will be taught in a form of lectures combined with laboratory exercises and
demonstrations.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Information on the organisation of the course, special conditions for the award of
ungraded assessment, graded assessment or examination and study materials can be found
on the study material server
https://studium.fs.cvut.cz/studium/u12120/EMM-E211137/

## Syllabus of lectures

1. Measuring chain, measurement uncertainties and errors
2. Force, torque and mass measurement
3. Frequency - speed and position
4. Pressure
5. Temperature
6. Flow-rate
7. Vibration and oscillation, basic acoustics, noise and sound
8. Characteristics of combustion engines
9. Engine test-bench integration, automated data acquisition, types of dynamometers
10. Acquisition and evaluation of fast changing pressures, measuring chain description,
application of the 1st law of thermodynamics for a basic combustion analysis
11. Exhaust gas composition, environmental impact of combustion engine operation
12. Exhaust gas analysis, gaseous components and particulate matters
13. Legislation base
