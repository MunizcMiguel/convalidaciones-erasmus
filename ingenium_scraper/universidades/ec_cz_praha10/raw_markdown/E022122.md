# Branch Project II.

- Code: E022122
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

The conception of the subject is on an individual base the topics are related to
the professional profile of students. Students are guided to the application of possessed
knowledge in solution of a given problem together with an individual approach. The
achieved results are presented in the end of semester, if they are supposed to be
a part of bachelor thesis.
