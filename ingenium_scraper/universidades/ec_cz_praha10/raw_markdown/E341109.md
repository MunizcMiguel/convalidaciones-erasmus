# Metrology and Process Technology

- Code: E341109
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Learning outcomes of the course unit The course introduces the problems of engineering
metrology and the basics of process planning.

## Syllabus of lectures

1) Metrology, calibration, measurement uncertainty.
2) Measurement process capability and MSA measurement system analysis. Sources of
variability in measurement.
3) Measurement in 1 coordinate. Municipal gauges, length gauges, 1st and 2nd order
errors. Taylor's principle.
4) Measurement in 2 coordinates (measuring microscopes) and measurement in 3 coordinates
(CMM)
5) Shape and position deviations. Principles of measurement and evaluation, measuring
methods.
6) Surface texture, surface profile. Measuring methods.
7) Technological design, general terms, terminology
8) Analysis of component basis
9) Time tracking system
10) Labor standardization
11) Ergonomics of work
12) Lean production methods
13) Digital Enterprise, Industry 4.0

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

The aim of the study is to acquire basic metrological principles and skills. Learning
outcomes of the course unit the aim of the course is to acquaint students with the
basic principles and methods of process planning.
