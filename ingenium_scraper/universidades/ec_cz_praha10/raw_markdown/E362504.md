# Technical optics

- Code: E362504
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

The subject gives a detailed explanation of the principle of imaging of planar and
spherical surfaces based on the laws of geometrical optics. It also deals with monochromatic
and color aberrations and basic visual instruments.

## Syllabus of lectures

Light as electromagnetic radiation. Wavefronts and rays. Refraction index, optical
path. Polarization.
Fermats principle. Reflection and refraction at planar boundary. Total internal reflection.
Plane mirror and mirror systems. Refraction prisms: types and applications.
Crown and flint glass, dispersion, Abbe number. Dispersion prisms:types, applications.
Spherical sourface and systems of sourfaces image forming, ray tracing, principal
planes. Focal length, magnification.
Thin and thick lens: image forming, types. Graphical and mathematical ray tracing.
Thin lens combinations, system focus. Afocal attachment.
Lens abarrations: monochromatic, color. Corrections of aberrations.
Reading glasses. Magnifying glass: resolving power, magnification, types.
Microscope: optical layout, main characteristics, anatomy.
Image recording with a microscope. Inverted and stereomicroscope.
Illumination in microscopy. Abbe theory of image forming.
Contrast enhancing techniques in microscopy.
Lens telescopes: Galilean, Keplerian. Focusing mechanisms. Image erecting systems.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Problems related to the lecture content (calculations).
Laboratory tasks: Apex angle of a prism, Index of refraction and Abbe number, Focal
length of a thick lens, Resolving power and optical aberrations

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Understanding the laws of geometrical optics, image formation, the origin and nature
of the aberrations.
