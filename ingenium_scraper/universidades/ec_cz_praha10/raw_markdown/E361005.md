# Instrumental Technology

- Code: E361005
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

This subject gives students a detail review of technology used for instrumentation
production.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Attendance, elaboration of given tasks

## Syllabus of lectures

1) The watch lathe, automatic Swiss type lathe
2) Statistical production of balls and the production and assembly of bearings
3) Production technology of teeth wheels
4) Production of precision planar surfaces
5) Production of spherical surfaces, three-phase optical technology, production of
aspherical surfaces
6) Thin films technologies
7) Production of gratings and scales
8) Production and processing of glass
9) Preparation of single crystals
10) Production of springs and spring elements for instruments
11) Techniques surveying and production of holes
12) Special technologies for production instrument components - lasers, ultrasound
techniques, EDM
13) Technology of gluing and production of composites

## Syllabus of tutorials

Work on the MN80 lathe and milling machine
Work in the watchmaking lathe
Preparation of thin films
Production of grids by holographic techniques
Glass processing
Production of lentils

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Practice of small parts production using micromachining, watchmaker lathe, thin layers
steaming, holography technique, grid production and glass machining, optical fibers
production, single crystal production, springs, tooth gearing.
