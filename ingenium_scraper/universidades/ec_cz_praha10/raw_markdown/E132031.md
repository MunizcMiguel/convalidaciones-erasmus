# Engineering Design I.

- Code: E132031
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

The course is focused on building up the ability of future designers to express their
ideas through common communication language - technical drawing. During the course
students train and improve their skills in spatial imagination and engineering way
of thinking.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Writing instruments for hand sketching.
Basic technical drawing course, basic high school knowledge is sufficient.
Knowledge of CAD modeling is an advantage, but not necessary.

## Syllabus of lectures

1) Subject introduction. Lectures and practises content. Standardisation of the technical
documentation, lettering of drawings. Utilities for the practises and recommended
literature. Internet information.
2) Technical drawings (types, scales, formats, type of lines, folding of drawings).
Principles of the representation in technical drawings. Orthographic projection. Rules
of representation - number and choice of views.
3) Using and drawing of sectional views and sections. Interruption of views. Representation
of symmetrical parts. Special views, simplification of representation.
4) Principles of the dimensioning in technical drawings. Arrangement and systems of
dimensioning. Dimensioning of the basic geometrical bodies.
5) Representation and dimensioning of the standard elements (recesses, centre holes,
knurlings, threads, thread fasteners, their indication).
6) Accuracy of dimensions and its indication in the technical drawings. Indication
of the surface texture (surface roughness) in the technical drawings.
7) Edge state introduction, accuracy, roughness. Representation and dimensioning of
the compression spring

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Be able to express oneself graphically - a basic means of communication for engineers.
Train spatial imagination. Acquire knowledge and skills necessary for the following
subjects ED2, ED3, ED4, MEM1, MEM2. Breakdown of machine components into basic geometric
shapes - component analysis. Unify knowledge about creating drawing documentation
(philosophy of displaying and dimensioning described within ISO GPS). Display and
dimensioning of geometrically different components - increasing complexity and monitoring
the function of the component.
