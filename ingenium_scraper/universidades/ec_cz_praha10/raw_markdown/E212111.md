# Project I.

- Code: E212111
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Basic practical skills of work with advanced CAD/CAE/CAM systems. Knowledge improvement
in basic tasks of kinetostatics and dynamics and their applications in individual
task. Common principles of engine and vehicle design.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Fundamentals of work with CAD/CAE/CAM systems Creo or Catia V5 (introduction to part
solid modeling, creating of assembly and drawing, using CAE capabilities)
Ordinary differential equations a solving methods
Simple mechanism kinetostatics
Frequency analysis methods
Common design principles and similar criterions
Introduction to design problem solving
Sealing and tribology
