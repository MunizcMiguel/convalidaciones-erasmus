# Information Systems

- Code: E373504
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

Information Systems and Information Technologies (IS/IT) - gate in the époque of networks.
Concepts of Informatics. Representation of Information - basic models and types.
Essential types of information representation. Entropy. Basic computations. Acquisition
of information. Storing of information. Carriers of information. Information transfer.
Transfer channel. Quality of transfer channel. Information noise in the channel. Electronic
document, multi-medial information. Multiplex channels. Signals and signal carriers
(telephone, optic, radio, laser, ). Computer networks. Types. Communication protocols.
Computer Network services. Computer networks. Parameters of computer networks. Simulation.
Information technologies in the filed of Automatic Control. Data and Knowledge. Database
systems, data models, database management, Engineering applications. Design of database
systems.
Knowledge-based systems. Representation of Knowledge. Computing with uncertainties.
Modular structure of expert system. Types of expert systems. Expert systems in engineering
problem solving.
