# Distributed energy Systems II.

- Code: E151131
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

This course follows the course DISTRIBUTED ENERGY SYSTEMS I. Mainly discusses the
economics of the distribeted energy systems.
c)Economics of the DES
Energy market. Purchase of energy for the consumer.
Current and new market models in energetics and the consequences for the DES
Documentation for the zoning plan and the building permit
Project documentation basic and detailed design
Legislative aspects of the DES
Feasibility study in decentralized energetics
Practical aspects of the operation of the decentralized energy sources CTU UCEEB
The role of ESCO companies in energetics, energy contracting and energy performance
contracting
The Law on Energy Management, energy audit, energy assessment, eco-design, energy
labelling
