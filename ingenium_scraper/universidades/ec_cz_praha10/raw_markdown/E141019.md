# Electromechanical Systems in Transport and Mechanical Engineering

- Code: E141019
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

The aim of the course is to acquire the orientation in problems of electrical drives,
control properties of the electrical machines, power semiconductor converters as supply
sources for controlled drives and cooperation of electric drives with mechanical load
and with high level control systems in mechanical engineering and transport applications.

## Syllabus of lectures

General properties of driving systems, the thermal properties of electrical machines
Power and energetic properties of electrical drives
Control of DC motors
The general mathematic model and dynamic properties of asynchronous machine
Frequency and voltage control of asynchronous machines
The general mathematic model and dynamic properties of permanent magnet synchronous
machine
Overview of power converters, losses and cooling
Converters for drives with DC motors
Inverters for frequency controlled electrical drives, puls width modulation
Frequency converters, pulse rectifiers
Feedback torque, speed and position control in electrical drives
Feedback control of AC drives
Design and electromagnetic compatibility of pulse controlled drives
