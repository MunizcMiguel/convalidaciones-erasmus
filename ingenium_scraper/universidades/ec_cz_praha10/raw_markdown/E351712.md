# Modelling and Simulation II.

- Code: E351712
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Information about main principles of modeling of machines and their parts using finite
element method.

## Syllabus of lectures

Beam elements; geometry preparation of a FEM model; creation of a free and mapped
mesh; boundary condition definition; modelling properties of single bodies; modelling
properties of assemblies; analysis of specific designs.

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Importance of modeling in machine design; specification of problem area; main methods
for continuum discretization; main principles of FEM; beam elements described with
FEM; mass matrix and stiffness matrix; main 2D and 3D elements; constrains; boundary
condition specification and preprocessing; postprocessing and interpretation of results;
verification; multi-body systems; sensitivity analysis; parametric optimization; topological
optimization.

## Study materials (bibliography)

"Fenner, R.T.: Finite Element Methods for Engineers;
Reddy, J.N.: Introduction to the Finite Element Method;
Hughes, T.J.R.: The Finite Element Method: Linear Static and Dynamic Finite Element
Analysis"
