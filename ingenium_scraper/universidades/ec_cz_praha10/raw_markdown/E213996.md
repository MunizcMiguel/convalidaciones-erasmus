# Diploma Work

- Code: E213996
- ECTS: 20.0

## Synopsis

Each student will solve his individual theme under guidance of his/her individual
supervising department specialist from the university and of the coordinator from
the company, where he/she is working on the diploma thesis. Result is his/her diploma
thesis.
