# Finite Element Method I.

- Code: E111057
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

The course is focused on the interpretation of the essence and basic apparatus of
FEM in the mechanics of a deformable body. The variational principles in the statics
(principle of virtual displacements and the principle of minimum total potential energy),
deformation variant of FEM (construction of shape functions, expression of total potential
energy, kinematic boundary conditions) in one-, two- and three-dimensional continuum
are explained.

## Syllabus of lectures

1. Deformation and force approach in mechanics
2. Fundamental ideas of elastostatics - tensor, index and matrix notation, variational
principles: statically amissible stress, kinematically admissible strain, principle
of virtual displacements. Example for 1D.
3. Minimum of total potential energy principle. Example for 1D.
4. Variational principles in mechanics - extension for 3D-continuum.
5. Approximate solutions - Ritz's method. Example with Fourier base and with piecewise
linear base on the tension-compression bar.
6. Basic concepts of FEM - node, element, shape functions, u-delta operator, stiffness
matrix, equivalent nodal loads
7. Triangular plane element.
8. Choose of interpolation model.
9. Isoparametric element.
10. Theory of Kirchhoff plates. Plate elements, flat shell elements
11. Shells classification, stress.
12. Generalized linear constraint equation.
13. Numerical integration.

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

- understanding variational principles particularly of deformation approach
- understandig FEM as particular case of Ritz's method
