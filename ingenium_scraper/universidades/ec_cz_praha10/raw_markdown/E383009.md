# Communication and Dealing with People

- Code: E383009
- ECTS: 2.0

## Synopsis

The goal of the subject Communication and dealing with people is to improve and develop
students' existing communication skills and master the basic rules of communication
and negotiation in work practice. At the same time, there is a basic orientation of
the students in the wide range of literature and electronic resources in this area

## Requirements / prerequisites

Student requirements: active participation in exercises, solving practical tasks,
Verification of study results: submission of two written reports (analysis of a selected
speech, development of communication in the 21st century)

## Syllabus of lectures

Human communication (intrapersonal, interpersonal, group, inter-group, public speaking)
Communication processes, components, tools, skills
Communication as part of the qualification of every worker for an executive or leadership
position.
Listening, relational ties as a component of communication
Verbal communication (rhetoric), its types, means and tools, communication manipulation
and counter-manipulation
Non-verbal communication, its types, means and tools, the third layer of communication
Presentation and self-presentation
Negotiation incl. conflict resolution and negotiation with problem persons
How not to be deceived in communication
Non-contact methods of communication
Development of communication in the 21st century
