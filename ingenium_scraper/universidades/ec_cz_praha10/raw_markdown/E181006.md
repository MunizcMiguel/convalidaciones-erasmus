# Industrial Chemistry

- Code: E181006
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Thermodynamics of industrial chemistry (reaction kinetics, thermochemistry, mass and
enthalpy balance, entropy and Gibbs-energy balance, chemical equilibrium). Basic inorganic
and organic substances. Electrochemistry. Crude oil treatment and petrochemistry.
Plastic and rubber. Glass and ceramics. Cellulose and paper. Tensides. Biotechnology.

## Syllabus of lectures

1. Thermodynamics of industrial chemistry.
2. Basic inorganic substances.
3. Electrochemistry.
4. Basic organic substances.
5. Crude oil treatment.
6. Petrochemistry.
7. Plastic and rubber.
8. Tensides.
9. Ceramics and bioceramics.
10. Glass.
11. House - building materials.
12. Cellulose and paper.
13. Biotechnology.
