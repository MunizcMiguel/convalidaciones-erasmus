# Project II

- Code: E373132
- ECTS: 6.0

## Synopsis

Another preparatory project for final qualification work. Students work on a topic
chosen under Project I or choose a new topic. The aim is to study the issues in which
the diploma thesis will be awarded, or solve a design or experiments, which can lead
to the thesis. It includes writing the final report and presenting the results to
other students, project leaders and guests.
