# Cutting Tools

- Code: E341086
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Cutting tools characteristics. Cutting materials including heat treatment and surface
finish, application fields. Cutting tool geometry, determination and measurement.
Cutting tools elements design. Cutting tools design including dimensioning. Cutting
tools production. Basic tools groups description and their use (turning tools, milling
tools, etc.). Special cutting tools. Grinding, use and maintenance of cutting tools.

## Requirements / prerequisites

(nothing)

## Syllabus of lectures

1. Introduction, the importance of cutting tools.
2. Cutting materials (high speed steels, sintered carbides).
3. Cutting materials (ceramics, CBN, PKD).
4. Design elements of cutting tools - cutting part.
5. Design elements of cutting tools - clamping and other parts.
6. Turning cutting tools.
7. Milling cutting tools.
8. Drilling and threading cutting tools.
9. Cutting tools for difficult to cut materials and non-ferrous materials.
10. Toothing cutting tools.
11. Form cutting tools.
12. Cutting tools stress.
13. Cutting tools use and their grinding.

## Syllabus of tutorials

1. Introduction, geometry of cutting tools, examples, calculation.
2. Cutting tool geometry measurement.
3. Assessment of cutting tools.
4. Adjustment of cutting tools.
5. Cutting tool project assignment.
6. Project discussion.
7. Project assessment, conclusion.

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

To give the knowledge of the design and the use of cutting tools and their proper
choice for concrete application.
