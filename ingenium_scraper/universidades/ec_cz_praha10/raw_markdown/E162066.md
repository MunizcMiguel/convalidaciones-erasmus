# Heat Supply

- Code: E162066
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

District heating with heat generators in heat-only and combined heat&amp;power mode.
Heat generators. Heating nets. Delivery stations.

## Requirements / prerequisites

subjects Environmental Engineering

## Syllabus of lectures

Climate data. Design of heat power for space heating and hot water. Types of heating
nets. Determination of flowrates and pipe dimension. Hydraulic calculation of heating
net. Heat losses of distribution. Economically optimal insulation thickness. Stress
loads. Compensation of thermal dillatations. Delivery stations and components. Heat
cycles (Carnot, Rankin-Clausius). Heat generators for heating plants and heat&amp;power
plants.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Design of heat power for space heating and hot water. Heat demand. Hydraulic calculations.
Heat losses. Economically optimal insulation thickness. Stress loads and dillatations.
Heat cycles. Delivery stations.

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Students gain practical experience with design of heating nets and heat generators
for district heating systems.
