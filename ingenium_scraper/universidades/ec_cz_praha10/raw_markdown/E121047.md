# Hydro and Thermodynamics

- Code: E121047
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

The study subject aim is to expand the students knowledge gained from the subjects
Hydro- or Thermodynamics, namely those having the relation to his course of study
project and his Bachelor thesis topics. The background research of the appropriate
fields and definition of the bachelor thesis aim are the objectives for the student
under supervision of a supposed advisor of the thesis. The study subject is accomplished
by a presentation and follow-up discussion of the gained results defined in the bachelor
thesis. The final stage shall deepen the students presentations- and argumentative
skills.

## Syllabus of lectures

Basic equations and laws of Fluid Mechanics and Thermodynamics and their application
to basic problems.
Design of piping systems and pumps.
Basic functional principles and designs of pumps.
Fans. Basic structural arrangement, structural design of axial and radial fans.
Aircraft propellers.
Propeller types, design options.
Air propeller design methods.
Reciprocating internal combustion engines.
Basic division, specifications and differences, model calculation of a reciprocating
internal combustion engine.
Approaches based on theory of similarity, similarity numbers, use for design and construction
of machines.
Analytical and numerical tools for a modern approach to machine design.
Possibilities and approaches of verification of design parameters
Long-term monitoring of the condition of machines and technical systems.
