# Technical Application of Materials

- Code: E321086
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Main groups of engineering materials and their characteristics. Mechanical, physical,
chemical properties of metals, polymers, ceramics, metallic glasses, and composites.
Classification and designation of materials according to EN. Material and its role
in the engineering design of a technical object. Selection of material with respect
to utility properties. Typical applications of materials in power engineering, transportation,
chemistry, electrical engineering and biomedicine.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Presence in practical lectures, completed protocols from practical lectures
Written and oral exam

## Syllabus of lectures

Selected applications of materials in the chemical and food industries
Possibilities of using non-ferrous metals and their alloys
Current application spectrum for cast materials
Applications of composites in the aerospace industry
Use of composites in the automotive industry
Biomedical applications of metallic and non-metallic materials
Materials with special physical properties in the electrical industry
Significance of surface layers and coatings in applications in aggressive environments
Application of structural polymer materials
Use of tool steels for hot and cold work
