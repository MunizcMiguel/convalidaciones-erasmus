# CAD 2

- Code: E136032
- ECTS: 2.0

## Synopsis

Course follows Course Autodesk Inventor I. It is focused on usage of advanced and
specialised tools in CAD Inventor. Students may use taught skills in courses like
ED II, III and IV

## Requirements / prerequisites

Knowledge of Autodesk Inventor I

## Syllabus of lectures

Parametric modeling, Adaptive modeling (components), Creation of assembly, Generation
of part list, Designing of shaft, Drive constraint, The parameters used for many components,
Intelligent assembly with using skeleton, Derived assembly, Creation of gear drive
and belt drive (+ control), Designing of frame, Creation of weldments

## Syllabus of tutorials

1) Welded Assemblies
2) Sheet metal parts
3) Introduction into parameters in AI, derived parts
4) Advanced Techniques of parametric modelling
5) i-mates, i-parts, i-assemblies
6) Design Accelerator
7) Exploded Assemblies - Presentation
8) Free modelling, Plastic components
9) Inventor Studio
10) Assessment

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Improving skills in Autodesk Inventor. Introduction into specialised tools in Inventor.
