# Advanced Materials

- Code: E322045
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

The course characterizes individual groups of new construction materials. In addition
to the development and physical nature of these materials, the most commonly used
types of recently developed materials, their basic characteristics and mechanical
properties are listed. Their technological possibilities, design applicability and
methods of their designations are also presented.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Presence at practical lectures, completed protocols from practical lectures
Written exam

## Syllabus of lectures

Development, production and market of new materials
Modern technologies of iron and steel metallurgy
Designation of materials according to European standards
Modern steels of mass consumption
High-strength and stainless steels and Ni and Co alloys
Light metals and their alloys
Materials produced by powder metallurgy
Amorphous, nanocrystalline and microcrystalline metallic materials
Perspective ceramic materials
New types of polymeric materials
Developments in the field of composite materials
Biomaterials, superconducting and superplastic materials, shape memory materials
Surface layers and finishes
