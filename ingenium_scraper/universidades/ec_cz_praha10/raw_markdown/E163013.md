# Project II.

- Code: E163013
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Design of heating systems, heat distributors and systems for using recoverable source
of energy. Design of ventilation and air conditioning systems, including gas cleaning
and reduction of noise.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Command of: Design of heating systems, heat distributors and systems for using recoverable
source of energy. Design of ventilation and air conditioning systems, including gas
cleaning and reduction of noise.

Project, dimensioning and designing solutions of heating and air conditioning plants.

solutions.
