# Branch Project I., Department of Fluid Dynamics and Thermodynamics

- Code: E122121
- ECTS: 2.0

## Synopsis

The main objective of the course is to work on individually assigned semester project.
The student solves the issue in close cooperation with the leader of the topic. The
topic of the project is selected in connection with the future bachelor thesis.

## Syllabus of lectures

The content of the course is given by the topic after consultation with the supervisor
of the project.
