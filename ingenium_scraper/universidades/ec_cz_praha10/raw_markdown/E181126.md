# Process Equipment and Production Lines

- Code: E181126
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Students will get to know with the basics of the field of Process Engineering. They
will gain a comprehensive overview of the principles and design of machines and apparatus,
basic information on the description and characteristics of selected production lines
from the chemical, food and processing industries, including wastewater and gas treatment,
modern approaches to waste treatment and biofuel production. All lectured issues are
illustrated on simple examples and on demonstration experimental equipment in the
laboratories of the department.

## Syllabus of lectures

Basics of hydromechanical processes and principles of equipment.
Fundamentals of mechanical processes and principles of equipment.
Basics of thermal processes and principles of equipment.
Basics of diffusion-separation processes and principles of equipment.
Basics of construction of production lines and process equipment.
Selected equipment and technologies of the chemical industry.
Selected devices and technologies of the pharmaceutical industry.
Selected equipment and technologies of the food industry.
Selected equipment and technologies of wastewater treatment.
Selected equipment and technologies of waste gas cleaning.
Selected equipment and technologies of waste treatment.
Selected equipment and technologies for the production of advanced biofuels.
Selected equipment and technologies for capturing and processing CO2 emissions.
