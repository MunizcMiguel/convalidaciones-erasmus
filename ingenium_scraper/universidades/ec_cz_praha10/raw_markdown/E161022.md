# Environmental Engineering

- Code: E161022
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Application of basic knowledge in environmental engineering - HVAC systems.

## Syllabus of lectures

Indoor Environment
Thermal Comfort
Ventilation - principles
Psychrometrics
Natural ventilation
Mechanical ventilation.
Duct and fan design
Heat recovery
Air-conditioning
Heating load
Heating surfaces
Heating Systems
Heat Sources
Hot water preparation

## Syllabus of tutorials

Indoor environment - operative temperature, PMV and PPD index
Ventilation - mass balance of the ventilated room
Psychrometrics
Heating /cooling coil capacity.
Heat recovery.
Natural ventilation
Duct design
Dimensioning of air-conditioning (CAV system)
Heating load
Design of a panel radiator / floor heating
Design of circulation pump, safety valve, expansion vessel.
Dimensioning of heat sources

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Basic orientation in the field of heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC).
