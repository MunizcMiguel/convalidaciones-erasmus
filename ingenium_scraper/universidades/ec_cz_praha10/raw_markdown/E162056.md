# Sanitary Installations

- Code: E162056
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

Fundamentals for solving water-supply, gas and for waste draining by indoor building
and by civil amenities building.

## Requirements / prerequisites

Following requirements are demanded: Heating. Ventilation.

## Syllabus of lectures

Installation influence to lay-out solution and to architecture. Building water main
design, building connection to public water main. Inner canalization design and house
drains. Domestic waste water, storm sewer. Inner gas conduit design, low-pressure
and middle-pressure gas fixtures. Gas apparatuses.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Main water piping - analysis water demand, calculation distribution water piping,
examples connecting and wiring water piping. Prepare domestic hot water - calculation
warm service water demand, project of circulation piping. Public sewer - project sewers,
examples projects and connection houses on public sewer. Sanitary pipe work - project
of sanitary pipe work, project of drain roof. Gas engineering - project of gas fixture,
project and locating of gas meter and gas regulator. Gas apparatus - project of gas
apparatus, project of flue gas ducting.

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Needed knowledge and skills for engineering practise in area application of basic
princips of sanitary instalations with concentrated on gas, water and canalization
pipe systems.
