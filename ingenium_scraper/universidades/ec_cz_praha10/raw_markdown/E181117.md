# Heat Processes and Heat Exchangers

- Code: E181117
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

Theoretical foundations of thermodynamic and hydrodynamic calculation of heat exchangers
and their design. Theoretical foundations of thermodynamic calculation and design
of evaporators and dryers. Heat treatment of foodstuffs and nonconventional methods
of their heating (direct ohmic heating and microwave heating). The students will have
laboratory exercises/measurements in pilot plant laboratory where they will deal with
measurement on real processes and process apparatuses.

## Syllabus of lectures

Heat exchangers. Classification, functional description and design. The TEMA standard.
Thermal and hydraulic calculation of heat exchangers. Mass, enthalpy and exergy balance,
convective heat transfer and pressure losses of heat exchangers. Design and rating
of heat exchangers LMTD method, thermal effectiveness method and Roetzel-Spang method
(Heat Atlas).
Temperature profiles in shell and tube heat exchangers and compact heat exchangers
based on a solution of the differential equations describing temperature profiles
(Luo, Li, Roetzel). The zonal (cell) method.
An overall heat transfer coefficient. Convective heat transfer coefficients and pressure
losses in plate heat exchangers and shell and tube heat exchangers (Martin, Bell-Delaware).
Fouling in heat exchangers. Heat transfer intensification (modification of heat transfer
surface, swirlers, nanoparticles).
Compact heat exchangers. Finned heat exchangers. Fin effect.
A heat exchanger optimization and heat exchanger networks (EGM, PINCH)
Measurement of a heat transfer and pressure drop of the heat exchanger and its mathematical
model. Measurement of the convective heat transfer coefficient.
Heat transfer at pool and convective boiling (Chen) and condensation (Nusselt). Batch
heating and cooling.
Evaporation and design of evaporators. Boiling point elevation. Single and multi-stage
evaporators. Methods of energy consumption optimization (thermal vapor recompression,
mechanical vapor recompression, heat pump).
Drying and dryers. Contact, convective and spray drying. Single and multiple stage
dryers. Drying air and dried material characteristics (wet air, sorption isotherm).
Mass and enthalpy balance of drying process. Mathematical models of a first and second
stage of drying. Spray dryers (atomizing of dried material, drying of droplet).
Thermal treatment of foodstuffs. Unconventional method of the heating (direct ohmic
heating and microwave heating).
