# Practicum in Automatic Control

- Code: E376021
- ECTS: 2.0

## Synopsis

Laboratory tasks in our Automatic Control Laboratory: Dosage of liquid, bathyscaphe,
a system of three tanks - level control in the tanks, controlled digital PID controller
UDC 3000 , water levitation, air levitation, hot air model, variable speed DC motor,
electric motor with a generator, motor control „Mendocino“, ball on a stick, ball
on the ellipse, teaching model robot OSKAR 95, virtual laboratory control via the
Internet, remote access.
