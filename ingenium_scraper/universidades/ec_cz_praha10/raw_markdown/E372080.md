# Measurement in Engineering

- Code: E372080
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Static and dynamic properties of measurement instruments. Measurement chain, measurement
errors, they sources and correction. Summary of principles, sensors and transducers
(temperature, position, force, pressure, speed, acceleration, torque, flow, humidity,
liquid level). Calibration and verification of measurement instruments.

## Requirements / prerequisites

see moodle

## Syllabus of lectures

1.Measurement system, Static and dynamic characteristics of transducers. Accuracy
and sensitivity of transducers and instruments. Uncertainty of measurements
2.Time constant of transducer and its determination, Electronic processing circuits,
discretization of analog signals
3.Temperature transducers I - resistive, thermocouples
4.Temperature transducers II - semiconductor sensors, infrared
5.Force and torque transducers -resistive, inductive, capacitive and piezoresistive
sensors
6.Pressure transducers I - resistive, capacitive, humidity
7.Pressure transducers II- inductive, piezoresistive
8.Position and distance transducers I
9.Position and distance transducers II
10.RPM, speed and acceleration - mechanical, impulse, inductive, resistive, capacitive
and semiconductive sensors
11.Flow
12.Liquid level transducers

## Syllabus of tutorials

1.Uncertainties of measurement
2.Optics I
3.Optics II
4.DC and AC bridges

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

To master basic principles of sensors and theirs correct use. To master correct evaluation
of experimental data.
