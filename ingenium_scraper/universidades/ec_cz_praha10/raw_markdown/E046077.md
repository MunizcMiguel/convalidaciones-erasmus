# German Beginners

- Code: E046077
- ECTS: 2.0

## Synopsis

Mapped to the Common European Framework of Reference level A1. Basic vocabulary of
everyday lifein a written and spoken form. understanding and use of basic expressions
of general scientific terminology.

## Requirements / prerequisites

mastering material that has been taught, 2 tests

## Syllabus of lectures

Strong verbs in the present tense, adjectives, clauses with dass, ordinal numbers,
prepositions, prefixes, reflexive verbs, future tense, reported questions
Food, where we live, being ill
