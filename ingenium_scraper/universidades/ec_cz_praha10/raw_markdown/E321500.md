# Technical Materials I

- Code: E321500
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

The course is intended for students of the bachelor's study program Theoretical Basis
of Mechanical Engineering and is a free continuation of the subjects Material Science
I. and II. and 21st Century Materials. Students will get acquainted with the applications
of a wide range of technical materials, lectures will be supplemented by invited lectures
by experts from practice and excursions to some topics.

## Syllabus of lectures

Introductory lecture
Stainless steels and alloys
Materials for castings
Plastics in mechanical engineering
Biomaterials
Coating
Non-ferrous metal alloys
Reinforcement mechanisms of metallic materials in practice
Composites in mechanical engineering
Lecture by an expert from practice
New types of metallic materials
Ceramics for construction applications
Technical materials as you already know them

## Syllabus of tutorials

Marking of steels
Materials for castings
Plastics
Coating
Reinforcement mechanisms of metallic materials
Composites
Excursions (biomaterials)
Excursion (heat treatment)
Excursions (composites)
Student presentations
