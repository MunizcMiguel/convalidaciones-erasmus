# Constructive Geometry

- Code: E011018
- ECTS: 5.0

## Synopsis

The subject is focused on geometric objects in the space - curves, surfaces and solids
and their properties and mutual relations.

## Syllabus of lectures

Methods of projection. Analytic geometry. Planar kinematic geometry. Helix. Surfaces
of revolution. Helicoidal surfaces. Envelope surfaces. Developable surfaces. Smooth
transition developable surfaces.

## Syllabus of tutorials

Methods of projection. Analytic geometry. Planar kinematic geometry. Helix. Surfaces
of revolution. Helicoidal surfaces. Envelope surfaces. Developable surfaces. Smooth
transition developable surfaces.

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

Monge projection, Obligue projection, Orthogonal axonometry, Linear perspective, Kinematical
geometry, Kinematical geometry, Surfaces of revolution, Surfaces of revolution, Helix,
Helicoidal surfaces, Developable surfaces, Developable surfaces, Envelope surfaces,
Envelope surfaces
