# Instrumentation for Automatic Control II.

- Code: E371509
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

Modern means of pneumatic automatic control, design of sequential circuits and their
simulation in SW
Fluidsim environment, the use of electric equipment and automatic control, signal
filtering, panel 2
position and PID controllers, their design and setting

## Syllabus of lectures

ˇ Partition of automatic control devices according to various criteria
ˇ Pneumatic control - production and compressed air, air in the company hospodářstbí
ˇ Types of pneumatic motors of standard and special, structure, function, application
ˇ Types of cabinets, labeling, design, function, application
ˇ Proposal sequential pneumatic circuits / 1
ˇ Proposal sequential pneumatic circuits / 2
° SW medium for design and simulation FLUIDSIM, creating applications, examples from
practice
ˇ Analog. digital circuits and control systems, signal filtering, analog and digital
filters, realization
ˇ Digital controllers, the issue of their constructions, methods of experimental setup
ˇ Electro-mechanical, electropneumatic and electrohydraulic actuators, design, design
ˇ Static and dynamic properties of DC servo motors
ˇ Motion solenoids, stepper motors
ˇ Communication in industrial systems, interfaces, TIA / EIA 232, 422, 423, 485, USB
