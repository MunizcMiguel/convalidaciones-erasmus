# Transmissions

- Code: E211700
- ECTS: 4.0

## Synopsis

The course provides a general summary of transmissions for various applications which
the student in the specialization designer-calculator meets. The gearing basics of
production and transport machines will be briefly explained, important calculations
will be discussed in more detail on the examples of transmission devices of motor
vehicles.

## Syllabus of lectures

Transmission layouts of motor vehicles
Transmission layouts of transport machines
Transmission layouts of production machines
Friction elements in transmissions (dry clutches, synchronizers, multi-plate clutches/brakes)
Mechanical gearboxes of motor vehicles
Hydrodynamic transmissions (clutches, converters, retarders)
Planetary gearsets (functions, calculation basics)
Automatic and two-clutch gearboxes of motor vehicles
Differentials
Hydrostatic transmissions
PTO shafts, freewheels, multi-axle drive
Variators
Transmissions with power flow split and their applications

## Study objective (learning outcomes)

The topic gives overview of all existing transmission types. The focus is given to
transmissions of motor vehicles, but briefly are described also the gearboxes used
in transport devices and planes.
