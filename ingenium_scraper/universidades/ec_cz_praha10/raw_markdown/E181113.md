# Reactor and Bioreactor Design

- Code: E181113
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

Design of equipment where reactants are transformed to products due to chemical or
biochemical reactions. Principles of designing basic types of reactors and bioreactors
using reaction kinetics. Basic design of structural configurations, using special
materials, and other aspects important from the operational point of view (sealing,
oscillations). Applications in chemical, pharmaceutical and food industries.

## Syllabus of lectures

Basis of chemical kinetics and chemical equilibrium.
Kinetics of homogeneous and heterogeneous reactions.
Basic types of reactors, batch and continuous systems.
Piston-flow reactors, ideally mixed batch and continuous reactors.
Reactor cascades and non-ideally mixed systems.
Choice of reactor type and optimization with respect to maximum production.
Bioreactors, basic terms and basic technological scheme of the process.
Bioreactor types, kinetics of biochemical processes, limitation of microorganism growth.
Configurations of supply and distribution of components limiting the microorganism
growth.
Calculations and design of basic bioreactor types, influence of hydrodynamics and
other factors on production.
Fundamentals of technical design for reactors and bioreactors from laboratory to industrial
scale.
Material for reactors and bioreactors, hygienic design, technological design.
Sealing of reactors, oscillation of movable structures, natural frequencies.
