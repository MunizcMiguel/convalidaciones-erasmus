# Complex functions and integral and discrete transforms

- Code: E013019
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

Complex functions, derivatives and integrals for complex functions, Taylor and Laurent
series, residuum in singular point. Laplace tramsform including the discrete form.
Transfer function, convolution. Applications for technical problems with ordinary
and partial differential equations. Fourier transform including the discrete form.
Amplitude spectra, filters.
