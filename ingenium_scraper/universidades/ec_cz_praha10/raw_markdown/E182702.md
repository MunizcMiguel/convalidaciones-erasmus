# Measurement on Process Equipment

- Code: E182702
- ECTS: 3.0

## Synopsis

Measurement and evaluation of process characteristics, material and energy balances
on selected process equipments in the pilot hall and laboratories of the Department.

## Syllabus of lectures

1. Introduction. Safety instructions
2. Rheology - rotary viscosimeter and injection viscosimeter
3. RTD
4. Mixing I - homogenization
5. Mixing II - suspendation
6. Fluidization (fixed bed, fluidized bed)
7. Laboratory fermenter (mass transfer)
8. Film evaporator
9. Convective dryer (drying curve)
10. Spray dryer
11. Ohmic heating I
12. Ohmic heating II
13. Presentation of experiment results
