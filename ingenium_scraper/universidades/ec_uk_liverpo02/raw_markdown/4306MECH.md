# Engineering Practice (4306MECH)

- Level: FHEQ Level 4
- Credits: 20 UK credits (≈ 10 ECTS)
- Catalogue version: 2025.01
- Teaching period: September (28 Weeks); language of instruction: English

## Overview

This 20 credit module is delivered across both semesters 1 and 2. Semester 1 will focus on developing professional and academic standards. Semester 2 will focus on practical skills, design and manufacturing.

## Content / description

**Professional standards:**
identifying hazards and managing risk; cyber security; project management; initiating professional development.
**Academic standards:**
finding and using information; written and oral communication skills; referencing; plagiarism and appropriate use of generative AI.
**Technical skills:**
laboratory based experimental work; workshop based machining and assembly skills; computer-aided design and technical drawings.

## Learning and teaching strategy

This 20 credit module is delivered across both semesters 1 and 2. Semester 1 will focus on developing professional and academic standards. Semester 2 will focus on practical skills, design and manufacturing.

## Other learning experiences

This module introduces students to a range of professional and practical engineering skills.

## Learning activities

- Lecture: 21 hours
- Online: 15 hours
- Off Site: 32 hours
- Practical: 18 hours

## Learning outcomes

- MLO1: Evaluate the societal role of the professional engineer.
- MLO2: Conduct a case study investigating the causes and potential mitigation factors for a chosen high profile engineering disaster.
- MLO3: Safely conduct machining, fabrication and assembly operations in an appropriate workshop environment.
- MLO4: Communicate the technical specification of individual machined components and assemblies.
- MLO5: Communicate the findings of experimental work to a wide audience of technical and non-technical stakeholders.
