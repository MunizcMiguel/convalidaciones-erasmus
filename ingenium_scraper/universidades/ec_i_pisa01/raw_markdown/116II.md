## Learning objectives

The aim of this course is to introduce the principal models, methods, and tools for managing and organizing firms. The knowledge gained will enable students to comprehend the particular contexts they encounter and also to recognize, articulate, and address challenges.

The course also includes an optional project for examining real Italian ICT companies' balance sheets, available from the AIDA Bureau van Dijk database (which can be accessed by using UNIPI login details). Participants will form groups of up to five to review a selected firm's financial statements. Groups will present their findings through a written report and oral presentation at the course's conclusion. The project aims to equip students with practical skills for the analysis of economic and financial real cases and enhance their teamwork, reporting, and presentation abilities within a set timeframe.

In addition, students opting to undertake the project regarding the balance sheet analysis will develop technical skills as well as capacities in terms of teamwork, writing a report, and presenting the results in a limited time frame.

## Assessment methods

The assessment includes a verbal examination, which is conducted after one or two written assignments that may be reviewed during the oral segment. Answers to the written assignments will be published on the course website. No study materials are allowed to be used during the exams. The written portion requires students to complete the task(s), showcasing their understanding of the fundamental concepts covered in the course. In the oral exam (which typically occurs on the same day as the written test unless there's a large number of examinees), the student's ability to discuss the main contents of the course with competence, critical awareness, and expressive correctness will be assessed.

A mid-term test will be proposed in the last weeks of the course, which, if passed, can be used instead of the exercises proposed at the beginning of the exam session.

There will be an optional project available during the course. Participants in this project may opt for their performance to be considered as part of their total exam evaluation. To pass the exam, comprehension of the complete syllabus is necessary, except for the section addressed by the optional project (balance sheets and analysis of balance sheets).

## Assessment criteria of knowledge

The exam consists of an oral test preceded by one or two written exercises (their solution will be published on the course website) that may be subject to further discussion during the oral test. During the exercises, no teaching material will be allowed. During the written test, the student must solve the exercise(s) to demonstrate his/her ability to apply the basic principles illustrated during the course. During the oral test (which, except for very crowded sessions, will take place on the same day as the written test), the student's ability to discuss the main contents of the course with competence, critical awareness and expressive correctness will be assessed.

A mid-term test will be proposed in the last weeks of the course, which, if passed, can be used instead of the exercises proposed at the beginning of the exam session.

During the course, an optional project regarding balance sheet and balance sheet analysis will be offered. Students opting to undertake the project can ask for this project to be considered in their final exam assessment. In this case, the section of the program the project addresses (balance sheet and balance sheet analysis) won’t be part of the oral exam.
