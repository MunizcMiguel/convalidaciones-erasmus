## Learning objectives

The student who passes the exam will have acquired a strong understanding of the physical models used in mechanics and static electromagnetism, as well as a solid grasp of the related fundamental mathematical tools and the connection between theoretical and experimental physics

## Skills

Ability to describe the motion in space of point-like objects or extended bodies using an appropriate vector formalism. Ability to deduce the equations that describe the time evolution of the motion of objects subjected to external forces or actions.

Ability to understand and describe simple phenomena in electrostatics and magnetostatics.

## Prerequisites

Basic skills in:

- Mathematical analysis: derivatives, integrals, solving linear and quadratic equations, solving simple differential equations.
- Euclidean geometry: elementary vector calculus, matrices.

## Contents

Physical quantities and measurement methods.

Vector quantities and operations with vectors.

Kinematics.

Dynamics of a point mass.

Universal gravitation and forces at a distance.

Reference frames.

Work and energy.

Dynamics of systems.

Rotational dynamics.

Electrostatics.

Magnetostatics
