# Random Signals in Telecommunications and Computer Engineering

ECTS: 6 · Year 2 · Semester 4

## Objectives (learning outcomes)

After this teaching unit, the student should be able to achieve the following learning objectives:

LO1- Manipulate probabilities, discrete and continuous random variables, including the transformation of a random variable into a different one, and also know algorithms to generate samples of a given random variable by computer simulation;

LO2- Characterize and manipulate random signals in communication systems, including noise and multipath fading, and analyse filtering operations on random signals;

LO3- Analyse and model stochastic signals through computer simulation.

## Programme (contents)

PC1 - Introduction to sample space and probability.

PC2 - Foundations of probability theory, random variables, operators, and fundamental metrics.

PC3 - Probability models and their applications in telecommunications engineering and computer science, including the computer-based generation of random variable samples.

PC4 - Random signals and noise with applications to telecommunications engineering and computer science.

## Evaluation (assessment)

Assessment can be carried out in one of the following two ways:

A) Continuous Assessment:

This consists of two written tests. A midterm written test, to be taken during the semester, and a second test held on the scheduled date for the first examination during the first examination period. The final grade for the teaching unit is calculated as the simple average of the scores of the two written tests, with each test contributing 50% to the final grade. The minimum score required for each test is 8.0. The average of the two tests must be equal to or greater than 9.5 in order to pass the unit. Assessment through option A is only available during the first examination period, in accordance with the general assessment regulations at Iscte.

B) Assessment by a Final Exam:

This consists of a written exam worth 100% of the final grade. The student can sit for exam either on the first or on the second examination period. Students who are eligible under Iscte regulations can also take the exam in the special examination period. The minimum passing grade in the exam is 9.5.
