# Network Security and Management

ECTS: 6 · Year 3 · Semester 6

## Objectives (learning outcomes)

OA1 - To distinguish, evaluate and implement different available protocols or applications essential for computer network support and management.

OA2 - To know different security methods in computer networks and systems and identify solutions for potential security threats

OA3 - To distinguish, evaluate and implement in an integrated form, with other protocols, different available security techniques.

OA4 - Taking into account concrete network management and security problems, the student should be able to use, in an integrated manner, systems, applications, services and/or tools that solve those problems.

## Programme (contents)

CP1 – Session and Data Management in Distributed Applications: session management and data storage in distributed applications;

CP2 – Management of Overlay Networks: Ad-hoc or structured overlay networks; file access;

CP3 – Management of Network Equipment and Services: architecture; SNMP protocol; monitoring; ASN.1; encoding;

CP4 – Introduction to Security: information attacks and security; cryptography and security services; ciphers and authentication using symmetric, asymmetric, and hash algorithms; cipher chaining; public key algorithms; key distribution infrastructures and digital certification; authentication; digital signatures; non-repudiation; integrity;

CP5 – Multi-layer Network Security: security using PAP, CHAP, EAP, RADIUS, IPSec, SSL/TLS, S-HTTP, PGP, DNSSEC; firewalls; blockchain; application-layer security.

## Evaluation (assessment)

Assessment Summary:

Continuous Assessment During the Semester:

30% – 1st written test, held mid-semester.

35% – 2nd written test, held on the same day as the regular first season exam. A minimum weighted average of 8 (out of 20) is required across both written tests.

35% – 5 laboratory assignments (9%, 9%, 7%, 5%, 5%), with a minimum weighted average of 8 (out of 20).

If a student has a temporary incapacity status that coincides with a lab session, the result of up to two lab assignments may be replaced by the score of the written test.

First Season Exam:

Written exam worth 100%, covering CP1 to CP5.

Second Season Exam:

Written exam worth 100%, covering CP1 to CP5.

Special Season Exam:

Written exam worth 100%, covering CP1 to CP5.
