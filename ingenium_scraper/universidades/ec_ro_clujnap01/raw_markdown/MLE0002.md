## 7. Objectives of the discipline (outcome of the acquired competencies)

7.1 General objective of the • To acquire elementary knowledge about differential and integral calculus for
discipline real-valued functions of one or several real variables.
• Sequences and series of real numbers
• Power series
• Taylor series
• Riemann integrals, improper integrals
7.2 Specific objective of the
• Partial derivatives. Gradient. Higher order derivatives.
discipline
• Extremum points
• Gradient descent methods
• Constrained optimization
• Double integrals and triple integrals
