7.1 General objective of the • Knowledge of computer architectural models, processor operation, use of
discipline computer information representation systems.
• The student learns about computer architectural models, processor operation,
and the use of computer information representation systems.
• Introduction to assembly language programming, which ensures
understanding of the architecture and functioning of a microprocessor.
• Understanding the impact of the 80x86 processor architecture on the
