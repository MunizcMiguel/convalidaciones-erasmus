# Russian 5 (o,w)

- **Code**: LC-8555

- **Credits**: 3 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Language and Communication Studies > Compulsory Foreign Language > Russian

## Learning outcomes

You will practise media literacy and your skills at writing messages using various tools. You will be able to discuss familiar topics and initiate conversations. You will perceive and gather information important to you from texts with predictable content.

The course is conducted as hybrid teaching, allowing students to choose to participate in either classroom or remote sessions.

## Content

More extensive practice of speaking and writing messages in ordinary situations, listening comprehension, and modelling of real situations, such as hosting guests. Delivering a very simple presentation. More extensive practice of expressions of time and verbs of motion.
