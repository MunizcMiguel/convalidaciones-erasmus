# Electrical Energy Systems

- **Code**: ELEC-C8002

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Electronics and Electrophysics

## Learning outcomes

After the course, the student understands the basic structure and operation principles of power systems. He/she/they can solve technical problems of power networks, understands the basics of power transmission and power balance, knows the basics of electrical safety.

## Content

Power markets, power generation, power transmission in three phase network, basic structure and components of power networks, electrical safety, power quality, demand response and future energy systems.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: ELEC-C4110 Piirianalyysi 1, or corresponding basic knowledge of circuit theory
