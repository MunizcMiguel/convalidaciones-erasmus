# Stochastic Phenomena in Communications

- **Code**: ELEC-C7250

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Information Technology

## Learning outcomes

After the course, the student 1) knows the principles of data traffic modelling in different timescales, 2) can apply Markov processes to model the traffic process of simple communications and IT systems, 3) can analyze these models to evaluate the performance, 4) can define and apply the basic concepts in information theory (entropy, etc.), 5) can distinguish between lossless and lossy data compression and can describe different compression methods, 6) can calculate the capacity of a simple communications channel.

## Content

Recap of basic probability. Introduction to stochastic processes and the Poisson process. Discrete-state and continuous-time Markov processes and discrete-time Markov chains. Data traffic modeling in different time scales and different traffic types. Various queuing systems and their application to performance evaluation. Information theory and its significance in information technology. Forms of entropy and their relationship to channel and source coding. Basic compression methods. Capacities of discrete-time, continuous-time and multiuser channels.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: First year math courses and basics in probability theory.
