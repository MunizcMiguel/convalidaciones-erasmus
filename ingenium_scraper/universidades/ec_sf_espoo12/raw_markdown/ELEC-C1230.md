# Control Engineering

- **Code**: ELEC-C1230

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Automation and Robotics

## Learning outcomes

The student masters the basics of continuous-time control theory and understands the principles and analysis methods of dynamical systems. She/he can design controllers by different methods and verify the operation of the closed loop system by analytical means and through simulation.

## Content

Fundamentals of analogue control. Dynamical systems. Frequency domain models. State space models. Design and analysis of control systems. The PID controller. Basic principles of digital control. In the exercises the Matlab/Control System Toolbox and Simulink are used.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: Basic knowledge of university level mathematics and physics. For example: Basic course of differential equations.
