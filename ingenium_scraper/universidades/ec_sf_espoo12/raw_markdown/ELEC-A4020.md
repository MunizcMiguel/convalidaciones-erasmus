# Workplace Learning

- **Code**: ELEC-A4020

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Vapaasti valittavat opinnot tai toinen sivuaine > Elective studies

## Learning outcomes

To perceive the connection of knowledge and skills acquired in learning needs of working life. To reflect own strenghts and clarify career plans. To develop and observe engineering skills and general employability skills while working.

Specific goals are defined individually before the course.

## Content

Working with the company and a live-work development of professional skills. Engineering skills are, e.g., presentation and interpretation of data, use of computational models, asking proper questions, problem definition, acquisition of information and evaluation. General working life skills include managerial and employee skills, team skills, oral and written communication.
