# Basics of Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer

- **Code**: EKO-C2001

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Electrical Energy Engineering

## Learning outcomes

- Use of ideal gas and Van der Waals equations of state. - Calculation and interpretation of the compressibility factor. - Calculation and interpretation of the vapor quality factor. - Interpretation of state diagrams. - Understanding the first law of thermodynamics. - Calculation of work, internal energy change, and heat along a process path. - Calculation of thermal efficiency for a cyclic process. - Understanding enthalpy and the energy balance of a steady flow process. - Calculation of thermal efficiency for power plant processes. - Understanding entropy and the second law of thermodynamics. - Definitions and use of fundamental relations to calculate the entropy change of an ideal gas. - Definitions and use of Gibbs and Helmholtz free energies to calculate state quantities. - Understanding the heat pump cycle and calculation of the COP coefficient. - Definition and use of isentropic efficiency in power plant and heat pump processes. - Use of Fourier's law in one dimension to determine temperature profile in a solid. - Determining the heat transfer coefficient in the case of several material layers for a plate-like element. - Evaluation of the convective heat transfer coefficient. - Analysing heat transfer through a solid material layer into a flowing fluid. - Radiation heat transfer between two surfaces and application to the analysis of a solar thermal collector.
