# Engineering design basics B

- **Code**: KON-C2003

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Automation and Robotics

## Learning outcomes

To identify and understand basic concepts in machine construction and methods for technical documentation.

## Content

Introduction to three-dimensional computer-aided design (CAD) software in mechanical engineering, including part and assembly modeling as well as technical documentation. Introduction to mechanical engineering calculations and machine elements, as well as manufacturing technology. Not for Energy and mechanical engineering (EKO) students.
