# Semiconductor Technology

- **Code**: ELEC-C9310

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Electronics and Electrophysics

## Learning outcomes

After the course, the student will be able to name and explain the key concepts and vocabulary of semiconductor technology, understand the properties of semiconductors and know how to apply methods with which and how to influence them, can name the key fabrication techniques of semiconductor technology, can give examples of semiconductor applications.

## Content

Properties of semiconductors, charge carriers and their motion, energy bands, pn-junction, device manufacturing technnology, semiconductor applications

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: Modern physics or corresponding knowledge
