# German 7a (o,w)

- **Code**: LC-6371

- **Credits**: 3 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Language and Communication Studies > Compulsory Foreign Language > German

## Learning outcomes

At course completion, you will:

- be familiar with the main events of recent German history

- have deepened your knowledge of technology, business, or arts in different eras

- be able to discuss and write about these themes.

## Content

Cultural knowledge of Germany from the viewpoints of technology, business and arts in the Weimar Republic, during national socialism, in the era of two Germanies, during the reunification, and thereafter.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: LC-6350 German 5 or LC-6360 German 6 or equivalent knowledge
