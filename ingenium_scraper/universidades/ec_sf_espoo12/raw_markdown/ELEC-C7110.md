# Fundamentals of Information Technology

- **Code**: ELEC-C7110

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Information Technology

## Learning outcomes

The student should learn the fundamental concepts, objectives, and principles of information systems and services. After the course, the student shall be able to master some simple but useful mathematical models and be able to apply them to solving practical problems. Finally, the student should develop general insight in the complex system consisting of various information and communications technologies and services.

## Content

Part 1: Fundamentals of information and communication technology 1) The development of information technology 2) Computer organization and architecture 3) Operating systems and virtualization 4) Basics of transmission methods 5) State machines and communication protocols 6) The most important information networks 7) Internet Part 2: Basics of Data Analysis 8) Sampling and basics of digital signal processing 9) Machine learning and artificial intelligence 10) Audio and speech processing Part 3: The societal implications of information and communication technologies 11) Energy consumption and CO2 emissions 12) The digital transformation
