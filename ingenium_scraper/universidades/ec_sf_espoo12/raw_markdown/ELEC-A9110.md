# Classical Physics

- **Code**: ELEC-A9110

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: General Studies

## Learning outcomes

The main objective is that the student constructs for him/herself an idea of the most important abstractions, principles, definitions and concepts of classical mechanics and thermophysics, as well as the ability to apply them in simple calculations. This requires students to form mental structures and mathematical skills, with which s/he is able to translate the principles and definitions of physics into mathematical expressions.

## Content

- Mathematics: applications of vector and differential calculus, differentiation and integration, trigonometry on physics

- Kinematics: translational and rotational motion, relative motion and Galilean coordinate transformations

- Dynamics: Newton's laws of motion, particles and rigid body dynamics; momentum

- Energy considerations: work, power and energy; potential energy and conservation laws

- Oscillatory and wave motion: harmonic oscillator, undamped, damped, and forced oscillation; mechanical waves

- Basics of thermodynamics: temperature, heat, thermodynamical processes, thermal phenomena

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: High school physics (courses 1 and 3-5) and advanced mathematics (courses 1-7), or corresponding knowledge and skills.
