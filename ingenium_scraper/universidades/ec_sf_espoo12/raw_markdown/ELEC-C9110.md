# Circuit Analysis

- **Code**: ELEC-C9110

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Electronics and Electrophysics

## Learning outcomes

After the course, students can analyze the operation of small alternating- and direct-current circuits with suitable methods. They understand the concepts of complex effect, filters, etc., and can deepen their knowledge in future courses.

## Content

The basic circuit elements: resistance, capacitance, inductance, independent and controlled sources, operational amplifiers and transformer. Analysis methods for DC and AC circuits: circuit transformations, mesh, nodal, superposition, Thevenin's, and Norton's methods. Basic filters and resonance circuits. Power matching and reactive power compensation. Analysis of symmetrical three-phase circuits.
