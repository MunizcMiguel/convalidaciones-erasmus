# Analysis of Electrical Systems

- **Code**: ELEC-C9120

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Electronics and Electrophysics

## Learning outcomes

After the course, students can analyze the transient behavior of circuits using the Laplace transform and know concepts that pertain to system functions. The student knows the most essential characteristics and properties of various amplifier models and understands their effect on the transfer function. The student is able to model the frequency dependency if the amplifer models, knows the limitations caused by amplifier non-idealities, and can to analyze asimple amplifier circuits. The student can analyze the most common operational amplifier circuits and the affect of non-idealities in operational amplifier circuits.

## Content

Transient analysis and Laplace transform. Driving-point and transfer functions, stability. Frequency response and Bode diagram. Studying multifrequency signals. Amplifier models and frequency response. Operational amplifier circuits and the effect of nonidealities.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: ELEC-C9110 Circuit analysis or ELEC-C4110 Circuit analysis I or equivalent knowledge
