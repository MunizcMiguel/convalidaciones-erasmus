# German 6 (o,w)

- **Code**: LC-6360

- **Credits**: 3 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Language and Communication Studies > Compulsory Foreign Language > German

## Learning outcomes

At course completion, you will:

- be able to express yourself fluently in both speech and writing in different situations

- be able to give presentations

- have expanded your knowledge of the German-speaking countries and their cultures.

## Content

Improving your spoken and written expression in German, giving presentations, practising demanding grammar. Topics include German media, working life, the natural environment, sustainable development, studying, cultural knowledge of the German-speaking countries.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: LC-6350 German 5 or equivalent knowledge
