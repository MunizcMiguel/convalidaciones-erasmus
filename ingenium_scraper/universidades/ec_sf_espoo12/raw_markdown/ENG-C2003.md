# Basic course on fluid mechanics

- **Code**: ENG-C2003

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Electrical Energy Engineering

## Learning outcomes

The central learning objective of the course is to build up the student's all-round education in fluid mechanics. The weight is on learning of central concepts, terminology, flow phenomena and solution techniques for basic fluid mechanics problems. A student, who has completed the course, is able to use the terminology in fluid mechanics and has an ability to explain the meaning of the central concepts in fluid mechanics, the principles behind the fundamental equations and how these equations can be simplified. The student has an ability to explain the basic idea of dimensional analysis and to apply it in fluid mechanics e.g. for the scaling of model test results. The student is able to solve simple problems in fluid mechanics e.g. to determine the flowrate, pressure loss or pump head in pipe system and to determine forces or moments on bodies or characteristics of rotating flow machinery based on fluid velocity.

## Content

Fluid at rest and pressure; Bernoulli equation and its application; control volume approach and conservation principle in fluid mechanics; energy equation, losses and the extended Bernoulli equation; differential equations; potential flow and superposition of solutions; dimensionalanalysis; pipe flow; rotating flow machinery; boundary layer and flow around bodies; lift and drag.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: High school mathematics and physics, Useful: Differential and integral calculus 1-3, KJR/COE-C1001 or ENG/COE-C1002 and ENG/COE-C1003
