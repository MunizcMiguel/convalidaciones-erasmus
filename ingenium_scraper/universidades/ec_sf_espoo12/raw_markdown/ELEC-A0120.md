# Planning of Studies

- **Code**: ELEC-A0120

- **Credits**: 1 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: General Studies

## Learning outcomes

Student is capable in planning and monitoring his studies and reflect one's learning skills and motivation.

## Content

Academic advising, making a study plan and monitoring the progress of studies. Answering to the study well-being questionnaire and feedback of it. Content varies each year.
