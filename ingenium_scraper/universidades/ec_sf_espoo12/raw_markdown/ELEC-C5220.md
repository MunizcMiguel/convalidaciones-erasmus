# Machine Learning in Information Technology

- **Code**: ELEC-C5220

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Information Technology

## Learning outcomes

After completing the course

- You know general principles and concepts of machine learning, especially neural networks and deep learning, and their most essential methods

- You can apply the learned in some real world problems in applications of information technology, especially of speech technology, signal processing and communications

- You can describe problems of machine learning in speech technology, signal processing and communications

## Content

Machine learning methods and applications in speech technology, signal processing and communications

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: Basic skills in machine learning and statistics, for example, courses CS-C3240 Machine Learning, and MS-A0503 First Course in Probability and Statistics .
