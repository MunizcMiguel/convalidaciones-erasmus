# Electricity and Magnetism

- **Code**: ELEC-A4130

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: General Studies

## Learning outcomes

The student will understand the concepts electric field and magnetic field, what phenomena create these fields, recognise basic electromagnetic phenomena, and how they are related to each other. The student will be able to describe electromagnetic phenomena using elementary mathematical formalism, will be able to solve simple electromagnetic problems applying mathematical tools, and will understand how time-dependency in fundamental quantities leads to electromagnetic waves. The student will also be able to apply the learned concepts in future studies and in professional life.

## Content

Electric charge, Coulomb's law, electric field, Gauss' law, potential, and capacitance. Current, resistance, and electromotive force. Interaction between magnetic fields and currents. Electromagnetic induction, Faraday's law, eddy currents, and inductance. Maxwell s equations in integral form and electromagnetic plane waves. Light reflection and refraction, dispersion and polarization, and interference and diffraction.
