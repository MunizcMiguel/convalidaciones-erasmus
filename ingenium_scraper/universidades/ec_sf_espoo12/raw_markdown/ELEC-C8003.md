# Electrical Energy Conversion

- **Code**: ELEC-C8003

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Electrical Energy Engineering

## Learning outcomes

The student can:

- explain why power electronics and electric machines are needed in a wind turbine;

- calculate the operating point of a three-phase electric machine;

- draw and explain a block diagram of a typical cascade control structure;

- implement a simulation model for an electric machine drive.

## Content

The course deals with electric machines and drives, power electronics, and selected application examples (e.g. wind power, solar electricity, electric vehicle drivetrain). More detailed content is:

- mechanics (equation of motion, load torque, transmissions);

- simple magnetic circuits;

- direct current machine, externally-excited synchronous machine (permanently magnetized as a special case);

- phasors and space vectors;

- H-bridge, diode bridge, three-phase converter, harmonics;

- cascade control.​

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: Circuit analysis, electricity and magnetism, control engineering.
