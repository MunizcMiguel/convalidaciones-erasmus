# Quantum Information

- **Code**: ELEC-C9440

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Electronics and Electrophysics

## Learning outcomes

The student will understand basic ideas of quantum information as it applies to quantum computation and communication.

## Content

Qubits, quantum gates and circuits. Basic notions of quantum information. Elements of quantum computation and simple algorithms. Basics of quantum communication and its applications.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: ELEC-C9420 Introduction to quantum technology/ ELEC-C9220 Moderni fysiikka/ELEC-C3220 Kvantti-ilömiöt and MS-A0011 Matrix algebra, or corresponding knowledge and skills.
