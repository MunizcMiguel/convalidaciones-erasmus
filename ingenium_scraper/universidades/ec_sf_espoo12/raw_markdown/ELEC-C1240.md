# State-Space Models and Discrete-Time Control

- **Code**: ELEC-C1240

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Automation and Robotics

## Learning outcomes

After the course, the student

- Understands the principles of computer-based discrete-time control

- Can form discrete-time linear state-space models

- Understand the basics of state-space control (controllability, observability, state feedback)

- Understands the formulation of control problems as optimization

- Understands the effect of stochasticity in control systems

- Understands the effect of non-linearities in control

## Content

State variable models

Discretization in time

Stability of state-space models

State-space control design: state feedback, controllability, observability, state observer

Basics of optimal control

Implications of stochasticity in control systems

Nonlinearity and control

Data-driven models in control. Model Identification.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: ELEC-C1230 Control Engineering or equivalent knowledge
