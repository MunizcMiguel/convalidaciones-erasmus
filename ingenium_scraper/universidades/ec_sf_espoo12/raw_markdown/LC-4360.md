# French 6 (o,w)

- **Code**: LC-4360

- **Credits**: 3 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Language and Communication Studies > Compulsory Foreign Language > French

## Learning outcomes

At course completion, you will:

- be able to express yourself fluently in both speech and writing in different situations.

- be able to give presentations

- be able to follow and discuss news in French.

- have expanded your knowledge of French-speaking medias and their cultural features.

## Content

Improving your spoken and written expression in French, giving presentations, practising demanding grammar, possible visits to off-campus French-language events. Topics include French media, the workplace and work cultures, studying, knowledge of French-speaking countries and cultures.

## Prerequisites

Compulsory prerequisites: LC-4340 French 4 (3 cr) or equivalent knowledge

Recommended prerequisites: LC-4350 French 5 (3 cr) or equivalent knowledge
