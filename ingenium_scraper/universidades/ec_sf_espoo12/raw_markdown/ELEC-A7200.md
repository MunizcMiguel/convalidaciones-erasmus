# Signals and Systems

- **Code**: ELEC-A7200

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: General Studies

## Learning outcomes

The student should learn the basic principles of signal and system analysis, know the most common signal transforms and understand the frequency domain representation of the signal and learn to use for signal analysis. After the course the student should master filtering of deterministic signals with linear lowpass and bandpass filters and know the principles of analog to digital transform and the utilized mapping parameters.

## Content

Nature of signals, frequency analysis of signals, linear systems and their signal analysis, modulated signals, calculation exercises.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: Good skills in basics of differential and integral calculus and matrix algebra, such as in courses MS-A000X Matrix algebra, MS-A02XX Differential and integral calculus 2, or equivalent knowledge.
