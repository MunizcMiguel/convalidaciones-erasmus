# Modern Physics

- **Code**: ELEC-C9220

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Electronics and Electrophysics

## Learning outcomes

The course aims to familiarize the student with the theories of modern physics, basics and concepts. The student will understand how the properties of matter can be explained on the basis of quantum mechanics, and how they are applied in modern technology. In addition s/he will learn, when and how classical mechanical considerations must be modified according to the special theory of relativity.

## Content

Basic formalism and phenomena of quantum mechanics, the quantum structure of matter, introduction to quantum informatics, basics of special relativity.

## Prerequisites

Compulsory prerequisites: ELEC-A9110 Classical physics/ELEC-A3110 Mechanics and ELEC-A4130 Electricity and magnetism, or corresponding knowledge and skills.

Recommended prerequisites: ELEC-C4140 Field theory, ELEC-A7200 Signals and systems, MS-C1420 Fourier analysis, MS-A0502 Probability theory and statistics, or corresponding knowledge and skills.
