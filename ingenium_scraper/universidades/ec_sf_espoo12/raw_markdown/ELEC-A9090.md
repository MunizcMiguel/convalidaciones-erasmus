# Artificial intelligence in professional use

- **Code**: ELEC-A9090

- **Credits**: 2 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: General Studies

## Learning outcomes

The student can responsibly use AI tools to support studies and work and can critically evaluate AI-generated answers.

## Content

Artificial intelligence tools and their use in solving professional problems. The student has gained experience with advanced AI.
