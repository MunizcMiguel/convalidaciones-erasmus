# Fundamentals of Automation and Control Engineering

- **Code**: ELEC-C1110

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Automation and Robotics

## Learning outcomes

The student knows the most important application areas of automation and has a general overview of the field, understands the significance of measurements, actuators and feedback as part of the system, is able to build a simple system model from differential equations and simulate it. Understands the basics of model calibration and learning a model from data. Knows the basics of the PID controller. Understand how automation and control technology is used to control robots and industrial processes. Understands the basics of robot kinematics, dynamics, and control.

## Content

System models as differential equations. Optimization / calibration of a known model. Data-based models. Measurement (sampling / aliasing) and discrete time. PID controller. Understanding higher-dimensional systems, such as robots, robot kinematics, dynamics, and control.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: MS-A010* Differentiaali- ja integraalilaskenta I ja ELEC-A3110 Mekaniikka. Or equivalent skills in math and physics.
