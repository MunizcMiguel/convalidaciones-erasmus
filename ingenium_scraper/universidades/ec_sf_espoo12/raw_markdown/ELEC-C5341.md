# Sound And Speech Processing

- **Code**: ELEC-C5341

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Information Technology

## Learning outcomes

After the course students can descibe essential phenomena, methods and applications of sound and speech processing. Students can apply them and basic tools of signal processing to implement sound and speech processing algorithms. Students can apply basics of real time programming in sound and signal processing implementations.

## Content

Sound and speech processing methods and applications, like digital filters and oscillators, room acoustics and binaural audio technology. Functioning of the speech production mechanism and main features of speech signal, fundamentals of speech analysis and speech feature extraction, main areas of speech technology.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: Basics in digital signal processing, for example the course ELEC-C5232 Digital Signal Processing
