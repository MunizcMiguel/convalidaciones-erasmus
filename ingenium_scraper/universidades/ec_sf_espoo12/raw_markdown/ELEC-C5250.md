# Basics of Acoustics and Psychoacoustics

- **Code**: ELEC-C5250

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Information Technology

## Learning outcomes

You will learn the basics of sound as a physical phenomenon and how hearing works as well as perception of sound. You will also learn the basics of the anatomy of the ear, the function of nerve cells and the most important research methods in psychoacoustics. In addition, you will learn how different music instruments produce sound, how different rooms color the sound you perceive, and how human speech is produced. Moreover, you will learn to solve related mathematical problems and analyze sounds with ready made tools. With your learning, you will be able to learn more about acoustics independently and gain a good foundation for studying psychoacoustics and hearing function.

## Content

The course introduces the basics of acoustics as well as hearing. Through the anatomy of the ear, we move on to psychoacoustics and go through the properties of hearing, e.g. critical bands, masking phenomena, and pitch perception. Musical acoustics are introduced through the sound production mechanisms of musical instruments, and correspondingly, the human speech production system is presented physiologically in order to understand what kind of signal speech is. Finally, we go through the basics of room acoustics and explain how rooms shape the sound we perceive and what is noise.
