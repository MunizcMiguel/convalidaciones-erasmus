# LC-4381 French 8 – Communiquer et argumenter (o, w) (3 op)

- **Code**: LC-4381

- **Credits**: 3 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Language and Communication Studies > Compulsory Foreign Language > French

## Learning outcomes

After completing the course, students will be able to:

- communicate in written and oral communication situations in working life

- function in student exchange and work placement situations and in job interviews master argumentation: organizing debates; defining problems; searching for and classifying ideas or arguments; creating outlines, introductions, conclusions, and transitions.

- express their opinions and make their voices heard in negotiations.

- understand argumentative texts and their structure.

## Content

The course introduces students to the French tradition of rhetoric and argumentation by studying articles, essays and presentations. Practical language skills are developed through conversation exercises, practicing meeting etiquette, and giving presentations.
