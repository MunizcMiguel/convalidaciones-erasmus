# Spanish 6 (o,w)

- **Code**: LC-2360

- **Credits**: 3 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Language and Communication Studies > Compulsory Foreign Language > Spanish

## Learning outcomes

After completing the course, the student

- manages communicative situations in everyday and work life both orally and in writing

- understands communication related to abstract topics

- is able to participate actively in communicative situations and produce clear and detailed texts

- is well acquainted with the customs and conversational culture of Spanish-speaking countries and acts accordingly

## Content

- Communicating about diverse concrete and abstract topics, such as identity, thinking and learning

- Strengthening oral expression according to skill level

- Expressing perspectives, assumptions and goals

## Prerequisites

Compulsory prerequisites: LC-2350 or equivalent knowledge.

Recommended prerequisites: LC-2350 of the equivalent knowledge.
