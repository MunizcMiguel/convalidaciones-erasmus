# Entrepreneurial skills in the workplace

- **Code**: ELEC-C0111

- **Credits**: 3 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: General Studies

## Learning outcomes

The course aims to develop the student’s understanding of entrepreneurial skills and their versatile application in a career. Upon completing the course, the student recognizes the importance of a proactive attitude in learning new things and implementing solutions.

Upon completing the course, the student is able to:

- Justify the significance of entrepreneurial skills in engineering work and on diverse future career paths.

- Apply problem-solving, customer understanding, and creative methods in engineering work.

- Identify business opportunities and their development paths.

- Use various communication styles and methods and apply them to make their expertise visible and to develop their professional identity.

- Internalize the importance of self-management and taking responsibility during studies and in the working life.

## Content

The course develops entrepreneurial thinking and entrepreneurship skills that can be utilized in a variety of organizations and career paths without the aim of starting one's own company. Through lectures, exercises, and reflection tasks, participants explore topics such as creativity, communication, and personal expertise, and consider their own future and the essential skills needed in the workplace.
