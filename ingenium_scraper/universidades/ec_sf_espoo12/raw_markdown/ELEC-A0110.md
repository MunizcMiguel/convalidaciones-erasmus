# Introduction to Studies in the Degree Programme in Electrical Engineering

- **Code**: ELEC-A0110

- **Credits**: 1 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: General Studies

## Learning outcomes

After completing the course the student is prepared to university studies. He is capable to search information and take care of study matters. He finds the support services he needs and can plan the studies in short and long term. Student can do a study plan and course schedule and enroll to courses. Student knows how to use the essential IT systems and the library. Student gets practise in time management.

## Content

Orientation to university, student services and studies. Adamimic advising. Making a study plan, planning and monitoring own studies, time management skills. Get to know IT systems. Content varies each year.
