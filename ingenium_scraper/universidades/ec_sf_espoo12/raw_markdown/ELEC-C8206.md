# Automation Systems 2

- **Code**: ELEC-C8206

- **Credits**: 10 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Automation and Robotics

## Learning outcomes

The student has deepened the understanding from the Automation systems 1 course regarding information technology in automation and building automation. The student acquires working knowledge for software development design and project management in the upper layers of the automation pyramid

## Content

Information technology in automation and building automation. Automation application development. Human and machine readable data models in automation projects.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: ELEC-C8205 Automation systems 1 or equivalent knowledge
