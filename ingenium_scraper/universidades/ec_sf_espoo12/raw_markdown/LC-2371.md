# Spanish 7a (o,w)

- **Code**: LC-2371

- **Credits**: 3 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Language and Communication Studies > Compulsory Foreign Language > Spanish

## Learning outcomes

After completing the course, the student

- manages challenging communicative situations in everyday and work life both orally and in writing

- understands fairly well communication related to abstract topics

- can participate actively in communicative situations and produce clear and detailed texts

- can summarize and combine information gathered from various sources

- is well acquainted with the customs and conversational culture of Spanish-speaking countries and acts accordingly

## Content

- Communicating about diverse concrete and abstract topics, such as the relationship between humans and nature

- Strengthening oral expression according to skill level

- Expressing perspectives, conditions and probability

## Prerequisites

Compulsory prerequisites: LC-2360 of the equivalent knowledge.

Recommended prerequisites: LC-2360 of the equivalent knowledge.
