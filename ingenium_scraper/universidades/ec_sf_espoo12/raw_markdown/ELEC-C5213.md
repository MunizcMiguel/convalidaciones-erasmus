# Statistical Signal Modelling

- **Code**: ELEC-C5213

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Information Technology

## Learning outcomes

Student understands basic characteristics and concepts of stochastic vectors and processes and can calculate most essential key figures for different kind of stochastic variables and processes and define their dependences.

Student understands using power density spectrum in modelling and analysis of signals and basic charasteristics of optimal and adaptive filters and their use in noise cancellation and signal prediction.

Student can use computer in analysis of stochastic vectors and processes.

## Content

basic characteristics, concepts, key figures and dependences of stochastic vectors, processes, and distributions, signal modelling, analysis, and prediction

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: Recommended basic skills in matrix calculations, using mathematical softwares, probability and statistics and signal analysis. Suitable courses include MS-A000x, ELEC-A5140, MS-A050x and especially ELEC-A7200 Signals And Systems.
