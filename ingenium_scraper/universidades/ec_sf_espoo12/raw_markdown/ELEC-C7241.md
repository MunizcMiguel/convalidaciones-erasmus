# Computer Networks

- **Code**: ELEC-C7241

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Information Technology

## Learning outcomes

Upon completing the course, the student will be able to: • explain the concepts of computer networks and the principles of packet networks • understand the concepts of the Internet protocol stack and the functioning of key protocols • understand the basics of routing and be familiar with the most common routing algorithms and protocols.

## Content

Principles and key concepts of packet switched networks, Concept of the protocol stack. Link layer: its functions and protocols. Main MAC-algorithms. Internet protocol stack. Main routing principles, algorithms, and protocols. Evaluation of the performance of the computer networks.
