# Spanish 5 (o,w)

- **Code**: LC-2350

- **Credits**: 3 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Language and Communication Studies > Compulsory Foreign Language > Spanish

## Learning outcomes

After completing the course, the student

- is able to interact in most communicative situations both orally and in writing

- understands the main part of oral and written communication, for example at work, in studies and on their free time

- is able to produce coherent texts and describe e.g. events, wishes and goals

- is able to present and briefly justify their opinions and plans

- is familiar with the cultural practices and conversational norms of Spanish-speaking countries and will be able to act accordingly

## Content

- Discussing studies, skills, and working life

- Discussing societal topics, such as well-being, environment, and politics

- Expressing opinions, feelings, and wishes
