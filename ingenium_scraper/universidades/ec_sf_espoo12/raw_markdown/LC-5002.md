# National Language Requirement (Swedish) Oral Test

- **Code**: LC-5002

- **Credits**: 1 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Language and Communication Studies > Second national language of Finland > Swedish as the second national language

## Learning outcomes

- The goal is for students taking the test to demonstrate such satisfactory oral proficiency in Swedish as the Aalto University degree regulations requires, and as is required by law (Act 424/2003 and decree 481/2003) of State personnel in bilingual authorities and as is necessary for their field.

## Content

- The test consists of a presentation along with a small group discussion about the own field of study.

## Prerequisites

Compulsory prerequisites: - The Swedish entry level test, which can be found in MyCourses using the search terms “ruotsin lähtötasotesti + your own degree programme ARTS, BIZ, ENG, CHEM, ELEC or SCI” . - Based on the score obtained in the entry level test, the student will either - complete a Swedish course at the Language Centre that corresponds to their proficiency level, including the examinations arranged as part of the course or - demonstrate advanced familiarity with the Swedish language (e.g. language immersion background, Swedish as a second home language, military service completed in Swedish) as well as previously acquired good basic language skills in their own field of study in a separate general examination during the examination week. A student who wishes to complete the Swedish examinations as general examinations without course participation must obtain a minimum score of 35 in the entry level test and write a motivation letter of at least 300 words in Swedish in a video-proctored EXAM exam room without aids, describing their background and proficiency in Swedish and justifying the need to complete the examinations without course participation.
