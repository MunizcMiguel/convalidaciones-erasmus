# Fundamentals of Communications

- **Code**: ELEC-C7231

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Information Technology

## Learning outcomes

After completing this course, the student will (i) understand the basic functionalities of the physical layer of a digital communication system operating over a noisy and band-limited channel; (ii) understand the fundamental limitations posed on the rate and quality of signal transmission by the channel bandwidth and the signal power; (iii) understand the principles of designing the optimal receiver for a given digital communication system and analyzing the system performance in the case of an AWGN channel; (iv) know the most commonly used line coding and digital carrier modulation schemes; (v) understand the basic principles of OFDM and error control coding.

During the course, students learn and get to practice teamwork and presentation skills needed in the field.

## Content

Mathematics of waveforms. Carrier modulation. Baseband modulation, line codes, pulse shapes, digital modulation constellations. Signal space representation and optimal receiver design for AWGN channels. Performance analysis of digital communication systems in AWGN channels. Basic principles of OFDM. Introduction to error control coding.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: The basics of probability calculus and signal and system analysis, as well as basic usage of Matlab. For example courses MS-A0503, ELEC-A5140, ELEC-A7200.
