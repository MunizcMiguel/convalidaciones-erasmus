# Computer Architectures with RISC-V​

- **Code**: ELEC-C7440

- **Credits**: 5 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Information Technology

## Learning outcomes

After the course the attendee should understand the common architectural elements and inner workings of modern digital computer systems, as well as have some perspective on trade-offs present at the hardware-software interface.​

## Content

The course introduces the evolution of computer architecture and factors influencing the design of hardware and software elements of computer systems.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: Basics of the C programming language (e.g., ELEC-A7100)
