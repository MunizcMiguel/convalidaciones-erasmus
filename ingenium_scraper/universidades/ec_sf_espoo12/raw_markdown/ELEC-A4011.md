# Electrical Engineering Workshop

- **Code**: ELEC-A4011

- **Credits**: 8 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: General Studies

## Learning outcomes

After the course, students will be acquainted with the fundamentals of electronics, and they will have gained the experience of building a prototype as well as working in a project.

## Content

Basics of Arduino and other electronic components. Electrical circuits, sensors, connections and interfaces, combined with software and data networks.
