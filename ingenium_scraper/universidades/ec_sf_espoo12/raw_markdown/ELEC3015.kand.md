# Bachelor's Thesis and Seminar

- **Code**: ELEC3015.kand

- **Credits**: 10 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Major > Information Technology

## Learning outcomes

The goal of the seminar is to learn how to formulate a meaningful research problem out of a given problem together with an advisor, to search for scientific information needed to solve this problem, to classify and build scientific information to a written thesis and to report most essential results orally in scientific style.

Goals for developing working life skills and cooperational skills: student understands the effect of own work and oral communication to the functionality of the group and learns to follow common deadlines and ways of working in the course. Students are supported in developing scientific cooperational skills so that at the end of the course student is expected to succeed well in written and oral peer reviews of theses following appropriate style.

Goals of the bachelor's seminar include:

- critical search of scientific literature

- preparation of study plan

- scientific writing

- oral and written presentation skills of the scientific findings

## Content

Scientific thinking, collection, structuring and processing of information, linquistic and communicative skills.
