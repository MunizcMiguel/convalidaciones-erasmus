# German 5 (o,w)

- **Code**: LC-6350

- **Credits**: 3 ECTS

- **Programme module path**: Language and Communication Studies > Compulsory Foreign Language > German

## Learning outcomes

At course completion, you will:

- have improved your oral skills and be able to give short presentations

- be able to write various kinds of texts about everyday and working life

- have expanded your knowledge of the German-speaking countries.

## Content

Reviewing and deepening knowledge of German grammatical structures, expanding vocabulary, discussing current events, writing various kinds of texts, giving presentations. Themes include working life and studies, the natural environment, culture of the German-speaking countries, the economy, and technology.

## Prerequisites

Recommended prerequisites: LC-6340 German 4 or equivalent knowledge
