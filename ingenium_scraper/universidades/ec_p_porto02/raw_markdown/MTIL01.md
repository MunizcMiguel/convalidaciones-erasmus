# Competências Transversais: Montagem e Teste de Instalações Laboratoriais

- Teaching language: Suitable for English-speaking students
- Edition: Instance: 2025/2026 - 2S (of 18-02-2026 to 05-06-2026)

## Objectives

This course unit aims to promote basic transversal skills for an engineer with responsibilities in a laboratory context, namely in topics related to the identification and use of materials and tools useful in the assembly and testing of laboratory facilities.

## Learning outcomes and competences

Proficiently acquire: • skills in the field of experimentation (formulation and testing of hypotheses, experimental planning, data search); • critical and creative thinking skills in solving engineering problems; designing, implementing, testing and operating units in a laboratory environment.

## Program

The curricular unit is divided into five thematic workshops (oficinas temticas).

## Working method

Presencial

## Teaching methods and learning activities

Learning based on content exposure and practical application in a laboratory context. Problem-based learning will be encouraged. All participants will be given the opportunity to proactively participate in the proposed activities.

## Evaluation Type

Distributed evaluation without final exam

## Assessment Components

Designation Weight (%) In-person participation 50,00 Test 50,00 Total: 100,00

## Calculation formula of final grade

Final grade (CF) = 50% PO + 50% MT where: PO – presence in the workshops; MT – test including the workshops’ contents.

## Eligibility for exams

Attendance at least to 80% of the workshops.

## Examinations or Special Assignments
