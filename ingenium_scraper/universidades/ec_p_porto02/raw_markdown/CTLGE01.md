# Competências Transversais: Liderança e Gestão de Equipas

- Teaching language: English
- Edition: Instance: 2025/2026 - 2S (edio n. 1)

## Objectives

Objetives: This Transferable Skills Training Unit aims to raise students' awareness of the importance of leadership and teamwork skills training, providing reflective tools and encouraging practices for exercising constructive, transformative, and ethical leadership.

## Learning outcomes and competences

By the end of the training unit, students should be able to: • Understand and critically evaluate different approaches, theories, and leadership styles. • Identify cross-cutting aspects of team functioning, as well as their relational dynamics. • Identify and apply communication skills in a teamwork context and recognize their importance for leadership and for the well-being and functioning of teams. • Identify practices of humanized and transformative leadership, recognizing the critical factors associated with them. • Establish plans for introspection and self-evaluation to enhance leadership and team management abilities.

## Program

1. Leadership Approaches, Theories, and Styles 1.1 Traits, Behavioral, Situational, Transformational, and Servant Leadership 1.2 - Skills for Being a Leader 2. Work Teams 2.1 Characteristics and properties of work teams 2.2 Cohesion and Group Thinking 2.3 Conflict: its emerging nature and function 3. Communication 3.1 Determinants of human communication 3.2 The role of communication in establishing and managing interpersonal relationships 3.2. Reciprocity and feedback in the communication process 4. Becoming a Leader 4.1 Representations of leadership 4.2 The challenges associated with the transition to leadership positions 4.2 Strategies for self-monitoring and self-care
