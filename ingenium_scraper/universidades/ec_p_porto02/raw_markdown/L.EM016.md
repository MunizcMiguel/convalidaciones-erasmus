# Análise Matemática III

- Teaching language: Portuguese Obs.: As aulas sero em Portugus e todos os Professores esto habilitados a falar ingls em qualquer circunstncia.
- Edition: Instance: 2025/2026 - 1S

## Objectives

SPECIFIC AIMS: Development of the reasoning capacity of the students and knowledge of advanced mathematics for engineers, namely - identification and integration of ordinary differential equations - integration of systems of first-order ordinary differential equations - integral calculus over curves and surfaces with applications - applications of integral theorems of vector analysis - Fourier series and representation of periodic f

## Learning outcomes and competences

LEARNING OUTCOMES: Students should get solid theoretical and practical skills on - identification and integration of ordinary differential equations - integration of systems of first-order ordinary differential equations - calculation of the Laplace transform of real functions of a positive variable and application of its properties; - calculation of line integrals and application of their properties; - calculation of surface integrals and application of their properties; - application of integral theorems of vector analysis; - determination of the Fourier series of a periodic function.

## Program

Introduction to differential equations: general classification, definition of solution, and initial and boundary value problems. Ordinary differential equations of first order: the existence and uniqueness theorem; separable equations; homogeneous equations; linear equations (homogeneous and non-homogeneous). Some problems modeled by first-order equations: problems in mechanics and population dynamics. Exact equations and integrating factors. Nonlinear equations reducible to linear ones: the Bernoulli equation. Ordinary higher-order differential equations reducible to lower-order equations. Linear equations of order greater than one: general theory of homogeneous and non-homogeneous linear nth order equations. Existence and uniqueness theorem. General solution for homogeneous linear equations with constant coefficients. Linear non-homogeneous equations: the variation of parameters method. Systems of first-order linear equations: introduction and its relation with an nth order linear differential equation. Some examples. Basic theory of systems of first-order linear equations. Homogeneous linear equations with constant coefficients. Real or complex single eigenvalues case and repeated eigenvalues case. Fundamental matrices. The method of variation of parameters for non-homogeneous systems. The Laplace transform: definition and existence conditions. Laplace transform of some basic functions using the definition. Main properties of the Laplace transform: first and second translation theorems and the transform of the derivative. Inverse Laplace transform. Solution of initial value problems and of differential equations with discontinuous forcing functions, using the Laplace transform. Impulse functions and Dirac δ-function. The convolution theorem. Line integral of scalar and vector functions. Independence of path. Work done by a force. Green’s Theorem. Surface integral. Area of a surface, mass, center of gravity, centroid, and moment of inertia. Flux integral. Some differential operators. Stoke's and Gauss 's theorems. FOURIER ANALYSIS Fourier Series. Euler formulas. Even and odd functions, half-range expansions. Approximation by trigonometric polynomials and minimum square error. The wave equation, vibrating string. Solution by separation of variables.

## Teaching methods and learning activities

The classes will be theoretical-practical (TP), with the theoretical part consisting of oral presentations supported by slide projection or other technologies such as a digital table and also written on the whiteboard. In these classes, deduction and abstraction are considered fundamental. After each topic, application examples will always be resolved. In this practical part, students solve proposed exercises suggested in advance based on both the texts and the exercise book indicated. There will be attendance control in TP classes and the student cannot exceed the planned number of absences (25% of the planned classes). If the number of absences indicated is exceeded, the student will not be able to attend the subject and will not be able to take any exam in this subject, unless they have a special status (consult the FEUP pedagogical and assessment standards).

## Calculation formula of final grade

Total Classification = 50% of Test 1 + 50% of Test 2 The tests are not mandatory and have no minimum grade. At the regular exam period, it is always possible to do only one of the tests and miss the other one, as the missing test can always be done in the recourse exam. At the recourse exam, all the students are admitted. - The students who fail to pass for missing both tests have to do a final exam, which will cover all themes of the course unit. - The students who fail to pass can repeat the first or the second test (the bestgrade of both tests will be taken into account). Alternatively, they can take a final exam, which will cover all themes of the course unit. - The successful students can improve their grades at the recourse exam by taking a final exam covering all themes of the course unit. Grade 20 (in 20) is only possible with an oral exam.
