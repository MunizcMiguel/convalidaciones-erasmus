# Organização e Gestão de Empresas

- Teaching language: Portuguese
- Edition: Instance: 2025/2026 - 1S

## Objectives

This course aims at introducing students to the fundamental management and organizational concepts, providing an integrated perspective of the different functional management areas and organizational issues. The main concepts of management, organizations and main functional areas are presented. Emphasis on decision support of the management of production operations in industrial companies is given.

## Learning outcomes and competences

At the end of this course students should: Understand an organization, the different functional areas of management and how they are interconnected. Be aware of main management concepts, methods and tools. Be able to analyze and apply management methods and tools in real contexts. Understand the importance of behavioral competencies in the organizational and work context. Be able to solve some production operations management problems through appropriated tools and techniques application

## Program

1. BASIC CONCEPTS: THE COMPANY AND THE MANAGEMENT 1.1. Companies, Management and the role of the Manager 1.2. The evolution of management 1.3. The management process: Planning, Organization, Leadership and Control 1.4. Management levels: Strategic management and functional policies. 2.ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE 2.1. Elements of organizational design: chain and unit of command, span of control, centralisation and decentralisation, vertical and horizontal coordination, etc 2.2 Basic structures: functional, geographical, divisional, mixed, matrix. 3. FUNCTIONAL AREAS OF MANAGEMENT: FUNDAMENTAL CONCEPTS 3.1. Marketing Management: market; fundamental marketing concepts; the marketing mix. 3.2. People Management: the human resources function; motivation; leadership. 3.3. Operations management: Fundamentals of operations management; production processes; types of layouts; Quality Management. 3.5. Financial management: Introduction to financial management; basic accounting principles; break-even point. 4. DECISION SUPPORT IN INDUSTRIAL OPERATIONS MANAGEMENT 4.1. Forecasting: regression; simple and moving averages; exponential smoothing. 4.2. Inventory Control: economic order quantity; deterministic replenishment models. 4.3. Production Planning: production to order; lot production; MRP method; JIT systems

Presencial

## Teaching methods and learning activities

The course will combine a theoretical basis, supported on the presentation and discussion of concepts, with an applied component, through the use of case studies and a group work project in an organization. The following teaching methods are adopted: M1. The more theoretical lectures focus on the fundamental management concepts and tools, which provide the basis for the case study discussion and the applied work. M2. The more practical classes focus on real case study analysis and discussion with an emphasis on the application of the management concepts and tools. Students have to understand the problem, to exploit the practical concepts and to consolidate the learning process, having to discuss and present their recommendations to the classroom. M3. The group project consists in an analysis of a company's functional management areas and identifying potential improvements.
