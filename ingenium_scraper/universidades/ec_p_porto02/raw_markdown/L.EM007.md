# Termodinâmica I

- Teaching language: Portuguese
- Edition: Instance: 2025/2026 - 2S

## Objectives

The course aims to provide the fundamental concepts of Thermodynamics, develop an intuitive understanding of the subject, and highlight the physical principles underlying certain phenomena. It also aims to develop students’ skills in characterizing and analyzing real-world examples of thermodynamic processes and engineering systems based on thermodynamic machines.

## Learning outcomes and competences

Expected outcomes: Upon completion of the course, students will be able to understand and analyze energy-related phenomena, apply the fundamental laws of thermodynamics to physical systems, perform energy balance calculations, and evaluate system energy efficiency.

## Calculation formula of final grade

The distributed assessment for this Course Unit consists of: a short theoretical test on the Moodle platform covering the first part of the syllabus, to be completed during the semester without consultation, and accounting for 15% of the final grade; and two laboratory assignments to be completed during the semester (with no resit opportunity), accounting for 10% of the final grade. During the exam period, the evaluation will consist of a theoretical mini test covering the second half of the syllabus, without consultation, with a weight of 15% in the exam gradeand a practical test weighing 60% on the final exam grade, while maintaining the previous components of the distributed assessment (mini test (15%) + laboratory work (10%)). For other exam periods, the evaluation will consist of a theoretical test, covering all topics, without consultation, with a weight of 30%, and a practical exam, covering all topics with a weight of 60%, with the laboratory evaluation remaining at 10%. Failure to appear forthe laboratory work, theoretical tests, and practical exam is classified with a zero grade. The theoretical tests have a 15 minutes duration. The exams evaluation time is 105 minutes.
