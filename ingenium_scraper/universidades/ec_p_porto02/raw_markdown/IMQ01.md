# Introdução à Mecânica Quântica

- Teaching language: Portuguese and english
- Edition: Instance: 2025/2026 - 2S (of 18-02-2026 to 05-06-2026)

## Objectives

To provide the necessary background to understand the fundamental concepts of Quantum Mechanics and its technological applications.

## Learning outcomes and competences

After attending this course you should be able to explain the postulates of quantum mechanics, solve simple quantum mechanics problems, and understand basic concepts such as particle-wave duality, uncertainty principle, exclusion principle and quantum entanglement.

## Pre-requirements (prior knowledge) and co-requirements (common knowledge)

Linear Algebra and Physics I (Mechanics).

## Program

1. Classical mechanics summary. Hamilton equations and Poisson brackets. 2. History of quantum Mechanics. Theories of light. Black-body radiation. Photoelectric effect. Atomic models. 3. Mathematical background. Complex numbers. Theory of probabilities. Vector spaces on the complex field. "bra" and "ket" notation. 4. Quantum mechanics postulates. Time evolution of the quantum state. Schrdinger and Heisenberg pictures. 5. The quantum harmonic oscillator. 6. Spin. Pauli matrices. 7. Systems of two spins combined. Quantum entanglement.

## Working method

Presencial

## Teaching methods and learning activities

The subjects of the course will be delivered in 6 two-hour lectures, between the 4th and the 9th weeks of the semester. The student will have to take a few on-line quizzes and a written quiz on the 10th week of the semester.

## Evaluation Type

Distributed evaluation without final exam

Any student officially enrolled in the course who attends at least 4 of the 6 lectures is eligible to take the quizzes.

To improve the grade of the "written quiz" component, students can take a second written quiz during the 11th week of the semester.
