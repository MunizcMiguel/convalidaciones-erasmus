### Objectives of the Course

To learn the basic DC circuit analysis concepts practically and to use ammeter, voltmeter and oscilloscope.

### Course Content

Measurement of voltage, current and resistance. Node-voltage method and mesh current methods in dc circuits. Thevenin and Norton theorems. Linearity and superposition principles. RC, RL and RLC circuits. Oscilloscopes. Measurements with oscilloscopes.
