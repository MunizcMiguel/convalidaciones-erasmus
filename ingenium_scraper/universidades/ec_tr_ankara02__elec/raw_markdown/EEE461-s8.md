### Objectives of the Course

To learn the basic concepts related to biomedical equipment and physiological parameters.

### Course Content

Basic concepts of instrumentation. The origin of biopotentials. Biopotential amplifiers. ENG, EMG, EKG, EEG. Physiology and measurement of nervous, circulatory and respiratory systems. Therapeutic and prosthetic devices.
