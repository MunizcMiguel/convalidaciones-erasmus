### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to learn the calculation of energy distribution overhead line conductors, protection in transmission lines, planning of transmission lines.

### Course Content

Electric Power System Overview. Protection and Collective Power Supplies in Transmission Lines. Establishment of Transmission Lines.
