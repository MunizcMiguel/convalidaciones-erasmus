### Objectives of the Course

The aim of the proposed course is to convey the necessary knowledge about avionic systems to undergraduate students.

### Course Content

Classification of avionic systems, General information about geotechnics, radiotechnics and astronomical navigation instruments and systems, Collision Warning System, Navigation systems based on the use of Doppler radar, Inertial Navigation Systems (INS), Gyro-Gimballed Inertial Navigation System, Analytical (Strapdown) Inertial Navigation System , Ground Radio Navigation Systems, Basics and configuration of satellite radio navigation systems, Global Positioning System (GPS), GPS system errors and elimination methods, Differential Global Positioning System (DGPS), Supporting INS with external navigation information, Supporting INS with GPS schemes, Flight Elements and configuration of the control system, Autopilot principles and their use, Longitudinal and transverse motion control systems.
