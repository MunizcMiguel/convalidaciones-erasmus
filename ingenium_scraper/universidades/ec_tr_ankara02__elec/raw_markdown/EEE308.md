### Objectives of the Course

To learn the necessary concepts and techniques required for the analysis of numerical solutions and to apply these techniques using MATLAB.

### Course Content

Introduction to MATLAB for numerical solutions, Error Analysis, Root approximations, Matrices and Determinants, Differential Equations, Linear and Parabolic Regressions, Numerical Integration.
