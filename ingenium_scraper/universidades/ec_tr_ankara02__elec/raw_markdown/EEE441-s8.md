### Objectives of the Course

To learn the basic building blocks. of communication systems. To learn the basic time-domain, frequency-domain and statistical methods to design and analyze communication systems. To learn the amplitude and frequency modulations. To learn the effects of noise in amplitude and frequency modulations.

### Course Content

Communication Systems, Signals and Systems for Communication Systems, Random Processes, Continuous-Wave Modulation, Noise Analysis in Continuous-Wave Modulation Systems
