### Objectives of the Course

To learn the necessary concepts and techniques required for fundamental of computer networking.

### Course Content

ISO-OSI model with sublayers, Local area network protocols, Ethernet and the data link layer, Error detection and correction, Datalink protocols, Routing, Internetworking, Internet with tools.
