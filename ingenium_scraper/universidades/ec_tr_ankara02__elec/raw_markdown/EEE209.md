### Objectives of the Course

To provide students with the ability to identify concepts such as random variables, probability density functions and to use them in problem solving. To ensure that they can calculate the expected value, variance and standard deviation. To gain students the ability to perform parameter estimation and hypothesis testing

### Course Content

Basic concepts of probability and statistics. Random variables, functions of random variables. Multivariable distributions and densities. Independent random variables. Correlation, application of statistics to engineering systems
