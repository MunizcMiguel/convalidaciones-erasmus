### Objectives of the Course

The objective of this course is to apply the concepts and processes learned in EEE 391 Project Management course to a specific Electrical-Electronic Engineering problem and to prepare a project proposal based on this.

### Course Content

Application of the project management steps to a specific problem and preparation of conceptual design report. Carrying out project preparations towards entrepreneurship.
