### Objectives of the Course

Learning the design and analysis of filters, amplifiers, oscillators, modulators and demodulators used in communication systems.

### Course Content

Active and Passive filters, active filter design techniques. Transformer coupled wide-band and narrow-band circuits. Analysis and design of RF-IF amplifiers. Sinusoidal oscillators: LC and crystal oscillators. Power amplifiers. Frequency and amplitude modulators. Frequency and amplitude demodulators.
