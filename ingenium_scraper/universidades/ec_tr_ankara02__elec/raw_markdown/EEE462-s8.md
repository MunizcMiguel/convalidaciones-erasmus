### Objectives of the Course

Learning of the mathematical, operational principles and usage areas of medical imaging systems.

### Course Content

2-D signal processing. X-ray systems: Operational principles of roentgen tubes. Basic principles of Computerized Tomography. Nuclear Medicine and Gamma Cameras. Positron Emission Tomography (PET). Mathematical principles of Ultrasonography and medical applications. Magnetic Resonance Imaging Systems
