### Objectives of the Course

The objective of this course is to learn the concepts and processes associated with design projects.

### Course Content

Engineering design process. Project selection and needs analysis. Requirement specification. Concept generation and evaluation. System design: Functional decomposition and behavioral models. Testing. System Reliability. Teams and teamwork. Project Management. Ethical and Legal Issues. Oral presentation techniques.
