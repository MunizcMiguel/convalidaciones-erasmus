### Objectives of the Course

Making students familiar with hot topics of electrical engineering as well as current issues on engineering such as ethics, work safety, philosophy and art.

### Course Content

Seminars presented by experts in the fields on popular and hot topics of Electrical and Electronic Engineering as well as project planning, management, risk management, change management, engineering ethics, work safety, art in engineering.
