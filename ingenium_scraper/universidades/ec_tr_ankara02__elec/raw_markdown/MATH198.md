### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to teach the fundamentals of linear algebra and its applications in engineering.

### Course Content

Matrix algebra, Elementary operations, Determinants, Systems of linear equations, Vector spaces, Basis and dimension, Linear dependence and independence, Linear transformations, Inner product spaces, Matrix norms, Eigenvalues and eigenvectors, Diagonalization
