### Objectives of the Course

To learn breakdown in electronegative gases, lightning discharges and corona discharges

### Course Content

I-V characterics of gases. Electron emission processes. Ionization and deionization. Townsend and Streamer breakdown mechanisms. :Breakdown in electronegative gases. Corona discharges and loses. Breakdown mechanisms in solid and liquid insulations. Generation of A.C. D.C. and impulse voltages.
