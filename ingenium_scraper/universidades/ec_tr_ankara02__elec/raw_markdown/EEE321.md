### Objectives of the Course

To learn basic properties and importance of signals and systems in engineering applications, and to understand the methods used in the system analysis and synthesis

### Course Content

Continuous and discrete time signal and systems. Feedback, memory, stability, linearity, time-invariance. LTI systems defined by differential / difference equations. Convolution. Fourier series and transform. Modulation. Sampling. Laplace transform. Z-transform. Transfer function representation.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Understand the concept of signals and systems. |
| 2 | Know the basic system properties and meaning of linear time-invariant systems. |
| 3 | Can use the Fourier Series representations for periodic signals. |
| 4 | Understand and can use the Fourier transform methods for the analysis of continuous-time and discrete-time signals and systems. |
| 5 | Understand and can use the z-transform methods for the analysis discrete-time signals and systems. |
| 6 | Understand the differences and uses of different transform methods and know when to apply which. |
