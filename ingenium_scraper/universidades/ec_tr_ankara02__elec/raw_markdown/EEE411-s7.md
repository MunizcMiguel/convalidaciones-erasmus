### Objectives of the Course

To provide extensive knowledge of operation and design of digital IC?s including RTL, DTL, TTL, ECL, NMOS and CMOS technologies. To provide PSPICE simulations of digital IC?s.

### Course Content

TTL, MOS , CMOS Combinational Logic, NOT, AND, OR, NAND, NOR and XOR, XNOR derivative gates. IEC symbols, dynamic responses and their applications. Functions of combinational logic. Pulse generators and flip-flops. Triggering devices and Schmitt trigger. Sequential logic, counters, registers. Memory circuits, RAM, ROM, EPROM , PLA, magnetic and optical storage units. Interface circuits A/ D and D / A conversion.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Understand properties of digital circuits: VTC, logic swing, transition width, noise, fan in, fan out, transient characteristics, power dissipation and power delay product |
| 2 | Understand modes of operation of PN junction, MN Schottky, BJT, FET, MOSFET and CMOS in digital IC?s |
| 3 | Know operational principles of RTL and DTL gates and design these gates. |
| 4 | Analyze, design and simulate TTL, STTL ans LSTTL gates |
| 5 | Analyze design and simulate ECL gates |
| 6 | Know R-loaded, E-O loaded and E-D loaded NMOS inverters |
| 7 | Analyze design and simulate NMOS gates |
| 8 | Analyze design and simulate CMOS combinational gates |
| 9 | Know operational principles of CMOS drives, static and dynamic CMOS gates |
| 10 | Analyze memory circuits |
