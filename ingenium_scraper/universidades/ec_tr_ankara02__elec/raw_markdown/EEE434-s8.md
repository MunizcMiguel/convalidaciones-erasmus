### Objectives of the Course

The objective of this course is to learn the principles of speed control of DC and AC motors, and analysis and design of drive systems used for this purpose.

### Course Content

DC Motor speed and torque control methods and their applications. Choppers. Four quadrant operation. Induction motor speed and torque control methods. Scalar control techniques. Principles of vector control. Speed control in synchronous motors. Reluctance motors.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Know about the advantages of using electric drives. |
| 2 | Know the principles of speed control DC motors, induction motors and brushless AC motors |
| 3 | Know the power electronic converter topologies used for speed control of DC motors, induction motors and brushless AC motors, and to be able to analyze these converters. |
| 4 | Can perform modeling and simulation analysis of dc motors. |
