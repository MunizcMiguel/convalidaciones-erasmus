### Objectives of the Course

To learn the basic building blocks of communication systems. To learn the communication link analysis. To learn the modulation-coding trade-offs.

### Course Content

Signals and spectra. Formatting and baseband modulation. Baseband demodulation and detection. Communication link analysis. Channel coding. Modulastion nad coding trade-offs. Synchronization. Multiplexing and multiple-access. Spread spectrum techniques. Sourca coding..
