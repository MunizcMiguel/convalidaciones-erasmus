### Objectives of the Course

To learn the design and analysis of digital modulation techniques. To understand the foundations of information theory and coding.

### Course Content

Random variables and processes, noise. Sampling processes and pulse modulation methods. Baseband pule transmission. Bandpass digital modulation techniques. Introduction to information theory and coding.
