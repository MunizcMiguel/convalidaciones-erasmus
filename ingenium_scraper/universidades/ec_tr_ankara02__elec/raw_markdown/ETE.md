### Objectives of the Course

To be familiar with classification and characteristics of composite material and their applications.

To gain the knowledge about manufacturing methods of composites.

To know the testing methods related to composite materials.

### Course Content

Students who succeed this course:

1- Know characteristics of composites,

2- Know manufacturing and testing methods,

3- Know mechanical behaivor of composite,
