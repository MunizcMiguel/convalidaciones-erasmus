### Objectives of the Course

A comprehensive understanding of the fundamentals of digital logic circuits. To learn the Logic Circuit Design concepts and to use this concepts to carry out simple designs.

### Course Content

Circuit design and practice using logic circuit gates. Half-adder, full adder, multiplexer and flip-flop circuits measurements and simulation studies
