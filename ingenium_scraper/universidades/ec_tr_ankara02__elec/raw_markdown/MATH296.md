### Objectives of the Course

To gain the ability of using complex analysis techniques for the problems for which real analysis solution is very difficult or impossible.

### Course Content

Algebra of complex numbers, polar representation of complex numbers, complex functions (exponential, logarithmic, trigonometric, inverse trigonometric functions). Limit, continuity, derivative. Analytic functions. Cauchy-Riemann equations. Curvilinear integrals. Cauchy Integral Theorem. Sequences, series, singular points. Taylor?s, Laurent?s Series. Residues, Residue?s Theorem. Improper Integrals.
