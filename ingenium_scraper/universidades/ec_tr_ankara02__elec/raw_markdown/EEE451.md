### Objectives of the Course

The objective of this course is to learn transmission lines, waveguides, microwave systems, impedance transformation and impedance matching techniques.

### Course Content

Review of electromagnetic wave theory. Frequency and time domain analysis of transmission lines. TEM mode transmission lines. Rectangular and circular wave guides. Equivalent circuit analysis of microwave systems. Impedance transformation and matching techniques. Microstrip devices.
