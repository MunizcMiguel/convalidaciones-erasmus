### Objectives of the Course

To learn the existing electronic circuit elements and their application fields and to have enough information to learn about new electronic circuit elements.

### Course Content

Basic semiconductor concept. Physical electronics. Physics of P-N diodes. Bipolar junction transistors (BJTs). Field effect transistors (FETs). Transistor biasing and small signal models. Unijunction transistors (UJTs). P-N-P-N switching devices. Negative resistance microwave devices. Lasers.
