# Turbomachinery

- ECTS: 3
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 15-15

## Course description

Introduction to turbomachinery – compressors and turbines types presentation and analyse, Explanation of thermodynamic and flow-mechanic laws relevant to turbomachinery. Compressor and turbine work analyse - definition of isentropic and polytrophic efficiency. One dimensional cascade theory Mine line axial turbine and axial compressor flow analyse. Performance map of compressor and turbine presentation.
