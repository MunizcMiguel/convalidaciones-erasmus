# Automation and robotization of manufacturing and intralogistics systems

- ECTS: 5
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 2-2

## Course description

Construction of robotic manufacturing systems, including robotic manipulation and palletizing stations, as well as robotic machining stations. Tool systems in lathes, including tool heads and automatic tool measurement. Machining automation, exemplified by a multi-axis turning and milling center. Tool systems, including tool coding systems, tool magazines, control systems (autonomous and integrated). Computer systems for tool management, including instrumentation, automatic dispensers, and tool measurement systems. Programming selected machine tool functions (machine PLC), such as additional controlled axes, warehouse control, and management of semi-finished products and finished products.
