# Operational procedures and flight planning

- ECTS: 4
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 11-11

## Course description

The knowledge given to students is split into two group: a) General operational procedures binding in different types of aviation regulations b) Specific procedures valid for flight planning and preparation process Ad. a) Students are familiarized with the general overview of operational procedures binding at different types of aviation operations. Students are given with similarities and differences related to so called "big" and "small" aviation. Also origins of existing and employed procedures are presented Ad. b) The flight planning is a pilots and dispatchers activity strongly affecting on all kinds of flights. Students get informed what factors should be taken into consideration for proper flight preparation. They get shown with typical documents necessary for flight planning. Some exercises are conducted to plan some virtual flights
