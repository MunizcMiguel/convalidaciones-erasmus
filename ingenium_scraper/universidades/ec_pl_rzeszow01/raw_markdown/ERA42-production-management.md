# Production management

- ECTS: 4
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 12-12

## Course description

The objective of the subject “production management” is to produce goods services of right quality and quantity at the right time and right manufacturing cost. Main topics of the subjects: Components of a production system; Aim of production; Classification of production system, Phases of product development; Economic analysis of product development; Productivity; Planning and Managing Production Resources – MRP II / ERP; Just-In-Time approach; Kanban, ABC, Pareto and other analysis.
