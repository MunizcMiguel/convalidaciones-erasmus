# Dynamics of machines

- ECTS: 3
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 4-5

## Course description

LECTURE: 1. Basic definitions. Mechanisms, structure, links, kinematic pairs, kinematic chain, mobility, mechanism, machine, manipulator, robot. 2. Kinematics of selected planar mechanisms: manipulators, gears. 3. Gears, planetary mechanism, differential gear, the gear ratio, cylindrical and conical gear. 4. The dynamics of selected planar mechanisms: manipulators, gears. Reactions in kinematic pairs. Dynamical model of the mechanism movement. 5. Imbalances as a cause of mechanical vibrations. Balancing of masses of mechanisms with link in a rotating motion and complex motion. 6. Dynamics of rotors and turbines. LABORATORY: 1. Imbalance of rotating components on the example of the robot tool – experimental study. 2. Classes with software for machine diagnostics with visualisations, instruction, issues for analysis and tests in English. 3. Computer simulation of test rig consisting of the following components: electric motor, coupling, gearbox, belt transmission, ball bearings, rotor. 4. Simulation and visualization of test rig operations for the following faults: unbalance, bearings faults, looseness, belt faults, gearbox fault, shaft rub, misalignment. 5. Creating your own systems with the following elements: drivers (electric motor, turbine, diesel engine), transmission elements (gearbox, belt/pulley/sheave, coupling, fluid coupling), driven elements (pumps, fans, compressors), rotor, ball bearing. Analysis of frequencies generated by such machines.

## Remarks

Students should have completed “theoretical mechanics” classes
