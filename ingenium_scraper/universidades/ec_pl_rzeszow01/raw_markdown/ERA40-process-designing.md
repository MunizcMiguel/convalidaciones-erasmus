# Process designing

- ECTS: 3
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 12-12

## Course description

The classes in the subject “Process designing” primarily cover aspects such as:

- Selected elements of the production system, including structure, means of work, work items, products, shortages, waste, etc.

- Phases and stages of the technical preparation of production.

- Technical documentation.

- Planning the process of preparing and launching the production of a new product based on adopted technical and organizational assumptions, as well as the product’s structure.
