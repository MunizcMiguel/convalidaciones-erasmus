# Fundamentals of machine construction

- ECTS: 6
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 8-8

## Course description

Summary: engineering calculation, engineering materials, factor of safety, safety criterion for fluctuating stress, threaded fasteners: thread mechanics, power screw mechanism, elastic machine components: spring materials, spring systems, rubber elastic components, welded joints: welding processes, weld types, calculation of welded joints, interference fits.
