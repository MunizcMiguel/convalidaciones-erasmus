# Mechanics of materials

- ECTS: 5
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 10-10

## Course description

(Lecture) External and internal forces – description. Kinds of simple mechanical structures. Definition of stress. Normal and shear stress. Superposition principle. Linear and shape deformation. Tension and compression. Hook’s low. Young modulus (modulus of elesticity). Poisson’s ratio. Tension plot. Mechanical properties of materials. (Laboratory exercise) Tension test of steel specimen. Kinds of specimens used in experimental tension tests. Determination of the following parameters for steel: force-extension plot, stress- elongation plot, lower and upper yields stress (YS), ultimate tensile strength (UTS). Tension test of aluminum specimen. Division of strain on elastic and plastic. Determination of Young’s modulus from tension plot. Determination of the following parameters for aluminum alloy: stress-strain plot, ultimate tensile strength (UTS) apparent elastic limit, yield strength, Young modulus.
