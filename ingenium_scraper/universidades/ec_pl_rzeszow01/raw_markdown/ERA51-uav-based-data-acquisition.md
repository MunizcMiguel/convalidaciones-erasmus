# UAV based data acquisition

- ECTS: 2
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 15-15

## Course description

The main educational goal of the subject is to acquire knowledge and skills in the field of in- flight tests and data acquisition with the usage Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems (RPAS). Students will be familiarized with flight test preparation, data acquisition systems, data processing, data presenting and law aspects of this process.
