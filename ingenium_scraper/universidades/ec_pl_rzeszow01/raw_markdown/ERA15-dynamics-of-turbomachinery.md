# Dynamics of turbomachinery

- ECTS: 4
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 5-5

## Course description

(Lecture): Fundamentals of vibration theory. Description of free and forced vibrations. Problem of mechanical resonance. Frequency-amplitude characteristic. Influence of damping on the amplitude of resonant vibrations. Kinds of unbalancing. Methods used for balancing of rotors. (Laboratory exercise): Experimental modal analysis of the beam realized with the use of modern vibration system. Frequency-amplitude plot determination for the simple beam. Learning of experimental techniques for measure of vibration amplitude with the use of piezoelectric small-size accelerometers. Experimental modal analysis of the turbine blade. Determination of the following parameters: amplitude-frequency characteristic for the turbine blade, resonant frequencies. Learning of measurement methods used in vibration tests: laser scanning vibrometer and optical microscope.
