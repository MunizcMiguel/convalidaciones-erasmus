# Artificial intelligence based optimization in aerospace engineering

- ECTS: 4
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 2-2

## Course description

The main educational goal of the subject is to acquire knowledge and skills in the field of modern methods and techniques called artificial intelligence (AI) systems and their creative application to solve optimization problems in the area of airspace engineering. As part of the lectures, the students get acquainted with the theory of decision-making and learn various AI- based optimization methods. During the workshops, he or she solves optimization tasks by applying the methods, theories, and techniques presented during the lectures. Students propose to use them for solving selected problems that can occur in the area of aviation.
