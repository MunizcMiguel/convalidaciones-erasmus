# Aircraft engine construction

- ECTS: 4
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 1-1

## Course description

Introduction – presentation of types of turbine engines Thrust, specific thrust, fuel consumption, specific fuel consumption, engine efficiency – definitions Turbojet and turbofan and turboprop/turboshaft engine work analyse. Description of ideal and real processes in the engines:

- analyse of internal processes in the engine,

- engine components losses and efficiencies definition,

- engine cycle optimisation, Engine components analyse: inlets, fan/compressor, combustor/afterburner, turbine, propelling nozzle. Performance vs. flight speed and altitude analyse for different engine types.
