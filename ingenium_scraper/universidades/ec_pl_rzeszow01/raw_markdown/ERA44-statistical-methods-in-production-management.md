# Statistical methods in production management

- ECTS: 4
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 13-13

## Course description

Control methods in production. Statistical acceptance control. Statistical process control (SPC). Quality capability of machines and processes. Control charts and their characteristics. Design of experiments (DoE). Manufacturing process modelling using a fully determined two-level plan, considering interaction effects. Modelling the manufacturing process using a fully determined three-level plan. Optimization of manufacturing processes: essence and key criteria.
