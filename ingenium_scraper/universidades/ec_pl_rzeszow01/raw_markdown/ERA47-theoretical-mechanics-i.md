# Theoretical mechanics I

- ECTS: 6
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 14-14

## Course description

The object theoretical mechanics 1 is conducted on summer semester. It consists of two pieces: Statics and Kinematics. The subject matter of Statics: Basic principles, Axioms of statics , Systems of forces acting on a body, Constraints and their reactions, Concurrent force system, moment of a force, Couple of forces, Couple of forces theorem, An arbitrary planar force system, An arbitrary spatial force system, Friction,Trusses, Center of parallel forces with equal directions. The subject matter of Kinematics: I. KINEMATICS OF A PARTICLE - Radius vector, path of motion, equations of motion .Velocity vector, Equation of path, Acceleration vector of a point, Tangential and normal acceleration, Classification of the motion of a particle II. KINEMATICS OF A SOLID - Translational motion of a solid, Rotational motion of a solid, Plane motion, Resultant motion of a point.
