# Aircraft construction

- ECTS: 3
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 1-1

## Course description

The aim of the course is to relay basic knowledge to students at the area of general aspects of aircraft configuration development, aircraft general arrangement, overview of the aircraft design process, aircraft initial sizing, searching for the optimum variant, cost analysis and other optimization criteria, aviation regulations and airworthiness standards, aircraft weight analysis, design of main aircraft parts, performance groups and categories and aircraft performance and load analysis. The aim of the course is to introduce students to theoretical and practical knowledge in the scope of:

- aircraft configuration, performance and categories groups,

- phases of aircraft design, overview of design process, initial specification and sizing, searching for the optimum, impact of civil regulations and airworthiness standards.

- aircraft geometry, weight, aerodynamic, performance and cost analysis,

- analysis of general aircraft loads, design speeds, maneuvering load limits, gust loads. Aircraft flight envelope.

- Analysis of loads distribution - aerodynamic and weight.

- Design of main aircraft components.
