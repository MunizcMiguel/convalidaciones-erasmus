# Aerodynamics

- ECTS: 3
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 1-1

## Course description

The course of aerodynamics concerns some selected problems on low and high speeed aerodynamics. 2D panel methods for incompressible flow. 3D wing in incompressible flow: lifting line and vortex lattice method. Measurement techniques for subsonic flows. “shock-expansion” technique for airoils at supersonic speeds. Linearised theory of supersonic flows. Ackeret theory. 3D wing in supersonic flow. Withcomb “area rule”.
