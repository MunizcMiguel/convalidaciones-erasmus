# Drive systems of means of transport

- ECTS: 4
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 4-4

## Course description

Role and functions of drive systems. Types of drive systems used in means of transport. Mechanical drive systems. Clutches. Gearboxes. Drive shafts and axles. Drive axles. Final drives and differentials. Hydromechanical drive systems. Hydrostatic drive systems. Pneumatic (air) drive systems. Drive systems for conveyors: String conveyors, Stringless conveyors. Crane drive systems, including hoists and cranes. Hybrid drive systems for means of transport.
