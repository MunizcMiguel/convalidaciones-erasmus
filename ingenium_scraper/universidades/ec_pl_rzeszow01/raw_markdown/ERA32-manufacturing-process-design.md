# Manufacturing process design

- ECTS: 4
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 9-9

## Course description

Fundamental rules for the selection and planning of a manufacturing process. Structure of manufacturing processes (operations, treatments etc.). Selection of semi-finished products. Selection of material type, material form, tolerances, design and shape. Selection of proper tooling. Process design procedures. Manufacturing process design of different mechanical parts. Effect of operational speed on performance and economy. Using materials more economically.
