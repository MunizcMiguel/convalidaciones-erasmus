# Fluid mechanics

- ECTS: 4
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 7-7

## Course description

The course concerns basic ideas of fluid mechanics: mass, momentum, energy conservation principles for fluid flows. Vorticity. Euler Equation. Bernoulli Equation for ideal liquids. Pneumatic/hydraulic devices for velocity and flow-rate measurements: Pitot, Prandtl probes, Venturii nozzle. Navier-Stokes Equations, Capillary flows, Laminar flowmeter. Similarity rules. Reynods-Averaged Navier-Stokes (RANS) Equations. Models of turbulence. Turbulent flow in the pipeline. Linear and local pressure-loss coefficients. Euler Equation for ideal fluid flow machinery. Pumps and hydraulic turbines Pipeline-pump (or fan) systems. Water hammer in pipelines. Cavitation.
