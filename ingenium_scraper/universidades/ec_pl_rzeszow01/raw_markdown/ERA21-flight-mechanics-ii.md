# Flight mechanics II

- ECTS: 6
- Source: Erasmus incoming course offer, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Aeronautics (2026), pp. 7-7

## Course description

The aim of the course is to relay basic knowledge to students at the area of aircraft equilibrium, static and dynamic stability and control, both longitudinal and lateral, basics of aircraft simulations on stability, control and response. The aim of the course is to introduce students to theoretical and practical knowledge in the scope of:

- aircraft equilibrium, static stability a control – longitudinal and lateral.

- description of 3D aircraft motion – general equations of motions,

- determination of aerodynamic forces and moments,

- longitudinal dynamic stability – basic longitudinal motions,

- lateral dynamic stability – basic lateral motions,

- basics of modeling and simulation of aircraft maneuvers,

- basics of automatic stability and control.

## Remarks
