# Electrical Engineering 2 (3367)

- ECTS credits: 4 Weekly classes: 2lec+0sem+1labs+0ps+p
- Assessment: exam Type of exam: written

## Departments involved

Dept. of Theoretic Electrical Engineering and Electrical Measuring,
Faculty Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Automatics

## Abstract

The course Electrical Engineering 2 is part of the curriculum of the degree course Industrial Engineering. The
aim of the course is for students to get knowledge in the fields of Electrical Machines, Strong Current
Electronical Schemes for control of Machines. It is necessary for students to have a preliminarily knowledge of
the courses of Phisics, Mathematics and Electrical Engineering 1. The knowlidge on the Electrical Engineering
2 is used when learning Strong current Electrical Engineering.

## Course content

Constructional details, priciples of operation and supplement of DC Machines, Synchronous and Induction
Machines and Transformes. Special Electrical Machines. Strong Current Electronical Schemes for control of
machines.

## Teaching and assessment

The teaching process is divided into lectures and laboratory classes. In the laboratory classes students can
receive practical knowledge. There is a test control on basic topics at the beginning of the classes, which could
be either written or oral, having a 15- minute duration. The test control is meant to help exam presentation.
