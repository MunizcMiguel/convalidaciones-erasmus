# Societies-cultures-religions : Human Questions

- 2.00 credits
- 15.0 h
- Q1
- Language
- French

## Learning outcomes

At the end of this learning unit, the student is able to :
1
confront him/herself in a personal and critical way with the reflection developed during the lectures about the Christian faith.
2
argue his/her position, showing that he/she is conscious of the complexity of the questions at stake, also when confronted with various philosophical and religious traditions.
3
clarify the complex relationship between Christian faith and modernity, paying attention to the different language' levels and to their relevance in the context of the reflection about the meaning of life.
