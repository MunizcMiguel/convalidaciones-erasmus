## Content

The software to be defined and designed will be linked to mobile computing (an Android-compatible application).
The project will be opened. Each group will develop its own project and propose a schedule as well as intermediate steps.
An Agile approach (iterative and incremental development) will be implemented.
The development of the application will be done using generative AIs and other AI-based development tools. A critical usage of the tools is done, with a reflection from the students on the utility of these tools.

## Teaching methods

The project will be done by group of students (4-5 students per group)
Students will be encouraged to communicate in English on a technical theme, orally and/or in writing, to improve their communication skills in English about technical topics.

## Evaluation methods

The final grade will depend on
the grade obtained for the project, including the achievements and realization, the written document produced, the oral presentations and interactions, the work organization, and the reflection on this organization, and the mastery of the topics and concepts related to the project. This part of the grade can be individualized depending on the student's implication in his/her group during the semester (compulsory assistance, participation in activities, intermediate works, and graded production). The activities related to this part of the grade cannot be re-taken in the second session.
the grade obtained for an individual's written test taking place outside of the exam period.
The weight of the grade for the written test will be 30% if the grade is 10 or more, and 100% if the grade is 6 or less. For grades between 6 and 10, it will depend linearly on the grade according to weight = 1 - 0.7*(grade - 6)/4
The second session will consist of an individual exam in addition to individual work to be done beforehand. The individual work is being organised as a 2-week sprint. We recommend that you contact the professor as soon as possible (before the official start of the second session at the latest) to learn about the content of the work.
