# Projektledning

- **Credits**: 6 hp (ECTS)

## Intended learning outcomes

After the course the student should:

- Have a basic knowledge of relevant concepts, theories, models, methods, and tools in project management, organization, and governance throughout the entire project process.
- Be able to apply relevant concepts, theories, models, methods, and tools to various types of projects, for example through case descriptions.
- Be able to motivate and reflect on project models and methods based on the project context, prerequisites, and types of industrial projects.

## Course content

The course focuses on concepts, theories, and models for the management, organization, and governance of projects.

During the course, methods and tools for how projects can be initiated, planned, executed, controlled, and terminated are discussed.

Further, roles in projects, project economy, risk management, communication, hand-over of results, and evaluation of projects are covered.

## Teaching and working methods

The course comprises of lectures and seminars, as well as application of project methods in assignments.
