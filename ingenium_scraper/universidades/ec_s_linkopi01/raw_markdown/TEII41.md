- **Credits**: 6 hp (ECTS)

## Examination

| UPG2 | Individual PM and oral presentation | 2 credits | U, 3, 4, 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| TEN2 | Written examination | 3 credits | U, 3, 4, 5 |
| KTR3 | Tests and presentations | 0.5 credits | U, G |
