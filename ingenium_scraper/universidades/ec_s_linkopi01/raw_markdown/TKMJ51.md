# Hållbar utveckling och företagande

- **Credits**: 6 hp (ECTS)

## Intended learning outcomes

Students shall develop knowledge of corporate sustainability management, focusing on companies but also including public organizations. The course deals with important societal conditions that affect sustainability issues and how they should be managed. A main aim is to gain knowledge about strategies and tools that are important concerning sustainability management.

After having completed this course, the student shall be able to:

- Describe different aspects of sustainability, what the effects are, what the development looks like right now, and how they are connected to different industries.
- Describe and analyze companies' stakeholders, drivers, barriers, strategies, methods, innovation and policy development processes for managing sustainability aspects along the company's value chain.
- Based on the above knowledge, account for and critically analyze the company's management of sustainability aspects related to organization, product portfolio, communication and stakeholder collaboration.
- Based on the above knowledge, evaluate real cases of companies' handling of sustainability aspects, and propose well-motivated measures for the development of competitive sustainability work.
- Show ability to communicate analysis about sustainable business within the company and with the company's external stakeholders in English and Swedish

## Course content

There are three main parts in the course:

- Sustainability related to industry and global ecomomic development
- Corporate sustainability intelligence
- Corporate sustainability management

The following key words represent essential parts of the content: sustainable development; sustainability aspects, environmental and social issues; industry impact on the market; stakeholders; Sustainability Reporting; sustainability policy, policy processes and legislation; innovations, industrial dynamics and environmental economics; sustainable finance; sustainability strategic work; tools in sustainability work; management systems and sustainability; product development and commercial sustainability work; sustainable business models; organization, learning and development.

## Teaching and working methods

A central part is focused on orientation in sustainable business, which is linked to sustainability aspects and environmental analysis, as well as introducing essential terms and concepts. For this purpose, lectures, compulsory course assignments and course literature are central. The course assignment (UPG3) is carried out in groups and includes, among other things, reporting both in writing and orally. UPG3 is partly examined in English.
