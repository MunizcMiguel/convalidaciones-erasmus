# Ekonomisk analys: Ekonomisk teori

- **Credits**: 4 hp (ECTS)

## Intended learning outcomes

Having completed course the student should:

- will have knowledge of and be able to use the most common concepts and models for how economical decisions should be made based on given information and with a view to various economical goals within consumer theory.
- will have knowledge of and be able to use the most common concepts and models for how economical decisions should be made based on given information and with a view to various economical goals within producer theory.
- will have knowledge of and be able to use the most common concepts and models for how economical decisions should be made based on given information and with a view to various economical goals within market theory.

## Course content

Building mathematical models: Formulating and solving mathematical models applied on economic problems, optimization with constraints. Consumer demand: Estimating consumer demand. Theory of Producers: Production functions, iso-cost curves, expansion curves, Long-run and short-run problems, pricediscrimination, homogenous production functions. Market theory: Perfect competition, Monopoly, Monopolistic competition, dopoly, oligopoly, Cournot, Stackelberg and joint optimum solutions. The course introduces differences between business and economic approach and assumes an economically sustainable perspective.

## Teaching and working methods

The lectures are mainly concerned with the theoretical foundations of the areas covered. The lessons are devoted to problem solving and discussion. The seminars are devoted to presenting given problems.
