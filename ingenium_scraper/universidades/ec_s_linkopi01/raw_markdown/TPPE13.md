# Produktionsekonomi

- **Credits**: 6 hp (ECTS)

## Intended learning outcomes

Learning objective 1 After completing the course, the student shall have knowledge of and be able to use the most common methods for demand models and demand forecasting methods Learning objective 2 After completing the course, the student shall have knowledge of planning concepts and be able to apply methods for designing production systems and be able to use the most common methods in sales- and operations planning. Learning objective 3 After completing the course, the student shall have knowledge of and be able to use the most common methods in master planning, material planning and detailed planning Learning objective 4 After completing the course, the student shall have knowledge of and be able to use the most common methods for inventory management

## Course content

Introduction to manufacturing strategy. Engineering design. Design of production systems. Facility layout planning. Line balancing. Forecasting. Aggregate planning. Material Requirements Planning. Inventory control. Scheduling and sequencing.

## Teaching and working methods

The lectures are mainly concerned with theoretical issues and concepts. The exercises are devoted to demonstrations of problem solveing. The laboratories give a hands-on experience with treated techniques.
