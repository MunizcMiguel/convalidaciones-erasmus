# Matematisk statistik, grundkurs

- **Credits**: 4 hp (ECTS)

## Intended learning outcomes

The course gives an introduction to the mathematical modelling of random experiments, with a special emphasis on applications in science, technology, and economics. After completing the course the student will be expected to be able to:

- M1 (Probabilities): identify experimental situations where random factors can affect the outcome, explain basic probability theoretical concepts and carry out probability calculations related to basic probability laws and theories.
- M2 (Stochastic variables): distinguish between different types of random variables, set up relevant probability-theoretic models for random experiments, and calculate important quantities in probability-theoretic models.
- M3 (Approximations): use the limit theories to analyze some random trials.
- M4 (Stochastic processes): set up and analyze probability-theoretic models for certain types of quantities that vary randomly in time, e.g. in the form of time-discrete Markov chains

## Course content

- Random experiments and probabilities: Outcome spaces, events, combinatorics, probabilities, urn models, conditional probabilities, Bayes formula and the concept of independence.
- Random variables (one-dimensional): Discrete and continuous random variables, distribution function, probability function and density function.
- Measures of mode, dispersion, and correlation: Expected value, variance, and standard deviation.
- Probability distributions: Normal distribution, Binomial distribution, Poisson distribution, Exponential distribution, Uniform distribution, etc.
- Stochastic variables (Two-dimensional): Simultaneous/marginal probability function, simultaneous/marginal density function, and the concept of independence.
- Functions of random variables: Distribution derivation, distribution for sums of random variables and for maximum and minimum.
- Approximations: Law of large numbers, central limit value theorem and poisson approximation.
- Stochastic processes: Poisson process, discrete-time Markov chains.
