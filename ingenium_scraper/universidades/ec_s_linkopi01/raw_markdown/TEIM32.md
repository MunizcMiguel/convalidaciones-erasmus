# Industriell marknadsföring

- **Credits**: 6 hp (ECTS)

## Intended learning outcomes

The aim of the course is to provide students with a basic understanding of B2B marketing (Business to business, i.e. where the customers are companies), and how commercial and sustainability related considerations affect marketing decisions. The course shall prepare the student for advanced courses in marketing and strategy. After completing the course, a student should be able to:

- Describe central concepts within B2B marketing
- Analyse B2B companies and their environment from a marketing perspective
- Discuss companies' marketing strategies and marketing decisions, especially in B2B markets

## Course content

Course content includes market orientation, customer value, value propositions, branding, pricing, sustainable marketing, customer relationships, customer segmentation, buying behavior, marketing channels, market strategies, service marketing, sales and marketing communication.

## Teaching and working methods

The course consists of lectures, seminars, and assignment, as well as a project.
