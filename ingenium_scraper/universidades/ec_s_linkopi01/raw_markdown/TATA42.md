# Envariabelanalys 2

- **Credits**: 6 hp (ECTS)

## Intended learning outcomes

After completing the course, the student should be able to

- choose and apply methods to problems in all parts I-IV of the course, as described in the course content
- present and justify solutions to tasks within the course content using relevant concepts and clear reasoning

## Course content

Part I: Maclaurin and Taylor expansion

Taylor's formula. Expansion of elementary functions. Standard expansions. Different forms of the residual term: ordo and Lagrange. Applications include calculation of limit values, investigation of local extreme values ​​and error estimation in approximations.

Part II: Ordinary Differential Equations and Integral Equations

First order linear and separable equations. Integral equations. Linear equations of higher order with constant coefficients.

Part III: Generalized integrals (G), numerical series (N) and power series (P)

G+N: Convergence investigations and elementary estimates. Absolute convergence.

N: Calculation of some series. Leibniz criterion.

P: Convergence radius and range. Maclaurin series. Calculation of certain power series. Differential equations.

Part IV: Integral Applications

Area of ​​flat surfaces. Curve length. Volume of bodies of rotation. Area of ​​rotation surfaces.

Lectures and problem classes. The IT programme has a different organisation, due to the study programme syllabus.
