# ELECTRIC DRIVES M (91300)

- Credits: 6
- SSD: IIND-08/A
- Language: English

## Learning outcomes

Know and understand the fundamentals of static and electromechanical conversion systems.

Learn the configuration of basic power electronic conversion systems, of main electrical machines, either direct current (DC) or alternate current (AC) and of electric drives used in automotive sector.

Learn topology, control principles, input and output characteristics of main DC and AC electric drives.

Develop the ability to model power electronic converters, control system, electrical machines, and full drive systems with reference to application for torque and speed control.

Develop the ability to represent an electric drive in terms of energetic conversion system, for the integration in a multiphisic model of a vehicle.

## Course contents

Fundamentals of electromechanical conversion systems and fundamentals of electrical machines. (2 hours)

Revision of electromagnetic fields and EM field equations. Revision on ferromagnetic materials and permanent magnets. Current transformer.

DC machines (8 hours)

Topology and types of DC machines.

Fundamental equations and output mechanical characteristic. Steady state performance. Drives on one, two, four quadrants. Cooling of the machine. Control schemes.

Brushless machines with trapezoidal back emf. (5 hours)

Drive topology. Principle of operation and control. Output mechanical characteristics. Performance limitation.

Brushless machine with sinusoidal back emf. (5 hours)

Drive topology. Structure of the machine with surface and internal mount magnets.

Introduction to Clarke and Park coordinate transformation. Equation of sinusoidal brushless machine. Reluctance torque. Basic control scheme. Flux-weakening principle.

Principle of static conversion. (10 hours)

Power electronic components. DC/DC basic converter topology. One, two and four quadrant configuration. PWM modulation. Current regulator. Inverter.

Modelling of a Complete Electric Drive in Simulink (30 hours)

Development of a complete electric drive model, including the electrical machine, power electronic converter, PWM modulation, current and speed controllers, and mechanical load. Analysis of four-quadrant operation in the torque–speed plane, electrical and mechanical operating characteristics, and basic sizing criteria for motion control, automation, and automotive applications.

## Teaching methods

The course includes: theoretical lectures made with the aid of multimedia systems. The didactic material is uploaded before each lecture

Training and laboratory activities related to the realization of numerical model of selected energetic conversion systems and of a simple electric drive suitable for automotive application

## Assessment methods

Assessment consists of a project and an oral examination.

The project involves developing a computer model of an electric drive. The assignment will be provided during the course, and students will present their work at the examination.

The oral examination consists of two parts:

1. Presentation of the project and discussion of the results.

2. Assessment of theoretical knowledge through an oral discussion and calculations using worksheets and charts provided by the lecturer.

The final grade is weighted as follows:

- Project report: 50%.

- Oral examination: 50%.
