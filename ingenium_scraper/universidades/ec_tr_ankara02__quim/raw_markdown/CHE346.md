### Objectives of the Course

Teaching the properties of materials to be used in the field of engineering and equipping students with the knowledge to choose the right material according to the expected service.

### Course Content

Basic structure of matter, physicochemical, thermal and mechanical properties of the material, test methods. Classification of material. Iron and steel. Stainless steels. Non-ferrous metals. Plastic materials. Refractory materials. Cement, lime, plaster. Composite materials.
