### Objectives of the Course

This course aims to equip students working in various institutions and organizations with the competence to actively participate in the Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) process. This includes preparing EIA reports and examining and evaluating these reports, all of which are crucial steps before deciding on new investments. The course specifically focuses on ensuring that graduates, with a background in Chemical Engineering, possess the necessary skills for these tasks.

### Course Content

An overview of the Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) concept, its development in the world and Türkiye, and regulations; Planning and management of EIA studies; Identification of the affected environment and the indicators used in this identification; Determination and evaluation of the effects on the air, water, and soil environment; Determination and evaluation of noise impact; Determination and evaluation of impacts on the biological environment; Determination and evaluation of visual impact; Determination and evaluation of impacts on cultural and socioeconomic environments; Evaluation of project alternatives; Public participation; Preparation of written documents; Monitoring and control.
