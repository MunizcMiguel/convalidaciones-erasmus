### Objectives of the Course

To gain knowledge about fuel cells and technology and to research today's fuel cell technology.

### Course Content

Fuel cell fundamentals, types of fuel cells, fuel processing technologies for fuel cell systems, commercial applications, and industrial applications of fuel cells.
