### Objectives of the Course

To teach the fundamental concepts in physical chemistry necessary for chemical engineering

### Course Content

The general properties of gases, liquids, and solids. Kinetic theory of gases. Chemical kinetics and reaction mechanisms. Phase rule, phase equilibrium, and diagrams. Pure components, binary, and multicomponent systems. Chemical equilibrium. Surface chemistry. Electrochemistry.

### Course Methods and Techniques
