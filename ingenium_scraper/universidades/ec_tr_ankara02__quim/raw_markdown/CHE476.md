### Objectives of the Course

To show with examples how to benefit from the use of computers in different application areas of Chemical Engineering. To show how experimental data analysis is performed, how theoretically developed model equations can be solved, and how many equations necessary to define the system can be solved simultaneously. While doing these, make use of simple and accessible programs and make sure that the examples are directly application-oriented.

### Course Content

Use of computers in Chemical Engineering applications and design. Use of various computer programming techniques in determining transport properties, interphase balance calculations and calculations related to basic operations in processes.
