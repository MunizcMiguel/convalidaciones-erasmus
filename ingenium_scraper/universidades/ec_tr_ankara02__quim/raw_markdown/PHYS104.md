### Objectives of the Course

To examine basic electrics and magnetism phenomena in the nature and

learning of basic concepts. To gain the basic discipline of algorithm

development for analytical thinking and problem solving.

### Course Content

Electric fields, Continuous charge distribution and Electric fields, Gauss

Law and applications, Electric Potential, Electric Potential of Continuous

charge distribution, Capacitance and Dielectric , Current and Resistance,

electromagnetic force, Direct current circuits, Magnetic Fields , Magnetic

field sources, Electromagnetic induction, Faraday?s Law and induction,

Alternating Current Circuits , Alternating Current Circuits: AC sources,

resistors, capacitors, ınductors at ac circuits , ac circuits in series, power,

Electromagnetic Waves
