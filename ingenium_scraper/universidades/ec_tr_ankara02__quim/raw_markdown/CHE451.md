### Objectives of the Course

To understand the general philosophy to be followed in chemical process design and to use the previously learned basic concepts of material balance, energy balance, thermodynamics, basic processes, and reaction systems in the design of the basic equipment that forms the process. Making detailed equipment designs (at least a pipeline, a heat exchanger, a distillation column, and a reactor) and determining the optimum parameters using cost analysis. Learning and encouraging group work.

### Course Content

Design of equipment used in the chemical industry and determination of optimum operating conditions. Various design project studies and cost analyzes related to pipeline, storage, heat exchangers and separation processes. Computer aided design applications.
