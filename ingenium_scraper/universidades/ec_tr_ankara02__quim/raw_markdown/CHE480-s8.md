### Objectives of the Course

To teach production of boron minerals, boron concentrates, refined boron products and advanced boron products and usage areas of boron products.

### Course Content

Boron and its compounds, ores, reserves. Ore enrichment. Production of refined boron products. Advanced boron end products. Boron usage areas.
