### Objectives of the Course

To introduce some selected inorganic and organic technologies to students. To increase students' knowledge and skills regarding the use and preparation of process flow charts. Being able to form groups, work and research in groups. Improve written and verbal communication skills in Turkish.

### Course Content

Fundamentals of chemical technology. Water preparation and dirty water treatment methods in industry. Production of sulfuric acid, nitric acid and ammonia. Gas fuels. Synthetic organic chemicals. Polymers and Plastics. Some other chemical technologies.
