### Objectives of the Course

To gain the ability to apply the basic concepts learned in fluid mechanics, physical chemistry, and heat transfer through experiments.

To gain experience in designing and conducting independent experiments.

To teach methods of analyzing and evaluating experimental data.

To teach and apply the rules of safe working in the laboratory.

To gain experience in group work.

To gain written communication skills through the reports to be prepared.

### Course Content

Laboratory applications of physical chemistry, fluid mechanics, and heat transfer principles. Evaluation of experimental data and results. Written presentation.
