### Objectives of the Course

To provide information on coal, an important energy source for our country, starting from its formation and covering coal preparation, beneficiation, and the production of higher calorific value products from coal. The goal is for students to gain knowledge about the topics discussed.

### Course Content

Coal Formation and Classification. Physical and Chemical Properties of Coal. Methods of Coal Preparation. Washing and Drying of Coal. High-Temperature Processes. Coal Combustion Processes and Design.
