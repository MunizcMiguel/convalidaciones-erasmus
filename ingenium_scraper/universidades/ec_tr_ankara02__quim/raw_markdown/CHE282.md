### Objectives of the Course

The aim of the course is to teach methods for determining the thermodynamic properties of pure substances and mixtures, and to learn the analysis of phase equilibrium in single and multicomponent systems

### Course Content

The thermodynamic properties of pure substances and mixtures. Enthalpies of dissolution and dilution. Auxiliary functions. Thermodynamic properties of multicomponent systems, partial molar properties, fugacity, activity. Phase equilibrium in single and multicomponent systems
