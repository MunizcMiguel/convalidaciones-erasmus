### Objectives of the Course

Based on the fundamental concepts of chemical reaction kinetics and ideal flow reactor design, the examination of real reactors, heterogeneous reaction systems, and reactor modeling studies.

### Course Content

Steady-state and unsteady-state conditions in homogeneous flow reactors, heterogeneous reaction systems. Rate and selectivity in multi-reaction systems. Non-ideal flow reactors. Industrial reactors. Optimization, safety, and economics. Design studies. Design Project.
