### Objectives of the Course

Applies numerical methods to Chemical Engineering problems

Decides which numerical method would be appropriate in which situations

Writes and uses routines with Matlab to implement numerical algorithms and methods

### Course Content

Solutions of linear and nonlinear equations and systems of equations. Numerical methods for interpolation, integration and differentiation. Solution of systems of ordinary differential equations. Statistical analysis. Data analysis. Regression and correlation.
