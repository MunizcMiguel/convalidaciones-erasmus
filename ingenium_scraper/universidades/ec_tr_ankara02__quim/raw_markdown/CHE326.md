### Objectives of the Course

Introduction to steam systems, the importance of energy efficiency, providing information about relevant concepts.

### Course Content

Formation of steam, situations hindering heat transfer in steam systems, cavitation and its impact on the system, condensate recovery, functions and types of steam traps, and design of the main steam line.
