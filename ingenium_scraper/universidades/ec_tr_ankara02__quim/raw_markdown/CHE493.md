### Objectives of the Course

Applying optimization techniques to a variety of engineering problems.

### Course Content

Optimization and development. Engineering applications of optimization. Classification of optimization problems. Linear programming. Nonlinear programming. Some important unconstrained and constrained optimization techniques. Optimization examples for specific individual processes.
