### Objectives of the Course

Explaining the production and application areas of cement and concrete. Equipping with the characteristics, applications, repair methods, and topics related to concrete, an important material in engineering, and imparting practical applications of these aspects.

### Course Content

Cement production, clinker mineralogical compounds, types and properties of cement, pozzolanic cements, cement hydration reactions, concrete and mortars, properties of fresh concrete, factors affecting concrete quality, concrete additives, ready-mix concrete, alkali-aggregate reaction in concrete, sulfate corrosion in concrete.
