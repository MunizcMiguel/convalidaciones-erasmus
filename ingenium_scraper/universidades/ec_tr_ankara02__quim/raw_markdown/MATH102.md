### Objectives of the Course

To be able to learn and apply the concept of integral, to apply

convergence tests for positive quadratic series, to be able to

calculate limits and derivatives of multivariable functions and

calculate double integral.

### Course Content

The applications of definite integral, sequences, convergence tests for

series and positive series, limits and derivatives of multivariable functions,

double integral
