### Objectives of the Course

To teach the fundamental design and operating principles of fluidized bed reactors, which are increasingly finding more applications in chemical processes, and to apply them to various reactions.

### Course Content

Fluidized bed types and hydrodynamics. Fluid and solid movements. Heat transfer, mass transfer, and chemical reactions in fluidized beds. Design of fluidized bed reactors. Selected special project applications.
