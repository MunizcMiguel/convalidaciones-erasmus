### Objectives of the Course

To learn various organic production processes and to have information about chemical operations.

### Course Content

Coking of coal, coal chemicals, Petroleum and petroleum products, Soap and detergent, Paper pulp and industry, Sugar industry, Production of chemical fibers, Dyes and their applications.
