### Objectives of the Course

Information about Türkiye's general energy situation and energy efficiency

### Course Content

General situation of Türkiye. Structure of Turkish industry, efficient use of fuels and combustion systems, the importance of fuel, increasing heat transfer efficiency, waste heat recovery. Energy management program. Monitoring energy consumption and setting goals.
