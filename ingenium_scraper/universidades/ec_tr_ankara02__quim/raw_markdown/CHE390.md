### Objectives of the Course

A student who succeeds in this course is expected to be able to:

Based on the course content: The student who succeeds in this course is expected to understand the basic principles of the electrochemical biosensor, have knowledge about different application areas, biosensor preparation and how to extract the current response in electrochemical biosensors.

Based on the program outcomes of the course: It is expected that the student will have the ability to communicate in writing and verbally with the assigned homework and/or project reports and presentations.

### Course Content

Electrochemical examination of biosensors, examination of the performance properties and different application areas of electrochemical biosensors.
