### Objectives of the Course

To provide students with basic knowledge and calculations about chemistry, to provide information about engineering applications of chemistry, to understand the importance of chemistry in health, environment and industry.

### Course Content

Chemical kinetics, chemical equilibrium, acids and bases, solubility and complex ion equilibria, voluntary change, Entropy and Gibbs energy, Electrochemistry.
