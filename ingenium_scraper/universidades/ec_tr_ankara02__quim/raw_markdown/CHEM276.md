### Objectives of the Course

Students who successfully complete this course will:

Acquire knowledge about Chemical Bonding.

Have an understanding of the structures and synthesis of aliphatic and aromatic organic compounds.

Possess knowledge about the reactions of aliphatic and aromatic organic compounds.

Gain knowledge in Stereochemistry.

Acquire knowledge about Free Radicals.

### Course Content

Chemical Bonding, Aliphatic and Aromatic Compounds and Reactions, Stereochemistry, Free Radicals
