### Objectives of the Course

This course is designed to give the student with engineering skills and technology knowledge the basic concepts necessary to turn an innovative idea into a product or service and that product or service into a business. These basic concepts include economy, market, competition, demand, feasibility of the product or service and financing of the business, design, organization, market and marketing and quality. The aim is to ensure that the student, who has the basic formation of engineering, can also think like a businessman.

### Course Content

Developments in Science and Technology from past to present, examples of scientific and technological developments from the world and Turkey, transition from industrial society to information society, classical industry, advanced technology and R&D dimension, What is Innovation? What are the types of innovation? Examples, Is innovation innovativeness? What are the features that distinguish innovation from R&D? Country examples in innovation, our Intellectual Property Rights and Innovation Policy, Innovation in the Public Sector, Entrepreneurship and its rules, SME Definition, Incentive Law, State Support and Incentives and the institutions and organizations that provide them; (KOSGEB, TÜBİTAK, İŞKUR, Ministry of Science, Technology and Industry, Ministry of Development, Undersecretariat of Treasury, Ministry of Economy, Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Agriculture) Technology Development Zones, Organized Industrial Zones, Technoparks, Things to be done.
