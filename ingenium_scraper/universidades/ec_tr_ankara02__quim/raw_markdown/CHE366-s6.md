### Objectives of the Course

By understanding the economic and social impacts of air, water, and soil pollution, fostering conscious behavior to prevent environmental pollution.

### Course Content

Hydraulic cycle. Factors determining water quality. Saprobic system. Suspended, floating, settled matter. Thermal pollution. Inorganic pollution. Toxic, radioactive pollutants. Organic pollution. Microbial pollution. The effect of nitrogen compounds, phosphorus in water pollution. Surface water quality parameters and models.
