### Objectives of the Course

To provide information about the scope and history of biotechnology. To explain the use of biotechnology in industrial areas such as food, industry and environment (Ethanol and biopolymer production, enzyme and antibiotic production). To discuss the importance of biosafety and bioethics issues in biotechnology.

### Course Content

Morphological and chemical structures of microorganisms. Industrial Biotechnology. Enzyme biotechnology. Bioenergy. Environmental biotechnology.
