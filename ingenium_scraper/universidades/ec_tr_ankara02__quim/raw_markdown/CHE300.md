### Objectives of the Course

Learning the steps of the production process from raw materials to the final product in the institution where the internship is performed. Writing material and energy balances for selected/determined units.

### Course Content

An internship is performed in a facility where Chemical Engineering practices take place. A report must be submitted containing the mass and energy balances established on any equipment.
