### Objectives of the Course

The aim of the course is to provide information on the definition and classification of porous structures, synthesis and modification methods required to control the pore structure, applications of porous materials, characterization methods, and advanced material applications. Within the scope of the course, the goal is to develop the ability to decide on the appropriate material in applications where porous materials are used.

### Course Content

Porous Structures: Zeolites, Clay Minerals, and Other Natural Materials and Their Properties. Synthesis of Porous Materials. Industrial Applications. Determination of Properties of Natural and Synthetic Porous Materials.
