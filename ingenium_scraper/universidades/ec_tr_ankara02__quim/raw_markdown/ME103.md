### Objectives of the Course

To learn the fundamental technical drawing and make drawings by

using CAD software.

### Course Content

Introduction to technical drawing, AutoCAD basic commands and

applications, Principles of dimensioning, three dimensional

drawing techniques, Principles of sectioning, Introduction to 3D

solid modeling, AutoCAD applications
