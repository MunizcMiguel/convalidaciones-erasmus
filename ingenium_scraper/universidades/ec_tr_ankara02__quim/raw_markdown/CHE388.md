### Objectives of the Course

Associating science, technology, and engineering applications, discussing the contributions of past civilizations to the fields of science, technology, and engineering, and enabling students to design certain technological applications. Preparing projects for the purpose and examining the contributions of the past to current and future technological processes.

### Course Content

What is technology? Relationships between science, technology, and engineering. Technological processes in prehistoric, early, and middle ages. Contributions of different civilizations to technological processes. Examination of science, technology, and engineering applications of Central Asian and Middle Eastern civilizations. Overview of contemporary technologies.
