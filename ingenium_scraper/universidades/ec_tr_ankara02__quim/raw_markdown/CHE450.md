### Objectives of the Course

To learn various inorganic production processes and to have information about their chemical operations.

### Course Content

Soda Production, Chlorine and sodium hydroxide production, Fertilizer industry, Cement industry, Ceramics and glass industry, Boron and some other inorganic technologies
