### Objectives of the Course

Learning the terminology and perspective of sociology

### Course Content

The establishment of the nation state in Turkey, the development of capitalism, the tension between the statist-elites and the traditionalist-liberals, efforts to create a national bourgeoisie, the origins and structure of the classes in the stratification system, capital and bourgeoisie, income distribution.
