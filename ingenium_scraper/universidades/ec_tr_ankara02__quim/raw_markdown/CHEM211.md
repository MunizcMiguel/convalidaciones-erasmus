### Objectives of the Course

To provide fundamental knowledge in

chemical analysis,

covering errors,

gravimetric analysis,

titrimetric analysis,

aqueous solution chemistry,

ionic equilibria,

calculations in complex equilibria,

acid-base neutralization titrations,

precipitation titrations,

complex formation titrations, and electrochemistry,

to give examples of engineering applications.

### Course Content

Errors in chemical analysis, gravimetric analysis, titrimetric analysis, aqueous solution chemistry, electrolyte effect on ionic equilibria, calculations in complex equilibria, acid-base neutralization titrations, precipitation titrations, complex formation titrations, electrochemistry.
