### Objectives of the Course

To learn and to investigate the philosophy of constructing flow diagrams of chemical processes, to make the preliminary design of a complete process, to realize the importance of ethic and environmental values, to make cost analysis and pricing of products.

### Course Content

Complete design of a selected chemical process. Flow chart selection, examination and preparation. Investigation of the process from flexibility, safety, operatibility, controlibility and environmental effects points of views. Mass and energy balances. Selection and design of equipments. Detailed economical analyses of processes.
