### Objectives of the Course

To be able to establish and solve the material and energy balances necessary for the mathematical description of basic chemical and physical systems.

Demonstrate the necessity of numerical and analytical analysis techniques.

To be able to solve ordinary and partial differential equations in chemical engineering problems with package programs (polymath, matlab etc.)

Development of experimental planning and data analysis skills

### Course Content

Mathematical modeling of physical and chemical processes. Applications of ordinary and partial differential equations in chemical engineering problems. Analytical and computer techniques. Regression and empirical modeling.
