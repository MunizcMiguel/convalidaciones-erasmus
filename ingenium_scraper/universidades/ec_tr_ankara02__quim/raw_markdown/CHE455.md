### Objectives of the Course

To provide information on cathodic protection and prepare for project implementation.

### Course Content

General Principles of Cathodic Protection. Types of Cathodic Protection. Cathodic Protection of Systems such as Pipes, Tanks, Heat Exchangers, Ships, etc. Design of Cathodic Protection Systems. Operation and Maintenance of Cathodic Protection Systems.
