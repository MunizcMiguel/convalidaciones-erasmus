### Objectives of the Course

To provide information about electrochemical technologies used for environmental and clean energy production, to provide information about innovative electrochemical industrial applications, and to develop oral and written presentation skills.

### Course Content

Electrochemical applications in chemical production, separation and purification processes in industry. Electrochemical applications in clean energy production. Electrochemical reactors used in industry and their design features.
