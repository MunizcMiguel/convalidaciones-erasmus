### Objectives of the Course

To teach the basic principles of biotechnology and bioengineering. To give general information about bioprocesses. To introduce the fundamentals of enzymatic reactions and microbial growth. To teach the important parameters and bioreactors used for enzymatic and microbial production.

### Course Content

Definition of biotechnology and biochemical reaction engineering. Chemical substances in the cell structure. Enzyme kinetics. Immobilized enzyme technique and kinetics. Cell cultures. Cell kinetics and fermenter design. Mass transfer effects in bioprocess systems.
