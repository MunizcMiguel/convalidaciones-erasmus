### Objectives of the Course

To make the student understand the basics of original research, to teach the necessary methods to conduct research, to lay the foundation for theoretical/experimental studies to be carried out in the CHE492 course.

### Course Content

A project study that will synthesize the knowledge gained in the chemical engineering program.
