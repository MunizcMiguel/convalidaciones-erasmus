### Objectives of the Course

To introduce some basic processes frequently encountered in industry, to examine the parameters affecting efficiency and to examine the latest techniques.

### Course Content

Basic principles and usage techniques of industrial processes such as nitration, halogenation, sulphonation. Examples of typical industrial processes.
