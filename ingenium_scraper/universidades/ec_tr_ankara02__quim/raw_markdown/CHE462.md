### Objectives of the Course

The teaching of properties and technological uses of polymer materials used in the field of engineering, equipping students with the knowledge to choose the right material based on the expected service.

### Course Content

Polymerization mechanism, polymer structure, molecular weight, and classification of polymers. Polymeric refractories and petrochemical monomers. Synthesis methods and processes. Polymer additives and fillers. Polymer processing methods. Some industrial polymer processes and engineering.
