### Objectives of the Course

To compare the basic processes used in biochemical separation processes with the basic processes used in the chemical industry and to reveal the similarities and differences. To classify the basic processes used in the purification of biomolecules. Studying the different basic operations in each class.

### Course Content

Properties of biochemical substances. Basic bioseparation and purification processes of biomolecules. Filtration. Centrifugation. Extraction. Adsorption. Precipitation. Electrophoresis. Crystallization. Drying.
