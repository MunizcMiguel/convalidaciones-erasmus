### Objectives of the Course

Teaching and encouraging research on techniques and technologies for harnessing solar energy.

### Course Content

Sun and Solar Energy. Solar Collectors. Solar Water Heating Systems. Photovoltaic Cells. Solar Cooling. Solar Ponds. Utilization of Solar Energy in Distillation and Drying Processes.
