### Objectives of the Course

Teaching and implementing control engineering problem-solving methods through computer-aided approaches.

### Course Content

Introduction and Fundamental Concepts. Analysis of the transient response of continuous-time systems. Analysis of the transient response of discrete-time systems. Root-Locus plots, Nyquist plots, frequency response plots.
