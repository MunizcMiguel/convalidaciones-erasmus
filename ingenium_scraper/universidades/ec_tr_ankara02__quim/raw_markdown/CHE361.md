### Objectives of the Course

To introduce the basic concepts of electrochemistry

### Course Content

Electrochemical terms and concepts: Electrical conductivity. Electric charge. Current intensity. Ionic conductivity: Equivalent conductivity. Limiting Equivalent Conductivity. Electrolytic Equilibria: Acids and Bases. Degree of Dissociation. Hydrolysis. Electrochemical Cells: Electrode Potentials. Types of Electrodes. Electrolysis: Overvoltage. Dissociation Voltage. Corrosion. Cathodic Protection.
