### Objectives of the Course

The importance of engineering in fire safety

### Course Content

The structure and development of fire; extinguishing agents, devices, and usage techniques; fire materials used in workplaces; hazards at the fire scene (fire growth rate, high temperature, effects of fire components on spread, hazards in different fire stages: difficulty in breathing due to toxic gases, explosion, collapse, electricity, chemicals); fire safety measures in workplaces (regulations, directives, and standards, active and passive measures); fire safety organizations in workplaces.
