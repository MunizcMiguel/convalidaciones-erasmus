### Objectives of the Course

To inform the students about the comparison of natural gas, which is used as a chemical raw material and an energy source, with other fuels and raw materials, to know its benefits and drawbacks, to use it in industry, and to take the necessary safety precautions.

### Course Content

Properties, composition, extraction, pre-treatment, transportation, and storage of natural gas. Use as raw material for chemical products. Areas of use. Measurement, control, and safety systems.
