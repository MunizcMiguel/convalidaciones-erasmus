### Objectives of the Course

To convey to students the methods applied in both technology transfer and technology production and the experiences in this field.

### Course Content

Technology selection. Technology transfer. License agreements. Foreign capital investments. Patents, Know-How, brand agreements, R&D activities, technoparks, technology policies
