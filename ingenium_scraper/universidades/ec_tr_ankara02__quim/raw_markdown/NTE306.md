### Objectives of the Course

To convey general information about public relations to the student.

### Course Content

Subject of marketing, Scope, Strategic marketing, Marketing information system and marketing research, Final and industrial consumer behavior and markets, Market segmentation, Target market selection and demand forecasts, Product, Pricing, Promotion, Distribution, Organization, Implementation and control, International marketing, Service marketing, Direct marketing and internet marketing.
