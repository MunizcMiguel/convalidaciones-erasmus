### Objectives of the Course

Thorium production principles and its use in nuclear technology.

### Course Content

Recovery, enrichment and purification of thorium from ore and various sources, preparation and characterization of various compounds of uranium and thorium, determination of thorium, reactor types and fuel cycles, thorium-based fuel cycle and development of nuclear reactors running on thorium fuel.
