### Objectives of the Course

To make calculations regarding gravimetric analysis, titrimetric analysis, separation by precipitation, quantitative and qualitative analysis in chemical analysis. To provide an experimental understanding of acid-base, precipitation, complexmetric and iodometric titrations, to provide basic information and to give examples of engineering applications.

### Course Content

Analysis of Group I cations, II. Analysis of group cations, III. Analysis of Group Cations, Analysis of Group IV and V cations, General Cation analysis, Acid-base neutralization titration (Determination of acetic acid in vinegar), Precipitation Titration (Volhard method), Complexmetric titration with EDTA (Determination of hardness in water), Redox Titration (With KMnO4 oxalate and Ca determination), Iodometric titration (Cu determination)
