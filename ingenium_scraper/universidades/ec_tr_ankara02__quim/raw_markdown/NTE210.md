### Objectives of the Course

The organizational and personnel structure of the Turkish public administration will be understood, the procedures and conditions for entering public services, progress and promotion in the service, disciplinary penalties, basic issues about employment forms will be learned, the importance of in-service training and training of senior managers will be understood.

### Course Content

Personnel management principles and objectives. Public-private sector personnel management distinction. Commissioning of personnel. Rights, obligations of public personnel, prohibitions they must comply with, classification, evaluation, promotion, incentive for work, remuneration, discipline, trial, termination of duty.
