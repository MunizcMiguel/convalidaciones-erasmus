### Objectives of the Course

To be knowledgeable about energy technologies and to be able to apply engineering, science, and mathematics knowledge in this field.

### Course Content

Energy and its sources. Scope, efficiency, and cost of energy transformations. Chemical energy, combustion, reversible reactions, batteries, and fuel cells. Thermal energy. Nuclear energy. Solar energy. Geothermal energy.
