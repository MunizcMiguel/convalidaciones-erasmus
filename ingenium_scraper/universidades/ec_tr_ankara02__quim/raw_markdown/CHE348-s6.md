### Objectives of the Course

Teaching the properties of plastic materials to be used in engineering and equipping students with the knowledge to select the right material based on expected services.

### Course Content

Plastic materials and additives. Applications of plastics. Environmental impacts of plastics. Evaluation of plastic waste.
