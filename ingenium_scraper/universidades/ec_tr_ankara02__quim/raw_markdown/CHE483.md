### Objectives of the Course

? Transferred quantities definition and classification

? Defining and formulating transport phenomena

? Investigating molecular-level transport phenomena in chemical engineering systems

### Course Content

Transport laws and analogy. Molecular-level transport phenomena, transport properties. Establishment and solution of shell balances for momentum, energy, and mass transfer.
