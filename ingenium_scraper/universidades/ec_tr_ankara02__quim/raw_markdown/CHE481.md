### Objectives of the Course

Learning physicochemical analyses, heat and mass transfer, and kinetic concepts through experimental studies; Developing skills in group work, designing experiments, conducting experiments, obtaining and interpreting results, and writing scientific reports.

### Course Content

Heat and mass transfer, laboratory applications of chemical reaction engineering principles. Instrumental analysis and technology applied experiments. Evaluation of experimental data and results. Written presentation. It should be evaluated specially.
