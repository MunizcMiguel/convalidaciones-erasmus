### Objectives of the Course

To provide an overview of the general characteristics of fluids and introduce fundamental concepts related to fluid statics and flow.

To teach basic concepts about the equipment used in flow lines and the underlying fundamental principles.

To impart fundamental knowledge for pipeline design

### Course Content

Fluid statics and applications. Fluid dynamics. Viscosity, velocity gradient, laminar and turbulent flow, boundary layer. Mechanical energy balance, Bernoulli's equation. Frictional losses. Compressible fluids. Flow meters. Flow around immersed solid objects. Flow in packed columns. Mixing of liquids. Valves and connections. Pumps, blowers, compressors.
