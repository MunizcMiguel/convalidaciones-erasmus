### Objectives of the Course

Developing skills in water chemistry, purification methods, preparing water for industrial use, and disinfection.

### Course Content

The properties of water. Physical and chemical processes. Preparation of waters used in industry. Disinfection. Preparation of boiler feed and cooling waters.
