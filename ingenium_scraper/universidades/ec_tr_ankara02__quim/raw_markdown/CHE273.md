### Objectives of the Course

Providing fundamental thermodynamic knowledge necessary for the analysis and design of processes in the field of engineering.

Applying the laws of thermodynamics to systems.

Developing skills to solve potential thermodynamic problems using modern tools and techniques.

Cultivating the ability to continuously update oneself by following scientific and technological developments and gaining knowledge about current issues.

### Course Content

Thermodynamic equilibrium, concepts of temperature and reversibility. Work and heat. The First Law of Thermodynamics for closed and open systems. Ideal and real gases, equations of state. Thermodynamic properties of pure substances. The Second Law of Thermodynamics. Entropy. The Third Law of Thermodynamics. Compressors. Power cycles. Refrigeration and low-temperature processes.
