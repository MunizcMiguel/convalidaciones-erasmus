### Objectives of the Course

Investigation of the synthesis of laser systems and their dyes and providing information about laser systems and dyes.

### Course Content

Laser types and their applications, Characteristics of organic dye lasers, Classification, and chemical structures of laser dyes. Production of finished dye.
