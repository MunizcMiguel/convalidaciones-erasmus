### Objectives of the Course

To provide information about petrochemical technology, to examine production techniques and the situation of the sector in Turkey and around the world.

### Course Content

Development of petrochemical technology. Raw materials. Production of petrochemicals. Petrochemical industry and technologies used in Turkey.
