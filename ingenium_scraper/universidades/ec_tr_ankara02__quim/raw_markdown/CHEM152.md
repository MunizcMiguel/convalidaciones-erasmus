### Objectives of the Course

Students understand the basic principles of chemistry by performing simple experiments on the topics covered in general chemistry. Experimental observation and understanding of the chemical laws that form the foundations of chemistry, an applied science.

### Course Content

Recognition of Substances by Their Physical and Chemical Properties, Law of Constant Proportions, Determination of Gas Constant, Diffusion, Determination of Molecular Mass by Freezing Point Depression, Effect of Temperature on Reaction Rate, Chemical Equilibrium, Acid-Base Titration, pH and Indicators, Redox Reactions.
