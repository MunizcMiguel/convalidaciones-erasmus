### Objectives of the Course

With the engineering economics course, It is aimed to teach the principles of market and process research, to determine whether a project is feasible by performing cost analysis, and to provide the necessary knowledge and skills to compare investment options.

It is aimed that students taking the course will gain the ability to use engineering economics principles in investment decision-making processes.

### Course Content

Introduction to economics. Basic concepts and economic laws. Supply-demand mechanism and flexibility. Cost estimates. Interest. Depreciation. Profitability of investment. Preparation of preliminary feasibility reports on selected special topics
