### Objectives of the Course

Application of Mass Transfer Theories to New Separation Principles, Learning Various Separation Techniques

### Course Content

Principles and Applications of Separation Processes such as Osmotic Separation, Pervaporation, Membrane Processes, Reactive Distillation, Chromatography, etc.
