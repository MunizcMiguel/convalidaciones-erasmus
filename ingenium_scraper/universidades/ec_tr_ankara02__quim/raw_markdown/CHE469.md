### Objectives of the Course

Introducing the place and importance of adsorption and ion exchange in separation processes.

Development of rate and equilibrium relationships in adsorption processes.

Learning techniques for determining adsorbent properties

Using the learned information in equipment design.

Teaching the necessary methods for engineering applications of relevant theories.

### Course Content

Rate expressions and equilibrium relations for adsorption and ion exchange. Techniques for determining the structure and properties of sorbents. Designs of adsorption and ion exchange columns. Industrial applications.
