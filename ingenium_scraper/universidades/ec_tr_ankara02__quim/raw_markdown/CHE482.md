### Objectives of the Course

Refreshing what has been learned just before graduation, especially on mass transfer and process control. Gaining original experience by doing some routine experiments on these subjects. Determining a term experiment as a group in the light of all they have learned, proposing and running an experimental system, designing the experiment, creating the operating procedure, determining parameters, conducting the relevant experiment, evaluating the findings and drawing realistic results. Enable groups to activate and deactivate devices safely. Expressing knowledge and skills by presenting group term experiments as posters.

### Course Content

Laboratory studies on the application of chemical and physical principles to chemical processes. Experiments focused on mass transfer, simultaneous heat and mass transfer, process control, instrumental analysis, and technology application. Laboratory and non-laboratory applications.
