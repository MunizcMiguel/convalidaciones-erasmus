### Objectives of the Course

To teach the principles of mass transfer, which forms the basis of separation processes and is also one of the transport phenomena, to examine the basic methods of its application to separation processes, and to examine in detail gas absorption and distillation from separation processes.

### Course Content

Diffusivity, molecular diffusion flux, and Fick's laws. Mass transfer coefficients in laminar and turbulent flows. Interphase mass transfer. Mass transfer theories. Similarity theories. Continuous and gradual contact processes. Gas absorption and distillation.
