### Objectives of the Course

The aim of the Introduction to Chemical Engineering course is to introduce students to the Chemical Engineering Profession, institutions, and fields of study, and to raise students' awareness about the ethical responsibilities they must have while performing their duties in these fields.

Based on the stated objectives, the aim of the course is to show the basic concepts in Chemical Engineering and their applications in processes and to provide students with the ability to use this knowledge in other courses.

### Course Content

Introduction of Chemical Engineering and undergraduate education. Learning methods. Working places. Introduction of chemical processes. The present and future of chemical engineering in industry. Work safety. Engineering ethics.
