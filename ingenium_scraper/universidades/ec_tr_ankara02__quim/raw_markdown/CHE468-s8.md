### Objectives of the Course

To examine the synthesis of textile dyes, to obtain information about the dyeing and printing processes of various fibers, and to evaluate the dyeing and dyeing quality.

### Course Content

Dyeing and printing theory. Color fastness. Classification and properties of dyestuffs. Cellulosic, polyester, protein etc. dyeing of fiber.
