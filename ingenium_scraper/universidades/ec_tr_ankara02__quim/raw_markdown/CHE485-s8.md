### Objectives of the Course

In this course, the aim is to equip the students with competencies related to quality management systems in professional life.

### Course Content

Total Quality Management, Definition of Quality, Quality Management and Standardization, ISO 9000, 9001, 9004, and Other Standard Series, Preparation of Instructions and Procedures for ISO 9001.
