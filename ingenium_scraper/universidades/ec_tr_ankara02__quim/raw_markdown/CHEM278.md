### Objectives of the Course

Learning the fundamental laboratory techniques used in the separation and purification of organic compounds in chemical engineering applications, providing theoretical and practical knowledge as a whole.

Understanding the contemporary importance of organic chemistry in our daily lives.

### Course Content

Purification methods in organic chemistry and syntheses of some organic compounds

### Course Methods and Techniques
