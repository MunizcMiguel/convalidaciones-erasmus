### Objectives of the Course

To introduce the laboratory management systems and to gain the ability to serve as Laboratory Responsible.

To give information about the laboratory operating principles, Material Safety Data Sheet(MSDS ), chemical waste collection, storage and disposal, first aid, laboratory accreditation (TS EN ISO 17025).

### Course Content

Laboratory safety. the laboratory operating principles. chemical waste collection, storage and disposal
