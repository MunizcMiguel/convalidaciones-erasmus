### Objectives of the Course

Classification of instrumental methods, electromagnetic radiation and its properties, devices in optical spectroscopy, molecular UV-Visible region spectroscopy, molecular and atomic absorption, molecular fluorescence and chemiluminescence, atomic emission, flaming and electrothermal AAS, NMR, atomic absorption (AAS) and atomic emission (AES)., Infrared (IR), conductimetric and potentiometric methods. X-ray spectroscopy, voltammetric analysis, surface analysis methods, electron spectroscopy, mass spectrometry (MS), thermal analysis TG, DTA, DSC, chromatographic methods.

### Course Content

To teach the principles of instrumental analysis and basic information and calculations about chemical analysis, to introduce modern techniques and devices used in chemical analysis, to learn spectroscopic, chromatographic, and voltammetric devices and methods used in medical and food analysis, to give information about engineering applications of chemistry, to use instrumental methods in chemical analysis for health, to understand its importance in the environment and industry.
