# Biomaterials and Design of Medical Devices

## Recommended prerequisite for participation in the module

The module adds to the knowledge obtained previous in the study programme.

## Content, progress and pedagogy of the module

### Learning objectives

#### Knowledge

- Have knowledge about the structures of the principal biomaterials such as bone, muscle, cartilage, ligaments, connective tissues and biofluids.
- Have knowledge about the mechanics and remodeling of living tissue.
- Have knowledge about quality assurance and risk assessment procedures of medical devices.
- Have knowledge about the liability and responsibility of medical devices manufacturers throughout the product lifecycle.

#### Skills

- Be able to create valid models from experimental data and simulate the mechanical behaviors of living tissues using biomechanical simulation methods such as the finite element method.
- Be able to create simple models of human body structures such as bones and ligaments.
- Be able to classify medical devices according to current regulations.
- Be able to apply clinical assessments to document the performance and safety of medical devices according to the specific classification.

#### Competences

- Be able to combine insights from solid mechanics with the structure of biomaterials to draw informed conclusions about the behavior of living tissue.
- Be able to envision medical devices for specific purposes that can fulfill regulatory requirements.
