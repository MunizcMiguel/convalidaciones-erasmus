# Experimental Design and Biostatistics

## Recommended prerequisite for participation in the module

The module adds to the knowledge obtained previous in the study programme.

## Content, progress and pedagogy of the module

### Learning objectives

#### Knowledge

- Have knowledge of the principles of hypothesis-driven research.
- Have knowledge of different types of experimental designs and their ethical considerations.
- Have knowledge about the scientific quality criteria such as internal and external validity, reliability, causality, generalizability and different types of bias.
- Have knowledge of inclusion and exclusion criteria.
- Have knowledge of methods to test data for normality.
- Have knowledge of methods for parametric and non-parametric statistical tests.
- Have knowledge of methods for visualizing discrete and continuous statistical outcomes.
- Have knowledge of methods to estimate sample size.

#### Skills

- Be able to write an experimental protocol, including selection of the experimental design, sample size consideration and inclusion/exclusion criteria.
- Be able to select and apply the appropriate statistical method for a specific hypothesis.
- Be able to generate visualizations of discrete and continuous statistical outcomes.
- Be able to apply dedicated statistical data analysis software.

#### Competences

- Be able to reflect on the strength and weaknesses of a specific experimental protocol.
- Be able to reflect on the applied statistical methods and generate a conclusion.
- Be able to critically review the conclusions drawn based on an experimental protocol and statistical methods.
