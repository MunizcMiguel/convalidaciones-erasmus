## Educational Objectives

After taking part successfully, students have reached the following learning results

### Professional Competence

#### Knowledge

- The students are able to explain the functionality of different CMOS
devices and bipolar junction transistors in various electronic circuits.
- The students are able to explain how basic analog circuits function and
where they are applied.
- The students are able to explain the functionality of fundamental
operational amplifier circuits and their parameters.
- The students are able to identify fundamental digital logic circuits and
can discuss their advantages and disadvantages.
- The students are able to name different memory technologies, to explain their functionality and architecture and to identify their parameters.

#### Skills

• The students can calculate parameters of various CMOS devices and assess the fundamental effects of semiconductor physics that impact circuit design. • The students are able to analyze simple circuits with active components such as transistors, diodes, or operational amplifiers. • The students are able to dimension simple analog circuits with regard to design goals. • The students are able to analyze, design, and dimension digital circuits in different technologies.

### Personal Competence

#### Social Competence

• The students are able work efficiently in heterogeneous teams. • The students can solve problems and answer professional questions when working together in small groups.

#### Autonomy

• The students are able to assess their own learning progress and independently manage their learning processes. • The students are able to obtain the necessary information from the provided material and additional literature sources and they are able to put it into the context of the course.
