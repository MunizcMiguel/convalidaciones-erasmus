## Educational Objectives

After taking part successfully, students have reached the following learning results

### Professional Competence

#### Knowledge

After successfully completing the module, students possess knowledge that enables them to explain fundamental operating principles and functions of machine elements, to understand which requirements and selection criteria influence the choice of suitable machine elements, to classify typical application scenarios and industrial examples, to identify the theoretical foundations of common calculation approaches, and to know the essential standards, rules and representation forms of technical drawing for individual parts and assemblies including principles of dimensioning, tolerancing and bill of materials.

#### Skills

After successfully completing the module, students are able to:
- perform design calculations for the covered machine elements based on current state-of-the-art method
- transfer the knowledge acquired in the module to new requirements and engineering tasks and derive appropriate design solution
- understand and correctly interpret technical drawings and principle sketche
- evaluate simple designs, derive purposeful improvements and justify decisions on a technical basis

### Personal Competence

#### Social Competence

Students are able to exchange ideas about technical content within the framework of activating teaching formats, address questions, provide feedback, and contribute different perspectives in an appreciative manner. In doing so, they develop an initial understanding of cooperative working methods in engineering contexts and the importance of clear communication for a common technical understanding.

#### Autonomy

Students are able to independently deepen the knowledge acquired in exercises, use textbooks and relevant standards to further develop calculation methods and reflect on their own approach. They thereby build the ability to address engineering tasks responsibly and continuously enhance their own capability to act.
