## Learning objectives

The basic objective of this course is to introduce not specialists in the field students to the fundamental electric circuit theory and its core applications. The students will acquire basic knowledge and skills necessary for the analysis of electrical circuits in steady state and sinusoidal steady state, single phase and three phase, and static and rotating electrical machines.

## Prerequisites

Knowledge of the basic topics of the following courses: Calculus I, Calculus II, Physics I, Physics II, Geometry, Electrotechnics

## Teaching methods

40 lecture hours 20 hours of exercises and electrical circuits laboratory Lessons are mainly held in the traditional way through the use of the blackboard, with the support of Power Point slides subsequently made available to students. The exercises of analysis of electrical networks are solved by the teacher and / or the students

## Assessment methods

The assessment of learning outcomes attainment is performed through a written test and an oral interview. The score will also consider the quality of oral and written communication. The written test, aimed at evaluating the operational capabilities, provides for the solution of one or more problems related to the analysis of power grids, magnetic circuits, transformers, asynchronous motors. The oral examination, only for students who achieved a score greater than 17/30 in the written test, verifies the degree of theoretical knowledge (electrical components, theorems for network analysis, principles of operation of the transformer and the asynchronous motor, equivalent schemes) and the presentation skills of the student. The score of the examination by an average of the results obtained in the written and oral tests
