## Learning objectives

THE COURSE INTRODUCES THE ARCHITECTURES, TECHNOLOGIES AND PROTOCOLS FOR THE INTERNET OF THINGS, ALSO INTRODUCING THE MAIN APPLICATION AREAS. KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING ARCHITECTURE OF AN IOT APPLICATION. PROGRAMMING OF A MICROCONTROLLER AND SENSORS INTERFACING. CHARACTERISTICS OF THE DIFFERENT TYPES OF SENSOR (MEMS, SERVO). NETWORK PROTOCOLS. APPLIED KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING CREATE SIMPLE IOT APPLICATIONS IN WHICH THE DATA DETECTED BY ONE OR MORE SENSORS ARE PROCESSED THROUGH A MICROCONTROLLER.

## Prerequisites

IT IS PREFERABLE TO HAVE SOME PREVIOUS KNOWLEDGE ABOUT INTERNET PROTOCOLS.

## Teaching methods

THE COURSE INCLUDES THEORY LESSONS AND GUIDED EXERCISES AIMED AT DEMONSTRATING THE USE OF TOOLS, SOFTWARE STACKS AND HARDWARE COMPONENTS, FOR THE REALIZATION OF IOT APPLICATIONS. PRACTICE WILL BE AIMED AT APPLYING THE KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED FOR THE PROGRAMMING OF THE MICROCONTROLLER USING THE PYTHON LANGUAGE, THE DESIGN OF SIMPLE ELECTRICAL CIRCUITS TO INTERFACE AND CONTROL SIMPLE HW COMPONENTS AS LEDS, MOTORS AND SENSORS USING THE DIFFERENT PROTOCOLS (I2C, SPI, DIGITAL AND ANALOG I / O, PWM). THE HOURS OF THE LABORATORY WILL BE USED TO START THE REALIZATION OF THE PROJECT THAT WILL HAVE TO BE DISCUSSED AT THE EXAMINATION. IN ORDER TO PARTICIPATE TO THE FINAL ASSESSMENT AND TO GAIN THE CREDITS CORRESPONDING TO THE COURSE, THE STUDENT MUST HAVE ATTENDED AT LEAST 70% OF THE HOURS OF ASSISTED TEACHING ACTIVITIES.

## Assessment methods

THE EXAM INCLUDES THE COMPLETION OF A GROUP PROJECT AND A THEORETICAL TEST. THE PROJECT IS AIMED AT DEMONSTRATING THE ABILITY TO CREATE AN IOT APPLICATION USING THE TOOLS PRESENTED IN THE COURSE. PROJECTS WILL BE CONSIDERED SUFFICIENT IF THEY INVOLVE THE INTERFACING OF SENSORS CONNECTED VIA THE I2C OR SPI PROTOCOL, CONTROL OF A DEVICE VIA PWM, AND COMMUNICATION TO THE OUTSIDE VIA MQTT. THE PROJECT DISCUSSION WILL AIM TO VERIFY THE CONTRIBUTION PROVIDED BY INDIVIDUAL GROUP MEMBERS IN ITS REALIZATION. THE STUDENT'S ABILITY TO EFFECTIVELY PRESENT THE CONTENTS AND CRITICALLY DISCUSS THE TOPICS PRESENTED WILL BE EVALUATED. THE PROJECT SCORE WILL BE ASSIGNED BASED ON THE COMPLETENESS OF THE SYSTEM CREATED, THE ARCHITECTURAL COMPLEXITY, AND THE QUANTITY AND TYPE OF SENSORS IMPLEMENTED, AS WELL AS THE CONTROL AND INTERACTION INTERFACES. THE TEST AIMS TO VERIFY THE UNDERSTANDING OF THEORETICAL TOPICS WITH PARTICULAR REFERENCE TO THE MQTT, I2C, AND SPI PROTOCOLS, AND THE USE OF PWM FOR CONTROLLING MICRO AND SERVO MOTORS. THE FINAL GRADE WILL BE CALCULATED AS A WEIGHTED AVERAGE OF THE PROJECT SCORE (60%) AND THE TEST ON THEORETICAL TOPICS (40%). EXCELLENCE WILL BE AWARDED TO STUDENTS WHO ACHIEVE THE MAXIMUM SCORE IN BOTH TESTS AND COMPLETE A PROJECT USING SENSORS NOT PRESENTED IN THE LESSONS. EXCELLENCE WILL BE ATTRIBUTED TO STUDENTS WHO WILL OBTAIN THE MAXIMUM SCORE IN BOTH TESTS AND THE REALIZATION OF A PROJECT WITH THE USE OF SENSORS NOT PRESENTED IN LESSON.
