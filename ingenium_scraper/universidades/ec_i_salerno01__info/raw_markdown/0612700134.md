## Learning objectives

THE COURSE AIMS TO DEVELOP THE THEORETICAL AND PRACTICAL SKILLS NECESSARY FOR IMPLEMENTING STRATEGIES FOR THE MANAGEMENT AND SECURITY OF A COMPUTER NETWORK. KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING: - UNDERSTANDING OF THE MAIN SECURITY ISSUES AFFECTING SYSTEMS AND NETWORKS. - KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING OF THE SECURITY PROTOCOLS AND STRATEGIES PROVIDED BY THE TCP/IP STACK - KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING OF SOFTWARE-DEFINED NETWORKS. - KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING OF TOOLS FOR ENSURING SYSTEM AND NETWORK SECURITY. - KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING OF PROTOCOLS AND TOOLS FOR NETWORK MANAGEMENT. - KNOWLEDGE OF NETWORK VIRTUALIZATION ENVIRONMENTS. APPLIED KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING: - CONFIGURE HOSTS AND NETWORK DEVICES APPROPRIATELY. - APPLY SYSTEM CONFIGURATIONS TO IMPROVE SECURITY. - USE NETWORK MANAGEMENT TOOLS. - USE VIRTUALIZATION ENVIRONMENTS TO VALIDATE NETWORK CONFIGURATIONS. - USE POSIX BASH FOR NETWORK HOST MANAGEMENT.

## Assessment methods

THE ACHIEVEMENT OF THE COURSE OBJECTIVES IS CERTIFIED BY PASSING AN EXAM GRADED ON A SCALE OF THIRTY. THE EXAM INCLUDES A PRESENTATION OF A PROJECT CARRIED OUT IN GROUPS OF TWO AND AN INDIVIDUAL THEORETICAL TEST. THE PROJECT PRESENTATION IS INTENDED TO DEMONSTRATE THE ABILITY TO APPLY KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING THROUGH THE IMPLEMENTATION OF A SMALL-SCALE NETWORK INFRASTRUCTURE IN A VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENT. THE THEORETICAL EXAM AIMS TO ASSESS THE LEVEL OF KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING OF THE TOPICS COVERED IN THE COURSE, AS WELL AS THE STUDENT’S ABILITY TO PRESENT INFORMATION. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THE FINAL GRADE, THE PROJECT EXAM (THROUGH THE PRESENTATION AND DISCUSSION OF THE COMPLETED PROJECT) ACCOUNTS FOR 40%, WHILE THE INDIVIDUAL THEORETICAL TEST ACCOUNTS FOR 60%. HONORS MAY BE AWARDED TO STUDENTS WHO DEMONSTRATE EXCELLENT ANALYTICAL AND DESIGN SKILLS, TAKING INTO ACCOUNT THE QUALITY OF THEIR PRESENTATION AND THEIR INDEPENDENCE OF JUDGMENT.
