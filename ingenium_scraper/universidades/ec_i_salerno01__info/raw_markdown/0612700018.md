## Learning objectives

THIS COURSE WILL FOCUS ON THE USE OF COMPUTER TECHNOLOGIES IN AUTOMATION AND CONTROL SYSTEMS. KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING • INDUSTRIAL LOGIC CONTROLLERS. • PROGRAMMING LANGUAGE FOR INDUSTRIAL LOGIC CONTROLLERS. • DESIGN OF SEQUENTIAL LOGIC CONTROL SYSTEMS. • PROCESS DATA ACQUISITION, MONITORING, AND SUPERVISORY CONTROL SYSTEMS. APPLYING KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING • SELECTION, SIZING, CONFIGURATION AND PROGRAMMING OF INDUSTRIAL LOGIC CONTROLLERS. • DEVELOPMENT OF PROGRAMS FOR INDUSTRIAL LOGIC CONTROLLERS. • DEVELOPMENT AND VALIDATION OF THE SEQUENTIAL LOGIC CONTROL CONTROLLERS BY MEANS OF SIMULATION AND EMULATION ENVIRONMENTS. • SELECTION, SIZING, CONFIGURATION OF SOFTWARE PLATFORMS FOR PROCESS DATA ACQUISITION, MONITORING, AND SEQUENTIAL LOGIC CONTROL. • SELECTION OF AUTOMATION SYSTEM FIELD BUSES AND NETWORKS.

## Prerequisites

PREREQUISITES: FOR THE SUCCESSFUL ACHIEVEMENT OF THE COURSE GOALS A BASIC KNOWLEDGE OF STANDARD REGULATORS AND DIGITAL CONTROL ALGORITHM IS REQUIRED; THIS KNOWLEDGE CAN BE ACQUIRED IN THE COURSE FONDAMENTI DI CONTROLLI AUTOMATICI. A BASIC KNOWLEDGE OF COMPUTER PROGRAMMING, COMPUTER NETWORKS AND OPERATING SYSTEMS IS ALSO REQUIRED. PROPEDEUTICAL COURSES: NONE.

## Teaching methods

LECTURES, CLASSROOM AND LABORATORY PRACTICE. IN CLASSROOM PRACTICE, PROBLEMS ARE ASSIGNED, SOLVED AND DISCUSSED. IN LABORATORY PRACTICE PROBLEMS ARE SOLVED USING OFF-THE-SHELF INDUSTRIAL CONTROL ARCHITECTURE, AND VALIDATED USING PROCESS EMULATORS.

## Other information

THE COURSE IS HELD IN ITALIAN.

## Assessment methods

THE FINAL EXAM IS DESIGNED TO EVALUATE AS A WHOLE: THE KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING OF THE CONCEPTS PRESENTED IN THE COURSE, THE ABILITY TO APPLY THAT KNOWLEDGE TO SOLVE PROBLEMS OF AUTOMATION SYSTEMS CONTROL, INDEPENDENCE OF JUDGMENT, COMMUNICATION SKILLS AND THE ABILITY TO LEARN. THE EXAM CONSISTS OF AN ORAL DISCUSSION OF PROJECT TO SOLVE A REAL PROBLEM ASSIGNED TO EACH STUDENT OR SMALL GROUP OF STUDENTS DURING THE COURSE AND A WRITTEN TEST, STRUCTURED IN THE DESIGN OF ALGORITHMS TO CONTROL SIMPLE AUTOMATION SYSTEMS AND IN QUESTIONS ABOUT COURSE TOPICS. THE ALGORITHMS MUST BE IMPLEMENTABLE ON A CONTROL DEVICE ACCORDING TO INDUSTRIAL STANDARDS. THE ANSWER TO THE QUESTIONS ABOUT COURSE TOPICS WILL BE EVALUATED TO ASSESS THE ACQUIRED KNOWLEDGE AND ABILITY TO UNDERSTANDING, THE ABILITY TO LEARN, AND THE QUALITY OF PRESENTATION IN WRITTEN FORM. ADDITIONALLY, AN INTERVIEW MIGHT BE REQUIRED IN CASE OF CONCERNS. THE FINAL EVALUATION, EXPRESSED IN THIRTIETHS, DEPENDS ON THE MATURITY GAINED ON THE COURSE CONTENT, TAKING INTO ACCOUNT THE QUALITY OF WRITTEN TEST AND THE MAKING JUDGMENT DEMONSTRATED. THE CUM LAUDE MAY BE GIVEN TO STUDENTS WHO DEMONSTRATE WHO DEMONSTRATE THAT THEY CAN APPLY THE KNOWLEDGE AUTONOMOUSLY EVEN IN CONTEXTS OTHER THAN THOSE PROPOSED IN THE COURSE AND IF THE CONTROL ALGORITHMS HAVE BEEN DESIGNED CORRECTLY WITH A CLEAR EXPOSITION OF THE USED METHODOLOGIES. FOLLOWING THE DECISIONS OF THE DIDACTIC BOARD, THERE MIGHT BE A MID-TERM TEST ACCOUNTING TOWARDS THE FINAL RESULT.
