## Learning objectives

THE COURSE AIMS TO INTRODUCE THE MAIN SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES FOR WEB APPLICATIONS. KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING METHODS AND TECHNOLOGIES FOR WEB APPLICATION DESIGN AND IMPLEMENTATION. MARKUP LANGUAGES AND STYLE SHEET LANGUAGES. SERVER-SIDE AND CLIENT-SIDE PROGRAMMING LANGUAGES. APPLYING KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING DESIGN AND DEVELOP STATIC AND/OR DYNAMIC WEBSITES BY SELECTING THE MOST SUITABLE TECHNOLOGIES. IMPLEMENT SOFTWARE COMPONENTS FOR WEBSITES AND WEB APPLICATIONS TO BE INTEGRATED ON BOTH THE CLIENT SIDE AND THE SERVER SIDE.

## Teaching methods

THE COURSE IS ORGANIZED IN LECTURES (24 HOURS), CLASSROOM PRACTICES (24 HOURS), AND LABORATORY ACTIVITIES (24 HOURS). DURING THE CLASSROOM PRACTICES, EXAMPLES HELPFUL FOR STUDENTS TO DESIGN AND DEVELOP DYNAMIC WEBSITES ARE PRESENTED AND DISCUSSED. DURING THE LABORATORY ACTIVITIES, STUDENTS ARE ASSIGNED TO HANDS-ON PROJECT WORK, WHICH REQUIRES THE DESIGN AND IMPLEMENTATION OF A DYNAMIC WEBSITE, SO THAT THEY CAN PUT INTO PRACTICE KNOWLEDGE AND SKILLS ACQUIRED DURING LECTURES AND PRACTICES. IN ORDER TO PARTAKE IN THE FINAL ASSESSMENT AND TO GAIN THE CREDITS CORRESPONDING TO THIS COURSE, THE STUDENT MUST HAVE ATTENDED AT LEAST 70% OF THE HOURS OF ASSISTED TEACHING ACTIVITIES. STUDENT’S PRESENCE IS RECORDED THROUGH THE EASYBADGE APPLICATION.

## Assessment methods

THE EXAM IS COMPOSED OF A PROJECT WORK AND AN ORAL EXAMINATION. THE PROJECT WORK EVALUATES THE STUDENT'S ABILITY TO DESIGN AND IMPLEMENT DYNAMIC WEBSITES. STUDENTS WILL BE DIVIDED INTO WORKING GROUPS, MADE UP OF AT LEAST THREE MEMBERS, AND THEY WILL HAVE TO DEVELOP A DYNAMIC WEBSITE ACCORDING TO THE PROJECT SPECIFICATIONS PROVIDED DURING THE LESSONS. MOREOVER, STUDENTS WILL HAVE TO WRITE A PROJECT REPORT THAT DESCRIBES THE ADOPTED DESIGN CHOICES. THE PROJECT WORKS WILL BE EVALUATED BY CONSIDERING THE COMPLIANCE WITH THE ASSIGNED SPECIFICATIONS AND THE CORRECTNESS OF THE DESIGN AND IMPLEMENTATION CHOICES. THE ORAL EXAMINATION ASSESSES THE KNOWLEDGE AND SKILLS ACQUIRED BY THE STUDENT. THE FIRST PART OF THE ORAL EXAM AIMS AT EVALUATING THE PERSONAL CONTRIBUTION OF THE STUDENT TO THE REALIZATION OF THE PROJECT WORK. THE SECOND PART OF THE ORAL EXAM AIMS AT EVALUATING THE STUDENT'S SKILLS ON ALL THE THEORETICAL TOPICS COVERED DURING THE COURSE. STUDENTS OBTAIN THE MAXIMUM GRADE WHEN THEY ARE ABLE TO DISCUSS AND MOTIVATE THE DESIGN AND IMPLEMENTATION CHOICES MADE DURING THE PROJECT WORK, AND THEY CORRECTLY DISCUSS THEORETICAL TOPICS.
