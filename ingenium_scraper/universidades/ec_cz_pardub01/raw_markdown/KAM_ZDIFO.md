# Digital Photography

- **Form of course completion**: Course-credit
- **Accredited / Credits**: Yes, 2 Cred.
- **Type of completion**: Combined
- **Time requirements**: Lecture 2 [HRS/WEEK]
- **Language of instruction**: English
- **Semester taught**: Summer semester
- **Optional course**: Yes
- **Prerequisite courses**: N/A

## Course objectives

The aim of the course is to acquaint students with the problems of digital photography from the perspective of creating photographs, editing in the computer and publication.

## Requirements on student

To obtain the credit it is necessary to pass a computer test.

## Content

1. Photography, introduction, history, personalities - Czech and world
2. Photo technique, construction and function of the camera, aperture, time, ISO
3. Exposure - metering, modes
4. Focusing - modes, depth of field
5. Composition - composition elements and rules
6. Perspective, light, white balance, color models, color management
7. Graphic formats, editing in PC
8. Lightroom
9. Still life, products, macro, advertising
10. Landscape, architecture, interiors
11. Sports, children, animals, weddings, concerts
12. Reportage, documentary, street photo, portrait
