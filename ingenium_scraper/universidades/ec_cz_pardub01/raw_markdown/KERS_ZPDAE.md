# Data Transmission

- **Form of course completion**: Examination
- **Accredited / Credits**: Yes, 4 Cred.
- **Type of completion**: Combined
- **Time requirements**: Lecture 2 [HRS/WEEK] Tutorial 2 [HRS/WEEK]
- **Language of instruction**: English
- **Semester taught**: Winter + Summer
- **Optional course**: Yes
- **Prerequisite courses**: N/A

## Course objectives

The course provides to students essential knowledge of data tranmission through transmission lines and associated linear distortion effects. The students should acquire the main principles in pulse modulation, source coding, channel coding, data modems and data transmission services.

## Requirements on student

Attendance at seminars, laboratory and PC excercises. Students solve two tests during semester. Course is finished by oral exam, student must respond to questions from predefined set of topics.

## Content

1.Fundamental terms - data, channel capacity
2.Information theory basics
3.Analog and digital communication systems, units
4.Channel propagation, data transmission
5.Signal classification, characteristics of signals mean value, energy, power
6.The relationship between time domain and frequency domain
7.Pulse modulation
8.Quantization and sampling of signal + PCM
9.Line coding + laboratory
10.Source coding
11.Channel coding I.
12.Channel coding II.

## Competences acquired

The students acquire theoretical and practise knowledge and skills of data tranmission through metallic transmission lines, data codding and pulse modulation.
