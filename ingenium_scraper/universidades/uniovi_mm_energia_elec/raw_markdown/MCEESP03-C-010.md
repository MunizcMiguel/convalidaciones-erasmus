## Contextualización

(Esta asignatura se imparte exclusivamente en inglés. Por lo tanto, toda la información de esta guía se encuentra en ese idioma.)

This subject is included in the Equalization Stage of the master, during which all the students must complete 30 ECTS credits depending on their previous background

The main target is making students acquire the skills and the knowledge necessary to develop Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs) for Power Electronics Applications. This includes knowing how to use an ECAD program, such as Altium, Eagle or Cadence, at different levels (capturing the schematic, designing components footprints, placing components and tracks in a convenient way, etc.). It also deals with the main considerations that have to be taken into account when designing a Power Electronics PCB (size of tracks and vias according to driven current, clearance for isolation, priorities when routing, EMI problems, etc.).

The secondary target is making students acquire the skills and the knowledge necessary to develop hardware descriptions by means of the VHDL language. These descriptions will be used then for programming FPGAs and CPLDs.

Teaching activities may be carried out in collaboration with universities from the Ingenium alliance, such as lectures, seminars, and/or joint work or projects. Such collaboration may involve specific adjustments to the methodology and/or assessment systems.

## Requisitos

(Esta asignatura se imparte exclusivamente en inglés. Por lo tanto, toda la información de esta guía se encuentra en ese idioma.)

Knowledge of electronic components (resistors, capacitors, integrated circuits, …) is important in order to understand some of the features of the software tools presented in this subject. Having these basic notions will allow students to develop correct schematics that will eventually give rise to good simulation results and properly-designed PCBs.

For the second target, basic notions of logical circuits and components (logic gates, registers, flip-flops, etc) are advisable.

## Contenidos

(Esta asignatura se imparte exclusivamente en inglés. Por lo tanto, toda la información de esta guía se encuentra en ese idioma.)

Subject is organized in the following chapters:

PCB design

1. Introduction to electronic CAD: software, design environment, etc.

4. Sequential Hardware Description

## Evaluación del aprendizaje

(Esta asignatura se imparte exclusivamente en inglés. Por lo tanto, toda la información de esta guía se encuentra en ese idioma.)

**Ordinary call**

Items

Practical Work (PCB)

This component will be assessed through an applied PCB design exercise carried out during a laboratory session. The evaluation will focus on the student’s ability to apply the knowledge and techniques practiced in previous sessions. The activity will take place one week after the completion of the topics related to PCB design.

2. VHDL Assignment

This assignment assesses the student’s ability to design and implement hardware descriptions using VHDL. Development of the assignment will begin once the VHDL topic has been completed.  
The submission and oral defense will coincide with the start of the official evaluation period and will be scheduled in agreement with the students.

Grading

To compute the final grade, students must achieve a minimum score of **4.0** in each assessable element.

If this condition is met, the final grade will be calculated as:

FINAL GRADE = (PCB + VHDL) / 2

If the minimum score of 4.0 is *not* achieved in any element, the grade for the examination session will be the lower value between the calculated grade and **4.0**.

Additional Considerations

If the teaching staff has doubts regarding the authorship of any assessable work or suspects the use of unauthorized tools (e.g., AI-based assistance), the student may be required to defend the work in person.

**Extraordinary call**

Items

Practical Work (PCB)

This component will be assessed through an applied PCB design exercise carried out during the official extraordinary call date. The evaluation will focus on the student’s ability to apply the knowledge and techniques practiced in previous sessions.

2. VHDL Assignment

This assignment assesses the student’s ability to design and implement hardware descriptions using VHDL. Development of the assignment will begin once the VHDL topic has been completed. The submission and oral defense will take place during the final exam.

Grading

To compute the final grade, students must achieve a minimum score of **4.0** in each assessable element.

If this condition is met, the final grade will be calculated as:

FINAL GRADE = (PCB + VHDL) / 2

If the minimum score of 4.0 is *not* achieved in any element, the grade for the examination session will be the lower value between the calculated grade and **4.0**.

Additional Considerations

If the teaching staff has doubts regarding the authorship of any assessable work or suspects the use of unauthorized tools (e.g., AI-based assistance), the student may be required to defend the work in person.

**Differentiated Assessment**

Efforts will be made to facilitate both the submission and defense of the FPGA assignment, as well as the completion of the practical PCB test. To this end, the use of telematic means will be allowed whenever possible.
