## Contextualización

**The Master’s degree:**

The main goal of the Master’s Degree in “Electrical Energy Conversion and Power Systems” (EECPS Master) is the training of qualified staff in areas related to electrical energy management, emphasizing in power systems for renewable energies. The Master presents a double approach: scientific and professional. In the scientific thread, training focuses on the design of two main applications: Electrical Power Systems and Electrical and Hybrid Traction Systems. On the other hand, in the professional thread, training is focused on the management of electrical energy. Thus, the subjects of this thread have been designed attending to two main issues, such as the management of energy in large consumers and the generation and transmission of electrical energy in a liberalized market. Three main lines have been considered as keystones in the Master:

- Electrical Power Systems
- Electrical and Hybrid Vehicles
- Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energies

**The first semester:**

The first semester is intended to provide a uniform level of knowledge among students with different basic training. This equalization term offers a set of optional courses designed to promote the homogenization among students' knowledge. The teaching committee will study every application form independently, selecting 27 ECTS credits for every student among the optional courses. The additional 3 ECTS are dedicated to a compulsory introductory subject, called “introduction to power systems, renewable energies, electrical traction and energy efficiency”.

**The subject:**

The subject is included in the first module of the master, called “Equalization”. The dynamic analysis of electric machines is the focus of this subject.

**Ingenium Alliance:**

Teaching activities, such as conferences, seminars or joint projects, could be developed in collaboration with universities in the Ingenium Alliance. Such collaboration may require specific adjustments in methodology and/or assessment.

## Contenidos

- Stationary abc coordinate equations of three phase systems.
- Representation of complex variables and three phase systems.
- Modeling and analysis of the induction machine in dq coordinates.
- Modeling and analysis of the synchronous machine in dq coordinates
- Model of the induction machine in rotor flux oriented reference frame
- Model of the permanent magnet machine in rotor reference frame

## Evaluación del aprendizaje

**Ordinary Call**

**Assessable Elements**

The ordinary assessment consists of the following evaluable elements:

- **Final Written Exam (EX):** A written exam conducted at the end of the teaching period. It evaluates all theoretical and practical contents of the course. **A minimum score of 4/10 is required.** If this minimum is not met, the **final grade for the ordinary call will be capped at 4.0**.
- **Homework / Laboratory Work (HW):** Includes all assignments and laboratory activities carried out throughout the course. **All homework/lab tasks must be submitted to pass the subject.** A minimum score of **4/10** is required. Deadlines will be announced appropriately. **Late submissions will generally not be accepted.**
- **Attendance and Active Participation (AT):** Attendance and engagement during lectures and class activities. No minimum score is required for this element.

**Grading**

If all minimum requirements are met, the final grade for the ordinary call is calculated as:

Final Grade = (0.40 × EX) + (0.50 × HW) + (0.10 × AT)

If any minimum requirement is not met, the grade for the ordinary call will be:

min( Final Grade , 4.0 )

**Considerations**

In case of doubt regarding the authorship of any evaluable activity or the use of unauthorised means (e.g. external assistance), the student may be required to complete an individual defence of the work.  
Additional considerations may be included by the teaching staff when necessary.

**Extraordinary Call**

The extraordinary call follows **the same structure and weighting** as the ordinary call.

All components (EX, Assignment, Practical Cases, Participation) contribute with the **same percentages** and the **same minimum requirements**, and the final grade is computed using the same formula.

**Differentiated Assessment**

Differentiated assessment will be granted in accordance with University regulations.  
Each case will be examined individually, and an alternative assessment plan will be proposed considering the specific circumstances, while ensuring compatibility with the subject’s learning outcomes.
