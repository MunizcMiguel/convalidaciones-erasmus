## Contextualización

The Master’s degree:

The main goal of the Master’s Degree in “Electrical Energy Conversion and Power Systems” (EECPS Master) is the training of qualified staff in areas related to electrical energy management, with emphasis on power systems for renewable energies. The Master presents a double approach: scientific and professional. In the scientific thread, training focuses on two main applications: Electrical Power Systems and Electrical and Hybrid Traction Systems. On the other hand, in the professional thread, training is focused on the management of electrical energy. Thus, the subjects of this thread have been designed aiming at two main objectives (goals), such as the management of energy in large consumers and the generation and transmission of electrical energy in a liberalized market. Three main lines have been considered as keystones in the Master:

- Electrical Power Systems
- Electrical and Hybrid Vehicles
- Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energies

The third semester: The third term has been designed according to two possible tracks: professional and research. The first one is focused on the acquisition of the required competences for the management of electric energy, with a special emphasis on energy efficiency and renewable energies. The second track approaches the technology development and the industrial design established in the specific competences of the master lines: "Power systems" and "Electric / Hybrid vehicle".

The subject: This subject is intended to provide the student the basic knowledge on the design and operation of HEV and EV, with special focus the power electronics and machine design and control issues. Auxiliary electric systems present in HEV and EV as well as communications issues will also be covered. Energy storing and simulation of HEV and EV will be covered in detail in other subjected, not being therefore key topics for this subject.

## Evaluación del aprendizaje

**Ordinary assessment**

**Assessment elements**

The ordinary assessment consists of the following elements:

**Final written exam (EX) (40%):**  
Written exam to be taken at the end of the semester. It will assess the theoretical and practical contents of the course. A minimum mark of 4 out of 10 is required. In case of not reaching this minimum, the student must take the corresponding exam in the official examination period.

**Project report (PR) (25%):**  
Individual assignment to be submitted before the final exam. It consists of a written report (approximately 8 pages) covering topics included in the course. A minimum mark of 4 out of 10 is required.

**Project presentation (OP) (15%):**  
Oral presentation of the assignment (approximately 15 minutes), where the student will present the main results and conclusions. A minimum mark of 4 out of 10 is required.

**Computer homework (HW) (10%):**  
Assignments consisting of the simulation of electromechanical systems control using Matlab or equivalent tools. No minimum mark is required.

**Participation and attendance (PA) (10%):**  
Attendance to lectures and active participation in class activities. No minimum mark is required.

**Grading**

If all minimum requirements are met, the final grade is calculated as:

Final grade=0.40⋅EX+0.25⋅PR+0.15⋅OP+0.10⋅HW+0.10⋅PA

In case the minimum mark is not achieved in any of the mandatory elements (EX, PR, OP), the final grade will be the minimum between 4.0 and the result of the above formula.

**Considerations and Differenciated evaluation**

In case of doubts regarding the authorship of any assessment activity or the use of unauthorized resources (e.g., AI tools), the student may be required to carry out an oral defense.

Assessment will be continuous and differentiated, taking into account the different learning outcomes and the individual characteristics of students. When necessary, adaptations will be implemented without altering the core learning objectives.

In cases of students with specific circumstances (e.g., employment or justified constraints affecting attendance), alternative assessment measures may be applied, such as adapted deadlines or alternative formats, while maintaining the same evaluation criteria.

As an exception, the “Participation and attendance (PA)” component will be adapted in such cases and will be assessed through participation in individual tutorials and interaction via official communication channels.

**Extraordinary assessment**

The extraordinary assessment consists of the same elements as the ordinary assesment.

The final grade will be calculated as in the section "Grading".
