## Contextualización

The Master’s degree:

The main goal of the Master’s Degree in “Electrical Energy Conversion and Power Systems” (EECPS Master) is the training of qualified staff in areas related to electrical energy management, with emphasis on power systems for renewable energies. The Master presents a double approach: scientific and professional. In the scientific thread, training focuses on two main applications: Electrical Power Systems and Electrical and Hybrid Traction Systems. On the other hand, in the professional thread, training is focused on the management of electrical energy. Thus, the subjects of this thread have been designed aiming at two main objectives (goals), such as the management of energy in large consumers and the generation and transmission of electrical energy in a liberalized market. Three main lines have been considered as keystones in the Master:

- Electrical Power Systems
- Electrical and Hybrid Vehicles
- Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energies

The first semester:

The first semester is intended to provide a uniform level of knowledge among students with different basic training. This equalization term offers a set of optional courses designed to promote the homogenization among students' knowledge. The teaching committee will study every application form independently, selecting 27 ECTS credits for every student among the optional courses. The additional 3 ECTS are dedicated to a compulsory introductory subject, called “introduction to power systems, renewable energies, electrical traction and energy efficiency”.

The subject:

This subject addresses the control of modern electric ac drives using induction machines and permanent magnet machines. The student will learn how to control ac machines using field orientation and direct torque control concepts, how to design flux observers as well as the possibilities of sensorless control methods. The operation and control of regenerative drives and power converters will also be covered.

The subject is included in the first module of the master, called “Equalization”

## Evaluación del aprendizaje

**Ordinary Call**

**Assessable Elements**

The ordinary assessment consists of the following evaluable elements:

- **Final Written Exam (EX): 40%** A written exam conducted at the end of the teaching period, covering all theoretical and applied course contents. **A minimum score of 4/10 is required to pass the subject.**
- **Final Assignment — 40% (25% documentation + 15% presentation)** The assignment comprises:
  - A **written report** of approximately **8 pages** (25%).
  - A **15‑minute oral presentation** (15%). The project may address topics from **any section of this course guide**. **A minimum score of 4/10 is required** in this component.
- **Computer Homework (HW): 10%** Homework consists of simulating electromechanical system control using **MATLAB** (or another simulation tool, if preferred and effective). No minimum score is required unless stated otherwise.

**Grading**

If all minimum requirements are met, the final grade for the ordinary call is calculated as:

Final Grade = (0.40 × EX) + (0.40 × Assignment) + (0.10 × HW)

Where the 0.40 for the assignment comes from:

Assignment = (0.25 × Documentation) + (0.15 × Presentation)

If any minimum requirement is **not** met, the final grade for the ordinary call will be:

min( Final Grade , 4.0 )

**Considerations**

In case of doubt regarding the authorship of any evaluable activity or the use of unauthorised means (e.g. external assistance), the student may be required to perform an individual defence of the work.  
The teaching staff may add further considerations when appropriate.

**Extraordinary Call**

The extraordinary call follows **the same structure and weighting** as the ordinary call.

All components (EX, Assignment, Practical Cases, Participation) contribute with the **same percentages** and the **same minimum requirements**, and the final grade is computed using the same formula.

**Differentiated Assessment**

Differentiated assessment will be provided in accordance with University regulations.  
Each request will be examined individually, and an alternative assessment plan will be proposed when justified, ensuring alignment with the learning outcomes of the subject.
