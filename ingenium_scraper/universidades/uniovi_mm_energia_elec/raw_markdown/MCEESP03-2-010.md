## Contextualización

The Master’s degree:

The main goal of the Master’s Degree in “Electrical Energy Conversion and Power Systems” (EECPS Master) is the training of qualified staff in areas related to electrical energy management, emphasizing in power systems for renewable energies. The Master presents a double approach: scientific and professional. In the scientific thread, training focuses on the design of two main applications: Electrical Power Systems and Electrical and Hybrid Traction Systems. On the other hand, in the professional thread, training is focused on the management of electrical energy. Thus, the subjects of this thread have been designed attending to two main issues, such as the management of energy in large consumers and the generation and transmission of electrical energy in a liberalized market. Three main lines have been considered as keystones in the Master:

- Electrical Power Systems
- Electrical and Hybrid Vehicles
- Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energies

This subject is located in the second year Master´s, third semester. The students are skilled in simulation, power topologies and so on. Also, they are also skilled in DSCs, how to develop software, and how to control medimum complex systems. The aim of this subject is to present the different storage energy systems, such as batteries, flywheels, etc

## Requisitos

It is desirable knowledge about power topologies, simulation, digital control devices, such as DSC, DSPs, Microcontrolers, and soon. Also, power electronics basis and electricity bases are required. In the same way, basic software knowledge as Mathcad, Mathlab, Excel, etc is a must

## Evaluación del aprendizaje

ORDINARY CALL:

To evaluate the subject, it will be necessary to attend at least to 90% of the classes. (5% of the final mark in every part)

Several tasks will be proposed (30%-60%)

A simple project, to be made in groups of three/four students will be proposed (20%-60%)

Optionally, the professors can propose an exam (up to 40%)

In order to success the subject, it will be necessary to obtain a minimum mark in every part of the subject: Batteries, Hydrogen, Flywheels, Energy managment and Resonant inverter design & Caps and super caps. This minimum will be 2,5 (over ten) : in this case, the final mark will be obtained as follows:

Final mark= (batteries + Flywheels + Hydrogen + Energy Managment+ 2\* Resonant inverter)/ 6, provided that all of the individual marks are > 2.5 (over ten)

The deadline for the reports: at the end of the subject.

For those students who have selected diferentiated evaluation or those students who will attend to extraordinary call, the assesments will be the same: the students should have to deliver the task reports and attend to the exams, just in case. As far as Resonant Inverter part is concerned, the students will be allowed to acces to the teaching labs, in order to carry out the project. The schedule will be agree with the responsible professor
