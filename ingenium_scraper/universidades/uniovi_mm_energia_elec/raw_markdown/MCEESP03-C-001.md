## Contextualización

This subject is shared among two Master Courses, the Master’s Degree in “Electrical Energy Conversion and Power Systems” (EECPS Master), from the University of Oviedo, and the Erasmus Mundus Master Course in Sustainable Transportation and Electrical Power Systems (EMMC STEPS), given at the University of Oviedo along with the University of Nottingham, the University of Rome – La Sapienza and the Polytechnic Institute of Coimbra. Most of the information is the same for both Master Courses, but in some cases there are slight differences that must be taken into account by the students, mainly in the evaluation procedure.

The Master’s course:

The main goal of both Master’s Degrees (EECPS Master and STEPS Master) is the training of qualified staff in areas related to electrical energy management, emphasizing in power systems for renewable energies. The Master presents a double approach: scientific and professional. In the scientific thread, training focuses on the design of two main applications: Electrical Power Systems and Electrical and Hybrid Traction Systems. On the other hand, in the professional thread, training is focused on the management of electrical energy. Thus, the subjects of this thread have been designed attending to two main issues, such as the management of energy in large consumers and the generation and transmission of electrical energy in a liberalized market. Three main lines have been considered as keystones in the Master:

- Electrical Power Systems
- Electrical and Hybrid Vehicles
- Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energies

The first semester:

The first semester is intended to provide a uniform level of knowledge among students with different basic training. This equalization term offers a set of optional courses designed to promote the homogenization among students' knowledge. The teaching committee will study every application form independently, selecting 27 ECTS credits for every student among the optional courses. The additional 3 ECTS are dedicated to this introductory subject, called “introduction to power systems, renewable energies, electrical traction and energy efficiency”.

The subject:

The aim of the first subject is to motivate the new students, giving then a global vision of the Masters’ program contents. The subject is included in the first module of the master, called “Leveling/equalization stage”. Students will devote the first two weeks to attend an Introductory course held at the University of Oviedo. The aim of this course is to introduce the main objectives of the program, making a brief review of all the contents they are going to study and also to orient the student and make sure they chose their appropriate strand. External professors belonging to companies in the field of Electrical Power Systems, Electric and Hybrid Electric vehicles and Energy Efficiency will give seminars about the state of the art of the involved technologies. Besides, the Student Mentoring Programme will be presented in collaboration with professors of the universities involved in the Master’s Degree Course.

Besides, students will have the opportunity to know each other.

Teaching activities may be carried out in collaboration with universities of the INGENIUM alliance, such as lectures, seminars and/or joint assignments or projects. Such collaboration may entail specific adjustments to the methodology and/or assessment systems.

Ahead are the competences and learning results of the subject:

Basic Competences:

- Be original in the development and application of ideas, within a research environment.
- Solution of problem in new and unfamiliar multidisciplinary environments, related to its knowledge area.
- Integration of knowledge, facing the complexity of issuing judgments and sentences parting from some information that includes ethic and social liability constraints.
- Ability of communicating justified decisions and conclusions, to specialized and unspecialized listeners.
- Ability of autonomous learning.

Generic Competences:

- Write, interpret scientifically and communicate orally documents in the field of the master's master's degree's main lines (electric power systems, electric and hybrid vehicles, energy efficiency and renewable energies), to specialised audiences.
- Know the main multidisciplinary scientific journals focused on the master's degree's main lines, as well as the main public and private R+D+i institutions relevant to these lines.
- Teamwork skills, recognising the different roles within a group and the different forms of organisation of work teams.
- Ability to manage information: search, analysis and synthesis.
- Ability to assimilate and communicate technical and economic information written in English.
- Ability to plan and organise their work.

- Skills of initiative, entrepreneurship, concern for quality and motivation for achievement.

Specific Competences:

- Knowledge and ability to explain and argue the importance of power systems in the management of electrical energy.
- Understanding and ability to explain and argue the technological foundation upon which sit the power system.
- Identification of the different applications of power systems in the three trend topics of the master: power systems, electric / hybrid vehicle and energy efficiency.
- Understand the energy pricing process, understanding the different components of the final price and pricing of electricity.

## Evaluación del aprendizaje

This section describes the criteria, instruments and procedures through which student learning will be assessed in the subject Introduction to Renewable Energy Power Systems, Electrical Traction and Energy Efficiency (MCEESP03-C-001).

**Ordinary Examination Period**

**Assessable elements**

The ordinary evaluation consists of the following assessable elements:

- **Individual technical report (TR):** Work to be carried out throughout the course, consisting of an individual technical report on a topic chosen from those proposed by the teaching staff (maximum 2 students per topic). The report must follow the master's standard template (IEEE conference template), be between 4 and 6 pages in length, include clearly specified references and sources of information, and be uploaded to the virtual campus by the established deadline. Optionally, students may receive feedback (first revision) with an initial grade; if the mandatory/suggested changes are implemented and a final version is uploaded before the later established deadline, the grade will be revised. Late submissions will not generally be accepted. No minimum grade is required for this element.
- **Attendance at in-person sessions (AT):** Student participation and attendance at course sessions will be assessed. For this element to contribute positively to the final grade, students must have attended at least 80% of the sessions. No additional minimum grade applies.

**Grading**

The final grade for the ordinary examination period is calculated as:

**Final grade = 0.9 × TR + 0.1 × AT**

where AT takes the value 10 when attendance is equal to or greater than 80%, and 0 otherwise.

Furthermore, as a prerequisite for passing the subject, students must satisfactorily complete certain specific tasks related to basic software environments. These tasks will be communicated by the teaching staff in due course.

**Considerations**

Students enrolled on a part-time basis will have access to a specific differentiated assessment procedure. Under this scheme, the attendance grade (AT) will not be taken into account, and the final grade will be calculated proportionally to the remaining assessable elements:

**Final grade (part-time) = TR**

Additionally, in justified cases of non-attendance on the original date of any assessment, alternative dates may be arranged.

The use of references in the report is intended to support the student's own ideas, not to directly copy information. Copying someone else's information without proper referencing constitutes plagiarism and may result in an automatic fail of the subject, even if detected at the optional first revision stage.

Teaching activities may be carried out in collaboration with universities of the INGENIUM alliance (through lectures, seminars and/or joint assignments or projects). Such collaboration may entail specific adjustments to the methodology and/or assessment systems.

If the teaching staff has any doubts regarding the authorship of any assessable item or the use of unauthorised means (e.g. artificial intelligence tools), the student may be asked to provide an in-person defence.

**Extraordinary Examination Periods**

The extraordinary examination period consists of the submission of an individual technical report (TRE) covering the content of the subject. The report must meet the same formal requirements as those established for the ordinary period: master's standard IEEE conference template, between 4 and 6 pages, with clearly specified references and sources of information.

The grade obtained in the extraordinary examination period corresponds directly to the grade awarded for the submitted report:

**Final grade = TRE**

**Considerations**

If the teaching staff has any doubts regarding the authorship of any assessable item or the use of unauthorised means (e.g. artificial intelligence tools), the student may be asked to provide an in-person defence.

Teaching activities may be carried out in collaboration with universities of the INGENIUM alliance (through lectures, seminars and/or joint assignments or projects). Such collaboration may entail specific adjustments to the methodology and/or assessment systems.

The format and submission conditions for the report will be the same as those established for the ordinary examination period. The submission deadline will be the official date established for the extraordinary examination period.
