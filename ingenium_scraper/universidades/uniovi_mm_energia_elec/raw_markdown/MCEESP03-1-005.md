## Contextualización

**The Master’s course:**  
The main goal of the Master’s Degree in “Electrical Energy Conversion and Power Systems” (EECPS Master) is the training of qualified staff in areas related to electrical energy management, emphasizing in power systems for renewable energies. The Master presents a double approach: scientific and professional. In the scientific thread, training focuses on the design of two main applications: Electrical Power Systems and Electrical and Hybrid Traction Systems. On the other hand, in the professional thread, training is focused on the management of electrical energy. Thus, the subjects of this thread have been designed attending to two main issues, such as the management of energy in large consumers and the generation and transmission of electrical energy in a liberalized market. Three main lines have been considered as keystones in the Master:  
· Electrical Power Systems  
· Electrical and Hybrid Vehicles  
· Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energies  
  
**The second semester:**  
The second term offers several compulsory courses for all the students. These subjects will promote the acquisition of the common skills of the Master. This term includes a subject called "Lab", designed to develop and build a functional experimental prototype based on the theoretical knowledge acquired during the first two semesters. The work done in this subject will serve as a starting point for the Master’s Thesis.  
  
**The subject:**  
The basic aim is to integrate previous knowledge to develop a project of some importance, learn how to deal with the specific instrumentation material of a power systems laboratory, understand safety issues when handling electrical equipment and develop learning skills and independent work.

## Requisitos

The students must certify that they have passed basic skills and competencies in power electronics, power plants, electric machines, and control systems, and automation. This can be either accomplished at his/her incoming student profile and CV or, if not covered there, by passing the related subjects of the first semester.

## Evaluación del aprendizaje

**Ordinary assessment**

**Assessment elements**

The ordinary assessment consists of the following elements:

**Projects (PR):**  
Students will complete the proposed projects throughout the course. These projects assess the acquisition and application of the course contents. This is a mandatory element.

**Oral presentation (OP):**  
Students will present their developed projects orally, either individually or in groups. The presentation will include explanation of the work carried out, results obtained, and conclusions. This is a mandatory element.

**Real / Simulated task performance tests (ST):**  
Students will carry out simulations and an implementation of an experimental rig (and prototype) related to the course contents. These activities assess the practical implementation of knowledge and are considered mandatory.

**Observation techniques (PA):**  
Attendance to presential sessions and active participation in class activities will be evaluated. A minimum attendance of 50% is required.

| Evaluation systems | Proposed percentage |
| --- | --- |
| Oral tests (individual, group, presentation of topics/projects, etc.) | **30%** |
| Works or projects | **50%** |
| Observation Techniques (logs, checklists, etc.) | **10%** |
| Real / Simulated Task Performance Tests | **10%** |

**Grading**

The final student’s qualification will be obtained as follows.

- The 50% of the student’s mark comes from the assessment of the proposed projects. It is a mandatory proof.
- Another 10% will come from the simulations developed by the student, considered as a simulated task performance test. It is a mandatory proof.
- A 30% will come from oral presentation of the developed project by the students. It is a mandatory proof.
- Finally, the 10% left comes from the assistance to the presential hours (a minimum of 50% is required).

If the minimum attendance requirement is met and all mandatory elements (PR, OP, ST) are completed, the final grade is calculated as:

Final grade=0.50⋅PR+0.30⋅OP+0.10⋅ST+0.10⋅PA

In case the minimum attendance requirement is not fulfilled or any mandatory element is not completed, the final grade will be the minimum between 4.0 and the result of the above formula.

**Differentiated Assessment:**  
Assessment will be continuous and differentiated, taking into account the different learning outcomes and the individual characteristics of students. A variety of assessment instruments will be used to ensure a comprehensive evaluation of knowledge, skills, and competences. When necessary, adaptations will be implemented to address diverse learning needs, such as adjustments in assessment formats (oral/written), extended time, or alternative evidence of achievement, without altering the core learning objectives.

Additionally, real-life circumstances affecting students’ participation will be considered. For instance, in the case of students who are employed or have other justified constraints impacting attendance or performance, flexible assessment measures may be applied, such as adapted deadlines, alternative tasks, or adjusted assessment conditions, **while maintaining the same evaluation criteria and level of achievement required for all students.**

As an exception, the “Observation Techniques” component will be adapted in such cases and will be assessed based on the student’s participation through individual tutorials and engagement via official communication channels.

Each assessment instrument will be aligned with the corresponding learning outcomes to ensure a fair, equitable, and individualized evaluation process.

**Extraordinary assessment**

The extraordinary assessment will consist of the submission of the required projects (PR), an oral presentation (OP), participation along the course (PA) and/or additional tasks or simulations (ST), as determined by the teaching staff, covering all course contents.

The final grade will be calculated proportionally according to the weights defined for the ordinary assessment.
