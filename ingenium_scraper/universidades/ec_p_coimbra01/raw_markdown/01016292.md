# Introdução às Comunicações

- **Code:** 01016292
- **Year:** 3
- **Academic year:** 2025-2026
- **Language of Instruction:** Portuguese
- **Mode of Delivery:** Face-to-face
- **Duration:** SEMESTRIAL
- **ECTS Credits:** 6.0
- **Type:** Elective
- **Level:** 1st Cycle Studies
- **Subject Area:** Telecommunications

## Recommended Prerequisites

Signals and Systems.

## Teaching Methods

Lectures with presentation and discussion of the course topics using audiovisual resources complemented with the resolution of illustrative exercises. Laboratory classes for a complement of the lectures, by using some educational kits and software tools that will help the students. Preferentially industrial partners will be invited to present the case study.

## Learning Outcomes

The objective of this curricular unit is to provide the basic training in communication systems and networks, allowing the students to understand the available technology and the methods to overcome its limitations.

## Work Placement(s)

No

## Syllabus

1- Introduction to communications systems

2- Transmission signals and transmission media

3- Sampling and pulse modulation

4- Analog to digital conversion

5- Baseband digital transmission principles

6- Passband digital transmission principles

7- A case study.
