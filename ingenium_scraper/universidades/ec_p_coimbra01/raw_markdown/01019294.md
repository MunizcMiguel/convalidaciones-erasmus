## Recommended Prerequisites

Not applicable.

## Teaching Methods

Lectures with working group for the development of transversal skills and seminars for presentation of main areas of electrical and computer engineering.

## Learning Outcomes

This course aims to provide students with information on the various specific areas in which electrotechnical and computer engineering broken down and their associated career opportunities.

It is also intended to provide students with some transversal skills relevant to their evaluation along academic path.

## Work Placement(s)

No

## Syllabus

Introduction to the main areas of Electrical and Computer Engineering intervation;

Bibliographic research methods and information analysis in science and technology;

Group work management;

Communication in science and technology (Reporting).
