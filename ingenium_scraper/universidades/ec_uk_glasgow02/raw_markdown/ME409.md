## Educational Aim

The individual projects aim to expose the students to real engineering/research problems so that they can
independently apply their knowledge and intellectual ability to solve them. This module provides an opportunity for the
students to demonstrate their engineering skills and knowledge, critical thinking, technical writing and presentation
abilities, maturity and independence at a level expected from a near-graduate level student.

## Learning Outcomes

On completion of the module the student is expected to be able to:
LO1 Analyse and evaluate research in the field of Mechanical Engineering over an extended period.
LO2 Make a technical contribution to the field of study and research associated with the project
LO3 Developed skills in technical writing to an appropriate standard and in the correct style
LO4 Communicate and discuss the evolution of the project, its aims, outcomes and conclusions with a panel of
fellow professionals

## Syllabus

The student will carry out appropriate research and scholarship to meet the learning outcomes.

## Principles of Assessment and Feedback

(within Assessment and Feedback Policy – search for ‘Academic policies & procedures’ at: https://www.strath.ac.uk/)
Help clarify what good performance is.
Regular feedback will be obtained through meetings with the supervisor, to give clear guidance about performance
and progress. This will tend to be informal in nature, but students should recognise this as feedback and be
encouraged to reflect on it.
Encourage 'time and effort' on challenging learning tasks.
Regular meetings with the supervisor will encourage the student to work steadily over the whole project to achieve
a high quality end result. The first oral exam will give two assessors an opportunity to give feedback on the quantity
and quality of work.
Ensure that summative assessment has a positive impact on learning.
Students will be shown the assessment criteria in advance and given guidance on the numerical marking scheme.
This will allow them to focus effort on achieving good learning outcomes with the aim of achieving a high mark.
Facilitate the development of self-assessment and reflection in training
Students will be encouraged to reflect on their progress throughout the project and plan their work effectively to
meet the deadline. A written project plan will be required at the first oral exam to facilitate time management and
focus in the second semester.
Encourage positive motivational beliefs and self-esteem
Completion of an individual project can be highly motivating and a source of satisfaction for the students.

## Coursework / Submissions deadlines (academic weeks):

Form detailing the project title, aims at objectives to be submitted in advance of the oral exams.
First oral November. Progress brief, Gantt chart and logbook to be submitted ahead of oral exams week.
Second oral March - technical notes and logbook to be submitted in advance of oral exams week.
Thesis to be submitted in April (first week of exam diet).
SUBMISSION DEADLINES WILL BE STRICTLY APPLIED. LATE SUBMISSION OF REPORTS/PAPERS OR NON-
ATTENDENCE AT ORAL WILL BE RECORDED AS “ABSENT” AND ZERO MARKS AWARDED.
Any personal circumstances that may cause late submission should be recorded and processed according to
standard University procedures.
The mark for the Technical Notes is conditional on attendance at the second oral examination. Students who
do not attend the second oral examination will receive a mark of 0 for the Technical Notes.

## Resit Assessment Procedures:

In exceptional circumstances, further ^^work over the summer may be permitted, followed by submission of a revised
technical paper/dissertation (prior to commencement of the July/August exam diet) and oral examination. This will be
evaluated and assessed on a case by case.
For students registered as Academic Assessment Only: Standard assessment will apply.
For students who are Reattending or Registered With Attendance: Standard assessment will apply.
^^Students must contact the Module Registrar for details as soon as results confirm that a resit is required.
PLEASE NOTE:
Students need to gain a summative mark of 40% to pass the module. In addition, students must perform at an
adequate standard in the second Oral exam to pass the module overall. Students who fail the module at the
first attempt will be re-assessed during the July/August exam diet. This re-assessment will consist of project
resubmission and oral exam.
