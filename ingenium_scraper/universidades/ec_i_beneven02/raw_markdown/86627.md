## Contents

Data acquisition systems: • Data acquisition system architectures; • Electronic circuits for analog signal processing; • Conditioning circuits; • A/D converter characteristics; • Standard for the metrological characterization of A/D converters; • Microcontrollers for the implementation of data acquisition systems; Signal generators: • Function generations; • Arbitrary waveform generators based on D/A converters; • Standard for the metrological characterization of D/A converters. Biomedical instrumentation: • Biopotentials; • Conditioning circuits for the acquisition of biopotential signal; • Electronic instrumentation for the acquisition of electromyogram (EMG), electrocardiogram (ECG), and electroencephalogram (EEG) signals. Measurement systems based on the Internet of Things (IoT) paradigm: • General architecture of an IoT system; • Digital communication interfaces for smart transducers. Every theoretical part is followed by several activities carried out in the laboratory. The laboratory activities are listed below: • Data acquisition systems: design, implementation, and testing of a data acquisition system; • Biomedical instrumentation: design, implementation, and testing of an electrocardiograph; • Measurement systems based on the Internet of Things (IoT) paradigm: design, implementation, and testing of a distributed measurement system.

## Learning objectives

The course aims at providing tools and methods for the metrological analysis, prototyping, and testing of data acquisition systems. In particular, the course includes laboratory activities for the prototyping and testing of conditioning circuits and data acquisition systems in several application fields, from the biomedical up to distributed measurement systems based on the Internet of Things (IoT) paradigm.

## Prerequisites

## Assessment methods

The final exam is oral and includes a discussion on the laboratory activities and the verification of the theoretical aspects. The evaluation will be attributed for 1/2 on the basis of the discussion regarding the laboratory activities and for 1/2 on the basis of the clarity and completeness in the exposition of the theoretical topics.
