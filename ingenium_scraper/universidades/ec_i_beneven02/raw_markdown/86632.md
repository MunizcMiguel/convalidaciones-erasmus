### Contents

Module 1 - SENSORS Course Content: The course is divided into two main parts: Sensors (36 hours): This section covers the physical principles, types, and applications of sensors. Smart Sensors (12 hours): This section introduces the concepts of smart sensors, advanced processing, and future perspectives. Part 1: Sensors This part of the course will cover: - Primary principles and variables - Characteristics and terminology - Physical principles - Optical components - Occupancy and motion detectors - Position, displacement, and level - Velocity and acceleration - Force, strain, and tactile sensors - Pressure - Flow - Acoustic sensors - Humidity - Light detectors - Temperature - Chemical sensors Part 2: Smart Sensors This part of the course will cover: - Structure, definitions, and concepts - Advanced processing and sensing techniques - Smart sensors - The future of smart sensor systems Module 2 - SENSORS LABORATORY Course Content: This module will cover: - Key characteristic parameters of sensors: transfer function, sensitivity, uncertainty, and hysteresis. - Laboratory exercises on tools and methods for processing and analyzing data from conventional sensors. - Basic electronic instrumentation for data acquisition and the metrological characterization of sensors. - Lab exercises on conventional sensors (environmental, mechanical, and optical). This includes the experimental evaluation and extraction of characteristic parameters: transfer function, sensitivity, repeatability, and hysteresis. - Fiber optic sensors. - IoT platforms: An introduction to the IoT and its basic components (sensors, data processing boards, and actuators). This includes using Arduino as a platform for creating IoT systems and interfacing with analog and digital sensors using acquisition boards. - Lab exercises with Arduino data acquisition boards. This involves acquiring signals from analog and digital sensors, displaying data on an LCD screen, and activating visual/acoustic alarms when predefined thresholds are exceeded. - Electronic circuits for conditioning sensor output signals. - The design, implementation, and performance evaluation of an electronic system comprised of a sensor, an acquisition board, and an actuator.

### Learning objectives

Module 1 - SENSORS Learning Objectives: To provide an in-depth understanding of the physical principles, characteristics, and applications of sensors, with a particular focus on smart sensor systems. Students will acquire the theoretical and practical skills needed to design, select, and integrate sensors into real-world applications (e.g., IoT, automation, environmental monitoring). Module 2 - SENSORS LABORATORY Learning Objectives: The aim of this course is to provide the fundamental knowledge and techniques necessary for the design and implementation of sensor-based electronic systems used in industrial plants and the Internet of Things (IoT). The course includes hands-on laboratory exercises focused on: Sensor characterization. Designing and implementing electronic systems (sensor - acquisition board - actuator) for industrial and IoT applications. Evaluating the performance of developed systems by identifying appropriate figures of merit. Upon completion of the course, students will be able to: Use electronic instrumentation to acquire sensor output signals. Interface with various types of sensors. Analyze and interpret data provided by sensors. Address the challenges of designing and implementing electronic systems. Understand the differences between expected and actual behavior.
