# WIRELESS NETWORKS (86636)

## General information

- **Degree programme:** ELECTRONICS AND BIOMEDICAL ENGINEERING (866)
- **Curriculum:** PIANO ELETTRONICA - ELE
- **Year of study:** 3

- **Type of activity:** COMPULSORY SUBJECTS, CHARACTERISTIC OF THE CLASS

- **Attendance:** Not mandatory

## Contents

Communication Channels and Capacity Free space. Attenuation and distortion. Multipath channel. Empirical models of communication channels. Fading and its characterization. Channel capacity. Digital modulation Modulator and demodulator. ASK, PSK, and FSK schemes. Transmission on an AWGN channel. Transmission on a fading channel. Channel coding. Infrastructure-based wireless networks Wireless network architecture. WiFi and WLAN. IEEE 802 protocols. Cellular networks. Dynamic resource allocation. Ad Hoc wireless networks Access protocols. Routing. Topology control: connectivity, coverage, distributed algorithms, energy efficiency.

## Reference texts

Andrea Goldsmith, Wireless Communications, Cambridge University Press, 2005. Carlo Fischione, An Introduction to Wireless Sensor Networks, Open Access KTH, 2014

## Learning objectives

Knowledge of the main transmission techniques and wireless connectivity models. Knowledge of the characteristics of networking algorithms, with particular attention to energy efficiency and the design of wireless networks for the Internet of Things (IoT).

## Prerequisites

Probability, random variables, signals.

## Teaching methods

Classwork lectures

## Assessment methods

Oral exam with questions on the topics covered in class to assess the student's level of knowledge.
