## Integrated course (common part) / Partition: 8854-1 / Partition: 8854-2

### Contents

Complex numbers: Polar form; geometric representation of complex numbers; trigonometric form; calculus with complex numbers; powers and roots of complex numbers. Complex functions depending on complex variable: Topology; functions depending on complex variable; complex analytic functions; Cauchy-Riemann equations; harmonic functions; series of functions; complex power series; theorems of convergence; power series on the boundary of the convergence set; differentiation; holomorphic functions and power series; Taylor series. Complex functions: Limit; derivative; exponential function; Logarithm; trigonometric and hyperbolic functions; general powers. Line integral in the complex plane; basic properties; Cauchys integral theorem; Cauchys integral formula; consequences; properties of analytic functions; zeros of analytic functions; identitys principle for analytic functions; properties; Laurent series; analyticity at infinity; singularities; theorems; Residues; residues at infinity; how to calculate residues; examples; the residue theorem; applications of Cauchys integral theorem and residues theory; The Jordans Theorems; some integrals; Fresnels integrals; Integrals of the rational functions; Eulers function Gamma(z) and properties. Laplace transformation: Laplace transform; convergence; examples; exponential functions transform; constant functions transform; circular functions transform; hyperbolic functions transform; step functions transform; power functions transform; properties and formulas; periodic functions; further applications; differentiation and integration of transforms; convolution; inverse transform; applications to the differential equations. Fourier series: periodic functions of period T; Fourier coefficients; Fourier series; convergence; even and odd functions; exponential Fourier series; L^2 space; orthogonal and orthonormal systems. Fourier transform: Fourier transform of L^1 functions; examples; inverse transform; properties and fundamental formulas. PDEs: Elliptic, parabolic, hyperbolic equations; Laplace and Poisson equations; heat flow; vibrating string; wave equation; separation of variables; problems with initial and boundary data.

### Reference texts

Complex analysis : an introduction to the theory of analytic functions of one complex variable Author: Lars V Ahlfors; Material of study is furnished during the course

### Learning objectives

The main aim in this course is to furnish tools of real and complex analysis with applications to the calculus of particular integrals, and applications to the solving of ordinary differential equations, and of PDE's. Examples and applications will be given by using MATLAB

### Prerequisites

Exam of Mathematics
