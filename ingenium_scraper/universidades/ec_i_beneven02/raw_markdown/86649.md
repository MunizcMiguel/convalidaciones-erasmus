## Contents

The detailed teaching programme includes the following topics: Introduce students to the use of satellite data (explaining their main characteristics and the differences between radar and optical data based on acquisition sensors), processing techniques and tools. Basic techniques and advanced techniques for image processing will be presented. Google Earth Engine (GEE) will be introduced as an online satellite data catalogue. Some basic codes will be introduced (ie. Java script) to access and filter raster and vector data directly from the GEE data catalogue, along with ways to view it and download processed products. Some outline will be given of the use of Python for image processing. The main focus is on application cases of interest in Remote Sensing, but the objective is to generalize the use of processing tools for images that are not necessarily remote sensing (eg biomedical images), discussing their most important characteristics and differences. Some preliminary concepts of Machine Learning and Deep Learning and their application to image processing will be introduced.

## Learning objectives

The aim of the course is to provide students with the tools for image processing with classical and advanced techniques. The main focus is on Remote Sensing images, but among the objectives is the generalization of the use of processing tools to images that are not necessarily remote sensing (eg, biomedical images), discussing their most important characteristics and differences. The student will further develop the ability to work as a team, to make efficient presentations, also developing his communication skills.

## Prerequisites

For the purposes of taking the final profit examination, the student must have previously passed the following examinations: Mathematics, Geometry and Algebra

## Teaching methods

Consistent with the overall teaching hours, teaching activity is approximately divided as follows: - Lectures (approximately ’80%); - Self-study activities, with hours reserved for group work and individual student study. Slides will be used to present the main course topics, as well as reference sites for data and processing techniques. Scientific articles for particular Case Studies may be distributed. The course also includes hours of interactive teaching carried out through: - educational interventions aimed at the entire class or its subgroup in the form of presentations and operational suggestions on how to solve a problem; - support from course tutors and technology tutors (experts on some of the topics), as per the CdS teaching guidelines. - brief interventions by the students (e.g. through presentations of their specific activities to be discussed with the rest of the class). Teaching does not include mandatory forms of attendance, but participation in teaching activities is strongly recommended. Online, access to shared material (code, GitHub pages, slides) will be guaranteed, which is also accessible to working and non-attending students. Students with valid certifications for DSA, disability or other educational needs are invited to contact the teacher or disability contact person of the CdS/Department at the beginning of the course, in order to agree on any teaching methods that, in compliance with the objectives of the teaching, take into account individual learning needs.

## Other information

We hope to forge engineers who are experts in the use of tools useful for image processing and capable of generalizing what they have learned. Contact the teacher for further information not included in the teaching sheet.

## Assessment methods

Students will have to prepare reports (final and during the course) summarizing the activities carried out in processing the data on the assigned case studies. Report development activities and the application part will be discussed in class, but carried out by each student (or groups of up to three people), not in class. The reports will then be presented orally in the class. The final grade is based on the assessment of the work done and the ability to present it orally.
