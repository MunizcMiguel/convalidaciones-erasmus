# MATHEMATICS (803003)

- **Degree programme:** ELECTRONICS AND BIOMEDICAL ENGINEERING (803)
- **Curriculum:** PIANO STATUTARIO

- **Area:** MATHEMATICS, INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY AND STATISTICS

- **Grading:** Voto Finale
- **Attendance:** Not mandatory
- **Teaching methods:** Lezione

## Contents

Elements of set theory, real and complex numbers, combinatorial calculus. Limits of real sequences. One variable real functions: limits of real functions, differential calculus for real functions of a real variable, applications of differential calculus. Integrals of real functions of a real variable. Numerical series. Power Series. Real functions of more real variables Differential calculus for real functions of more real variables. Curves and curvilinear integrals. Differential forms. Double integrals. Ordinary differential equations.

## Reference texts

Analisi Matematica Uno -- P. Marcellini - C. Sbordone. Analisi Matematica Due -- N. Fusco - P. Marcellini - C. Sbordone. Elementi di Analisi Matematica Uno -- P. Marcellini, C. Sbordone. Elementi di Analisi Matematica Due -- N. Fusco - P. Marcellini - C. Sbordone. Esercitazioni di Matematica, (Vol. 1, Parte I e II; Vol. 2, Parte I e II) -- P. Marcellini- C. Sbordone.

## Learning objectives

The main aim of the course is to furnish the basic notions of Mathematical Analysis necessary for physical and engineering applications

## Prerequisites

Elementary functions, equations and inequalities, trigonometry, conicals.

## Teaching methods

Lectures related to the theoretical topics of the course. Lectures on the exercises and on the solution of practical problems. Students with certified SLD, disabilities, or special educational needs should contact the professor and department coordinator early in the course to agree on suitable teaching accommodations that align with learning objectives.

## Assessment methods

The exam aims to check knowledge and skills acquired with respect to the objectives of the course. It consists of: a) a written exam (2 hours) consisting of exercises concerning the main topics of the course b) an optional oral test, concerning the theoretical topics of the course. In addition, two midterm examinations are planned, replacing the comprehensive written test. The evaluation will take into account: the expository clarity, the understanding of the topics covered and the mastery of scientific language. The final grade, expressed out of thirty, will be composed on the basis of the degree of achievement of the previous requirements of the written and / or oral exam. Students with certified SLD, disabilities, or special educational needs should contact the professor and department coordinator early in the course to arrange suitable exam accommodations.
