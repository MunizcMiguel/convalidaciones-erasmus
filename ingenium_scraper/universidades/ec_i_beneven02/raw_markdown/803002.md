## Contents

Notions of trigonometry and analytic geometry: equations of lines, circumferences, ellipses, parabolas and hyperbole. Definitions and properties of the trigonometric functions. Vectors. Vector spaces: fields and vector spaces. Vector subspaces and affine subspaces. Linear independence, generators, bases. Linear functions and matrices: operations between matrices. Change of basis formulas. Kernel and image of a linear function. Theorem of the dimension. Properties of the determinant of a matrix. Eigenvalues and eigenvectors. Characteristic polynomial. Scalar product and norms: quadratic forms and associated matrices. Positive definite quadratic forms. Orthogonal vectors and matrices. Projections. Scalar product in Rn: basic properties and Cauchy-Schwarz inequality. Euclidean norm, basic properties and triangular inequalities. Systems of linear equations: matrix formulation and solutions. Rank and Rouché-Capelli theorem. Different methods for solving a linear system.

The main aim of the course is to furnish the basic notions of Linear Algebra and Euclidean Geometry

## Teaching methods

Lectures related to the theoretical topics of the course. Lectures on the exercises and on the solution of practical problems. Students with certified SLD, disabilities, or special educational needs should contact the professor and department coordinator early in the course to agree on suitable teaching accommodations that align with learning objectives.

## Assessment methods

The exam aims to check knowledge and skills acquired with respect to the objectives of the course. It consists of: a) a written exam (2 hours) consisting of exercises concerning the main topics of the course b) an optional oral test, concerning the theoretical topics of the course. The evaluation will take into account: the expository clarity, the understanding of the topics covered and the mastery of scientific language. The final grade, expressed out of thirty, will be composed on the basis of the degree of achievement of the previous requirements of the written and / or oral exam. Students with certified SLD, disabilities, or special educational needs should contact the professor and department coordinator early in the course to arrange suitable exam accommodations.
