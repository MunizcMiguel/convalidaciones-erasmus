## Contents

Physical properties of fluids, dimensional analysis, fundamentals of fluid mechanics, mixing and diffusion, particle separation, CFD modeling.

## Reference texts

Nguyen N-T. e Wereley S. T., Fundamentals and Applications of Microfluidics, Artech House ed., 2019

## Learning objectives

The goal of the course is to provide the fundamentals of microfluidics. In particular, students will acquire knowledge in fluid mechanics as well as skills related to the development of computer simulations aimed at modeling fluid-mechanical systems.

## Prerequisites

Knowledge from Mathematics and Physics courses is required. In particular, a basic understanding of differential and integral calculus, as well as vector calculus, is necessary.

## Teaching methods

Lectures are in person, and classroom exercises are carried out covering all topics addressed in the course. Students with certified SLD, disabilities, or special educational needs should contact the professor and department coordinator early in the course to agree on suitable teaching accommodations that align with learning objectives.
