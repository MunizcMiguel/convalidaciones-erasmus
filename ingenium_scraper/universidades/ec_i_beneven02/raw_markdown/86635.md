## Contents

The 6-CFU course syllabus covers the following topics: Introduction to IoT systems; defintions and implementation technologies. Basic knowledges on guided and wireless EM waves. Review of wire and planar antennas. Review of main parameters of transmitting and receiving antennas. Link budget for antennas. Miniaturized and on-chip antennas. Systems for Radio-Frequency IDentification (RFID): main aspects, applications in LF, HF and UHF bands, review of tags and readers. Main IoT technologies: Bluetooth, WiFi, Zig-Bee, Z-Wave, UWB. Wireless power transfer ed energy harvesting. Weareble antennas and “on-body” communications. Lab experiences: design of dipolar and planar antennas by using Matlab toolbox. Antenna measurement. Programming of a simple IoT system .

## Reference texts

Main textbooks: - Ulaby, Electromagnetics For Engineers, Pearson College Div. - Balanis, Antenna Theory: Analysis and Design, Wiley. - Hall, Antennas and Propagation for Body-Centric Wireless Communications, Artech House - Slides and class notes. - Manuals of software and instrumentation.

## Learning objectives

The aim of the course is to give basical knowledges of electromagnetic wireless devices and the main functionalities and tools to design IoT systems. At the end of the course the student will be able to identify the main tools for transmitting and receving information in an IoT system .

## Prerequisites

No further prior knowledge is required. However, basic knowledge of Mathematics, Basic Physics, Programming is helpful.
