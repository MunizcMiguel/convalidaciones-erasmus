## Goals

- **C1**: To introduce students to the chemical, electrochemical, and physical principles underlying lithium-ion battery operation.
- **C2**: To provide students with fundamental knowledge of lithium-ion cell structure, materials, performance metrics, and main battery chemistries.
- **C3**: To develop students’ understanding of key battery phenomena, including charge transfer, losses, efficiency, thermal behaviour, ageing, and degradation.
- **C4**: To familiarize students with lithium-ion battery production processes, characterization methods, testing procedures, and industrial standards.
- **C5**: To introduce students to the fundamentals of lithium-ion battery modelling and to show how models support battery analysis, design, and performance prediction.
- **C6**: To familiarize students with the role of battery modelling in simulation, performance prediction, diagnostics, control, and battery management system design.

## Program content ensuring the achievement of the learning outcomes prescribed to the module

The course covers the chemical, electrochemical, electrical, thermal, and industrial foundations of lithium-ion batteries. It includes cell materials and structure, operating parameters, ageing and degradation, production processes, characterization and testing methods, safety requirements, basic battery modelling, and applications in electric vehicles and battery energy storage systems. Tutorials support the course through calculation-based examples and problem solving.

## Prerequisites and additional requirements

Basic knowledge of mathematics, general physics, and electrical circuits at undergraduate engineering level. Students should be able to perform simple engineering calculations, use units correctly, rearrange formulas, and interpret basic graphs. Prior knowledge of electrochemistry is not required. Basic high-school chemistry (moles, concentration, redox reactions, oxidation states) is recommended.
