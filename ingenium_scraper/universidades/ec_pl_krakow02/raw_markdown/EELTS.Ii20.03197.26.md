## Goals

- **C1**: The aim of the course is to provide student with basic knowledge about electromagnetic disturbances occurring in power networks. The student should understand the physical and technical essence of phenomena, sources of disturbances, methods of their assessment, technical effects (from the perspective of the energy supplier and consumer), methods of their elimination, applicable regulations and standadrs. He should know how to assess the quality of energy supply at the highlighted point of the power grid. Should obtain knowledge on the cooperation of distributed (including renewable) energy sources with the power system.

## Program content ensuring the achievement of the learning outcomes prescribed to the module

1. Introduction to the issues of electromagnetic compatibility, electromagnetic disturbance classification, basic definitions, standardization, basic assessment of the power quality 2. Classification of voltage RMS disturbances, slow voltage changes (sources, method of numerical description and measurement principles, effects, voltage stabilization methods); quick voltage changes 3. Voltage fluctuations - sources, effects, method of numerical description and measurement principles, ways of flicker eliminating, location of flicker sources, propagation of flicker in the power system, summation of voltage fluctuations 4. Voltage and current distortion - power theories in circuits with distorted waveforms, sources, effects, method of numerical description and measurement principles, methods of elimination, source location, harmonic propagation in the power system, passive and active filtration, interharmonics 5. Impact of power electronic converters on the power supply network, power electronic interfaces of distributed energy sources and consumers 6. Voltage dips - sources, effects, method of numerical description and measurement principles, methods of elimination, location of sources, propagation in the power system, DVR system, contract for the power supply 7. Asymmetry of voltages and currents - sources, effects, method of numerical description and measurement principles, methods of elimination, location of sources, propagation of asymmetry in the power system 8. Capacitor as an element of the power system - reactive power compensation and filtration systems 9. High frequency disturbances in power electronics systems 10. Distributed energy sources and voltage quality 11. Energy quality and the electricity market 12. Uninterruptible power supply systems, energy storage
