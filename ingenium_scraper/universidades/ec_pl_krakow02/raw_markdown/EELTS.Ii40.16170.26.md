## Goals

- **C1**: The aim of the course is to acquaint students of the first degree of electrical engineering using the finite element method for modeling and analysis of electromechanical energy converters. During the laboratory classes, the ANSYS Maxwell 2D/3D package will be used to model and analyze all phenomena related to electromagnetism, which the students have learned at earlier stages of their studies. During the course, students will learn the principles of MES modelling, creating object geometry, defining materials, defining boundary conditions, grid generations and various types of analysis possible in ANSYS software. The examples used for analysis in the laboratory classes will be based on electromechanical energy converters known to students in the Electromechanical Energy Conversion subject. (4th semester of first degree studies).

## Program content ensuring the achievement of the learning outcomes prescribed to the module

The aim of the course is to acquaint students of the first degree of electrical engineering using the finite element method for modeling and analysis of electromechanical energy converters. During the laboratory classes, the ANSYS Maxwell 2D/3D package will be used to model and analyze all phenomena related to electromagnetism, which the students have learned at earlier stages of their studies. During the course, students will learn the principles of MES modelling, creating object geometry, defining materials, defining boundary conditions, grid generations and various types of analysis possible in ANSYS software. ## Program content

| No. | Program content | Course's learning outcomes | Activities |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. | 1) Theoretical fundamentals of the Finite Element Method (2h) 2) Model building principles in the Ansys Maxwell 2D package (1h) 3) Magnetostatic analysis in XY coordinates system, parametric analysis, interpretation of results (1h) 4) Magnetostatic analysis in a cylindrical coordinates system, 3D model generation (1h) 5) Transient type analysis, defining moving elements of the model, defining AC currents and voltages (2h) 6) Modeling of eddy current phenomenon (1h) 7) Modelling the external power supply circuit in the Maxwell Circuit module (1h) 8) Model building rules in Ansys Maxwell 3D package (2h) 9) Modelling of an extensive power supply system in Simplorer module (1h) 10) Modeling of rotating machines in the RMxprt module (2h) | W1, W2, U1, U2, U3, U4, K1, K2 | Lectures |
| 2. | 1) Creating 2D object geometry in XY and axial-symmetric coordinates systems, boundary conditions defining, forcing defining, post-processing results 2) Choke operation simulation using magnetostatic analysis in the XY coordinates system, parametric simulation, analysis of results 3) Analysis of the operation of an electromagnetic actuator modeled in an axisymmetric system, parametric simulation, analysis of results, automatic 3D model generation 4) Modeling and transient analysis of the electromagnet, post-processing and presentation of the results of dynamic state analysis 5) Creating the geometry of 3D objects, defining the size and generating the mesh, creating animations of moving models 6) Model of a simple generator with permanent magnets, magnetostatic analysis, parametric simulation, results analysis 7) Simulation of dynamic states of the 3D model of the reluctance motor, creation of motor operation animations 8) Modeling of eddy currents in the transformer core, determination of core losses 9) Coupled analysis of the model with an external power supply circuit created in the Maxwell Circuit and Simplorer modules 10) Model of the PMSM machine in the RMxprt module 11) Optimization of the PMSM machine model in the RMxprt module 12) Project completion | W1, W2, U1, U2, U3, U4, K1, K2 | Laboratory classes |

### Conditions and the manner of completing each form of classes, including the rules of making retakes, as well as the conditions for admission to the exam

In order to obtain a positive final grade, it is necessary to obtain a positive grade from laboratory exercises and pass a test. The condition to pass the laboratory exercises is to be present at all the exercises and to pass the final project evaluation. A failed project may be submited on the date indicated by the teacher.

### Method of determining the final grade

To obtain a positive final grade, it is necessary to obtain a positive grade from the computer laboratory (Ol), and pass the test from the content presented in the lecture (Ot). The final grade (Ok) complies with the study regulations and is determined on the basis of weighted average grades: Ol, Ot, Ok = 0,7• Ol + 0,3• Ot.

### Manner and mode of making up for the backlog caused by a student justified absence from classes

The condition of passing the calsses is the presence at all laboratory exercises, in the absence of the exercise should be done in an additional period organized by the teacher at the end of the semester.

## Prerequisites and additional requirements

Basic knowledge of the theory of electric machines and principles of electromechanical energy conversion, computer skills. These requirements are met by all students who completed the modules during their studies: Fundamentals of Computer Science, Electromechanical Energy Conversion, Electric Machines. These subjects are compulsory for students of the 4th and 5th semesters of electrical engineering, modules A and B.

### Rules of participation in given classes, indicating whether student presence at the lecture is obligatory

Lecture: – Mandatory presence: No – The rules of participation in classes: Students participate in classes learning the next teaching content in accordance with the subject syllabus. Students should keep asking questions and clarifying doubts. Audiovisual recording of the lecture requires the consent of the lecturer.

Laboratory exercises: – Mandatory presence: Yes – The rules of participation in classes: Students perform laboratory exercises according to the materials provided by the teacher. Completion of classes is based on the presentation of results obtained in the project implemented in subgroups. Completion of the module is possible after passing all laboratory classes.
