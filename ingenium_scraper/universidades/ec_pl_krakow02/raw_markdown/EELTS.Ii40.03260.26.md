### Conditions and the manner of completing each form of classes, including the rules of making retakes, as well as the conditions for admission to the exam

The condition of obtaining credit within the module is an active participation in the work of the Scientific Circle under the care of the Circle Supervisor or another academic employee of the Faculty or an active participation in other scientific research conducted at the Faculty in writing, in an hourly enough time to obtain the number of ECTS points of the module. Obtaining the above-rated credit is influenced by the presentation of partial or final results of the project or the effects of other own work (carried out as part of the scientific research carried out at the Faculty), in the form of a report, scientific publication or during a scientific conference (e.g. the annual Conference of AGH Student Circles).

### Method of determining the final grade

Active participation in the preparation of the selected project - not less than 3.0. Active participation in the preparation of the selected project and the partial report - not less than 3.5. Active participation in the preparation of the selected project and the partial and final report - not less than 4.0. Active participation in the work on the preparation of the selected project, the partial and final report and presentation of the results - not less than 4.5. Active participation in the preparation of the selected project and presentation of results during a scientific conference or in a journal scoring by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education - 5.0.

### Manner and mode of making up for the backlog caused by a student justified absence from classes

Activities within the Scientific Circle are not associated with the formal requirement to participate in regular classes that take place at fixed times, but require systematic work on the project or participation in other studies, accounted for the results obtained and partial and / or final reports.

## Prerequisites and additional requirements

Participation in the work of the Scientific Circle is possible after submitting a declaration of participation and confirmation of taking care of the project implemented by the student (group of students), by the Supervisor of the Circle or another employee of the Faculty. In the case of participation in the implementation of research work carried out at the Faculty, it is necessary to confirm the student's initial commitment to participate in the study, signed by an independent researcher.

### Rules of participation in given classes, indicating whether student presence at the lecture is obligatory

The student should actively participate in the implementation of the project (individual or group). The main assessed elements are: ability to pose a problem and determine the methods of solving it; independence of performing project-related works, ability to prepare a report and multimedia presentation of the completed project.
