### Method of determining the final grade

A test verifying the students' knowledge of the topics presented in the lectures will be performed at the last lecture. It is necessary to obtain positive Laboratory Grade (LG) and Lecture Test Grade (LTG). Weighted average (WAV) of both grades is calculated: WAV = 0.7 LG + 0.3 LTG. The final grade (FG) will be calculated as follows: if WAV>4.75 then FG:=5.0 else if WAV>4.25 then FG:=4.5 else if WAV>3.75 then FG:=4.0 else if WAV>3.25 then FG:=3.5 else FG:=3. If the positive grades LG and LTG were obtained in the first term and the calculated FG is less than 5.0, FG will be increased of 1/2, if the student has participated in at least 75% of lectures.
