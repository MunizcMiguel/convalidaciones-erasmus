# Problem Based Learning

## Content, progress and pedagogy of the module

### Learning objectives

#### Knowledge

- Define problem-based learning (PBL) based on learning theory and relate to own experiences.
- Identify different types of problems and projects and relate them to own practice.
- Describe knowledge production in problem-based project work and relate it to philosophy of science.
- Explain how digital and analogue technologies support learning and collaboration.

#### Skills

- Plan and manage problem-based project work.
- Analyse and evaluate own study practices, including the use of digital and analogue technologies to enhance individual and collaborative learning processes.
- Identify, analyse, formulate and assess the relevance of complex problems from a systems perspective.
- Critically evaluate problem solving using technology assessment with particular focus on sustainability and ethical considerations.

#### Competences

- Develop study practices adapted to a problem-based and project-organised learning environment.
- Evaluate and critically assess scientific practices regarding problem analysis and solution.
- Evaluate, create and adapt theories, methods and technologies (digital/analogue) to ensure the best possible application in learning situations and collaborative processes.
- Assess own progression in understanding and applying PBL principles based on own experiences and learning theory.
- Critically reflect on the role and impact (short- and long-term) of digital technologies in your own learning and collaboration processes.
