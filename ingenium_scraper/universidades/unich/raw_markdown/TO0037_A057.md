## Module: ANALYSIS OF BIOMEDICAL SIGNALS (TO0038)

### Language of instruction

Italian

### Contents

1. Biomedical Signals 2. Techniques for the acquisition of biomedical signals 2. Types of biomedical signals 3. Techniques for the processing of biomedical signals 4. Spectral and decomposition analyses 5. PCA-ICA 6. FIR-IIR filters

### Reference texts

Practical Guide for Biomedical Signals Analysis Using Machine Learning Techniques: A MATLAB® Based Approach. Abdulhamit Subasi. Elsevier Academic Press R.M. Rangayyan, Biomedical signal analysis, IEEE Press, 2002;

Handouts and materials provided by the teacher on the e-learning platform.

### Learning objectives

The course provides the theoretical and methodological foundations for analyzing biomedical signals in the time, frequency, and time-frequency domains, with particular emphasis on:

- physiological signal acquisition and digitization;
- filtering and preprocessing;
- spectral analysis;
- feature extraction for diagnosis and monitoring;
- an introduction to the analysis of non-stationary signals.

Upon completion of the course, students will be able to:

- model a discrete biomedical signal;
- apply digital transforms and filters;
- interpret results from a physiological perspective;
- implement simple algorithms in MATLAB or Python.

### Prerequisites

Knowledge of elements of physiology, analysis and physics. Knowledge of rudiments of Matlab coding

### Teaching methods

The program will be developed through lectures and exercises, and project works

### Other information

All the techniques presented in the course will see practical application to real biological signals. For this purpose, the characteristics of the following biomedical signals will be presented: - ECG; - EEG; - EMG; - EGG; - fNIRS; - PPG. For each type of signal, the physiological conditions will be highlighted and the processing techniques normally used in the clinic or in the research field will be introduced. Honors are awarded to those who pass the written test by correctly answering all the questions. The test must be completed in 120 minutes. ### Extended programme

**Module 1 – Review and Fundamentals (6 hours)**

• Classification of Biomedical Signals (Deterministic/Stochastic, Stationary/Nonstationary)

• Continuous and Discrete Signals

• Sampling and the Nyquist-Shannon Theorem

• Quantization and Noise

Applications: ECG, EMG, EEG, PPG, fNIRS

**Module 2 – Time Domain Analysis (8****hours)**

• Statistical Parameters: Mean, Variance, RMS

• Autocorrelation and Cross-Correlation

• Convolution

• Impulse Response of LTI Systems

Examples: Analysis of RR variability in the ECG.

**Module 3 – Frequency Analysis (10 hours)**

• Fourier Series and Transform

• DFT and FFT

• Power Spectral Density (PSD)

• Physiological Spectral Interpretation

Applications:

• EEG Bands (delta, theta, alpha, beta, gamma)

• EMG Spectrum

• Respiratory Analysis

**Module 4 – Digital Filtering (8 hours)**

• FIR and IIR Filters

• Low-pass, high-pass, and notch filter design

• Network Noise Removal (50 Hz)

• Adaptive Filtering (basic information)

Practical Example: ECG Signal Cleaning

**Module 5 – Time-Frequency Analysis (8 hours)**

• STFT and Spectrogram

• Introduction to Wavelets

• Non-stationary Signals

• Applications to Dynamic EEG and EMG

**Module 6 – Feature Extraction and Introduction to Pattern Recognition (8 hours)**

• Feature Extraction in Time and Frequency

• Dimensionality Reduction (PCA)

• Introduction to Supervised Classification (basics)

• Connection to Medical Devices and AI

**Laboratory Activities (12 hours)**

MATLAB Exercises:

• Sampling Simulation

• FFT and PSD Calculation

• Digital Filter Design

• Spectrogram Analysis
