## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Evaluation Methods

Student learning is assessed through an oral exam, consisting of a presentation of the main concepts related to Fiber Optic Communication Networks. For students with disabilities or specific learning disabilities (SLD), who have submitted a request for support to take the specific exam at the University Disability/SLD Info Point, the exam procedures will be adapted in accordance with the University guidelines: https://www.univpm.it/Entra/Elenco_siti_tematici_1/Disabilita_e_DSA_Servizio_di_accoglienza

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Evaluation Criteria

To successfully pass the exam, students must demonstrate a thorough understanding of the fundamental concepts of the course. The learning assessment takes into account the knowledge acquired on all course topics covered during the lectures. Evaluation will be based on the following indicators: completeness, presentation, and relevance. The exam as a whole assesses knowledge and understanding, the ability to apply acquired skills, presentation skills, and the ability to learn and develop independent judgment. The highest score is awarded to students who demonstrate in-depth knowledge of the course content, methodological rigor, and appropriate technical vocabulary.

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Measurement Criteria

Student learning is assessed via an oral exam. The final grade is awarded on a scale of 30; the exam is considered passed if the grade is 18 or higher.

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Final Mark Allocation Criteria

For a successful overall assessment, students must earn at least 18 points (out of 30). Honors may be awarded to students who achieve the maximum grade of 30/30 and demonstrate the ability to independently apply the knowledge and skills acquired, even in contexts other than those covered in class. To this end, the quality of the presentation (use of appropriate language), the ability to correlate the various course topics with each other and with other disciplines, and the overall demonstrated independent judgment will be taken into account.

## LEARNING OUTCOMES - Knowledge and Understanding

The course provides the basic knowledge needed to understand the operation of optical telecommunications networks. It explores aspects relating to terminals, nodes, and transport and access infrastructures, with a focus on emerging technologies.

## LEARNING OUTCOMES - Capacity to apply Knowledge and Understanding

Students will be able to recognize the main characteristics of an optical network and solve simple problems related to its sizing. They will be able to perform simple calculations related to quality of service. They will acquire the skills to identify the most suitable techniques and materials to guarantee a certain service.

## LEARNING OUTCOMES - Transversal Skills

The student will be required to acquire skills regarding: - independent judgment (the ability to choose between different technological solutions), - communication skills (the use of appropriate technical language when presenting), - learning skills (the ability to keep up to date with emerging standards and technologies).
