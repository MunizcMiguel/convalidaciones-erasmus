72 hours of frontal lessons. The teaching is active on the University's Moodle platform

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Evaluation Methods

The assessment of the learning level is based on participation in a 30-question closed-ended quiz via the University LEARN portal. For students with disabilities/invalidities or specific learning disorders (DSA), who have submitted a request for support to take the specific exam at the Disability/DSA Info Point of the University, the exam methods will be adapted in light of what is recommended by the University guidelines.

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Evaluation Criteria

The evaluation is based on verifying knowledge of the notions and concepts presented in the course. Passing the test requires demonstrating that you have at least sufficient knowledge of the program topics.

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Measurement Criteria

The test is given a mark out of thirty (for each correct answer 1 point is assigned, for each incorrect answer 0 points). The minimum grade needed to pass the test is 18/30.

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Final Mark Allocation Criteria

The awarding of honors is reserved for those who have achieved the maximum mark and who have shown a superior level of depth in any interviews to be agreed with the teacher.

## LEARNING OUTCOMES - Knowledge and Understanding

The teaching aims to make known and understood the principles of electromechanical energy conversion, the operating principles, circuit modeling and the salient characteristics of static and rotating electrical machines, fundamental electrical drives, electrical energy storage technologies , the devices and electrical networks used for energy generation and electric mobility.

## LEARNING OUTCOMES - Capacity to apply Knowledge and Understanding

The student must be able to analyze in practice the electrical circuits relating to electric machines for the generation of electrical energy and for electric mobility. This ability will be expressed through a series of professional skills, such as: 1) the ability to use electrical engineering tools to understand the functioning of electrical machines; 2) the ability to model, select and size electrical machines for power generation applications. 'electrical energy 3) ability to select the type of electric drive in various application scenarios; 4) the ability to size the electrical energy storage system adequate in various application scenarios 5) the ability to analyze and size electrical devices and networks for electric mobility.

## LEARNING OUTCOMES - Transversal Skills

In general, the student will also develop the following transversal skills: ability to use adequate mathematical tools for practical purposes, ability to analyze machines and drives relating to application contexts of interest, ability to analyze problems inherent to energy generation and the creation of systems complexes for electric mobility. Carrying out unguided sizing and design exercises within a set time frame will help improve the degree of self-assessment and the ability to manage the solution to a concrete problem with time limits.

## Contents

References to Electrotechnics and Electromagnetism in matter. Single-phase and three-phase networks. Low voltage electrical networks, power lines, electrical devices, electrical safety. Magnetic circuits, variable reluctance sensors, electromechanical gripper. Single-phase and three-phase magnetic transformer. Rotating electrical machines: asynchronous, synchronous, direct current. Electrical systems for the conversion, transport and management of energy, including renewable energy.
