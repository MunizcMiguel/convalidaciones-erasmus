# Electric power engineering

- ECTS: 3
- Plan semester: 4 (summer term)

## Course description

Classification of power plants. Basic parameters characterizing the operation of these facilities. Non-renewable energy sources: conventional thermal power plants, nuclear power plants. Renewable energy sources of various types: OWF, WF, biofuels, hydroelectric power plants, solar panels, photovoltaics. Operation of power distribution networks. Testing the operational properties of a power system model. Testing emergency conditions in a power system. BD, 11-09-2025
