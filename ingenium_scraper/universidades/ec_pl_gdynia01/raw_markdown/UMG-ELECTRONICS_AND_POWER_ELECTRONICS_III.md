# Electronics and Power Electronics III

- ECTS: 2
- Plan semester: 4 (summer term)

## Course description

Laboratory exercises – thyristors; working conditions of power components; triacs; power transistors; power converters; rectifiers; safety arrangements of thyristor and transistor systems, active and passive power.
