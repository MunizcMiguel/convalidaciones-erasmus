# Materials engineering II

- ECTS: 1
- Plan semester: 2 (summer term)

## Course description

Measurement of magnetic losses and their components in magnetic materials. Measurements of dielectric constant and tg. Measurements of dielectric strength. Measurements of volume and surface resistivity of dielectric materials.
