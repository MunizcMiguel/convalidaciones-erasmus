# Marine Communication Equipment

- ECTS: 2
- Plan semester: 5 (fall term)

## Course description

Radionavigation, communications and technical systems. GMDSS. Emergency. Antennas. Radio and telephone communications on ship. Automatic telephone operator. EPIRB. Transceiver. Satellite communications terminals. Signals.
