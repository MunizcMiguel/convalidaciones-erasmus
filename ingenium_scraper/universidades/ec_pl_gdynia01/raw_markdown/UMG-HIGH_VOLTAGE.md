# High Voltage

- ECTS: 2
- Plan semester: 3 (fall term)

## Course description

Technic and Engineering. Ionization and deionization processes. Nature and forming of electric stresses, insulation configurations. Break-down strength of gases, solid and liquid dielectrics, discharge development, partial discharges. Exploitation insulation systems. Inner and outer over voltage characteristic, atmospheric discharges, overvoltage and lighting protection.
