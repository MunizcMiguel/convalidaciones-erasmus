# Materials engineering I

- ECTS: 1
- Plan semester: 1 (fall term)

## Course description

Conductivity, dielectric constant, magnetic permeability. Classification of electrical materials. Conducting materials. Conductivity of metals. Semiconducting materials. Semiconductors. Dielectric materials. Dielectric materials polarization. Dielectric constant. Dielectric strength. Durability of dielectric materials. Combustibility. Classification and applications of dielecrtric materials. Magnetic materials. Diamagnetic, paramagnetic and ferromagnetic.
