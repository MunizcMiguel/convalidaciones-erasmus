# Ship Electrical Power Generation and Distribution

- ECTS: 2
- Plan semester: 5 (fall term)

## Course description

Marine electrical power plants. Shaft generators. Synchronization and work in parallel of ship’s generators. Ship voltage regulators. Protection of ship generators. Emergency power plants. Systems of electrical energy transmission and distribution on ships. Functions and structure of ship switchboards. Ship cables. Power management systems on ships. Main electric propulsion. 2
