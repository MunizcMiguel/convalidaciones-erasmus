# Fundamentals of electrotechnics II

- ECTS: 6
- Plan semester: 3 (fall term)

## Course description

Three phases circuits. Non sinusoidal voltage and current waveforms, harmonic analyses, Dirichlet‘s conditions, Fourier series, Pearseval‘s theorem, The transient in D.C and A.C circuits, Laplace transform, inverse Laplace transforms, a solution of differential equations (operational method). Transmission lines, basic phenomena and quantities, phase delay, wavelength, velocity of propagation, current and voltage relationships
