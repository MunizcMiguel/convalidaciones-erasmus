# Digital Circuits Theory and Technology I

- ECTS: 3
- Plan semester: 1 (fall term)

## Course description

Numeration systems and codes. Theory of digital circuits. Basic digital circuits. Digital signals and gates, logical symbols. Analysis and synthesis of combinational circuits and sequential circuits. Realization of combinational and sequential circuits. Functional blocks of the average scale of integration. Synthesis of a typical synchronous and asynchronous circuits.
