# Digital Circuits Theory and Technology II

- ECTS: 2
- Plan semester: 2 (summer term)

## Course description

Synthesis of combinational circuits, characteristics of digital circuits. Arithmetical blocks. Bistable flip-flops. Synthesis of sequential circuits. Counters and registers of integrate scale. Circuits of time dependences. Circuits of steering a stepper motor, an anchor lift. Steering the crane model with programmable circuits CPLD or FPGA. Computer simulation of advanced digital circuits.
