# Ship Electrical Drives Control Systems II

- ECTS: 3
- Plan semester: 5 (fall term)

## Course description

Equations of motion. Energy storing. Machinery characteristics – parameters, schemes, equations, power distribution, starting, braking, control. Drive system with DC and AC machine. DC and AC motor automatic control systems. Marine and industrial applications. Ship diesel-electric propulsion systems.
