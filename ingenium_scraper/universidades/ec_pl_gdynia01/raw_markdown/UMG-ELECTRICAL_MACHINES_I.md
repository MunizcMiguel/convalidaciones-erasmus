# Electrical Machines I

- ECTS: 2
- Plan semester: 3 (fall term)

## Course description

Introduction to electric machines theory; basic principles and concepts, design elements, construction materials, definitions and classification. DC and AC machines; structure, principle of operation, basic characteristics, exploitation properties. Transformers; structure, basis of operation, 3-phase transformers, exploitation properties.
