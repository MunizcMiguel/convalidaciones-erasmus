# Metrology II

- ECTS: 2
- Plan semester: 3 (fall term)

## Course description

Temperature sensors, differential photodetector, analog and programmable transducers, converters, 4-20mA standard, HART transmitters, measurement channels and their application for selected non-electrical quantities measurements, diagnostics of the measurement channels.
