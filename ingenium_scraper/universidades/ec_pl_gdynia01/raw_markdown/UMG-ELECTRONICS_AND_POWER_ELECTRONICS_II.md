# Electronics and Power Electronics II

- ECTS: 4
- Plan semester: 3 (fall term)

## Course description

Working conditions of power components. Thyristors. Triacs. Power transistors. Power converters. Rectifiers. Safety arrangements of thyristor and transistor systems. Active and passive power.
