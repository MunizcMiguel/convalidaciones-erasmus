## CURRICULUM CONTENTS

Lecture

Topics discussed in lectures:
3D scanners, classification of 3D scanners, information gathering density, information gaps, information gathering methods, example scanner designs, scanner applications.
The process of generating a model of mechanical parts using 3D scanners.
Point cloud, methods for evaluating the correctness and editing of the point cloud of mechanical parts and devices.
The process of generating a 3D model based on a point cloud.
The process of scanning mechanical parts and equipment.
Reconstruction of damaged objects and their re-design.
Definitions of photogrammetry of machinery and mechanical equipment and industrial objects, methods of recording digital images, histograms of digital images, advantages and disadvantages of photogrammetry.
Center projection. Imperfections of photography, errors of radial and tangential distortion, spherical and comatose aberration.
Photogrammetry of machinery and mechanical equipment and industrial facilities, computational systems of photogrammetry, marker systems.
The course of photogrammetric scanning of machinery and mechanical equipment implemented under laboratory conditions.
Reverse engineering methods used in extracting information about mechanical parts and devices. Reproduction of their modes of operation and manufacturing costs. Secondary engineering of studied mechanical parts and devices. Reproduction of technical documentation of mechanical parts and devices.

Lab

Laboratory exercises include practical learning:
Scanning of mechanical parts and devices with 3D visible light scanners,.
Editing of received point clouds of scanned parts and mechanical devices.
Generating meshes of triangular elements spanning the received point clouds.
Applying textures to surface models.
Reverse engineering process on a selected part of a mechanical device.
3D model construction in Catia software, based on the point cloud.
Perform photogrammetry of a selected part of a mechanical device.
