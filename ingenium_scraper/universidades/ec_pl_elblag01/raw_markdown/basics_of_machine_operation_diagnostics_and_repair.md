## CURRICULUM CONTENTS

Lecture

Basic concepts and definitions of the components of an operational system. Operational requirements of technical objects. Exploitation system of machines. Characteristics and operational division of machines and equipment. Technical and operating documentation of machinery and equipment. Performance characteristics of machinery. Principles of safe use of machinery. Diagnosis and monitoring of the condition of an operation object. Diagnostics technical diagnostics and its assumptions. Types of diagnostic tests. Physico-chemical processes as sources of signals diagnostic. Vibroacoustic diagnostics of machinery and equipment. Principles of machine operation. Basic wear processes in the process of operation. Lubrication systems. Principles of lubricant selection. Maintenance system Technical inspections of machinery and equipment. Overhauls of machinery and equipment. Phases of the technological process of overhaul. Costs and profitability of machinery operation.

Tutorial

1 Introduction. Developing instructions for a machine
2. Division of complex technical objects into operating subsystems.
3. Determination of operating characteristics of machines
4. Planning periodic maintenance and overhaul of machines.
5. Identification of types of damage to machines.
6. Measures - wear indicators for selected elements of subsystem
7. Selection of lubricants to the assumed operational conditions
