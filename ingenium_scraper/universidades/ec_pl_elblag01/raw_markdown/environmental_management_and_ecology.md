## CURRICULUM CONTENTS

Lecture

Life processes, interactions, and adaptations. The movement of materials and energy through living communities. The successional development of ecosystems. The abundance and distribution of organisms and biodiversity in the context of the environment. Diversity of ecosystems. The main ecosystems for the stable biosphere. Influences of human activity on the nature and the intensity of changes.
The fundamental problems of habitat loss and fragmentation, habitat disturbance and the non-sustainable exploitation of species in both aquatic and terrestrial ecosystems.
Specific environmental aspect, such as the use of energy or natural resources in institutions.
Institutions benefits of environmental management system (EMS) implementation. Requirements of ISO 14001, implementation and benefits.

Lessons l

Project preparations on International Standard to implement an environmental management system (EMS) for small- /medium-sized enterprises. The rules of creating an enterprise's environmental policy.
