## Learning objectives

The course is designed to provide students with the tools to be able to know and evaluate the main cellular communication systems, starting from the first generation systems, up to providing some information on the future 6G systems. During the course, the main simulation tools for mobile radio networks will be analyzed. Knowledge and understanding: At the end of the course, the student must know and understand: - the architecture (components and functionality) of mobile radio networks; - the operating mechanisms of the modulation systems used; - the main activities carried out for the design and management of a mobile radio network Ability to apply knowledge and understanding At the end of the course, the student must: - be able to analyze the behavior of a mobile radio network - be able to identify the characteristics of a mobile radio network in order to design a part of it to meet the specifications. - be able to use the methodologies to correctly configure a mobile radio network based on the characteristics of the channel. - be able to apply the tools and criteria for the analysis of the properties of mobile radio networks. - be able to define the parameters of a mobile radio network based on standard specifications. - be able to design advanced digital telecommunication systems and understand the project specifications required for the creation of mobile radio networks and provide adequate solutions. Autonomy of judgment At the end of the course, the student will know the main solutions currently existing for cellular networks in terms of network architecture, protocols, interfaces, procedures and management of radio resources. Communication skills At the end of the course, the student must be able to organically illustrate the interdependence of the different modules that make up a mobile radio network. Learning skills The evolution of mobile devices requires a high capacity for autonomous updating. The course will enable students to independently understand the technical documentation made available by the communities of telecommunication system manufacturers and by the main companies producing development software with particular reference to radio frequency systems for mobile radio networks.

## Teaching methods

The course is organized with - lectures with the support of graphic presentations - exercises based on LabVIEW simulation software, - practical exercises on hardware systems (spectrum analyzers, signal generators, SDR card antennas) The teacher is available to provide explanations and clarifications both during the interval between consecutive hours of lessons, and during the time of receipt or by appointment, both by e-mail, IM (MSTeams) and social networks on the official page of the course of study.

## Assessment methods

During the course, a series of projects will be assigned to be carried out in progress, organized in groups of 2/3 using the simulation software used during the laboratory exercises. These projects will be evaluated with a score of 10/30. The learning assessment is carried out at the end of the course through an oral exam that may contain - open-ended questions - numerical exercises in order to verify - knowledge - skills - competences that cover the entire program of the course. Each question/exercise is assigned a maximum score. Each exercise is evaluated with a score between 0 and the maximum score assigned. The maximum score is assigned in the case of a correct answer, while a lower score is assigned in the presence of errors. Errors due to distraction or misunderstandings of the question attributable to possible ambiguities in the formulation of the question itself, will have a lower weight than conceptual errors clearly caused by partial knowledge of the topic. The maximum sum of the scores obtained in the questions/exercises will be 22/30. The final grade is obtained as the sum of the scores obtained in each question and the score assigned to the project in progress. The maximum score for the test is 32. Those who obtain an overall score of 32 will have a grade of 30 cum laude.
