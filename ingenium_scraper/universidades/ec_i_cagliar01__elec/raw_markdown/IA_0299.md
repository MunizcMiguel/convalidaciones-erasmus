## Contents

Technologies and architectural solutions for cloud, fog, and edge networking and computing (19 hours of theory and 3 hours of exercises) • Fundamental concepts and terminology • Virtualization and containerization • Core elements and enabling technologies of cloud computing • Basic, advanced, and specialized architectures Introduction to the fundamentals and development of Amazon Web Services (AWS) architectural solutions (16 hours of theory, 3 hours of exercises, and 19 hours of laboratory) • Basic concepts and global infrastructure • Security and Identity and Access Management (IAM) • Compute, storage, database, networking, and content delivery services • Design principles according to the AWS Well-Architected Framework

## Reference texts

Thomas Erl, Ricardo Puttini, and Zaigham Mahmood. Cloud computing: concepts, technology & architecture. Pearson Education, 2019. During the course, students will be granted access to the materials (presentations, videos, and activities) from the AWS Academy Cloud Foundations and AWS Academy Cloud Developing courses.

## Learning objectives

The objective of the course is to explain: the fundamental principles of cloud computing, fog computing, and edge computing; the basic principles of virtualization and containerization; the fundamental and advanced architectures of cloud systems; cloud security concepts; the main Amazon Web Services (AWS) offerings related to compute, storage, databases, networking, and content delivery; best practices in design according to the AWS Well-Architected Framework; and methodologies for developing and migrating applications in cloud and containerized environments. Knowledge and understanding The course is designed to provide students with knowledge and understanding of: the models, terminology, and characteristics of cloud computing; virtualization and containerization techniques; cloud architectures, from fundamental mechanisms to advanced and specialized ones; cloud security concepts and identity management; the main AWS services and how to use them. Applying Knowledge and understanding The course also aims to enable students to apply their knowledge and understanding to: configure and use AWS services through the console and practical labs; design scalable, resilient, and secure cloud architectures; develop and migrate web applications to containerized environments managed on AWS; implement caching, content delivery, and load balancing solutions. Making judgment The skills acquired will enable students to assess the most appropriate architectural choices and cloud services based on security, performance, scalability, and cost requirements, as well as to adopt the best practices outlined in the AWS Well-Architected Framework. Communication skills The teaching approach, which combines theoretical lectures and practical exercises, will train students to communicate concepts and solutions clearly and effectively, describe cloud architectures and configurations, and interact with both technical and non-technical audiences. Learning skills Through the course, students will be able to integrate knowledge acquired in networking and distributed systems with practical expertise in cloud computing and AWS services. Practical labs and exam simulations will also provide the ability to independently explore new cloud technologies, assess their applicability in different scenarios, and clearly present the proposed solutions.

## Prerequisites

Knowledge The student must have adequate knowledge of Internet access technologies and network protocols. Furthermore, a basic knowledge of English is required. Not necessary, but advisable: Python programming basics. Skills The skills acquired from previous teachings concern the ability to analyze the basic architectures of telecommunication networks. Expertise The skills acquired in previous preparatory teachings are essential for understanding, interpretation, critical analysis, and resolution of network architectures and distributed systems.

## Teaching methods

Teaching is delivered in a traditional format, with face-to-face lectures supported by slide presentations. In addition, guided classroom exercises and laboratory activities will be carried out using dedicated software.

## Other information

During the course, the slides and the traces of the exercises will be provided.

## Assessment methods

Students will be evaluated through a written and an oral test. The exam will be considered passed, i.e. the grade reported will be no less than 18/30, if students demonstrate that they have acquired: sufficient knowledge of the most appropriate terminology; the ability to identify the basic elements of a cloud networking system, their main characteristics, and the main data analysis platforms and techniques. To achieve a score of 30/30, students must demonstrate that they have acquired: excellent knowledge of all the topics covered during the course; the ability to analyze a complex cloud networking system; the ability to compare different technologies to identify the most suitable ones that best satisfy the requirements of complex applications; the ability to propose the level of scalability and functionality to be integrated that is best suited to the reference context.
