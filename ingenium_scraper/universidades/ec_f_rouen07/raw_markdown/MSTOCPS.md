# Oral Communication & Presenting Skills

Module code: MSTOCPS
Duration: 14 h

## Objectives

- At the end of this module students will:
- Have a clear model of what constitutes successful and unsuccessful
- presentations
- Have practiced giving formal presentations in English.
- Be more aware of their own downfalls when presenting

## Topics

- Methods for putting together an oral presentation
- Practice
