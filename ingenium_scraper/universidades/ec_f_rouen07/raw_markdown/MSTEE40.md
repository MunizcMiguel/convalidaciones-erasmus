# Bibliographical Study

Module code: MSTEE40
Duration: 12 h

## Objectives

- At the end of the module, students will be able to :
- Learn to create and conduct a short oral presentation on a technical
- topic given to them at the beginning of the semester
- Acquire basic skills and methods on searching for information and
- oral presentations
- Enhance their skills to search for information and conduct oral
- presentations
- Learn to deliver an effective oral presentation in order demonstrate
- their understanding of the subject

## Topics

- Team working
- Information searching
- Oral presentation
