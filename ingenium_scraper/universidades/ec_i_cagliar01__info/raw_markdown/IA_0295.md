## Learning objectives

The course aims to provide students with theoretical and practical skills in the development of interactive digital content using Unity, one of the most widely used development environments for prototyping 2D and 3D applications, games, simulations, and immersive experiences. Knowledge and understanding The aim of the course is to provide students with the knowledge and skills needed to create interactive digital content, virtual 2D and 3D environments, and simple multimedia applications using the Unity game engine. Applying Knowledge and understanding The course also aims to enable students to apply their knowledge and understanding in evaluating development tools for interactive prototyping, implementing game logic and dynamic elements through Unity scripting, organizing project structures, and optimizing functionality for standard devices (desktop and mobile). Making judgment The acquired skills will allow students to select the most effective strategies for designing interactive environments aligned with project goals. They will be able to choose the most suitable approaches among various programming, layout, and interaction solutions, and critically assess the outcomes of their implementations. Communication skills The teaching approach and assessment methods encourage students to clearly articulate their design choices, the techniques used, and the functionalities developed in their projects. Additionally, students will enhance their ability to produce technical documentation and present their work through intuitive interfaces and visual feedback. Learning skills Through hands-on activities and practical project development, students will be able to integrate the knowledge gained in other courses with advanced digital tools for multimedia content creation. The course framework fosters independent exploration of new Unity features and support libraries, as well as the ability to investigate emerging technologies in creative and interactive domains, presenting concise and effective summaries of their results.

## Prerequisites

Knowledge The student should have a basic knowledge of object-oriented programming. Skills The skills acquired from previous courses concern the ability to analyse and structure simple interactive applications. Expertise The expertise gained in foundational courses helps to understand the development logic of interactive and multimedia environments.

## Teaching methods
