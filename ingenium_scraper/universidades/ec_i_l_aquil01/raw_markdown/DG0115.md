## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, students will have acquired theoretical, technical, and design knowledge of both medium and low voltage electrical power systems. They will be able to understand their structure and operation, as well as to define and dimension the best distribution system lay out, allowing a correct coordination with the overcurrent, overvoltages, ground fault protection systems. They will be able to choose and calculate the electric loads required for every building service (lighting, motive power and energy in general) and to design an electrical power system guaranteeing both energy efficiency and electrical safety.

## Learning objectives

The main objective of the course is the analysis of the Electric Power Systems supplied both in Medium and in Low voltage. In particular, the course covers the theoretical foundations and the construction characteristics of the electrical systems used by civil, tertiary or industrial users, starting from the interface with the Distributor both in medium and low voltage. Significant attention is paid to the in-depth study of the issues relating to the electrical safety of the systems and the quality of the electrical service and voltage. Finally, the course has a significant applicative and practical value as it allows the student to acquire methods and procedures useful for the manual and PC-assisted design of electrical systems and their practical implementation.

## Prerequisites

The course requires knowledge of topics covered in other previous courses, such as: Electrotechnics and Electrical Machines.

## Contents

Definition of electrical system, legislation and standard, control certification, type of electrical system, frequency, voltages: levels, categories, classes, safety, classification based on neutral status. Analysis of the design power, load diagrams, duration curve, utilization factor, load factor, contemporaneity factor, use of factors, bottom-up calculation, conventional power, thermally equivalent operating current. Conventional load and main types of users, photometric quantities, lighting project, FM user, asynchronous motors, types of service, concentrated users, elevators and hoists, thermal power plants, HVAC and UTA / CTA, pumping systems, start-up, inrush current, magnetization . Types of electrical networks, radial, redundant, ring, meshed, electrical panels, electrical conduits, electrical cables. Design calculations, permissible voltage drop method, amperometric moments method, mono and bi-powered ring. Calculation of overcurrents, overload, short circuit, components of the current, crest factor, thermal stress, specific energy, electrodynamic stresses, calculation of the current, current of short circuit. Switching and protection devices, reference characteristics, re-establishment curve, withstand voltage, circuit-breaker, MV circuit-breakers, automatic circuit-breakers for LV, protection relay, thermal relay, magnetic relay, magneto-thermal release, electronic release, tripping, conventional tripping and non-tripping current, specific through-energy, contactors, fuses, conventional tripping and non-tripping current, current limiting characteristic. Conditions for overcurrent protection, overload protection, short circuit protection, pipeline protection, back up protection, selectivity of protections, motor protection. Protection from direct and indirect contacts, mass and extraneous mass, earth resistance, ground potential, contact voltage, effects of current on the human body, safety curve, earth system, earth resistance calculation, earth leakage breaker, contact protection indirect in systems: TT, TN, IT. Electrical cabins. Definitions, typologies, CEI 0-16, connection rules and schemes, sizing of the MV side components, transformers, sizing of the LV side components, overcurrent protection, protection against earth faults, protection from internal transformer faults, earthing systems of the cabins. The power factor correction of the plants: origin and consequences of the voltage-current phase shift. Determination of the power factor. Choice, sizing and protection of a power factor correction panel. Overvoltage classification (internal and external origin), coordination of insulation, protection and SPD, lightning protection. Emergency, backup and safety power supply, uninterruptible power supplies (UPS), static UPS, rotating UPS, generators. Obligation of the project, type of project, project documentation. Introduction to Power Quality, definitions and standards, disturbances, the PQ in Italy, continuity indices, voltage quality, user susceptibility, causes of disturbances and mitigation methods, harmonic distortion, effects of harmonics, filters, technical solutions and economic considerations, classification and main types of compensation systems, PQ costs.
