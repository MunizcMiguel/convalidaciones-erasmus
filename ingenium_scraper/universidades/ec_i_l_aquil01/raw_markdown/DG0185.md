## Learning outcomes

The aim of this course is to provide the student:

- the knowledge necessary for testing and design in the field of mechanical design

- the principles and basic methodologies that are used in the modern engineering for structural and functional design and verification of the machines

- the skill of identifying whether the problem can be properly simplified or divided into more basic problems;

- the skill to appropriately choose materials and construction technologies for the construction of mechanical components.

## Learning objectives

The course offers fundamental topics to face mechanical design from the constructive and structural point of view.

On successful completion of this module, the solution of exercises and simple constructive problems, carried out during the exercises, will help the student to improve: the skill to select the relevant information, the degree of autonomy of judgment and the use of vocabulary and appropriate terminology to present, in written or verbal form, the arguments and results of the study.

## Prerequisites

For the comprehension of the subject covered in the course, is required the basic knowledge of mechanics of material and solids, and the kinematics and dynamics of rigid bodies.

## Contents

FIRST PART:

1. Basic of Structural analysis and Machine Element Design

2. Stress Theory of Failure (Rankine’s, Tresca-Guest’s and Huber-Von Mises-Henkey’s theories)

3. Mechanical material behavior: Relation between Stress and Strain (elastic stress-strain relationships)

4. Thermal strain and residual stresses

5. High-Cycl Fatigue design

SECOND PART

1. Belts, Gears, Bearings and Springs

2. Nonpermanent joints (Screw)

3. Shaft/hub connections

## Teaching methods

The course is divided into theoretical and tutorial lessons.

Theoretical lessons: the models and theories underlying the course of Machine Element Design are described.

Exercise lessons: exercises concerning the topics of the course are proposed and carried out.

## Assessment methods

The examination consists of two tests: a written and an oral one.

The written test concerns the design or structural verification of components or simple mechanical systems, with the help of the methodologies, models and mathematical tools illustrated during the lessons.

The oral discussion will be on one or more topics dealt with in the course; the student is asked to explain the main concepts and theoretical bases learned by answering questions and discussing his/her written paper.

The final grade will result from the two tests as a whole.
