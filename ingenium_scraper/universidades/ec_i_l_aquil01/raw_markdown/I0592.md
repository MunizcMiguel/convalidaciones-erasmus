## Partition: ENGLISH LANGUAGE B2 (Gruppo 2)

### Language

English

### Learning outcomes

On successful completion of the course Students acquire a good knowledge about the structure of Contemporary Standard English and important cultural aspects of interlinguistics communication.  
- They apply this knowledge both in receptive and productive writing and speaking skills, appropriate for their upper-intermediate level.  
- They can analyze and evaluate language structures

### Learning objectives

The course aims at reaching the B2 level of English - upper intermediate level - based on the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR). The official level descriptor is “upper intermediate”. At this level, students can function independently in a variety of academic and professional environments in English, although with a limited range of nuance and precision. At this level the students can understand the main ideas of complex texts and standard spoken language, live or broadcast.  
  
Thus, the course aims at:  
- improving all four language skills, speaking, listening, reading and writing to achieve level B2;  
- engaging students through realistic contexts and examples of published scientific/technical texts.

### Prerequisites

Level B1 of English (Common European Framework) is required to attend  
the course in a profitable way.

### Contents

The program is based on the review of the English language functions,  
grammar structures as well as to the introduction of the main lexical and  
structural elements of the English language for Engineering.  
• comparative and superlative adjectives  
• countable and uncountable nouns  
• relative pronouns  
• prefixes and suffixes  
• compound nouns and adjectives adverbs of intensity and frequency  
• present - present simple, present continuous, past perfect  
• past - past simple and past continuous  
• future - will, be going to and present continuous  
• present perfect simple and present perfect continuous  
• past perfect  
• conditional sentences (all 3 types)  
• modal verbs  
• simple phrasal verbs  
• -ing form and infinitive  
B2 Grammar points:  
• Modal Verbs  
• Present perfect'  
• Past perfect  
• Conditional tenses  
• direct speech  
• conditionals (If...will / If...would); (if clauses)  
• how to express the concept of habit with 'used to'  
• passive form  
• Passive tenses  
• Phrasal Verbs  
Lexical morphology:  
• Relative pronouns  
• Expressions of quantity and quality  
• Compound nouns  
• Idiomatic expressions.  
  
Differences between formal and informal language  
  
• Adverbs, adjectives and prepositions  
  
Lexical contents/Semantic areas:  
  
• Sports and hobbies  
  
· Lifestyles - home & family  
  
• Environmental issues  
  
• Science and Technology  
  
• Business and Employment  
  
Vocabulary, with specific activities to strengthen the technical vocabulary in each Module; Grammar to consolidate the most common morphosyntactic structures in the technical language; Skills, through texts and activities to develop the Listening, Speaking and Writing skills.

### Teaching methods

Lectures, in-class training and exercise, homework, narrative text reading and work with other linguistic input.

The interactive nature of the course involves the consistent and active participation of the students.

### Assessment methods

The final test is assessed as Passed or Failed.

The assessment includes a writing/reading test aimed at assessing:  
- the functional, structural and lexical knowledge/skills;  
- the ability to understand a technical or scientific text.

### Reference texts

Grammar and Vocabulary for First and First for Schools Book with Answers, Cambridge University Press
