## Learning outcomes

Upon successful completion of this module, the student should - have a deep knowledge of fluid properties and the govern equations of fluid flow - be able to apply the acquired competencies to investigate a interpret fluid dynamic problems to be faced in engineering - have the ability to read and understand textbooks on related topics.

## Learning objectives

The goal of this course is to provide the basic principles of fluid motion and the main characteristics of fluid/body interaction for all the problems of interest for engineers.

## Prerequisites

The student must have a good knowledge of the most relevant topics of mathematics and physics; in particular, she/he must know derivatives, integrals, partial differential equations, basic principles of mechanics and thermodynamics.

## Contents

Topics of the module will include 1) general properties of fluids 2) fluid statics 3) Eulerian and Lagrangian description of fluid kinematics 4) Conservation equations in integral form 5) Differential equation for fluid motion 6) Dimensional analysis and similitude 7) Internal flows (pipe flows) 8) External flows; lift and drag 9) Laminar and turbulent flows

## Teaching methods

The course will consist of lectures and classroom exercises. The students themselves will carry out the tests; the activities will deal with the topics of the lessons in progress.

## Assessment methods

The oral exam will consist of questions to assess the acquired knowledge and comprehension of the main topics in fluid dynamics. Moreover, the exam will aim to assess the capability to solve elementary problems in fluid mechanics.

## Reference texts

Fundamentals of Fluid Mechanics (7 th edition), by Bruce R. Munson, Alric P. Rothmayer, Theodore H. Okiishi, Wade W. Huebsch, Ed. John Wiley & Sons, Inc.
