# OPERATIONAL RESEARCH (DG0309)

- **Attendance:** Not mandatory
- **Teaching methods:** Lecture

## Language

Italian

## Learning outcomes

Acquisition of the following skills:

- to model industrial problems as Mathematical Optimization problems;
- to solve Mathematical Optimization problems using appropriate solution algorithms;
- to interpret the solutions provided by algorithms to support decision-making processes.

## Learning objectives

The course introduces the fundamental methodologies of Operations Research for the management of industrial problems through mathematical modeling and the use of solution algorithms.

## Prerequisites

Basic notions of Linear Algebra, Geometry and Mathematical Analysis.

## Contents

- Introduction to Operations Research;
- General Information on Mathematical Optimization Problems;
- The Modeling Approach;
- Introduction to Linear Optimization;
- Geometry of Linear Optimization;
- Simplex Algorithm;
- overview on Duality and Sensitivity Analysis;
- Optimization in Complex Systems.

## Teaching methods

Lectures including methodological and theoretical aspects and exercises.

## Assessment methods

Assessment of the learning process:

Active learning is supported through model development sessions and exercises focused primarily on methodology.

Assessment of learning outcomes:

Written exam assessing:

- Modeling skills (approximately 10 points);
- Knowledge of algorithmic procedures (approximately 10 points);
- Knowledge of methodological and theoretical aspects (approximately 10 points).

Grades will reflect the following levels of preparation:

- Sufficient: basic modeling skills, basic knowledge of methods and theoretical foundations.
- Adequate: fair modeling skills and knowledge of methodological and theoretical aspects.
- Good: ability to correctly model management problems such as Mathematical Optimization problems, good ability to use methodological tools to solve Mathematical Optimization problems, and good knowledge of theoretical aspects, including advanced ones.
- In-depth: ability to correctly and efficiently model management problems such as Mathematical Optimization problems, excellent ability to use solution methods and algorithms, and in-depth understanding of all theoretical aspects in relation to modeling and algorithmic aspects.
