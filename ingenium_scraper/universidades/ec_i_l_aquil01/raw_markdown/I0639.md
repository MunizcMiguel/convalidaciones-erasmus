## Learning outcomes

By passing the exam, the student should be able to: - to know the main characteristics of the materials used in the structural field. - Knowledge of the main production processes of plastic, composite and ceramic materials. - Demonstrate ability to compare the characteristics of different materials in the structural field. - Demonstrate the ability to select a manufacturing process for a given component. - Demonstrate the ability to select a machining process appropriately given the material specifications and desired characteristics. - Demonstrate the ability to select the most appropriate joining process to match components of the same material and different materials given the Project specifications.

## Learning objectives

The objectives of the course are to provide the student with the basic concepts of manufacturing processes and advanced materials used in the structural and non. In particular, the first part of the course aims to provide the main information concerning polymeric, composite and ceramic materials and their forming, processing and joining processes. The second part of the course is focused on advanced processing es. Laser beam machining, electron beam, plasma arc, ultrasound, water jet, and surface treatments.

## Prerequisites

The student must know the basic notions of Materials Science and Manufacturing Processes.

## Contents

Topics of the module include: - Chemical and physical characteristics of polymer components. Polymer component forming processes. Main methods of characterization of polymeric materials. - Chemical and physical characteristics of composite components. Composite component forming processes. Main methods of characterization of composite materials. - Powder metallurgy. Forming of a powder component. - Chemical and physical characteristics of ceramic components. Ceramic component forming processes. Main methods of characterization of ceramic materials. - Main limitations of traditional processing - Advanced processing: laser beam, electron beam, plasma arc, ultrasound, water jet, and surface treatments. Thermal, chemical and mechanical joining processes.

## Teaching methods

The teaching activities used in the course (and the related learning activities required of the student) are: - lectures in order to acquire knowledge and understanding; - interaction with students through the proposition of problems and decision-making situations, in order to develop the ability to understand and identify appropriate solutions; - participation in guided exercises and group work, in order to develop the ability to communicate and support theses; - participation in laboratory activities to develop skills in applying knowledge and understanding; - specific software exercises to apply knowledge and develop skills; - preparation of written reports (including group reports) in order to promote synthesis and implementation capacity.

## Assessment methods

Preliminary verification of the skills of the class: there is no formal verification of the pre-requisites required; the teacher, in the first lesson, it highlights to the students what are considered basic knowledge deemed indispensable and whose eventual deficiencies must be filled for a correct use of the course. Ongoing assessment of the learning process: the active participation of students is stimulated throughout the course through the teaching methods described above. This active participation allows the teacher to verify the progress of the learning process. Assessment of learning outcomes (final exam): the final exam is based on the conduct of an oral test, consisting of three questions on the main contents of the course, aimed at ascertaining the knowledge and understanding by the student of the theoretical principles underlying the discipline.
