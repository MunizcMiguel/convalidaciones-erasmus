## Learning objectives

The goal of this course is to provide the main concepts for the analysis and design of electronic circuits and systems, both completing and enhancing the basic concepts already studied in the course “Elettronica I”. Mainly analog electronic is addressed, with special emphasis on performances and applications.

On successful completion of this module, the student should:

- have profound knowledge of basic principles of operation of electronic circuits,

- have knowledge and understanding of the behavior and of the main applications of basic electronic circuits,

- understand and explain the performances and characteristics of simple electronic circuits and systems,

- understand the fundamental concepts of electronic circuits and systems and their application to information and power management systems,

- demonstrate skill in circuit analysis and design,

- demonstrate capacity for understanding problems of information and power management and for addressing their solution by means of electronic circuits and systems.

## Prerequisites

The student must know the basic notions of general circuit theory, contained in the exams Fisica II, Elettrotecnica, Elettronica I.

## Contents

Topics of the module include: Recap on: Current mirrors, active loads and references: current mirror, active loads, amplifiers with current mirrors and active loads, voltage and current references, biasing of transistors. Operational amplifiers: Transconductance amplifier, internal structure and characteristics of an operational amplifier; real operational amplifier, applications (base circuits, active filters, impedance simulators, oscillators). Analog circuits: Transistor-based multipliers, Gilbert cell, Phase-Locked Loop circuits, modulators, filters, noise analysis. Power amplifiers. Optical devices: Photodiodes, LED and laser diodes, solar cells. Optical transmission systems. Active and passive filters: low-pass, high-pass and band-pass filters. Main types and design formulas. Active filters: Sallen-Key filters. Analog/digital circuits and systems: Practical realization of logic ports; A/D and D/A converters; microcontroller systems; internal structure of a microcontroller. Examples of applications to microcontroller systems. Software: Open source simulation software “LTSpice”, Analog Devices Corporation. C Language.
