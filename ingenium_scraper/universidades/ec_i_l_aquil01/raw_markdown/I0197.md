## Partition: GEOMETRY (Cognomi A-G)

### Language

Italian

### Learning outcomes

Students will compute, analyze, and communicate quantitative data using mathematical and logical methods to solve problems.

### Learning objectives

The course satisfies the following core competencies: Communication, Critical Thinking, Information, Literacy, Quantitative Reasoning, and Scientific Reasoning..

### Prerequisites

A solid background in secondary school algebra and trigonometry.  
To this aim, students are advised to attend the mathematics pre-courses offered in the two weeks prior to the start of the courses.

### Contents

Sets, binary relations, functions or mappings. Equivalence relations and partitions of a set. Binary operations, groups, fields, and real vector spaces. Subspaces of vector spaces. Spanning sets and linear indipendence. Basis and dimension. Vectors in R^n. Matrices. Operations with matrices and their properties. Rank of a matrix and its properties. The determinant of a matrix and its properties. Evaluate determinants using elementary operations. The inverse of a matrix. Introduction to system of linear equations. Application of Gauss-Jordan method to system of linear equations. Introduction of eigenvalues and eigenvectors. Matrix diagonalization. Introduction to the analytical geometry of space: lines and planes. Conics (quadratic curves) in the plane. Euclidean and afine classification of quadratic curves. The sphere, the cone and the cylinder.

### Teaching methods

All lectures are presented to the students by blackboard. In the event of a health emergency, distance learning can be used.

### Assessment methods

The Geometry exam is divided into two tests.  
The FIRST TEST (commonly known as "the written test") aims to ascertain the ability to carry out some exercises.  
The SECOND TEST (commonly known as the "oral") aims to ascertain the knowledge of the basic notions and the ability to prove some theorems. Normally this second test is also carried out through a written work unless the student asks to take it orally.  
To participate in the second test it is NECESSARY to have passed the first.  
In each appeal both the first and second tests are carried out; in case of large numbers, the second test can be extended even in the days following the date of the appeal.  
During each test it is possible to withdraw.  
At the end of the second test, the commission (taking account of both tests) decides whether or not the student has passed the exam. In the first case, moreover, the commission proposes a grade to the student.  
The student can:  
- refuse the vote; in this case the student loses both tests and in a subsequent appeal he will have to take both tests again;  
- accept the grade and record the exam;  
- ask for the application for Lauds (only if the proposed grade is 30/30); if the student was then unable to answer this question, the mark would still remain 30/30.

### Reference texts

2) Carfagna, Piccolella, Complementi ed esercizi di Geometria e Algebra lineare, Zanichelli  
3) Luca Mauri, Enrico Schlesinger  
Esercizi di Algebra lineare e Geometria, Zanichelli  
4) Course notes.
