## Learning outcomes

- knowledge of Boolean algebra;

- knowledge of the hardware implementation of Boolean algebra, from an analog and digital perspectives;

- knowledge of basic combinatorial circuits;

- knowledge of basic sequential circuits;

- capability of description and synthesis of finite state synchronous digital systems in direct hardware logic;

## Learning objectives

The aim of the lectures is to introduce the student to the concepts, methodologies and implications of the use of digital circuits and basic systems using direct hardware and programmed logic. At the end of the course, the student will be able to understand the physical and logical transition from analog to digital electronics and to use the most appropriate methodologies for the synthesis of digital finite state machines.

## Prerequisites

Recommended: knowledge of the basic circuit notions;

## Contents

-Introduction to the fundamental concepts of digital electronics

-Boolean algebra, synthesis methods and reduction methods

-Introduction to basic analog electronics for the implementation of logic gates

- Combinatorial digital electronics, from combinatorial to sequential digital electronics

-Synchronous and asynchronous systems: representation models of a finite states machine

- Logical description of a states machine

## Teaching methods

-Theoretical lessons;

-Exercises in the laboratory.

## Assessment methods

Written exam containing two theoretical questions, and a synthesis project of a digital system in direct hardware.

At the time of the exam, students will have to submit a brief report on the activities carried out in the laboratory.
