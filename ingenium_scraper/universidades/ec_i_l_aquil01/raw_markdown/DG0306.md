## Learning objectives

The goal of this course is to give to the student the mathematical and statistical tools necessary for the Statistical Process Control (SPC) in R&D activities of new processes and in the optimization of the existing ones. Particular emphasis is given to chemical and biotechnological processes. The statistical design of industrial experiments is one of the most important topic of the course (DOE). After the course the student will have the know-how necessary to follow each step of process development of new chemical and biotechnological processes and in the optimization of the existing ones.

## Contents

Topics of the module include:  
• Steps utilized in the process development and its optimization: lab-scale tests, pilot plant tests, industrial application of the developed processes. Scale-up and Scale-down.  
• Main statistical parameters: average, variance, standard deviation, covariance, range  
• Histogram and main distribution of probabilities  
• Confidence intervals, sampling theory  
• Statistical tests, power of the test, control charts, Cp and Cpk  
• Analysis of the Variance (ANOVA)  
• Linear and non-linear regression. Parameter estimation of mathematical models (algebraic and differential models)  
• Factorial experiments with more factors and levels. Data analysis  
• Two-levels factorial experiments: Yates method  
• Confusion in two-level factorial experiments  
• Fractional factorial experiments, evaluation of aliases  
• Response Surface Methodology (RSM) in the optimization of chemical and biotechnological processes  
• Lab-scale experimental tests

## Assessment methods

Summative assessment: Written test followed by an oral examination. An optional mid-term written test will be also provided at the end of the course, in order to help the students to split the workload. If a student passes this written exam, he/she will take a final oral test concerned the course contents. The written test (to be solved within 3 hours) consists in 2-3 exercises each composed by several questions. It is aimed to verify the ability in the use of some the statistical tools in some contexts by using proper laws and correct formalism for their solution. A large number of previous written tests and corresponding detailed solutions are available on the Module website. This allows the students to be well aware of the kind of test he/she will have to deal with and how the required output should look like. The oral examination will take place within the same or the next exam session of the passed written test. It will typically last less than 1 hour and will cover the areas of the written answers that need clarification, and additional subjects (belonging to the whole program of the course) proposed by the teacher. Criteria of evaluation will be: the level of knowledge of the statistical tools described in the course, as well as the ability to apply them; the property of use of the scientific language; the clarity and completeness of explanations.
