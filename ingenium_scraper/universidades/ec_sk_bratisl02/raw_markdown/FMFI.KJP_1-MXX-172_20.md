## Learning outcomes

This course is aimed for foreign students to learn the fundamentals of the Slovak language with the
focus on basic communication as well as all other language skills- listening comprehension,reading
and writing.

## Class syllabus

The sylabus is targeted at the comprehension of the basics of the Slovak language for the absolute
beginners (A1) and this course is a follow up course to the Slovak language course 1.
