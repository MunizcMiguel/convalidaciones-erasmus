# Linear Algebra (FMFI.KAG+KAI/1-)

## General information

- Type of activities: lecture / practicals
- Number of hours:
- per week: 2 / 2 per level/semester: 26 / 26
- Form of the course: on-site learning
- Number of credits: 6
- Recommended semester: 3., 5.
- Educational level: I.

AIN-152/22

## Course requirements (assessment)

Continuous assessment: graded homework, quizzes, activities in exercises, midterms. The student
must obtain at least 55% of points from the semester in order to take the final exam.
Examination: an examination consisting of a written and an oral part. The student must obtain at
least 50% of the points from the written part. Approximate scale: A 90%, B 80%, C 70%, D 60%,
E 55%
Scale of assessment (preliminary/final): 50/50

## Learning outcomes

After completing the course, students will be familiar with the fundamentals of linear algebra
needed in informatics and computer graphics.

## Class syllabus

Matrices and matrix operations, determinants.
The concept of a group, specific matrix groups.
Systems of linear equations.
Solution spaces, vector spaces.
Scalar product, vector product in 3-dimensional space.
Linear and affine spaces.
Eigenvectors and eigenvalues.
