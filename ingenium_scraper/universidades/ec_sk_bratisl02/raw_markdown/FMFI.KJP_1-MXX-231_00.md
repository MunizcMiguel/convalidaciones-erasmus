## Learning outcomes

The objective of the classes is to provide the students with knowledge of technical English in their
field of study and experience with technical English sources sufficient to make the able to use
technical language for their later study and professional purposes.

## Class syllabus

The subject continues the program of English (2). Students take classes of special English for their
field of study: English for mathematics, English for physics, English for computer science, English
for management and economic and financial mathemathics.
The subject requires advanced knowledge of general English.
