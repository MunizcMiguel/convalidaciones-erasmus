# Cognitive Laboratory (FMFI.KAI/1-AIN-408/22)

## General information

- Type of activities: course
- Number of hours:
- per week: 2 per level/semester: 26
- Form of the course: on-site learning
- Number of credits: 2
- Recommended semester: 1., 3., 5.
- Educational level: I., I.II.

## Course requirements (assessment)

Interim evaluation: presentations
Indicative assessment scale: A 90%, B 80%, C 70%, D 60%, E 50%
Scale of assessment (preliminary/final): 100/0

## Learning outcomes

Acquisition of methods for studying various cognitive phenomena (data collection and analysis)
using an online cognitive laboratory.

## Class syllabus

Demonstration of 40 standard experiments in the fields of neurocognition, mechanisms of
perception, attention systems, memory processes, speech production and perception, knowledge
representation (concepts and mental ideas), judgment and decision-making processes.

## Recommended literature
