## Learning outcomes

The objective of the subject is to provide the students with experience and knowledge of technical
English and thus make them ready to use English sources of information for later study and
professional career.

## Class syllabus

This is a continuation of the course English (1) designed for pre-intermediate students. Fundamental
vocabulary is presented through selected topics in mathematics, physics and informatics. The
lessons also contain revision of elementary grammar. Generally, it is a necessary preliminary to
advanced programs.
