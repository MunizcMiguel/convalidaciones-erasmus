## Learning outcomes

After completing the course, students will be able to:
- Identify basic ethical issues related to the development and use of IT and AI technologies.
- Apply ethical frameworks to analyze specific cases in computer science.
- Understand the relationship between technology, values, and society.
- Consider the impacts of IT and AI decisions on user privacy, responsibility for their data, and the
transparency of the processes of those processing them.
- Recognize dilemmas from the perspective of the different interests of different stakeholders.
- Approach critically the design, use, and regulation of digital systems.

## Class syllabus

1. The role of ethics in IT and AI
2. Values and technology
3. Transparency and accountability of algorithms
4. Bias and fairness in AI systems
5. Privacy and digital identity
6. Control, surveillance and autonomy
7. Societal impacts of informatization and artificial intelligence
8. Ethics of interaction: chatbots, assistants and social AI
9. Sustainability of digital systems
10. Interoperability, standards and digital inclusion
11. Research ethics and academic integrity with a focus on the use of AI tools
12. Democracy, power and regulation in the age of AI
