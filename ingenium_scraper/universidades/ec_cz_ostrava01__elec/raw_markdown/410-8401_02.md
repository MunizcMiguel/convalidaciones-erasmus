# Safety in Electrical Engineering I

## Type of Course Unit

Optional

## Level of Course Unit

First Cycle, Second Cycle

## Semester when the Course Unit is delivered

Winter, Summer Semester

## Mode of Delivery

Face-to-face

## Language of Instruction

English

## Prerequisites and Co-Requisites

Course succeeds to compulsory courses of previous semester

## Summary

Upon successful completion of the course, the student:

– explains the basic principles of safety in activities involving electrical equipment,

– applies the requirements of legal and technical regulations related to electrical safety,

– assesses risks associated with the operation of electrical equipment and proposes appropriate preventive measures,

– applies the principles of safe working practices when working with electrical equipment in accordance with applicable regulations.

## Learning Outcomes of the Course Unit

The aim of the course is to develop the student's ability to understand legal and technical requirements for safety when working with electrical equipment, to identify and assess risks associated with the operation of electrical equipment, and to apply appropriate principles of protection against electric shock. The student acquires the competences necessary for carrying out activities involving electrical equipment safely and for the responsible application of applicable legal and technical requirements.

## Course Contents

1. Safety in electrotechnics. Terms, sense, regulations and standards. Skills in electrotechnics according to Czech law - Vyhl.50/1978, Zák. 262/2006-labour code, codes 22/1997, NV 17/2003 Sb., NV 176/2008 Sb., validity area.

2. Identificatin by colours – conductor, tell-tales, indicators, actuators, control devices according to Europian standards - EN 60445 ed.3, EN 60446 ed.2, ČSN 33 0165, EN 60073 ed.2, warning boards.

3. Basic principles of protection against electric shoc - philosophy of protection against electric shock(HD 60364 – 4 – 41), ambiente influence, touch voltage (HD 60364 – 1, 5-51, HD 60364 – 4 – 41 ed.2)

4. Supply systems (HD 60364 – 1), the way of protection against electric shock (HD60364 – 4 – 41), external influences and internationa protection code (HD 60364 – 5-51, EN 60529 )

5. Lines/cables – design and protection of line, permissive current load HD 60364 – 5 – 52, HD 60364 – 5 – 523, components for protection in low voltage systems.

6. Live working, operation of electrical installation, preparation of work location - communication, important parts from EN 50110-1, definition of live working zone, vicinity zone, preparation of work zone for dead working,

7. The first aid by electric shock, fire extinguishing of electrical devices and installation
