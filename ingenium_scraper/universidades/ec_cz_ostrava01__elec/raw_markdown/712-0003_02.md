# Czech Language for Foreigners a/I - mobility - exchange programmes

## Type of Course Unit

Optional

## Level of Course Unit

First Cycle, Second Cycle

## Semester when the Course Unit is delivered

Winter, Summer Semester

## Mode of Delivery

Face-to-face

## Language of Instruction

English

## Prerequisites and Co-Requisites

Course succeeds to compulsory courses of previous semester

## Summary

The participant of the course can read simple sentences focused on elementary communication and understand their content, he/she can react to questions concerning himself/herself, place of residence, family and friends. The grammar is limited to necessary extend. The instruction includes intercultural communication.

## Learning Outcomes of the Course Unit

The instruction takes place on the level of low-thereshold courses with the output level A1 of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages - speaking, writing, reading and understanding.

Units 1-3
