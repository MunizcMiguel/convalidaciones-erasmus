## Learning objectives

EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVES: THE COURSE AIMS TO PROVIDE STUDENTS WITH A COMPLETE PICTURE OF THE BASIC ELEMENTS OF CLASSICAL MECHANICS WITH PARTICULAR REFERENCE TO THOSE TOPICS CLOSELY RELATED TO ENGINEERING. KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING: THE COURSE AIMS TO PROVIDE KNOWLEDGE OF THE BASIC CONCEPTS OF CLASSICAL MECHANICS IN A CONCISE AND CORRECT WAY FOR THE APPLICATIONS. ABILITY TO APPLY KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING: THE OBJECTIVE OF THE COURSE IS TO MAKE THE STUDENT ABLE TO MASTER THE THEORETICAL KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED AND TO BE ABLE TO SOLVE EXERCISES RELATED TO CLASSICAL MECHANICS PROBLEMS. COMMUNICATION SKILLS: THE COURSE AIMS TO PROVIDE THE STUDENT WITH THE ABILITY TO EXPLAIN THE KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED IN A CLEAR AND RIGOROUS WAY. AT THE END OF THE COURSE THE STUDENT MUST BE ABLE TO CORRECTLY STATE DEFINITIONS, PROBLEMS AND THEOREMS RELATING TO THE CONTENTS OF THE COURSE. MAKING JUDGMENTS: STUDENTS ARE MOTIVATED TO LEARN CRITICALLY AND RESPONSIBLY ALL THE TOPICS EXPOSED DURING LESSONS IN THE CLASSROOM AND TO ENRICH THEIR JUDGMENT CAPACITIES THROUGH THE STUDY OF THE EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS INDICATED BY THE TEACHER.

## Prerequisites

PREREQUISITES: THE COURSE REQUIRES THE KNOWLEDGE OF BASIC MATHEMATICS TOPICS: ALGEBRA, ELEMENTARY GEOMETRY, LOGARITHMS, TRIGONOMETRY, BASIC ELEMENTAL DIFFERENTIAL AND INTEGRAL CALCULUS, FIRST AND SECOND DEGREE EQUATIONS AND INEQUALITIES, ALGEBRAIC SYSTEMS.

## Assessment methods

THE EXAM CONSISTS OF A WRITTEN AND AN ORAL TEST. IN THE WRITTEN TEST THE STUDENT SOLVES THREE PROBLEMS CONCERNING VECTOR CALCULUS, CINEMATICS AND DYNAMICS. THE SOLUTION OF EACH PROBLEM IS ASSIGNED A SCORE FROM 0 TO 10. THEREFORE THE RATING RANGES FROM 0 TO 30. UPON REACHING THE SCORING THRESHOLD OF 18 FOR THE WRITTEN TEST, THE STUDENT CAN TAKE THE ORAL TEST, WHICH CONSISTS OF THREE QUESTIONS DRAWN FROM THE QUESTION REPOSITORY ("DOMANDARIO" COLLECTION) PUBLISHED FOR THE COURSE YEAR ON THE TEACHER'S WEBSITE. THE ANSWERS TO EACH QUESTION ARE EACH ASSESSED WITH A SCORE FROM 0 TO 10, AND THEREFORE THE SCORE FOR THE ORAL TEST RANGES FROM 0 TO 30. THE EXAM RATING OUT OF THIRTY WILL BE ESTABLISHED BY AVERAGING THE RATING OF THE WRITTEN TEST WITH THAT OF THE ORAL TEST. ACCORDING TO THE NUMBER OF ATTENDING STUDENTS, THE TEACHER RESERVES THE POSSIBILITY TO ADMINISTER MIDTERM TESTS, THE SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF WHICH WILL GRANT DIRECT ACCESS TO THE ORAL EXAM. THESE TESTS WILL CONSIST OF PROBLEM-SOLVING EXERCISES FOCUSED ON SPECIFIC PARTS OF THE SYLLABUS. THE GRADE OF 30 CUM LAUDE IS AWARDED TO THE STUDENT WHO, IN ADDITION TO HAVING ACHIEVED A SCORE OF 30/30, HAS DEMONSTRATED MASTERY OF THE SUBJECT AND INDEPENDENT JUDGMENT. LANGUAGE PROPERTY AND CORRECTNESS ARE THE OBJECT OF ASSESSMENT BOTH WRITTEN AND ORAL.
