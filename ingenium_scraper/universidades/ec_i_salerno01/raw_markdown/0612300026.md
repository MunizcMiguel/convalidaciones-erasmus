## Learning objectives

1. After passing all assessments for the courses included in the curriculum and earning the corresponding credits, the student—regardless of the number of years enrolled at the university—is eligible to take the final examination, for which the credits specified in the curriculum are awarded. 2. The final examination consists of the preparation and defense of a thesis that is primarily application-oriented. The thesis may also be written under the guidance of a faculty advisor—a tenured professor or researcher—whose field of instruction is included in the curriculum; in such cases, a second faculty advisor and/or a co-advisor is permitted, the latter of whom need not be directly involved in the program’s teaching activities. The thesis may be developed within the scope of the degree program’s disciplines, including practical exercises and activities carried out during any internships. The defense consists of a public presentation before the committee referred to in paragraph 3 of this article. The preparation of the thesis and the defense are conducted in accordance with the guidelines available on the degree program’s webpage, under the Teaching/Final Exam section. 3. The committees for final examinations are appointed by the Department Chair or a person designated by him or her, and are normally composed, in accordance with the University’s General Regulations on Teaching, of 11 full members, including the chair, and in any case of no fewer than five members. Committees may be divided into subcommittees, consisting of at least three faculty members. The subcommittees shall, in any case, meet to conduct the final evaluation collectively. 4. The final exam is evaluated by assigning a grade that contributes to the final degree grade on a scale of 110. The evaluation takes into account the content of the paper, its presentation, and any other elements deemed relevant. The minimum final grade is 66 out of 110. The administration of the final exam and the announcement of the final result are public. 5. The thesis is submitted to the committee referred to in paragraph 3 at the time of the final examination. It may receive a final exam grade (VOTO_PF) not exceeding 3/110, or 4/110 if the degree is earned within three academic years, to be added to the base grade (VOTO_BASE) to determine the final degree grade: VOTO_LAUREA = VOTO_PF + VOTO_BASE. The VOTO_BASE is calculated using the following formula: VOTO_BASE=(4.1*MP-7.8), where MP represents the credit-weighted average of the grades obtained in the exams. The distinction “Lode” may be awarded only if all of the following conditions are met: the base grade, when added to the final exam grade and any rounding, equals or exceeds 110/110; the curriculum includes at least one graded exam with honors; the supervisor(s) submits a reasoned and detailed request for honors using the grade proposal tools; and the committee is unanimously in favor. Educational activities carried out abroad during the nominal duration of the degree program and recognized by the Academic Council with a number of curricular credits equal to NCFU are evaluated for the purpose of assigning the degree grade: the base grade may be increased by a score equal to NCFU/12 for a total not exceeding 1 point. Rounding up to the next whole number applies only to the final degree grade if the first decimal place is 5 or higher. For the purposes of enrollment in the Master’s Degree Program in Mechanical Engineering or the Master’s Degree Program in Management Engineering at the University of Salerno, an applicant is considered to have adequate academic preparation if they have achieved, in their Bachelor’s degree program, a weighted average grade of at least 24/30 based on course credits, or an equivalent evaluation if the Bachelor’s degree was earned abroad. For further details, please refer to the academic regulations for these degree programs. The preparation of the thesis and the defense are conducted in accordance with the guidelines available on the degree program’s webpage, under the “Academic Program/Final Exam” section.
