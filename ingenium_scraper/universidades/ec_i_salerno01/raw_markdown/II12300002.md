### Learning objectives

THE COURSE’S MAIN AIM IS TO STRENGTHEN BASIC MATHEMATICAL KNOWLEDGE, AND TO DEVELOP AND TO PROVIDE USEFUL TOOLS FOR A SCIENTIFIC APPROACH TO THE PROBLEMS AND PHENOMENA THAT STUDENTS ENCOUNTER IN PURSUIT OF THEIR STUDIES. THE MAIN EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVES ARE: - KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING UNDERSTANDING THE TERMINOLOGY USED IN MATHEMATICAL ANALYSIS; KNOWLEDGE OF DEMONSTRATION METHODS; KNOWLEDGE OF THE FUNDAMENTAL CONCEPTS OF MATHEMATICAL ANALYSIS (SEQUENCES AND SERIES OF REAL-VALUED FUNCTIONS, REAL-VALUED FUNCTIONS OF SEVERAL VARIABLES, DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS, MULTIPLE INTEGRALS, CURVES AND CURVILINEAR INTEGRALS, SURFACES AND SURFACE INTEGRALS) - APPLYING KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING KNOWING HOW TO APPLY THEOREMS AND RULES DESIGNED TO SOLVE PROBLEMS; KNOWING HOW TO CONSISTENTLY BUILD SOME DEMONSTRATIONS; KNOWING HOW TO BUILD METHODS AND PROCEDURES FOR THE RESOLUTION OF PROBLEMS; KNOWING HOW TO SOLVE SIMPLE DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS, MULTIPLE INTEGRALS, CURVILINEAR AND SURFACE INTEGRALS, LINEAR SYSTEMS OF EQUATIONS. - MAKING JUDGMENTS KNOWING HOW TO IDENTIFY THE MOST APPROPRIATE METHODS TO EFFICIENTLY SOLVE A MATHEMATICAL PROBLEM, TO BE ABLE TO FIND SOME OPTIMIZATIONS IN THE SOLVING PROCESS OF A MATHEMATICAL PROBLEM. - COMMUNICATION SKILLS ABILITY TO WORK IN GROUPS, TO ORALLY PRESENT A TOPIC RELATED TO MATHEMATICS. - LEARNING SKILLS THE STUDENT HAS TO DEVELOP THE LEARNING SKILLS THAT WILL BE NECESSARY FOR INSERTING HIM IN THE FOLLOWING STUDIES WITH A HIGH AUTONOMY OF STUDY, AND CRITICALLY FACE MORE GENERAL PROBLEMS.

### Prerequisites

STUDENT MUST HAVE THE BASIC KNOWLEDGE OF MATHEMATICAL ANALYSIS, WITH PARTICULAR REFERENCE TO: ALGEBRAIC EQUATIONS AND INEQUALITIES, THE STUDY OF A REAL-VALUED FUNCTION, SEQUENCES AND NUMERICAL SERIES, LIMITS OF A REAL-VALUED FUNCTION, CONTINUITY AND DIFFERENTIABILITY OF A REAL-VALUED FUNCTION, FUNDAMENTAL THEOREMS OF DIFFERENTIAL CALCULUS.

### Assessment methods

THE EXAM IS COMPOSED BY A WRITTEN TEST AND AN ORAL INTERVIEW. TO PASS THE EXAM THE STUDENT HAS TO PASS BOTH THE WRITTEN AND ORAL TEST. WRITTEN TEST: IT CONSISTS IN SOLVING 4 PROBLEMS SIMILAR TO THE ONES WHICH HAVE BEEN STUDIED DURING THE COURSE. IN MORE DETAILS, THERE COULD BE EXERCISES OF THE FOLLOWING TYPES: - THE STUDY OF A PARAMETRIC LINEAR SYSTEM OR THE STUDY OF A MATRIX WHICH IS DIAGONALIZABLE (AT MOST 8 POINTS); - THE STUDY OF CONIC SECTIONS OR A PROBLEM ON ANALYTICAL GEOMETRY (AT MOST 8 POINTS); - THE SOLUTION OF A DIFFERENTIAL EQUATION AND/OR OF A CAUCHY PROBLEM (AT MOST 7 POINTS); - EXTREMA OF A FUNCTION IN TWO VARIABLES (AT MOST 7 POINTS); - THE STUDY OF A DIFFERENTIAL FORM (AT MOST 7 POINTS); - THE SOLUTION OF A CURVILINEAR INTEGRAL OR SURFACE INTEGRALE (AT MOST 7 POINTS); - THE SOLUTION OF A MULTIPLE INTEGRAL (AT MOST 8 POINTS); THE TIME FOR THE WRITTEN TEST IS USUALLY TWO HOURS. SCORES ARE EXPRESSED AS BANDS, FROM BAND A TO BAND D AND IT IS BASED ON THE SUM OF THE SCORES OBTAINED IN EACH EXERCISES ABOVE. THOSE SCORES DEPEND ON THE STUDENT'S ABILITY IN APPLYING OWN KNOWLEDGE. TO BE ADMITTED TO THE ORAL TEST A MINIMUM SCORE OF 18 IS REQUIRED. STUDENTS WILL HAVE TWO HOURS FOR THE WRITTEN TEST. ORAL INTERVIEW: IT IS SUBJECTED TO BEING SELECTED IN THE PREVIOUS WRITTEN TEST. IT NORMALLY TAKES 20 MINUTES. IT AIMS AT EVALUATING THE KNOWLEDGE OF ALL TOPICS, AND COVERS DEFINITIONS, THEOREM PROOFS, EXERCISE SOLVING. IT WILL BE PASSED PROVIDED A SUITABLE KNOWLEDGE OF THE FOLLOWING TOPICS HAS BEEN ACHIEVED: FUNCTIONS OF TWO VARIABLES, DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS, DIFFERENTIAL FORMS, INTEGRALS OF FUNCTIONS OF TWO VARIABLES. FINAL EVALUATION: THE FINAL MARK, EXPRESSED ON A SCALE FROM 18 TO 30 (POSSIBLY WITH LAUDEM), DEPENDS ON THE MARK OF THE WRITTEN EXAM, WITH CORRECTIONS IN EXCESS OR DEFECT ON THE BASIS OF THE ORAL INTERVIEW.
