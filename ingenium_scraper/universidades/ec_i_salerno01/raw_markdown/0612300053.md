## Learning objectives

SEMINARI AZIENDALI “Is what I learned in college enough to get a job, or should I pursue a master’s degree?” “I don’t know how to prepare a presentation.” “There are no career prospects at that company.” “How do I write a resume?” “During my first interviews, how can I effectively showcase what I’ve learned?” There are many questions that a student nearing graduation asks themselves regarding their first steps into the working world, which is often unfamiliar to them. The fear of not being adequately prepared, but also the thought that “something else” is needed—such as fluent English or the ability to use technical or general software (spreadsheets, preparing presentations, which soft skills are most useful, etc.). There is also a lack of a clear understanding of the tasks to be performed within the various departments of a single company, where some technical staff deal with product-related issues, while many others handle aspects related to plant management, distribution logistics, and more. They are also interested in the relationship with other professionals and with other engineers both inside and outside the company. To address these and other questions, the Department of Industrial Engineering organizes seminars and meetings between students and companies throughout the two semesters of its programs, with the aim of introducing students to the professional world and the sectors in which companies across the country operate, as well as to facilitate training, internships, and work placements, thereby strengthening cooperation between the university and industry. A section is also dedicated to presenting job opportunities and internationalization programs.
