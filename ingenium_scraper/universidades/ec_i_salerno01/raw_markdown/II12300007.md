## Contents

COURSE CONTENT 1) METALLIC MATERIALS (6 HOURS THEORY, 4 HOURS EXERCISES): THE PRODUCT TO BE MANUFACTURED AND THE STUDY OF MATERIALS. THE SOLID STATE: CRYSTAL LATTICES AND LATTICE DEFECTS. 2) MECHANICAL BEHAVIOR OF METALLIC MATERIALS (10 HOURS THEORY, 5 HOURS EXERCISES): FORCES AND STRESSES, UNIAXIAL LOADS. PLASTIC DEFORMATION MECHANISMS. WORK HARDENING MECHANISMS, EFFECT OF TEMPERATURE AND SPEED. HOT AND COLD PLASTIC DEFORMATIONS. ELASTIC DEFORMATION WORK AND PLASTIC DEFORMATION WORK. 3) METAL ALLOYS AND STEELS (10 HOURS THEORY, 5 HOURS EXERCISE): SOLID SUBSTITUTIONAL AND INTERSTITIAL SOLUTIONS. LAW OF PHASES. BINARY STATE DIAGRAMS. THE IRON-CARBON SYSTEM. NUCLEATION AND GROWTH OF GRAINS. DIAGRAMS OF AN ISOTHERMAL PHASE TRANSFORMATION. DIFFUSION IN THE SOLID STATE, SEGREGATION PHENOMENA. 4) HEAT TREATMENTS (6 HOURS THEORY, 4 HOURS EXERCISE): CLASSIFICATION OF HEAT TREATMENTS OF METAL ALLOYS. HEAT TREATMENTS OF STEELS, MARTENSITIC TRANSFORMATION AND THERMAL HYSTERESIS, TTT DIAGRAMS. 5) MECHANICAL TESTS (6 HOURS THEORY, 4 HOURS EXERCISES): BRINELL, VICKERS, KNOOP AND ROCKWELL HARDNESS TESTS. MICROHARDNESS TEST. IMPACT TEST. TENSILE TEST: SIZING TEST PIECES, UNIT LOADS AND CHARACTERISTIC VALUES, TEST REPORTS

## Learning objectives

OBJECTIVES: THE COURSE AIMS TO PROVIDE THE BASIC CONCEPTS ON THE STRUCTURE, MECHANICAL BEHAVIOR AND PROPERTIES, OF METAL ALLOYS, EVEN AFTER HEAT TREATMENT, USED FOR THE PRODUCTION OF STRUCTURAL COMPONENTS IN THE MANUFACTURING INDUSTRY. THE AIM IS TO DIRECT STUDENTS TO UNDERSTAND THE CONNECTIONS BETWEEN THE PROPERTIES OF MATERIALS, THEIR TRANSFORMATIONS AND THE POSSIBILITIES OF USE IN THE VARIOUS FIELDS OF ENGINEERING. KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING: - CHARACTERISTICS AND STRUCTURE OF METALLIC MATERIALS - MECHANICAL BEHAVIOR OF METALLIC MATERIALS - INFLUENCE OF HEAT TREATMENTS ON THE MECHANICAL CHARACTERISTICS OF METAL ALLOYS APPLIED KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING: - READ AND INTERPRET THE STATE DIAGRAM OF A TWO-COMPONENT SYSTEM. - ANALYZE TTT DIAGRAMS. - DETERMINE THE STRESS-STRAIN CURVES FROM THE MACHINE DIAGRAM OF A TENSILE TEST. - INTERPRET THE RESULTS OF MECHANICAL CHARACTERIZATION TESTS. MAKING JUDGEMENTS: - KNOW HOW TO CHOOSE THE APPROPRIATE MECHANICAL TEST AND KNOW HOW TO INTERPRET THE RESULTS FOR THE PURPOSE OF SELECTING MATERIALS. COMMUNICATION SKILLS: - ABLE TO DESCRIBE, IN WRITTEN FORM, CONCISELY AND ORALLY PRESENT WITH LANGUAGE SKILLS THE OBJECTIVES, THE PROCEDURE AND THE RESULTS OF THE ELABORATIONS CARRIED OUT. LEARNING SKILLS: - ABLE TO APPLY THE KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED TO CONTEXTS DIFFERENT FROM THOSE PRESENTED DURING THE COURSE AND EXAMINE IN DEPTH THE TOPICS COVERED USING MATERIALS OTHER THAN THOSE PROPOSED.

## Prerequisites

BASIC KNOWLEDGE ON: BASIC ELEMENTS OF THE STRUCTURE OF MATTER; BALANCING SIMPLE CHEMICAL REACTIONS; ALGEBRAIC AND TRIGONOMETRIC FUNCTIONS; ELEMENTARY OPERATIONS ON VECTORS; FORCES AND TORQUES.

## Teaching methods

TEACHING METHODS THE COURSE INCLUDES THEORETICAL LESSONS AND NUMERICAL EXERCISES IN THE CLASSROOM, WITH THE PROPOSAL OF EXERCISES AND PROBLEMS, TO BE CARRIED OUT ALSO IN COLLABORATION, WITH SUBSEQUENT ASSISTANCE BY THE TEACHER, IN ORDER THAT EACH STUDENT CAN PROCEED WITH A SELF-ASSESSMENT OF THE PREPARATION. DURING THE COURSE, EDUCATIONAL FILMS AND INTERACTIVE SIMULATIONS ARE ALSO OFFERED TO FACILITATE THE UNDERSTANDING OF THE TOPICS COVERED.

## Assessment methods

THE EXAM CONSISTS OF A WRITTEN TEST AND A COMPULSORY ORAL TEST. THE WRITTEN TEST IS CONSIDERED PASSED IF THE STUDENT REACHES AT LEAST 18/30 AND CONSISTS OF 3 NUMERICAL EXERCISES AND 1 OPEN-ENDED THEORETICAL QUESTION, FOR THE DURATION OF 2 HOURS, THE TEST AIM TO ASSESS THE KNOWLEDGE OF THE METHODS TO SOLVE PROBLEMS INHERENT: MECHANICAL BEHAVIOR AND CRYSTAL STRUCTURE OF METALS, BINARY DIAGRAMS, HEAT TREATMENTS AND MECHANICAL CHARACTERIZATION TESTS. THE ORAL EXAM IS AIMED AT VERIFYING THE LANGUAGE SKILLS AND THE ACHIEVED COMPETENCIES ON THE COURSE CONTENTS. THE FINAL GRADE IS DETERMINED BY THE AVERAGE OF THE WRITTEN TEST AND THE ORAL TEST; A SUFFICIENT GRADE IS ACHIEVED IF THE STUDENT SHOWS KNOWLEDGE OF THE MECHANICAL BEHAVIOR OF METALLIC MATERIALS, THE IRON-CARBON DIAGRAM AND THE HEAT TREATMENTS OF STEELS; HONORS ARE AWARDED AT THE DISCRETION OF THE TEACHER IF THE STUDENT SHOWS AUTONOMOUS PROCESSING SKILLS AND LOGICAL CONNECTION BETWEEN THE TOPICS OF THE COURSE.
