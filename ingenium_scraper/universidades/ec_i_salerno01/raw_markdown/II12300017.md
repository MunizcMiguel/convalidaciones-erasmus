## Learning objectives

THE OBJECTIVE IS TO UNDERSTAND TWO FUNDAMENTAL ASPECTS OF THE ENTERPRISE: THE ORGANIZATION'S DESIGN AND THE ECONOMIC AND FINANCIAL RESULT. KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING THE COURSE INCLUDES THE SOCIAL AND ETHICAL RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE COMPANY'S DECISION-MAKING PROCESS THROUGH THE STUDY OF ECONOMICS AND ORGANIZATION. THE MAIN ACQUISITIONS ARE: THE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS; THE FINANCIAL ASSESSMENT OF THE COMPANY; THE DESIGN VARIABLES OF THE ORGANIZATION; ORGANIZATIONAL CONFIGURATIONS. APPLYING KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING STUDENTS AT THE END OF THE COURSE WILL HAVE TO: KNOW HOW TO INTERPRET THE COMPANY'S FINANCIAL AND FINANCIAL RESULTS FROM THE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS; UNDERSTAND RELATIONSHIPS AND CHARACTERISTICS OF THE ORGANIZATION'S MAIN DESIGN VARIABLES. MAKING JUDGMENTS AT THE END OF THE COURSE, THE STUDENT WILL BE ABLE TO DRAW UP INDEPENDENT ASSESSMENTS OF AN ENTERPRISE'S FINANCIAL AND ECONOMIC RELIABILITY THROUGH BUDGET DATA, ANALYSIS OF THE CONGRUITY OF ORGANIZATIONAL PROCESSES AND CONFIGURATIONS ADOPTED. COMMUNICATION SKILLS DURING THE COURSE, STUDENTS WILL HAVE THE OPPORTUNITY TO PRESENT THE RESULTS OF THE EXERCISES IN THE CLASSROOM AND TO ANSWER QUESTIONS ABOUT THE CHOICES MADE. LEARNING SKILLS AT THE END OF THE COURSE, THE STUDENT WILL ACQUIRE KNOWLEDGE OF ECONOMIC-FINANCIAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL VARIABLES THAT CHARACTERIZE THE COMPANY'S DECISION-MAKING PROCESSES.

## Assessment methods

THE ACHIEVEMENT OF THE OBJECTIVES IS CERTIFIED BY PASSING AN EXAM. THE EXAM PROVIDES FOR A WRITTEN TEST AND AN ORAL EXAM THAT TAKE PLACE ON THE SAME DAY. THE WRITTEN TEST CONSISTS OF ANALYZING A COMPANY BUDGET AND IT IS PASSED IF YOU ANSWER CORRECTLY AT LEAST 50% OF THE QUESTIONS. THE ORAL TEST CONSISTS OF A DISCUSSION OF APPROXIMATELY 40 MINUTES AIMED AT ASSESSING THE LEVEL OF KNOWLEDGE, COMPREHENSION AND LANGUAGE OWNERSHIP ACHIEVED BY THE STUDENT.
