## Learning objectives

KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING: AT THE END OF THE COURSE, STUDENTS WILL HAVE ACQUIRED THE THEORETICAL AND PRACTICAL KNOWLEDGE REQUIRED TO USE SCIENTIFIC INSTRUMENTATION FOR MEASURING DISPLACEMENTS AND STRAINS IN STRUCTURAL MATERIALS. STUDENTS WILL ALSO BE ABLE TO ANALYSE AND INTERPRET THE RESULTS OF EXPERIMENTS CONCERNING THE MEASUREMENT OF THE STRESS–STRAIN STATE OF MATERIALS, COMPONENTS OR STRUCTURES, WITH THE AIM OF CHARACTERISING THEIR MECHANICAL BEHAVIOUR UNDER SERVICE CONDITIONS. WITH SPECIFIC REFERENCE TO STRAIN-GAUGE MEASUREMENTS, THE KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED DURING THE COURSE WILL COVER A SUBSTANTIAL PART OF THE KNOWLEDGE REQUIRED FOR LEVEL 2 PERSONNEL INVOLVED IN NON-DESTRUCTIVE TESTING ACCORDING TO THE EN ISO 9712 STANDARD. FINALLY, STUDENTS WILL LEARN THE OPERATING PRINCIPLES OF DIFFERENT TYPES OF UNIVERSAL MATERIAL TESTING MACHINES AND WILL BE ABLE TO IMPLEMENT THE PROCEDURES OF THE MAIN METHODS USED FOR THE MECHANICAL CHARACTERISATION OF MATERIALS. APPLYING KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING: THE COURSE AIMS TO ENABLE STUDENTS TO APPLY, IN THE LABORATORY, THE KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED REGARDING THE CHARACTERISATION OF THE MECHANICAL BEHAVIOUR OF ISOTROPIC AND ORTHOTROPIC MATERIALS. STUDENTS WILL BE ABLE TO UNDERSTAND AND IMPLEMENT THE RECOMMENDATIONS TYPICALLY PROVIDED BY THE MAIN STANDARDS CONCERNING THE CHARACTERISATION OF THE STATIC AND FATIGUE STRENGTH OF MATERIALS. THROUGH 30 HOURS OF LABORATORY ACTIVITIES, STUDENTS WILL HAVE THE OPPORTUNITY TO IMPLEMENT EXPERIMENTAL SET-UPS AND USE THEM TO PERFORM MEASUREMENTS. WITH REGARD TO STRAIN-GAUGE MEASUREMENTS AND DIGITAL IMAGE CORRELATION TECHNIQUES, AT THE END OF THE COURSE STUDENTS WILL BE ABLE TO: A) SELECT THE SPECIFIC ANALYSIS TECHNIQUE ACCORDING TO THE TYPE OF MATERIAL, COMPONENT AND INFORMATION TO BE OBTAINED; B) CHOOSE AND ADJUST THE INSTRUMENTATION ACCORDING TO THE TYPE OF MEASUREMENT; C) RECORD AND CLASSIFY TEST RESULTS; D) PREPARE A REPORT PRESENTING THE RESULTS, INCLUDING THEIR INTERPRETATION AND THE LIMITS OF APPLICABILITY OF THE METHOD. THANKS TO THE KNOWLEDGE OF THE ABOVE-MENTIONED TECHNIQUES, STUDENTS WILL BE ABLE TO PERFORM STRAIN AND DISPLACEMENT MEASUREMENTS USEFUL FOR THE MECHANICAL CHARACTERISATION OF MATERIALS AND TO DESIGN EXPERIMENTAL MEASUREMENTS AIMED AT ASSESSING THE STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY OF A COMPONENT OR STRUCTURE. MAKING JUDGEMENTS: THE COURSE AIMS TO ENABLE STUDENTS TO CRITICALLY SELECT THE EXPERIMENTAL SET-UP MOST SUITABLE FOR THE CHARACTERISATION OF A GIVEN MECHANICAL BEHAVIOUR, ACCORDING TO THE TYPE OF MATERIAL, IN ORDER TO OBTAIN THE MAIN STIFFNESS AND STRENGTH DATA REQUIRED FOR STRUCTURAL DESIGN. THROUGH LABORATORY ACTIVITIES, STUDENTS WILL PLAY AN ACTIVE ROLE IN DATA COLLECTION, DATA ANALYSIS AND IN THE FORMULATION OF A CRITICAL SUMMARY JUDGEMENT REGARDING THE EXPERIMENTAL ACTIVITIES CARRIED OUT. COMMUNICATION SKILLS AND LEARNING SKILLS: STUDENTS WILL BE ABLE TO EVALUATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE MECHANICAL CHARACTERISATION OF MATERIALS AND TO INTERPRET THE PRESCRIPTIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS TYPICALLY FOUND IN INTERNATIONAL STANDARDS. THE CLASSROOM AND LABORATORY ACTIVITIES WILL BE DOCUMENTED THROUGH REPORTS PREPARED BY EACH STUDENT. THESE REPORTS WILL ENABLE STUDENTS TO DEVELOP SKILLS RELATED TO THE PRESENTATION OF MEASUREMENT RESULTS, THE DESCRIPTION AND PRESENTATION OF THE CHARACTERISTICS OF THE IMPLEMENTED EXPERIMENTAL SET-UPS, AND THE PROCESSING OF RESULTS ACCORDING TO SPECIFIC CODES, STANDARDS OR THEORETICAL PROCEDURES. STUDENTS WILL ALSO ACQUIRE THE ABILITY TO DESCRIBE, USING APPROPRIATE TERMINOLOGY AND TECHNICAL AWARENESS, THE CHARACTERISTICS OF THE MEASUREMENT SET-UPS USED IN EXPERIMENTAL MECHANICS.

## Teaching methods

THE COURSE INCLUDES 30 HOURS OF LECTURES AND 30 HOURS OF GROUP LABORATORY ACTIVITIES, FOR A TOTAL OF 60 HOURS, DELIVERED OVER APPROXIMATELY 12 WEEKS, TYPICALLY WITH 5 HOURS PER WEEK. LECTURES WILL MAINLY CONSIST OF ORAL PRESENTATIONS BY THE INSTRUCTOR, SUPPORTED BY SLIDES AND BOARD WORK. STRAIN-GAUGE ACTIVITIES, INCLUDING INSTALLATION AND MEASUREMENT, WILL PREFERABLY BE CARRIED OUT IN A CLASSROOM EQUIPPED FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF EXPERIMENTAL STRAIN-GAUGE SET-UPS. THE OTHER LABORATORY ACTIVITIES RELATED TO MATERIAL TESTING WILL BE CARRIED OUT BY STUDENTS, POSSIBLY DIVIDED INTO GROUPS, AT THE LABORATORY OF MACHINE DESIGN OF THE DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRIAL ENGINEERING. DURING THE LABORATORY ACTIVITIES, THE MAIN ELECTROMECHANICAL AND SERVO-HYDRAULIC MATERIAL TESTING MACHINES WILL BE PRESENTED. THEIR OPERATING PRINCIPLES AND MAIN CHARACTERISTICS WILL BE DISCUSSED, AND SOME OF THE MAIN ACCESSORIES AND COMPONENTS USED FOR THE MECHANICAL CHARACTERISATION OF MATERIALS AND COMPONENTS WILL BE SHOWN AND COMMENTED ON. LABORATORY ACTIVITIES WILL BE CARRIED OUT WITH THE CLASS DIVIDED INTO GROUPS. EACH GROUP WILL BE REQUIRED TO PREPARE A FINAL REPORT ON THE LABORATORY ACTIVITIES PERFORMED, INCLUDING THE DESCRIPTION, ANALYSIS AND PRESENTATION OF THE RESULTS. THIS REPORT WILL BE DISCUSSED DURING THE ORAL EXAMINATION. BOTH PARTICIPATION IN THE LABORATORY ACTIVITIES AND THE FINAL REPORT WILL BE ASSESSED AND WILL CONTRIBUTE TO THE FINAL GRADE, AS SPECIFIED IN THE “ASSESSMENT METHODS” SECTION OF THE COURSE DESCRIPTION.

## Assessment methods

LEARNING OUTCOMES WILL BE ASSESSED THROUGH AN ORAL EXAMINATION BASED ON THE DISCUSSION OF A WRITTEN REPORT CONCERNING THE COURSE EXERCISES AND LABORATORY ACTIVITIES. THE REPORT IS PREPARED BY A GROUP COMPOSED OF A MINIMUM OF 2 AND A MAXIMUM OF 4 STUDENTS AND WILL INCLUDE THE EXERCISES CARRIED OUT ON MATERIAL TEST DATA PROVIDED BY THE INSTRUCTOR. THE GROUPS WILL BE FORMED AT THE BEGINNING OF THE COURSE AND WILL REMAIN UNCHANGED THROUGHOUT THE COURSE, EXCEPT IN SPECIAL CASES THAT WILL BE ASSESSED BY THE INSTRUCTOR ON A CASE-BY-CASE BASIS. THE REPORT WILL BE ASSESSED DURING THE EXAMINATION AND WILL CONTRIBUTE TO THE FINAL GRADE. STUDENTS WHO REGISTER FOR THE EXAMINATION MUST SEND THEIR LABORATORY REPORT TO THE INSTRUCTOR IN PDF FORMAT NO LATER THAN ONE DAY BEFORE THE EXAMINATION DATE. THE EXAMINATION IS STRUCTURED AS FOLLOWS: • ONE QUESTION ON THEORETICAL ASPECTS OF THE MECHANICAL CHARACTERISATION OF MATERIALS; • REVIEW OF THE LABORATORY REPORT, INCLUDING THE DISCUSSION OF THE EXPERIMENTAL SET-UPS IMPLEMENTED AND THE RESULTS OBTAINED. THE FOLLOWING ASPECTS WILL BE ASSESSED: A) THE ABILITY TO COMPARE MEASUREMENT TECHNIQUES AND CHARACTERISATION METHODS; B) CLARITY OF EXPOSITION AND APPROPRIATE USE OF TECHNICAL TERMINOLOGY; C) THE ABILITY TO USE GRAPHICAL REPRESENTATIONS WHEN DESCRIBING THE TOPICS; D) THE QUALITY OF THE SUBMITTED REPORT IN TERMS OF COMPLETENESS, RIGOUR IN DATA PROCESSING, ORIGINALITY AND EFFECTIVENESS OF THE GRAPHICAL PRESENTATION, AND CORRECTNESS IN THE INTERPRETATION OF THE RESULTS. THE EXAMINATION WILL BE CONSIDERED SUCCESSFULLY PASSED IF THE ABOVE ASPECTS DEMONSTRATE THAT THE STUDENT IS ABLE TO PROPOSE A MEASUREMENT AND CHARACTERISATION SET-UP WITH AWARENESS OF THE SPECIFIC MATERIAL TYPE AND MECHANICAL PROPERTY BEING MEASURED, THE POSSIBLE SOURCES OF ERROR, AND THE CORRECT INTERPRETATION OF THE RESULTS.
