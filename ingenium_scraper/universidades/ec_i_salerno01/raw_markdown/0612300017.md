## Learning objectives

COURSE OBJECTIVES THE COURSE AIMS TO PROVIDE THE FUNDAMENTAL ELEMENTS RELATED TO THE SIZING AND MANAGEMENT OF MECHANICAL INDUSTRIAL PLANTS. SPECIFICALLY, IT EXAMINES THE MAIN PERFORMANCE PARAMETERS OF THESE SYSTEMS, DIFFERENT LAYOUT CONFIGURATIONS, PROCUREMENT TECHNIQUES, AND METHODOLOGIES FOR THE OPERATIONAL MANAGEMENT OF RESOURCES WITHIN A MODERN SYSTEM FOR THE PRODUCTION OF GOODS AND SERVICES. THE MAIN KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED, RELATING TO THE DESIGN PROCESS, WILL BE: METHODS FOR CALCULATING THE CHARACTERISTIC PARAMETERS OF PRODUCTION SYSTEMS, CRITERIA FOR SELECTING THE OPTIMAL LAYOUT TYPE, FOR SIZING PRODUCTION RESOURCES, AND FOR EVALUATING THE ECONOMIC VIABILITY OF INVESTMENTS. FURTHERMORE, ADDITIONAL KNOWLEDGE RELATED TO MANAGEMENT PROCESSES WILL INCLUDE: CRITERIA FOR IDENTIFYING BOTTLENECKS, FOR CALCULATING INDUSTRIAL PRODUCT COSTS, FOR MANAGING MATERIAL PROCUREMENT, FOR CHARACTERIZING THE COMPONENTS OF LEAD TIME, FOR OPTIMIZING THE USE OF HUMAN RESOURCES, FOR STUDYING WORK TIMES AND METHODS, AND FOR MANAGING MAINTENANCE – INCLUDING TYPES OF MAINTENANCE POLICIES AND THEIR RESPECTIVE AREAS OF APPLICATION. THE MAIN SKILLS DEVELOPED WILL BE: DEFINING THE OPTIMAL LAYOUT AND SIZING OF PRODUCTION RESOURCES AND THEIR EFFICIENCY, APPLYING THE MAIN REPLENISHMENT PROCUREMENT TECHNIQUES, IDENTIFYING STANDARD TIMES AND THE NUMBER OF OPERATORS REQUIRED IN PRODUCTION PROCESSES, AND IDENTIFYING AND MANAGING BOTTLENECKS. JUDGEMENT AUTONOMY THE STUDENT ACQUIRES THE ABILITY TO INDEPENDENTLY FORMULATE THE LAYOUT OF AN INDUSTRIAL PLANT AND APPLY APPROPRIATE MANAGEMENT TECHNIQUES IN THE PRESENCE OF THE STUDIED PROBLEMS. COMMUNICATION SKILLS THE STUDENT ACQUIRES THE ABILITY TO EFFECTIVELY COMMUNICATE ON TOPICS RELATED TO THE CONFIGURATION OF AN INDUSTRIAL PLANT AND THE MAIN MANAGEMENT PROBLEMS. LEARNING CAPABILITY THE STUDENT ACQUIRES THE ABILITY TO INDEPENDENTLY LEARN MORE IN-DEPTH AND SPECIFIC COMPETENCIES IN THE FIELD OF DESIGN AND MANAGEMENT OF INDUSTRIAL PLANTS.

## Prerequisites

IN ORDER TO BENEFIT FROM THE COURSE TOPICS, PRELIMINARY KNOWLEDGE, ABOUT THE MAIN MANUFACTURING TECHNOLOGIES USED IN THE MANUFACTURING SECTOR AND THE MECHANICAL DESIGN, IS NEEDED. IN ADDITION, THE KNOWLEDGE OF BASIC COMPUTER SKILLS IS REQUIRED, SUCH AS FLOW DIAGRAMS AND THE DEVELOPMENT OF SIMPLE PROCEDURES.

## Teaching methods

THE COURSE INCLUDES THEORETICAL LECTURES AND IN-CLASS EXERCISES. THE LATTER, WHICH ALTERNATE WITH THE THEORETICAL LECTURES DEVELOPED THROUGHOUT THE COURSE, REFER TO THE CONCEPTS PRESENTED AND AIM TO TEACH NUMERICAL METHODS FOR TACKLING TYPICAL PROBLEMS IN THE DESIGN AND MANAGEMENT OF INDUSTRIAL PLANTS.

## Assessment methods

ASSESSMENT – DESIGN MODULE THE ASSESSMENT FOR THE DESIGN MODULE CONSISTS OF A WRITTEN AND AN ORAL EXAM. THE WRITTEN EXAM LASTS 30 MINUTES AND IS GRADED OUT OF 30. ADMISSION TO THE ORAL EXAM REQUIRES A SCORE OF 18/30 OR HIGHER. THE ORAL EXAM COVERS 2 TOPICS, AND THE GRADE IS ALSO EXPRESSED OUT OF 30. THE FINAL GRADE IS CALCULATED AS A WEIGHTED AVERAGE OF THE WRITTEN GRADE (1/3) AND THE ORAL GRADE (2/3), PLUS POTENTIAL HONORS (LODE). ASSESSMENT – MANAGEMENT MODULE THE ASSESSMENT FOR THE MANAGEMENT MODULE CONSISTS OF A WRITTEN AND AN ORAL EXAM. THE WRITTEN EXAM (LASTING 60 MINUTES) COMPRISES A VARIABLE NUMBER OF QUESTIONS ON 3 TOPICS FROM THE SYLLABUS. THE QUESTIONS, APPROPRIATELY STRUCTURED IN NUMERICAL FORMAT, ARE DESIGNED TO TEST COMPREHENSION OF THE THEORETICAL TOPICS COVERED DURING THE COURSE. THE NUMERICAL FORMAT OF THE EXAM QUESTIONS IS RANDOMLY GENERATED FOR EACH STUDENT TO DISCOURAGE COLLABORATION AND ALLOW FOR A MORE ACCURATE INDIVIDUAL ASSESSMENT. THE THRESHOLD FOR ADMISSION TO THE ORAL EXAM IS 18/30. THE FINAL GRADE FOR THE MANAGEMENT MODULE IS OBTAINED THROUGH A WEIGHTED AVERAGE OF THE WRITTEN EXAM GRADE (8/10) AND THE ORAL EXAM GRADE (2/10), EXPRESSED OUT OF 30. HONORS MAY BE AWARDED WITH A WEIGHTED AVERAGE SCORE OF 30. OVERALL EXAM GRADE THE OVERALL EXAM GRADE IS OBTAINED BY AVERAGING THE GRADES FROM THE DESIGN AND MANAGEMENT MODULES. HONORS ARE AWARDED IF A SCORE OF 30 IS ACHIEVED IN BOTH MODULES, AND HONORS ARE AWARDED IN AT LEAST ONE OF THE MODULES. PASSING GRADE CRITERIA A PASSING GRADE IS OBTAINED BY DEMONSTRATING SATISFACTORY KNOWLEDGE OF THE MAIN MODELS FOR INVENTORY MANAGEMENT, THE TOOLS FOR ANALYZING AND SOLVING THE MAIN PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE DESIGN AND MANAGEMENT OF INDUSTRIAL PLANTS, THE METHODS FOR SIZING THE MAIN COMPANY LAYOUTS, THE METHODS FOR RESOURCE MANAGEMENT, AND THE MEANING OF THE MAIN PERFORMANCE INDICATORS.
