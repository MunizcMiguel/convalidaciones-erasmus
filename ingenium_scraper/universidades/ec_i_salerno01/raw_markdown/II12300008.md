# ENGLISH LANGUAGE (II12300008)

## General information

- **Degree programme:** MECHANICAL ENGINEERING (II123)
- **Curriculum:** STUDY PLAN STANDARD
- **Year of study:** 1
- **Academic year of delivery:** 2026/2027
- **Period:** AUTUMN SEMESTER
- **ECTS:** 3
- **Hours:** LAB: 30
- **Scientific sector (SSD):** LINGUA
- **Type of activity:** A FOREIGN LANGUAGE, FINAL EXAMINATION
- **Area:** Knowledge of at least one foreign language
- **Exam type:** Combined written and oral exam
- **Grading:** FINAL JUDGMENT
- **Attendance:** Not mandatory
- **Teaching methods:** LAB

## Contents

The student's learning path, along with the relevant course content, will be determined based on their initial proficiency level, as assessed by the placement test.

## Learning objectives

Knowledge and understanding of writing and speaking in English, including technical discussions in the field of specialization. After obtaining the credits, the student has an English knowledge equivalent to the B1 level.

## Prerequisites

The starting level of knowledge will be verified by an entrance test. On the basis of the resut, the student will be enrolled to an appropriate course.

## Teaching methods

The courses are taught by the Centro Linguistico di Ateneo – University Language Center (http://www.cla.unisa.it/index) with the methods specified by the Center. Typically, the courses are organized according to the following scheme: 1. classroom lessons. 2. exercises on important points and targeted listening exercises. 3. specific projections on complex issues in the original language. 2. testing and exam simulation

## Other information

The Consiglio Didattico - Degree Program Board may recognize the credits on the basis of previously acquired Certification of English Language Knowledge if issued by a Certified Body in accordance with current legislation. To this goal, the student is invited to submit the documentation to the Consiglio Didattico.

## Assessment methods

Test to be held at the Centro Linguistico di Ateneo aimed at verifying the achievement of the objectives. Further information can be found at https://www.cla.unisa.it/verifiche/index
