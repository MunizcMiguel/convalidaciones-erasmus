## Learning objectives

First, the course aims to introduce the students to the main concepts related to computer science from both a "cultural" and a technological point of view. It introduces the student to the fundamental aspects of computer science, such as algorithms, languages, programs, the general structure of computers, and the representation of information. It then gives notions on the fundamentals of information theory and computer architecture. Furthermore, it aims at teaching the fundamentals of programming and the use of a programming language for a computer, the C language, as a tool for solving real problems.
