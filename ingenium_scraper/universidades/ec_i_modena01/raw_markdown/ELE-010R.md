## Learning objectives

Know the basics of the design of logical networks and the fundamental elements of the architecture of a digital computer. Learn the basics of assembly programming languages For the training objectives, please refer to the section on expected learning outcomes

## Prerequisites

n/a

## Teaching methods

Traditional blackboard lectures are used for the theoretical part and are supported by laboratory exercises. Lessons are in Italian. Finally, one or two seminars are organised.

## Other information

Knowledge and understanding Binary representation of information logic circuits computer architecture Applying knowledge and understanding skills using binary representation boolean logic Analysis and synthesis of simple logic circuits, both combinational and sequential ones Assembly programming skills Making judgements: Ability to recognize and evaluate computer's features and performance. Ability to find the best solution to a given problem. Communication skills The student should present and revise his knowledge, with a particular focus on the lexicon and technical jargon. Learning skills: students will learn a very low-level language, far to their usual abstraction level and intuitive developing schema. Moreover, they have to delve into collateral aspects of the discussed arguments.

## Assessment methods

Laboratory test (eligibility for the oral exam) + oral exam (which defines the final score). Both tests will be carried out at the end of the course. Laboratory test (1 hour): development of an IA32 assembly function that interfaces with a main program written in c (provided to the student). The test takes place using the OLJAsm platform, available to the student throughout the academic year, including lessons. The student can consult all and only the material available on the OLJAsm platform. During the test it is allowed to use the platform and Visual Studio as a programming IDE or notepad. In case of carrying out in the physical laboratory, the PCs will be suitably configured with firewalls and surveillance will be in attendance. If the test is carried out remotely, the platform will request proctoring activation by sharing the screen with the teacher and surveillance of the student by means of a camera active on videoconference with the teacher. Oral exam with about 3 questions concerning the theory of computers. 10 points each question. No material available.
