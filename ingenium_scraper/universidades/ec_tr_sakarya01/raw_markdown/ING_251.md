### Dersin Amacı

By the end of the course, learners will be able to acquire English language skills at 'B1' level , in accordance with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), that they may need in their education, professional life, and everyday activities.”

### Dersin İçeriği

The course content comprises printed and/or electronic materials and activities designed to develop students’ English language skills at B1 level, in accordance with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), for use in educational, professional, and everyday contexts.
