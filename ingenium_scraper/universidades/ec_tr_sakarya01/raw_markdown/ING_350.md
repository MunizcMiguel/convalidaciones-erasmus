### Dersin Amacı

By the end of the course, learners will be able to acquire B2-level English language skills, in accordance with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), that they may need in their education, professional life, and everyday activities.”

### Dersin İçeriği

The course content comprises printed and/or electronic materials and activities designed to develop students’ B2-level English language skills, in accordance with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), for use in educational, professional, and everyday contexts.
