## Learning objectives

The course depicts a complete scenario on the techniques and methodologies currently established for the design of automatic machines and industrial robots; it simultaneously proposes basic concepts for the choice of commercial solutions to tackle problems usually found on the field, as motion of mechanical organs (electric motors, actuators) and preserving the stiffness of complex assemblies.

## Extended programme

The course initially highlights the currently available robotic solutions in the industrial field, describing the system typologies and the corresponding market penetration rate. Fundamental aspects of automatic machine design are then considered like maintining the stiffness in assemblies, motion of organs and transmission throughout the machine (belts, gears, ball screws). Evidencing the available tools to monitor the activity of the machine is also foreseen, both with respect to points inside it (proprioceptive sensors) and in the external environment (heteroceptive sensors). Considering their importance in the field of automation, the course evidences field of application and types of electric motors (DC, AC, step). Additionally, some basic concepts for the design of pneumatic and hydraulic systems serving for automatic machines are highlighted, with particular focus on the choice criteria for pumps and motors. A final discussion regards indications on the Machine Directive and the CE marking, fundamental elements for the machine introduction on the market.
