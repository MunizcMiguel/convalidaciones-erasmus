## Partition: CALCOLO NUMERICO (Cognomi M-Z)

### Language

Italian

### Learning objectives

The course aims to provide the theoretical and practical skills required to use numerical methods and algorithms in an informed manner to solve complex mathematical problems using computational tools. Students will learn to evaluate the accuracy, stability, and computational complexity of numerical methods, as well as how to implement them in the MATLAB environment.  
  
With reference to the Dublin descriptors:  
  
Knowledge and Understanding  
  
(CC1) Knowledge of mathematical principles and understanding of the role of mathematical sciences as a tool for analyzing and solving problems and models underlying industrial engineering, and mechanical engineering in particular. Knowledge of computer science principles and of algorithmic and numerical approaches to problem-solving.  
  
Applying Knowledge and Understanding  
  
(CA1) The ability to apply mathematical methods—with particular reference to numerical analysis—to model, analyze, and solve engineering problems using computational tools.  
  
Making Judgements  
  
(AG1) Ability to analyze data and information using established methods and tools, interpret results, and draw coherent conclusions,  
also with the support of provided guidance.  
  
(AG4) Ability to organize one's work in compliance with assigned commitments and deadlines, managing available time and resources, realistically assessing one's abilities, and contributing to the prioritization of activities.  
  
(AG5) Ability to recognize one's knowledge limits and the need to acquire new skills, identifying appropriate information sources and guided learning opportunities.  
  
Communication Skills  
  
(AC2) Ability to present problems, solutions, analyses, and results in a clear and structured manner through written reports and oral presentations, following standard formats and procedures.  
  
(AC3) Ability to graphically represent and communicate technical data, results, and engineering solutions through the preparation of drawings, diagrams, graphs, and tables, using established tools and conventions.  
  
Learning Skills  
  
(AP1) – Ability to acquire and update knowledge in the relevant engineering field, independently, although primarily under guidance, by using established educational resources and learning tools.

### Contents

Finite precision computation.  
Numerical methods for solving scalar nonlinear equations.  
Numerical solution of linear systems.  
Data and function approximation.  
Quadrature rules.  
Programming with Matlab.

### Prerequisites

Fundamentals of linear algebra and calculus

### Teaching methods

Lectures and Matlab laboratory

### Extended programme

Algorithms. Floating point arithmetics. Finite precision. Norms of matrices and vectors.  
Conditioning of a problem.  
Stability of an algorithm. Direct methods for linear systems: Gauss method and pivoting strategies.  
  
Iterative methods for finding the roorts of a nonlinear equation:  
Bisection, Newton and Secant methods; corresponding algorithms.  
  
Polinomial interpolation, spline interpolation.  
Least-squares problems.  
Quadrature rules: mid-point rule, trapezoidal rule, Simpson rule, composite formulas.  
  
MATLAB: Handling matrices, operations between matrices. Function and script files.  
If, while and for commands.  
2D e 3D plots. Built-in functions for linear systems, nonlinear equations, basic fitting.
