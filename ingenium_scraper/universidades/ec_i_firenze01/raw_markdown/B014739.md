## Learning objectives

(CC1) Knowledge and understanding of both mathematical principles and the role of mathematical sciences as a tool for the analysis and the problem solving of mechanical engineering problems. Knowledge of the principles of computer science and the algorithmic and numerical approach to problems.  
  
(CA1) Applying knowledge and understanding related to mathematical methods - with particular reference to differential equations, large-scale linear and nonlinear systems - to model, analyze and solve engineering problems, also with the help of IT tools.  
  
(CT3) Development of an adequate expression and technical discussion of own arguments  
  
(CT7) To respect commitments and deadlines

## Contents

Direct methods for sparse linear systems. Iterative methods for nonlinear systems of equations. Numerical methods for the Cauchy problem.  
Numerical methods for boundary value problems. Numerical methods for partial differential equations.

## Extended programme

Factorization of sparse linear systems. Jacobi, Gauss-Siedel and SOR methods. Newton method and its variants, linesearch strategies. Fixed point iterations. Numerical methods for the Cauchy problem: explicit Runge-Kutta and Runge-Kutta-Fehlberg methods. Implicit Runge-Kutta methods.  
Finite difference methods for boundary value problems. Classification of partial differential equations. Finite difference methods for partial differential equations. Methods of lines. Basics of finite elements analysis.
