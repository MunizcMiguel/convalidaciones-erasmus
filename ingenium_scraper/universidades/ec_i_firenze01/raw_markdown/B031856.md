## Module: ELEMENTS OF STRUCTURAL MECHANICS (B024531)

### Language

Italian

### Learning objectives

The course aims to provide the necessary tools to address structural problems both in the design and verification phases. The objective is achieved through the study of some fundamental elements of solid mechanics (concepts of stress, strain, and constitutive relations) and their application to the analysis and verification of structures.

### Contents

Theory of Structures  
Differential equation of the elastic line. Force method.  
Continuum mechanics: introduction to stress and strain analysis, constitutive equations, elastic equilibrium, and strength criteria.  
The Saint-Venant problem.  
Normal stress, straight and oblique bending, eccentric normal stress, torsion, bending with constant shear.  
Stability of equilibrium.  
Euler’s formula.

### Prerequisites

Knowledge acquired in the courses of Rational Mechanics and Mathematical Analysis.

Lectures and exercises

### Extended programme

1. Characteristics and Arrangement of Constraints  
1.1 Characteristics and arrangement of supports, kinematics of constrained structures, statics of constrained structures.  
1.2 Fundamental equations of statics, auxiliary equations, characteristics of stress, indefinite equilibrium equations for one-dimensional solids, determination of stress characteristics for statically determinate structures.  
  
2. Axial and Bending Behavior  
2.1 Tension test; Hooke’s law, constitutive equation for axial strains, differential equation of the elastic line for axial strains.  
2.2 Bending beam; constitutive equation for bending strains, differential equation of the elastic line for bending strains.  
2.3 Virtual work theorem, calculation of displacements for isostatic structures.  
2.4 Force method  
2.4.1 Müller-Breslau equations  
2.4.2 Three-moment equation  
  
3. Continuum Mechanics  
3.1 Concept of stress, Cauchy’s hypothesis, stress tensor, equilibrium equations, principal stresses and directions.  
3.2 Infinitesimal strain tensor, strains and principal strain directions.  
3.3 Elastic behavior of materials, isotropic linear elastic constitutive relation, elastic problem.  
  
4. The Saint-Venant Problem  
4.1 Simple axial force case  
4.2 Straight bending case  
4.3 Oblique bending case  
4.4 Eccentric axial force case  
4.5 Torsion of compact, closed, and open sections  
4.6 Approximate shear theory  
  
5. Strength and Stability of Structures  
5.1 Tresca and Von Mises criteria, introduction to serviceability limit state and ultimate limit state  
5.2 Instability of elastic equilibrium
