## Extended programme

- Waves and Optics-  
Vibrating string, d'Alembert equation, Fourier analysis, sound waves, electromagnetic waves, electromagnetic spectrum. Plane and spherical waves. Poynting Theorem. Dispersion in optical materials. Reflection in metals.  
Interference, spatial and temporal coherence. Fraunhofer and Fresnel diffraction. Geometrical optics. Optical instruments.  
Harmonic oscillator, resonance, quality factor, tuning circuits, linewidth.  
Laser: operating principles, applications examples.  
  
- Introduction to quantum science and technologies -  
Quantum physics. Qubit. Quantum interference. Photon polarization, spin1/2 and Stern Gerlach experiments, two-level atomic systems, quantum dots.  
Formalism of quantum mechanics. Dirac notation. Operators and matrices. Postulates of quantum mechanics. Change of basis. Unitary and hermitian operators. The uncertainty principle. Density matrix. Representation of a two-level system on Bloch sphere. Equation of motion for Bloch vector. Quantum harmonic oscillator. Atomic level structure and spectra. Entanglement. Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen paradox. The second quantum revolution; Quantum technologies; quantum manifesto and quantum flagship.  
  
-Statistical methods and applications in Physics-  
Measurements in physics and measurement instruments  
Systematic and statistical errors  
Systematic error propagation  
Methods for statistical data analysis: probability and random variables, probability density functions, expectation values, statistical error propagation.  
Examples of probability density functions: binomial, Poisson, uniform, exponential, Gaussian, chi squared.  
Central limit theorem  
Overview of Monte Carlo methods  
Parameter estimation and the method of maximum likelihood  
Measurements in quantum mechanics: review of basic concepts; precise and imprecise measurements in quantum mechanics; state preparation via measurement. Uncorrelated and entangled quantum states.  
Description of relevant experiments  
Laboratory visit
