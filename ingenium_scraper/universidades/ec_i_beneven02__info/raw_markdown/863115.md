## Learning objectives

The use of natural language analysis and artificial intelligence techniques underlies many of the software systems we use every day, such as chatbots, personal assistants, automated translators, and analytics systems. This course aims to introduce the fundamentals of natural language processing, in particular, information retrieval, natural language parsing, and machine learning applied to natural language. Expected learning results: Knowledge and Understanding Upon successful completion of the course, students will understand the theoretical foundations and core algorithms of Natural Language Processing (NLP), Information Retrieval (IR), and Machine Learning applied to textual data. In particular, they will understand the main approaches to language representation, lexical, syntactic, and semantic analysis techniques, probabilistic and neural language models, Transformer architectures, and Large Language Models (LLMs), as well as the methodologies used to evaluate the performance of NLP systems. Applying Knowledge and Understanding Students will be able to design and develop Natural Language Processing applications using dedicated software tools and libraries, implementing text processing pipelines, Information Retrieval systems, and text classification models. They will also be able to use pre-trained language models, design effective prompts, integrate Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) techniques, and develop simple agent-based systems to address real-world problems. Making Judgements Students will acquire the ability to critically evaluate different NLP techniques for specific application problems, selecting the most appropriate models, text representations, training methodologies, and evaluation strategies. They will also be able to interpret experimental results, identify the limitations and challenges of different models, and assess aspects such as robustness, explainability, and the overall quality of the developed solutions. Communication Skills Students will be able to clearly and effectively communicate methodologies, algorithms, and results related to Natural Language Processing systems, using appropriate technical terminology in both written and oral form. They will also be able to present and justify their design decisions during the development of NLP applications and effectively discuss technical solutions with audiences possessing a computing background. Learning Skills Given the rapid pace of innovation in the field, students will develop the skills required to independently deepen their knowledge of advanced Natural Language Processing techniques and tools by consulting scientific literature and technical documentation. They will also be able to adapt to ongoing advances in generative artificial intelligence, Large Language Models, and agentic AI systems, continuously updating their professional knowledge and skills throughout their careers
