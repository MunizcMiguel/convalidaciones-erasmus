## Contents

- Introduction to databases and database management systems - Conceptual data models: Entity-Relations model, object model - Conceptual Database design - Relational data model: modeling concepts and notation of the relational model, relational algebra, normalization and normal forms - Logical Database design - SQL language: definition of data and integrity constraints, queries, data manipulation, views - Transactions: general concepts - Introductory notes on the physical organization of data and indexes - Introductory notes on database security and access control

## Learning objectives

The aim is to study the fundamental aspects relating to databases and databases management systems, to the design of databases, to the definition and execution of queries on a database. The course will deal with relational database systems, but the provided methods and principles have general validity and can also be applied in the case of models and systems other than relational ones. Knowledge and understanding By the end of the course, students will be able to design and implement non-trivial relational databases, as well as define and execute queries on data stored in a relational database. Applying knowledge and understanding Students will be able to apply the methodologies and technologies learned throughout the course. Judgment skills Students will be able to evaluate and interpret potential approaches for solving problems related to the design and implementation of a relational database. Communication skills Students will be able to communicate the topics covered in the course in a clear, rigorous, and critical manner. Learning skills Students will develop the ability to learn methodologies and technologies for the development of relational databases.

## Prerequisites

## Assessment methods

The achievement, by the students, of the objectives will be verified and evaluated by an exam consisting of a written exam and an oral discussion. The written exam, which has a maximum duration of three hours, consists in the conceptual and logical design of a moderate-sized database, including the development of SQL operations for creating and querying the defined database, and of open-ended theory questions and/or exercises. The subsequent oral discussion of the written exam may include exercises to be performed by the computer. The final grade is expressed in thirtieths.
