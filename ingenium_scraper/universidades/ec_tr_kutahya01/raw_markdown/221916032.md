### Objectives of the Course

The aim of the course is to teach undergraduate students of Mechanical Engineering how to directly or indirectly release, store, transport, render harmless to the recipient environment and contribute to the economy of all kinds of waste and residue in a way that will not harm the environment.

### Course Content

Types of solid waste, their impacts on the environment and disposal methods

### Course Methods and Techniques

The course is conducted through lectures, question-and-answer, discussion, problem-solving, and case studies. Solid waste characterization, collection and transport systems, recycling, composting, incineration, and sanitary landfilling are covered with mass balances and basic calculations. Relevant legislation and management strategies are examined. Within project-based learning, a small-scale solid waste management plan is prepared; group work, reporting, presentation, and site visit techniques are employed.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Explain the types, sources, composition, and characterization methods of solid waste. |
| 2 | Interpret the fundamental principles, hierarchy, and relevant legislation/standards of solid waste management. |
| 3 | Apply the design principles and calculations of solid waste collection, transport, and transfer station systems. |
| 4 | Explain the operating principles of disposal/recovery methods such as recycling, composting, biomethanization, incineration, and sanitary landfilling. |
| 5 | Analyze the solid waste quantity, composition, and appropriate management methods for a settlement or institution using mass balances and basic calculations. |
| 6 | Evaluate solid waste management systems in terms of environmental, economic, sustainability, and public health aspects, and develop appropriate solution proposals. |
