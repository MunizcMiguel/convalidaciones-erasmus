| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Understands the Atatürk reforms by delving into the historical roots of the Turkish revolution. |
| 2 | Drawing strength from Turkish history and the Turkish National Struggle, she/he stands up for her state and nation. |
| 3 | Accurately identifies internal and external threats directed at the Republic, secularism, and the Turkish nation, and becomes knowledgeable about these threats. |
| 4 | It develops policies tailored to them by getting to know Türkiye. |
| 5 | She/He is aware of the difficulties encountered during the establishment of the Grand National Assembly of Turkey.(dişil) |
| 6 | Understands the challenges of the National Struggle. |
| 7 | It analyzes the terms of the Treaty of Sèvres. |
