### Objectives of the Course

The aim of the course is to teach the political and cultural events that took place from the foundation of the Ottoman Empire to its collapse, to examine the diplomatic relations of the Ottoman Empire with other states, to analyze the social and cultural dynamics in the Ottoman Empire, and to learn the organization of the state.

### Course Content

Political and cultural history of the Ottoman Empire from its foundation to its collapse.

### Course Methods and Techniques

The course is conducted through lectures, question-and-answer, discussion, chronology and map studies, primary/secondary source analysis, and text reading and interpretation. Documentary/film analysis, student presentations, group work, short written assignments/reports, and a final exam are used. The political, social, economic, and cultural dimensions of Ottoman history are examined through case studies.
