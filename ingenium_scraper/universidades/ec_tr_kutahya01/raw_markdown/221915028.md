### Objectives of the Course

Introduction to Automatic Control: System, control and automatic control; variable types (to gain knowledge about input-output, control, disruptive variables)

### Course Content

Basic definitions and characteristics of Control Systems, Open and Closed Loop Control, Simple Control Modes, P,PI,PID Control, Analysis of physical systems using Laplace, Time response, Signal Flow Diagrams, State equations, Linearization, Adjusting PID Parameters, Stability and criteria, Locus of Roots, Frequency response concept, Modeling and simulation of control systems with MATLAB
