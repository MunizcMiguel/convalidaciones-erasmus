### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to enable students to understand the reverse engineering process holistically; teaching them the stages of measurement, digitization, data processing, CAD modeling, and additive manufacturing, starting from a physical object, both theoretically and practically. Furthermore, it aims to enable students to evaluate different measurement techniques, process point cloud data, and create functional engineering models from this data.

### Course Content

This course covers an introduction to reverse engineering, contact and non-contact measurement systems, and laser and optical scanning methods. It examines point cloud data processing, mesh generation, and surface modeling techniques. Furthermore, software used in reverse engineering is introduced, and additive manufacturing methods and their integration into production are explained. Supported by practical exercises, the course aims to develop students' skills in creating CAD models and producing prototypes from scan data.
