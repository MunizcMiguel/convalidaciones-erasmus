### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to equip mechanical engineering students with the competency to transform their engineering knowledge and skills into innovative business ideas and sustainable commercial ventures. It aims to enable students to understand the technology-oriented entrepreneurship ecosystem and to plan and manage engineering, production, financing, and management processes encountered during the lifecycle of a product or service, using a holistic project management approach.

### Course Content

The course covers; fundamental concepts and types of entrepreneurship, creativity and business idea generation techniques in engineering, Business Model Canvas, comprehensive business plan preparation (market research, production/R&D plan, financial plan, and management plan), examples of successful and unsuccessful ventures in mechanical engineering, organizational culture, communication, and entrepreneurship networks. On the project management side, the course covers; project life cycle, logical framework approach, scheduling and charting (Gantt, PERT/CPM), budgeting, control and reporting techniques, and project risk management (FMEA and risk matrix).

### Course Methods and Techniques

The course is conducted through lectures, question-and-answer, discussion, case studies, and project-based learning. Entrepreneurship ecosystem, idea development, business model canvas, value proposition, market and feasibility analysis, SWOT/PEST analysis, risk management, resource planning, budgeting, and financial feasibility are covered with applied examples. In project management, work breakdown structure (WBS), Gantt charts, PERT/CPM, agile and traditional approaches are used. Teamwork, entrepreneurship simulation, presentations/pitching, reporting, and feedback techniques are employed.
