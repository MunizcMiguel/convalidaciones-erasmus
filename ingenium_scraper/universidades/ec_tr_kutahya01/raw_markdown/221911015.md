### Objectives of the Course

To train students about history of manufacturing engineering, sub-disciplines of mechanical engineering, skills necessary for a degree in manufacturing engineering and curriculum, and career opportunities in

mechanical engineering.

### Course Content

History of mechanical engineering, its areas of interest and its relationship with the other engineering disciplines. Sub-disciplines of mechanical engineering, design, materials, mechanical and thermal

sciences.
