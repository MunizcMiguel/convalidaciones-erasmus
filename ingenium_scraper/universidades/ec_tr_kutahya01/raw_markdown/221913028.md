## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Students with problems related to resource and design can be solved using the principles of the course. |
| 2 | In addition to theoretical and practical knowledge of welding methods, she knows the basic concepts of welding technology |
| 3 | Provides the ability to recognize problems related to welding techniques, propose solutions and develop practical solutions. |
| 4 | Calculates stress/strength in welded constructions. |
| 5 | Compares welding with other industrial-scale manufacturing methods. |
| 6 | Designs welded joints. |
