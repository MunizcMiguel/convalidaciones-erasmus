### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to enable students to learn the concepts, basic principles, and rules of occupational health and safety and their development in the world and in Türkiye; to recognize physical, chemical, biological, and psychosocial risk factors that threaten employees in working environments; and to develop proactive measures against occupational accidents and diseases. The course also aims to provide students with knowledge and awareness of health and safety principles, health surveillance, safety signs, and personal protective equipment used to protect employees.

### Course Content

Development of occupational health and safety concepts and rules in the world and in Türkiye; physical risk factors; chemical risk factors; biological risk factors; psychosocial risk factors; occupational accidents and proactive measures against work accidents; health surveillance and occupational diseases; health and safety principles in workplaces; health and safety signs; personal protective equipment and its use for the protection of employees.
