### Objectives of the Course

During the course, students learn the definition of statistics, frequency tables, probability, variation analysis, and regression analysis. The aim of this course is to equip students with the ability to interpret problems statistically.

### Course Content

Data analysis, probability, probability distributions, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, regression, correlation, ANOVA.

### Course Methods and Techniques

Lecture, question and answer, discussion, problem solving
