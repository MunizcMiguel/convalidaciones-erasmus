### Objectives of the Course

Students being able to draw solid models, assembly and manufacturing drawings in computer environment.

### Course Content

Creating of sketches, solid models from sketches, assemblies from solid models by using a CAD program. Creating of manufacturing drawings from solid models or assemblies.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Students can create two-dimensional (2D) sketches. |
| 2 | Students can create three-dimensional (3D) solid models. |
| 3 | Students can create three-dimensional (3D) assemblies. |
| 4 | Students can create manufacturing drawings. |
| 5 | Students can examine the geometric properties of the parts and assemblies they design and modify their designs using the analysis and visualization tools of CAD software. |
