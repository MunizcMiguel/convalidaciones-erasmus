### Objectives of the Course

Students being able to perform machining operations of any part for CNC using a CAD/CAM package.

### Course Content

C-axis turning operations. Mill-turn operations. Multi-axis operations. Feature based CAM applications. DNC sytems and operating principles. Sending application of the CNC part programmes to CNC machines.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Explains the fundamental features, interfaces, and operating principles of contemporary CAD/CAM systems. |
| 2 | Selects and applies appropriate CAM strategies for turning, C-axis turning, mill-turn, and multi-axis machining operations. |
| 3 | Creates, simulates, and optimizes toolpaths for CNC manufacturing using a feature-based CAM approach. |
| 4 | Transfers prepared CNC part programs to CNC machines through DNC systems and carries out the manufacturing process. |
| 5 | Plans the manufacturing of a given part using integrated CAD/CAM and CNC systems, determines appropriate machining methods, and evaluates the manufacturing process. |
