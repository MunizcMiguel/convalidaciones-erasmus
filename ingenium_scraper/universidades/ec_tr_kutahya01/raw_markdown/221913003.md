## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Analyzes two- or three-dimensional systems under the influence of forces or moments |
| 2 | Learns the basic concepts and principles of statics and be able to use them in solving problems. |
| 3 | Calculates center of gravity, moment of inertia, and resultant forces |
| 4 | Calculates support reactions in structural systems |
| 5 | Analyzes structural systems such as trusses, beams and cables and draw their internal force diagrams |
