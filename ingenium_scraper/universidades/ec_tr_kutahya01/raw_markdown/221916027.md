### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to introduce the students nontraditional manufacturing processes and advanced applications in traditional manufacturing processes.

### Course Content

Modern applications in traditional manufacturing processes (Minimum quantity lubrication (MQL) technology, Cryogenic cooling technology), mechanical nontraditional manufacturing processes, thermal nontraditional manufacturing processes, chemical and electro chemical nontraditional manufacturing processes, hybrid nontraditional manufacturing processes
