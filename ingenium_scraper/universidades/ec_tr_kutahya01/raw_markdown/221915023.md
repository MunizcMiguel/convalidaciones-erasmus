### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to provide students with an understanding of the basic principles, operating principles, and system components of heating and ventilation systems; to develop their ability to determine heating loads and ventilation requirements, select appropriate systems and equipment, and perform basic design and sizing calculations. The course also aims to enable students to evaluate indoor air quality and comfort conditions and develop sustainable engineering solutions by considering the energy efficiency and environmental impacts of heating and ventilation systems.

### Course Content

This course covers the basic principles of heating and ventilation; the operating principles of heating and ventilation systems; determination of heating loads and ventilation requirements; basic system components such as boilers, heaters, pumps, fans, piping, and duct systems; hot-water, steam, and warm-air heating systems; natural and mechanical ventilation systems; air distribution systems; indoor air quality and thermal comfort conditions; basic design and sizing principles of heating and ventilation systems; and energy-efficient and sustainable heating and ventilation applications.
