### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is providing heat and work relations, which are main fields of engineering interests, basics of energy conversion and engineering applications

### Course Content

Main principles of thermodynamics, measurement methods of pressure and temperature, temperature scales, zeroth law of thermodynamics, Pure substance ,phase-change processes, The ideal gas equation ,Heat and Work
