### Objectives of the Course

The general aim of this course is; The aim is to provide students with basic machining information and explain in detail the processes of using hand tools, drilling, turning, milling and grinding.

### Course Content

Used in machining; hand tools, drill, shaper, etc. introduction of machine tools; Use of cutters and tools. Operation types and cutters used in these machines. Sharpening drills and lathe tools.

## Weekly Detailed Course Contents

| Week | Topics | Study Materials | Materials |
