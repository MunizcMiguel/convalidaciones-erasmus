### Objectives of the Course

Be accomplished with the basic knowledge of conventional steam boilers. Be equipped with the basic knowledge of efficiency of boilers Have the basic knowledge of fuels and combustion. Be equipped with the basic knowledge on boilers auxiliary elements Acquire a capacity to analyze and design of boilers. Be accomplished with the basic knowledge of chimney

### Course Content

This course includes the introduction of steam boilers and the auxiliary components of boilers.
