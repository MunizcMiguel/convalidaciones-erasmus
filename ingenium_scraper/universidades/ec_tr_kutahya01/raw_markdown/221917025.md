### Objectives of the Course

Providing students with the ability to understand and speak sign language to enable active and effective communication with disability groups they may encounter in their professional lives.

### Course Content

To teach sign language used by hearing-impaired individuals and to gain the necessary skills to use the language in social life.

### Course Methods and Techniques

The course is conducted through lectures, demonstration, practice, role-play, pair/group work, video watching, sign practice, gamification, and feedback methods. The Turkish Sign Language (TİD) alphabet, basic vocabulary, sentence structure, hand shapes, facial expressions, and body language are developed through practical exercises. Deaf culture and communication ethics are also included.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Recognize and use the Turkish Sign Language (TİD) alphabet and basic hand shapes. |
| 2 | Express basic vocabulary and frequently used daily expressions in sign language. |
| 3 | Explain the sentence structure, grammatical features of TİD, and its differences from Turkish. |
| 4 | Use facial expressions, gestures, and body language effectively in sign language communication. |
| 5 | Gain awareness of Deaf culture, the communication needs of hearing-impaired individuals, and communication ethics. |
| 6 | Communicate interactively at a basic level in sign language and express themselves. |
