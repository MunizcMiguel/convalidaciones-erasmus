### Objectives of the Course

Motor vehicle concept, historical development and classification; Vehicle characteristics and energy balance; Engine characteristics and power requirement of vehicles; Clutches, gearboxes, transmission shafts; Differential, axles and tire mechanics; Vehicle aerodynamics; Forces in vehicle motion.

### Course Content

To learn the vehicle technology concepts required in engineering education and to gain the ability to use them.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | The fundamental structures of vehicles, including their engines and drivetrains, are defined and explained. |
| 2 | The engine, transmission, suspension, steering, brakes, and electrical and electronic systems are analyzed. |
| 3 | Basic engineering calculations for vehicle systems are performed, and appropriate design criteria are determined. |
| 4 | Current developments in alternative fuels, electric, and hybrid vehicle technologies are interpreted and compared. |
| 5 | Solutions to engineering problems encountered in vehicle technology are developed, and maintenance and repair processes are evaluated. |
