### Objectives of the Course

It is aimed to help students gain the fundamental knowledge of mechanics, statics and dynamics during their engineering education

### Course Content

Physics and measurement, Motion in one dimention, Vectors, Motion in two dimension, Rules of Motion, Circular Motion,Work and Kinetic Energy, Potantiel Energy and Conservation of Mechanical Energy, Linear Momentum and Collisions, Rotation of a Rigid Body

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Explains measurement and basic unit systems |
| 2 | Performs operations related to vector quantities. |
| 3 | Defines the concepts of position, displacement, velocity and acceleration related to motion. |
| 4 | Examines the motion of an object in 1, 2 or 3 dimensions. |
| 5 | Understand the basic equations of motion with constant acceleration. |
| 6 | Understands the inertial properties of objects and Newton's laws of motion and how to use them. |
| 7 | Explains the concepts of work, kinetic energy, potential energy and conservation of mechanical energy. |
| 8 | Explains the concepts of impulse, momentum and collision. |
