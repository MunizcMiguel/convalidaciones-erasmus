### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to enable students to systematically identify and assess hazards in working environments, anticipate potential risks, and develop appropriate control and prevention measures. The course aims to provide students with knowledge and skills related to the design of ergonomic working environments, identification of necessary occupational safety inspections and documentation, prevention and response to fire and emergency situations, assessment of occupational health and safety risks arising from technical equipment and work activities, and understanding the rights and obligations of employees and employers within the legal framework of working life.

### Course Content

Risk management and risk assessment; ergonomics and the arrangement of working environments according to human physical and cognitive characteristics; occupational safety inspections and required documentation; fire risks, fire prevention, and emergency response; principles of ventilation and air conditioning; occupational health and safety in pressure vessels; occupational health and safety in welding and electrical work; occupational health and safety in lifting equipment and motor vehicles; occupational health and safety in hand tools and maintenance and repair work; employee and employer rights and obligations and labor law.
