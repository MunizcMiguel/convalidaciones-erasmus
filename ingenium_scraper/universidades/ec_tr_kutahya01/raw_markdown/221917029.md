### Objectives of the Course

Within the scope of this course, it is aimed that the students acquire basic knowledge by explaining the powder metallurgy method production stages, advantages, disadvantages, points to be considered in part design.

### Course Content

Powder metallurgy advantages, powder production and characterization, powder forming methods, sintering, additional processes.
