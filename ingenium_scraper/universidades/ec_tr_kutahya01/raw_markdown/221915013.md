### Objectives of the Course

To teach the fundamental principles of computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) systems; to provide knowledge and skills in preparing toolpaths for CNC lathes and CNC milling machines based on models created in a CAD environment, determining cutting tools and machining parameters, creating CAM operations, performing simulations, and applying them on CNC machines.

### Course Content

Basic concepts and working principles of CAD/CAM systems. Two and three-dimensional part modeling for CAM applications. Preparation of face turning, profile machining, grooving, drilling, and threading operations for CNC lathe operations. Tool selection, tool offsets, cutting parameters, and toolpath strategy creation. 2, 2.5, and 3-axis CAM operations for CNC milling operations, profile, pocket, grooving, surface machining, and drilling applications. Toolpath simulations, collision checks, post-processor use, NC code generation, and machining applications on multi-axis CNC machines.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Explains the concepts of CAD and CAM, the data flow between them, and their use in CNC turning and milling manufacturing processes. |
| 2 | Creates and edits two and three-dimensional part models using CAD software to suit CAM operations. |
| 3 | Plans and creates appropriate CAM operations for CNC turning operations (face turning, internal/external diameter machining, grooving, drilling, and threading). |
| 4 | Plans and execute s profile, pocket, groove, surface machining, and drilling operations using 2, 2.5, and 3-axis CAM operations for CNC milling. |
| 5 | Determines appropriate cutting tools, cutting parameters, and tool approach-plunge-retreat strategies in turning and milling CAM operations; evaluates the machinability of the created tool paths on CNC machines by verifying them with simulation. |
