### Objectives of the Course

To provide students with information on clean water, waste water, hot water, fire and rain water installations, building installation locations and sewage systems.

### Course Content

To design the entire water installation of a building

### Course Methods and Techniques

The course is conducted through lectures, question-and-answer, and problem-solving methods. Key calculations (flow rate, pressure loss, pipe sizing, pump selection) are reinforced with numerical examples. Students carry out a small-scale sanitary plumbing design under project-based learning using TS/EN/DIN and relevant codes.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Explain the basic components, working principles, and relevant standards/codes (TS, EN, DIN, etc.) of sanitary plumbing systems. |
| 2 | Perform flow rate, pressure loss, pipe sizing, and pump selection calculations for cold and hot water plumbing systems. |
| 3 | Apply design principles and calculation methods for wastewater, vent, and rainwater drainage systems. |
| 4 | Prepare schematic and layout drawings of a sanitary plumbing project in compliance with architectural plans. |
| 5 | Select plumbing components (pipes, fixtures, valves, pumps, tanks, heaters, etc.) according to system requirements, safety, and energy efficiency criteria. |
| 6 | Analyze a sanitary plumbing system holistically, evaluate problems encountered, and develop solution proposals. |
