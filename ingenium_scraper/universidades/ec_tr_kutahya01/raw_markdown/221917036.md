### Course Content

Polymer materials. Polymer chemistry. The synthesis of the polymer. Polymerization processes. properties of polymers, usage and classification. Polymer additives and fillers, polymer processing methods. Thermosets, elastomers, thermoplastics and their processing and production methods.
