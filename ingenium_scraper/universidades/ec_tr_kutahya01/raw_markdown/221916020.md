### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to teach students the basic principles of heat transfer and heat transfer mechanisms, and to develop their ability to mathematically analyze heat transfer problems involving conduction and convection and perform basic engineering calculations. The course aims to enable students to analyze steady-state and transient heat conduction in different geometries, heat transfer from finned surfaces, and forced and natural convection, and to solve engineering problems by using appropriate equations and correlations.

### Course Content

This course covers the basic concepts and principles of heat transfer; fundamental equations and mechanisms of heat conduction; one-dimensional steady-state heat conduction; heat conduction through plane, cylindrical, and spherical walls; heat transfer through composite walls and thermal resistances; fins and fin efficiency; transient heat conduction; basic principles of convective heat transfer; the concept of boundary layers; forced and natural convection; dimensionless numbers and convection correlations; and basic engineering calculations and applications of heat transfer problems.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Explains the basic principles of heat transfer and the mechanisms of heat transfer by conduction, convection, and radiation, and uses the fundamental heat transfer equations. |
| 2 | Analyzes steady-state heat conduction in plane, cylindrical, and spherical geometries and calculates heat transfer, temperature distributions, and heat losses using the thermal resistance approach. |
| 3 | Analyzes transient heat conduction and determines temperature variations and heat transfer using the lumped capacitance method and appropriate analytical and graphical methods for one-dimensional systems. |
| 4 | Analyzes heat transfer from extended surfaces (fins) and performs calculations of fin temperature distribution, heat transfer, efficiency, and effectiveness. |
| 5 | Analyzes heat transfer by forced and natural convection using boundary layer concepts and dimensionless numbers, and develops solutions to heat transfer problems using appropriate correlations and engineering calculation methods. |
