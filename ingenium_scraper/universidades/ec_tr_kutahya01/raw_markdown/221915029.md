### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to teach the definitions and rules related to the preparation and implementation of project details for natural gas appliances supplied by natural gas distribution networks and installed inside buildings, including calculations of installation pipe sizes, flue and ventilation cross-sections, as well as the selection of other installation components, and to develop students’ ability to solve problems encountered in natural gas installations.

### Course Content

This course covers the basic properties of natural gas and LPG; the production, processing, storage, and distribution of natural gas and LPG; the operating principles of natural gas and LPG fuel systems; combustion characteristics and combustion calculations of these fuels; basic components of natural gas and LPG installations, including pipes, valves, regulators, meters, and safety devices; the principles of design, sizing, and basic engineering of natural gas and LPG installations; gas-fired appliances and their applications; ventilation and flue systems; gas leak prevention, safety, and emergency procedures; and energy efficiency, environmental impacts, and sustainable gas fuel technologies.
