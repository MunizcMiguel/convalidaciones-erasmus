### Objectives of the Course

To teach the properties of the language, to teach the historical periods of Turkish, to enable the learners to understand the vocal and and structural properties of Turkish and use the punctuation well.

### Course Content

Historical of Turkish language, our language phonology and morphology, the types of words, rules of spelling and punctuation, expression abnormalities.

### Course Methods and Techniques

Lecture, Q&A, discussion, problem solving
