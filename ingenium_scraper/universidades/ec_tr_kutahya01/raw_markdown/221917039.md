| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|

| 2 | Analyzes the mechanical properties and design criteria of power transmission components. |
| 3 | Calculates torque, speed, efficiency, and endurance related to powertrains. |
| 4 | Compares mechanical, hydraulic, and electric powertrain systems and evaluate their appropriate applications. |
| 5 | Provides solutions to engineering problems arising in powertrains and to plan maintenance and repair processes. |
