### Objectives of the Course

The aim is to study the mechanical behavior of engineering materials, teach the basic principles of destructive and non-destructive testing methods, and develop the ability to select the appropriate testing method.

### Course Content

Topics covered include the classification of engineering materials, deformation and fracture behavior, tensile, compressive, bending, hardness, impact, fatigue, and creep tests, metallographic examination, and the basic principles and applications of visual, penetrant, magnetic particle, eddy current, ultrasonic, phased array ultrasonic, radiographic, and acoustic emission testing methods.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Classifies engineering materials and analyzes their deformation, fracture, and mechanical behavior from an engineering perspective. |
| 2 | Plans, implements, and interprets destructive testing (tensile, compressive, bending, hardness, impact, fatigue, creep, etc.) tests according to standards. |
| 3 | Prepares and examines the microstructure of materials using metallographic methods and relates it to mechanical properties. |
| 4 | Compares non-destructive testing methods and selects the appropriate testing method for different engineering applications. |
| 5 | Analyzes data obtained from material tests, uses them in solving engineering problems, and presents them in a technical report. |
