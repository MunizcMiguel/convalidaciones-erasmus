### Objectives of the Course

To gain the ability to design suitable parts for casting, design runners and feeders by using basic sciences and technological tools. To understand the casting methods and the differences between them and to be able to choose a method for casting a particular part. To introduce all materials and equipment used in casting. Defines tattoo. Categorizes forging methods.

### Course Content

Casting introduction, classification Casting part design, Runner, feeder design, Mold materials, properties and molding, Core preparation, Sand mold casting, Gypsum and Ceramic mold casting, Investment casting, Pressure casting, Foam model casting, Wax casting, Continuous casting, Melting and foundry furnaces, Casting alloys. Forging methods, its classification. Forging machines.
