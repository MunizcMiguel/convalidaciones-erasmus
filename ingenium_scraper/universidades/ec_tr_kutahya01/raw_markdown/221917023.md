### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to enable students to understand the historical development of science, the contributions of different civilizations to scientific progress, the formation of scientific thought, and the relationship between science and technology; and to raise awareness of the historical foundations of engineering practices.

### Course Content

This course covers the fundamental concepts of the history of science; the origin of science; scientific developments in the civilizations of Mesopotamia, Egypt, India, China, Greece, and the Islamic world; the Renaissance and the Enlightenment; the Scientific Revolution; the Industrial Revolution; the modern understanding of science; the relationship between science, technology, and society; the historical development of engineering sciences; and scientific ethics.
