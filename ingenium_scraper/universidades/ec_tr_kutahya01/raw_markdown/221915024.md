### Objectives of the Course

The purpose of this course is to give the students ability of preparing production plans, programs, schedules and developing and solving inventory models in production organizations.

### Course Content

General structure of production planning, inventory policies, inventory costs, discounts, lot-sizing in continuous and periodic review with dynamic demand, scheduling problems.
