## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Knows the flow types and configurations of heat exchangers (cross-flow, shell-and-tube, compact, etc.). |
| 2 | Understands the advantages and disadvantages of heat exchangers based on their applications. |
| 3 | Understands heat exchanger calculations (log mean temperature difference, effectiveness-NTU method). |
| 4 | Understands the characteristics of shell-and-tube heat exchangers. |
| 5 | Understands the application areas of heat exchangers in industrial facilities. |
