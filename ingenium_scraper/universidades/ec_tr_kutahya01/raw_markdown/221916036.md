### Objectives of the Course

With this course, students will learn about the basic concepts of fuels and combustion, classification and physicochemical properties of fuels, general properties sought in fuels, combustion equations, stoichiometric analysis of combustion reactions, solid, liquid and gaseous fuel combustion, combustion in engines, calculation of emission values of incomplete combustion products, emission values and environmental pollution.

### Course Content

Transfer of basic information about the chemical and physical properties of solid, liquid and gaseous fuels and the combustion systems of these fuels

### Course Methods and Techniques

The course is conducted through lectures, question-and-answer, problem-solving, and case studies. Fuel properties, combustion stoichiometry, air-fuel ratio, adiabatic flame temperature, combustion products, and emissions are covered with numerical examples. Laboratory applications, project-based learning, group work, reporting, and presentations are employed; simulation/analysis software is used when necessary.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Explain the classification and physical/chemical properties of fuels (heating value, density, viscosity, moisture, ash, volatile matter, etc.). |
| 2 | Write combustion reactions for stoichiometric and actual combustion conditions; calculate air-fuel ratio, equivalence ratio, and excess air coefficient. |
| 3 | Determine the composition and amount of combustion products using mass and mole balances; interpret flue gas analysis. |
| 4 | Calculate adiabatic flame temperature, heat of combustion, and combustion efficiency based on thermodynamic fundamentals. |
| 5 | Explain combustion-related pollutants (CO, NOx, SOx, particulates, volatile organic compounds, etc.) and their formation mechanisms. |
| 6 | Evaluate different fuels and combustion systems in terms of efficiency, emissions, and environmental impact, and recommend suitable fuel/combustion conditions. |
