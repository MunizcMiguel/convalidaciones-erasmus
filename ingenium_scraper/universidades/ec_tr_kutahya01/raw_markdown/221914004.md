### Objectives of the Course

To introduce basic strength concepts and to demonstrate simple analytical methods for calculating the strength of simple structural elements under various loads.

### Course Content

In this course, the basic concepts of stress and strain - Description of bars and their strength analysis - Strength analysis under axial loading, torsion and bending - Design criteria are explained.

### Course Methods and Techniques

Lecture, question and answer, discussion, problem solving
