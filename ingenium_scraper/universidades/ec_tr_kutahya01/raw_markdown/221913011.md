### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to introduce students to fundamental manufacturing processes such as machining and non-machining methods, casting, plastic forming, welding, powder metallurgy, and additive manufacturing; to enable them to understand the relationship between materials and manufacturing methods; to equip them with the competence to select the appropriate manufacturing method according to technical and economic criteria; to evaluate production quality; and to develop solutions to manufacturing problems encountered in engineering applications.

### Course Content

The course includes casting, plastic forming methods, structure of metal materials, plastic deformation mechanisms, types of deformation, manufacturing methods such as machining, forging, rolling, extrusion.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Bir makine parçasının tasarım ve fonksiyonel gereksinimlerini (geometri, tolerans, malzeme özellikleri vb.) analiz ederek uygun imalat yöntemini teknik ve ekonomik ölçütlere göre seçebilir. 866 By analyzing the design and functional requirements (geometry, tolerances, material properties, etc.) of a machine part, the appropriate manufacturing method can be selected according to technical and economic criteria. |
| 2 | It can analyze casting and powder metallurgy processes by comparing them in terms of solidification and sintering kinetics, microstructure development, and production defects. |
| 3 | It can compare welding, soldering, and bonding methods in terms of their working principles, connection characteristics, and quality requirements, and select the appropriate joining method. |
| 4 | It can evaluate the effect of process parameters on the final microstructure and mechanical properties based on temperature (hot, warm, cold) and deformation mechanisms in plastic forming methods. |
| 5 | It can compare machining and additive manufacturing methods in terms of surface quality, production cost, material usage, and design requirements, and select the appropriate production method. |
