### Objectives of the Course

It is aimed to help students gain the fundamental knowledge of mechanics, statics and dynamics during their engineering education

### Course Content

Coulomb Force, Electric Field, Electric Flux, Gausss Law, Electrical Potential, Capacitors, Current Formation and Resistor, Constant Current, Circuits of Direct Current, Kirchhoffs Laws, Magnetic Field, Biot-Savarts Law, Induction,Faradays Law, Lenzs Law

### Course Methods and Techniques

Lecture, Question-Answer, Discussion, Problem Solving
