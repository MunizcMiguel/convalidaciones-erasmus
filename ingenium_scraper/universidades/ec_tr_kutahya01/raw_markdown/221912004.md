### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to improve students’ English listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills at the CEFR A2–B1 level and enable them to meet their basic communication needs effectively in daily, academic, and professional contexts.

### Course Content

This course covers basic English communication functions in daily, educational, professional, and social contexts. Throughout the course, listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills are integrated through themes such as vacations, movies and entertainment, shopping, health, work life, technology, and future plans. Language structures including comparisons, present perfect, adjective forms, quantity expressions, time clauses, and everyday communication patterns are practiced through communicative tasks. The coursebook consists of 12 units with review/bonus sections after every three units.

### Course Methods and Techniques

The course is conducted through communicative language teaching, task-based learning, interactive lecturing, question-answer, pair and group work, role-play, listening-speaking practice, reading comprehension activities, guided writing tasks, video-supported instruction, technology-enhanced learning, and individual study. The speaking-focused design, video components, and the “one page = one lesson” format of Smart Choice 2 directly support these methods.
