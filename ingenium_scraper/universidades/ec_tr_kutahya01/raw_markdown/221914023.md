### Objectives of the Course

The CNC Machine Tools course aims to enhance students' professional competence in modern manufacturing technologies by providing them with theoretical knowledge and practical skills in the working principles, programming techniques, tool selection and determination of machining parameters, and machine operation of CNC lathes and milling machines.

### Course Content

CNC machines, application areas. Structure of CNC machines. Introduction of the CNC turning centres and programming of them. M and G codes. Turning cycles. Introduction of the CNC turning simulation

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Explains the basic structures and safe working principles of machine tools. |
| 2 | Explains the cutting tool systems and tool-work referencing processes used in CNC lathes and CNC milling machines. |
| 3 | Knows the program codes used in CNC machines and their meanings, and performs control panel operations. |
| 4 | Write programs using cycles and subroutines. |
| 5 | Students process workpieces using the programs they wrote on CNC lathes and CNC milling machines. |
