### Objectives of the Course

To learn the thermodynamic laws and concepts (work, heat, intrinsic energy, entropy, etc.) and acquire ability to apply some systems to thermodynamic laws.

### Course Content

The basic concepts of thermodynamics, the state equations, the laws of thermodynamics, thermodynamics systems, the thermodynamics potentials and itheir’s application of the simple systems.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Understands the basic concepts of thermodynamics and energy transformations. Applies the zeroth law of thermodynamics to the relationship between temperature and thermal equilibrium. |
| 2 | Understands the properties of pure substances. Relates phase change to material properties. Identifies and calculates state quantities. |
| 3 | Applies the relationships between pressure, temperature, and specific volume for gases. Determines properties using the ideal gas equation and calculates the work done during state changes. |
| 4 | Applies the First Law of Thermodynamics to cycles, closed and open systems, and performs system analysis. Calculates heat and work interactions as well as mass and energy balances. Determines heat and work relations for cycles in closed and open systems. |
| 5 | Defines the Second Law of Thermodynamics, applies it to a cycle and heat pumps, and calculates cycle efficiency and coefficients of performance. |
| 6 | Defines and determines entropy and entropy change. Demonstrates the principle of entropy and the increase of entropy, and applies it to thermodynamic systems |
