### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to use the knowledge gained in undergraduate education to find a solution to an engineering problem and to realize it as a product.

### Course Content

Determination of Topics / Formation of Project Groups / Planning and Division of Labor / Resource Research and Literature Review / Classification and Analysis of Resources / Determination of Tools and Methods to be Used / Analysis and Modeling / Design and Verification / Procurement and Application / Experiments and Tests / Analysis and Interpretation of Results / Problems Detection and Resolution / Reporting and Presentation

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Ability to define a complex engineering problem, determine an analysis method for its solution, and design the system or product to be presented |
| 2 | Ability to use appropriate tools and collect and process necessary data for complex problem analysis and solution |
| 3 | Ability to participate effectively in project work, write and present technical reports |
| 4 | Ability to follow developments in science and technology while solving complex problems, awareness of acting in accordance with standards and ethical principles |
| 5 | Ability to plan the project process, allocate tasks, use time and resources effectively, and evaluate project outcomes in accordance with the defined objectives. |
