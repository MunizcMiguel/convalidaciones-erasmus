### Objectives of the Course

To Introduce the approaches and mathematical models used in kinematic and dynamic analysis of machinery.

### Course Content

Mechanisms, kinematic diagrams, kinematic chains and mobility. Some basic mechanisms. Kinematic analysis and synthesis of mechanismsKinematics of gear tooth and cam mechanisms.

### Course Methods and Techniques

The course is conducted through lectures, question-and-answer, and problem-solving methods. Position, velocity, acceleration, and force analyses of mechanisms are covered using both graphical and analytical methods.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Explain the fundamental concepts of mechanisms, kinematic pairs, degrees of freedom, and kinematic chains. |
| 2 | Perform position, velocity, and acceleration analyses of planar mechanisms using graphical and analytical methods. |
| 3 | Evaluate force, moment, friction, and inertia effects in mechanisms through static/dynamic analysis. |
| 4 | Design/synthesize mechanisms such as four-bar linkages, cams, and gear trains for given motion requirements. |
| 5 | Solve mechanism design and analysis problems individually or in teams, and report and present the results. |
