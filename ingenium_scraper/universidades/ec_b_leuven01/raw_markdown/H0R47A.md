# Materials for Energy Conversion and Storage

Studiepunten (ECTS): 6

## Course objectives

This course provides a comprehensive view of the materials and fundamental principles underlying energy storage and energy conversion technologies.
It firstly covers fundamentals of electrochemical systems such as supercapacitors, batteries, fuel cells, electrolysis-based e‑fuels, and photoelectrochemical conversion devices focusing on their operating principles, material requirements, performance limitations, and recent breakthroughs.
Then, the course focuses on energy conversion mechanisms for energy harvesting. Light energy conversion includes photovoltaic technologies (established and emerging), mechanical energy conversion is addressed through piezoelectric and triboelectric materials, and thermal energy conversion focuses on thermoelectric and thermionic effects.
Emphasis is placed on the link between materials, physical principles, and device performance, supported by exercise sessions that apply the concepts to real energy systems..

## Content

Part 1: Fundamentals of electrochemistry
Introduction to physical concepts in electrochemistry
Electrode/electrolyte interface: energetic treatment and equilibrium
Theory of charge transfer over electrode/electrolyte interface
Electrode kinetics and mass transfer
Specifics for tortuous and porous electrodes
Introduction to solid-state electrochemistry
Part 2: Materials for energy storage
Principles and materials science aspects of batteries
Ion-intercalation batteries: Li-ion batteries
Na-ion batteries, solid-state batteries
Next generation batteries: Conversion materials, metal-air batteries
Interface engineering and interphase control for battery performance
Green hydrogen and e-fuels as energy carrier
Fuel cells
Part 3: Materials for energy harvesting
Light energy harvesting:
Si photovoltaic cells
Thin film cells (CdTe and CIGS)
Emerging technologies (organic and perovskites)
Mechanical energy harvesting
Piezoelectricity and materials
Triboelectricity and materials
Thermal energy harvesting
Thermoelectrics and materials
Thermionic and materials
Thermogalvanic devices
