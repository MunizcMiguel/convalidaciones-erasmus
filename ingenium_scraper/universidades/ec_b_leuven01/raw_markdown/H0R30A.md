# Traffic Flow Operations and Control

Studiepunten (ECTS): 6

## Course objectives

Can explain the fundamental traffic flow characteristics and traffic flow variables and their empirical distributions and correlations
Can process basic traffic flow data (Eulerian and Lagrangian data) and extract relevant traffic features
Can explain traffic flow models at different aggregation levels (macro, meso, micro), can derive their properties and can solve these models analytically and numerically for simplified problem instances
Can explain the working principles of selected traffic control measures based on road-side control, vehicle-to-infrastructure control or in-vehicle control and automation

## Content

10 courses on traffic flow operations (theory + models + data)
traffic variables, capacity, headway distributions, fundamental diagrams (FD)
macro: traffic flow models (1
st
order, higher order), cell transmission model (CTM), basic nodes (merge/diverge)
micro/meso: car-following, stability, lane-changing, gap acceptance, microsimulation, delay distributions
traffic data: cumulative plots, Adaptive smoothing method (ASM), floating car data (FCD), trajectory data, estimation of FD and capacity, Level of Service
other traffic types:
- pedestrians, cyclists, traffic without lane discipline, air traffic, bird swarms, drone formations, conveyor belt,…
8 courses on traffic flow control
traffic signal control (basic)
ramp metering, mainstream traffic flow control, variable speed limits, Specialist
autonomous and cooperative vehicles: cooperative cruise control, lane-change management
2 courses introducing students to mobility demand-side modelling and other more advanced mobility modelling techniques
