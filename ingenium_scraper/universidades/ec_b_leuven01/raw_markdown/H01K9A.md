# Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics

Studiepunten (ECTS): 6

## Course objectives

This course aims at an insight into the underlying physico-chemical principles for the behaviour of pure substances and mixtures and for phase equilibria and chemical processes in which matter may be involved.
This is indispensable prior knowledge for many aspects from chemical engineering, more in particular for the courses 'Scheidingsprocessen' and 'Chemische reactorkunde en bioreactorkunde'.

## Entry requirements

General and technical chemistry, part 1, especially with regard to 'Het chemisch evenwicht'

## Content

1. Scope and language of thermodynamics
– set the context for chemical engineering thermodynamics, a brief recap of concepts including first law, entropy and second law, open and closed systems.
2. Equations of state and intermolecular forces
Introduce various intermolecular forces for pure substance and corresponding states.
Describe Equations of states and their applicabilities.
Describe and calculate EoS for mixtures, understand the mixing rules.
3. Thermodynamic properties and relations
Introduce the key thermodynamics properties and partial derivatives.
Describe the thermodynamic property relations; how to apply them in solving first and second law problems.
Understand the role of departure functions and use them to calculate the changes in thermodynamic states.
4. Phase equilibria as a basis for separation processes
Describe and calculate different phase equilibria for mixtures: L-V and L-G equilibrium; L-L separation and L-L-V equilibrium; solubility of a solid and partition-equilibrium.
Vapor pressure depression, boiling point elevation, freezing point depression and osmotic pressure.
Equilibrium concerning surface phenomena.
5. Chemical reactions in one or more phases, possibly combined with phase equilibria
Study the thermodynamics of the chemical equilibrium and P & T dependency;
Multiple simultaneous chemical reactions; heterogenous chemical reactions;
Chemical reactions and phase equilibria combined.

## Content

The seminars of this course will serve as interactive sessions to demonstrate solutions to selected and adapted questions from the textbook and lectures. Students will also have the opportunity to ask questions.
The content of the seminars would be synchronised with the lectures on:
1. Basic thermodynamic laws and thermodynamic state properties;
2. Type of intermolecular interactions and methods to quantify them such EOS.
3. Thermodynamic properties and relations.
4. Phase equalibria criteria and fugacity, and applications of phase equalibria in multiple scenarios.
5. Chemical reactions and their combination with phase equilibria.
