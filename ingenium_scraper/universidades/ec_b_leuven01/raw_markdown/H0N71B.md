# Soil Mechanics

Studiepunten (ECTS): 6

## Course objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
Explain the basic principles of Soil Mechanics both theoretically and experimentally.
Understand the specific characteristics of soil as a porous medium (including pore water).
Evaluate and quantify groundwater flows.
Design conventional shallow foundations and quantify the soil pressure on vertical walls.
