### Objectives of the Course

To realize the importance of new and modern materials in terms of devoloping techmology. Teaching the properties, production processes,applications and design of these materials.

### Course Content

Introduction to Composite Materials. Unidirectional Composites. Transformation of Stress and Strain. Micromechanics of Lamina. Macromechanics of Lamina. Macromechanics of Laminat. Strength of Composite Materials.
