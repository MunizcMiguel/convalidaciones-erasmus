### Objectives of the Course

The main objective of this course is to introduce the students the basic design methods of feedback control systems using root locus and frequency response techniques.

### Course Content

Feedback Control Systems, Frequency Response, Design and Compensation, Root Locus Technique

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Student learns the basic concepts of root locus (RL) and its interpretation |
| 2 | Student gains the basic principles of feedback sensors, constructs models and integrates them with the control system block diagram. |
| 3 | Student observes the behavior of the control system designed by the use of a computer program. |
| 4 | Student learns the basic principles of Bode diagram technique and has knowledge of integrating into a control system. |
| 5 | Students gains the knowledge of designing P, PD, PD ve PID controllers. |
