### Objectives of the Course

Aim of the course is to explain vector analysis, linear equations of a system and complex functions in engineering.

### Course Content

Fundamental vector analysis. General integrals. Green, Stokes and Gauss thorems. Linear equations of a system. Eigen values and vectors. Complex functions.
