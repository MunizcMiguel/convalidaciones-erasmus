### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to inform the students about the foundry processes and properties of the equipments that are used, molding and core making methods, casting techniques, casting defects and cast irons.

### Course Content

Patternes, molding sand properties, molding methods, core properties, core making methods, solidification. Casting techniques, casting defects, cast irons.
