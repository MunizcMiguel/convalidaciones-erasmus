### Objectives of the Course

In this course the principles of production tecnologies are introduced. The basic principles of conventional machining and cutting theory are presented. Programming techniques of CNC machines are given. The differencies between conventional and unconventional machining techniques are also examined and perspectives of future machining technology are probed.

### Course Content

Metal removing, shaper,planer, turning, milling,drilling, grinding, honing, lapping, machinability,material handling and fixtures, gear manufacturing, metal saw, CNC machines and programming techniques, unconventional machining processes, chemical processes, electrochemical processes,modern mechanical machining techniques,thermal processes, welding.
