### Objectives of the Course

In this course, principles of mechanical vibrations are taught. The applications and importance of mechanical vibrations in the mechanical engineering field are presented. Analysis of mechanical vibrations and vibration isolation techniques are introduced.

### Course Content

Basic principles of mechanical vibrations, mechanical system components vibrated, 1-dof systems, damped and undamped vibrations, free and forced vibrations, torsional vibrations, system's response to stress, vibration isolation, vibrations of multi-dof systems.
