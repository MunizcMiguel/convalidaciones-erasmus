### Objectives of the Course

To introduce to the basic concept of physics, to develop skills formulating certain physical quantities in areas of the mechanics and solving the problems. To illustrate the necessity and importance of physics for other branches of natural sciences and engineering through applications in real life, and industry and technology.

### Course Content

Physics and Measurement; Vectors; Motion in One and Two Dimensions; Newton’s Laws of Motion; Circular Motion and Other Application of Newton’s Laws; Work and Kinetic Energy; Potential Energy and Conservation of Energy; Linear Momentum and Collisions; Rotation of a Rigid Object About Fixed Axis; Torque and Angular Momentum.
