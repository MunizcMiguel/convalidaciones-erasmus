### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to provide students with a comprehensive knowledge and awareness of addiction and to enable them to become conscious and

sensitive individuals in this regard. At the same time, it aims to contribute to the development of students as healthy individuals and citizens who are beneficial to society.

### Course Content

This course will provide students with basic knowledge and skills on basic concepts of addiction; types of addiction (substance use disorders and behavioral

addictions); causes and effects of addiction; addiction in law; policy and strategy methods in addiction prevention; communication, stress and coping skills, healthy living.

### Course Methods and Techniques
