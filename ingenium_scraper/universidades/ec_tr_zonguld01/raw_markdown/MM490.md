### Objectives of the Course

The main objective of the lecture are to provide the students to have some general knowledge about additive manufacturing and products and their properties, applications, features, fabrication method and its processing conditions

### Course Content

Giving an overall introduction about additive manufacturing to students.Technical terminology in additive manufacturing. Fundamental concepts about SLS, SLA, DLP, filament materials and 3D printer design. Fundamental new manufacturing method. General info about working fields of additive manufacturing
