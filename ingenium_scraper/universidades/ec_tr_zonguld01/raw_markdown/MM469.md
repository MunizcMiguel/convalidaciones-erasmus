### Objectives of the Course

Details of this course is to learn methods of welding and welding capabilities.

### Course Content

Welding Technology and Methods Development, Electric Arc Welding Systems and Equipment, Arc Method of Combustion and Formation of electrode,Covered Electrode Selection, Welding Machines,Methods for Gas Welding (MIG, MAG, TIG),Gas Welding and Oxygen Cutting Melting.
