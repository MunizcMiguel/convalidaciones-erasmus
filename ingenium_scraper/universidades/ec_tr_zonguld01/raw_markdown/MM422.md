### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this lesson is to give information to the students about presentation of continuous and discontinuous working transport instruments which are used in the machine industry, usage properties of transport tools, selection the member of the system, sizing and projecting of transport tools.

### Course Content

Bridge cranes (single and double girder cranes, lifting and transmission on mechanisms, cars, rails and wheels); elevator institution (human and load lifts); lift traffic, safety principles on lifts and durability calculations.
