### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to teach the basic concepts of automatic control, to give the principles of control techniques, and to show the usage and importance of automatic control in industry

### Course Content

Introduction, modelling, Laplace transform, first order systems, block diagrams, electric, mechanic, fluid and thermal systems and their models, frequency response, root locus.
