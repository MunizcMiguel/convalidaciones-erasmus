### Objectives of the Course

The objective of this course is providing students with the basics of air conditioning, cooling load, air conditioning systems; the ability of designing air conditioning systems.

### Course Content

Introduction of air conditioning. Moist air properties and psychometrics diagram... Thermal comfort and indoor air quality. Cooling load methods. CLTD/SCL/CLF method. Conditioning processes. Air conditionings systems. Simultaneously heat and mass transfer. Cooling tower, heat and mass transfer for dehumidifying surfaces.
