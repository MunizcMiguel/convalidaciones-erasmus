### Objectives of the Course

Applications using related softwares and computers in mechanical engineering design is introduced.

### Course Content

Phases of design, computer aided drafting, solid modeling, assemblies and technical drawings, analysis of dynamic systems, kinematic synthesis and optimization, virtual prototypes, computer aided manufacturing.
