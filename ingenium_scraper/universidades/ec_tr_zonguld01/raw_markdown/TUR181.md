### Objectives of the Course

Turkish Language Lesson aims to show students the characteristics of the Turkish language and the rules of operation with examples; to give them the ability and habit of expressing their feelings, thoughts, designs, impressions, observations, experiences in verbal and written form, accurately and effectively; to develop their vocabulary through written and spoken texts; to teach the rules of correctly understanding the texts they read or the programs they listen to; It aims to develop language skills, which are the basis of communication between individuals and masses.

### Course Content

This course will focus on the effective and correct use of written communication tools by students. Various types of written expression will be examined and analyzed from a critical point of view by working on comprehension, narration, reading and writing activities in the course. Punctuation marks and spelling rules, which are the basis of written expression, will be perceived, and correct use of these rules will be ensured for effective and robust expression.
