### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is providing students with the basic subjects related to the combustion phenomena.

### Course Content

Definitions related to combustion. Application areas of combustion process. Solid, liquid and gaseous fuels. Combustion theory. Combustion of solid, liquid and gaseous fuels. Chemical thermodynamics. Chemical equilibrium. Solid liquid and gas fuels combustion systems.
