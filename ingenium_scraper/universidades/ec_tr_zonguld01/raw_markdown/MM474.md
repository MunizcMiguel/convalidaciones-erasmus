### Objectives of the Course

Within the scope of this course:

It is aimed to gain a broad and robust understanding of revealing the technological error behind the failure, determining and preventing the causes of failure of materials that used in engineering applications.

### Course Content

Definition of failure. Failure analysis expertise. Technological failures. Types of failures. Causes and mechanisms of failure. Identification of failures. Failure analysis stages. Fracture. Fracture types. Fatigue. Corrosion Hydrogen embrittlement. Creep. Wear. Failure case studies.
