### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to provide engineering students with information about what Systems Engineering is, its scope and objectives, and to lay the foundations of systems engineering, thereby educating them about design, production processes and methods, starting from the concept stage.

### Course Content

Within the scope of Systems Engineering education;

The education covers the fundamentals of systems engineering throughout the product life cycle, from the initial emergence of a need to the disposal of the product. Information is provided on System Engineering, which is an interdisciplinary and integrative field that enables the successful implementation, use, and disposal of systems by defining, validating, and validating the processes followed in the design of complex systems, as well as the requirements, architectures, and designs of complex systems.
