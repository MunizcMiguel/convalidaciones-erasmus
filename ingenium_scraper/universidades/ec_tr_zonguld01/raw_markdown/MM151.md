### Objectives of the Course

This course aims at giving an overall introduction to mechanical engineering for the first year students. Through the course it is intended that the participants will gain analytical thinking, creative enterprising, questioning and team working capabilities. Preparing freshman mechanical engineering students for next level classes and informing them about the concept of mechanical engineering

### Course Content

Giving an overall introduction about mechanical engineering to the first year students.Technical terminology in mechanical engineering. Fundamental concepts about machine elements, materials and machine design. Fundamental mechanical strenght tests. General info about working fields of mechanical engineering
