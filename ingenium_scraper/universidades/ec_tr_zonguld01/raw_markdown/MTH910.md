### Objectives of the Course

The lectures will be given by the relevant unit managers

for each subject, and the students will be provided to learn the experiences of the field experiences of the real

application as well as the theory. With the application before and after the course in the program, it will be ensured that the information learned in the lessons will be reinforced. Thus, while gaining theoretical knowledge, they will also gain field experience.

### Course Content

Each stage of the integrated iron and steel production process will be discussed in detail and the holistic structure will be conveyed by processing the side processes that feed the production process.

## Course Learning Outcomes: Upon the successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| No | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|
| 1 | Knows the application process of integrated iron and steel process. |
| 2 | Knows raw material sources and usage areas and their effect on quality. |
| 3 | Knows the sinter process and its necessity. |
| 4 | Knows what coking is and its process. |
| 5 | Knows the methods of obtaining liquid raw iron, slag and recycling methods. |
| 6 | Knows steel production process and quality. |
| 7 | Knows what affects the quality of steel. |
| 8 | Knows casting technique and types. |
| 9 | Knows the types of rolling and rolling products. |
| 10 | Knows alternative energy sources and environmental measures taken. |
| 11 | Knows what, how much and how to produce in the production process and how planning is done. |
