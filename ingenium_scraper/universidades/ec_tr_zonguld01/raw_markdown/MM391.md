### Objectives of the Course

The aim of these lessons is to provide students information on design methodology, design philosophy and systematic approach to design problems.

### Course Content

Describing mechanical design problems and process, Understanding the problem and the development of engineering specifications, Determine requirements list, Elementary functions, create subfunction descriptions, Techniques of problem solutions, create and refine interfaces for functions, Product generation, Product evaluation and Value engineering, Design for assembly, Material and process selection in design, Design practices.
