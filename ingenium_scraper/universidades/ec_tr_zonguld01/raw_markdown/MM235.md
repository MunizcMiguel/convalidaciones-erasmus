### Objectives of the Course

To teach the basic principles of classical thermodynamics. To train students to identify, formulate and solve engineering problems related to thermodynamics.

### Course Content

The system and its environment, properties of a system, system balance, change of state, thermodynamic equilibrium, the ideal-gas equation of state, energy. The exchange of energy between the system and environment, change of state and thermodynamic process, reversible and irreversible processes, simple system enthalpy, specific heat at constant volume and constant pressure, pure substance, relations between pressure, specific volume and temperature . The first law of thermodynamics, the first law of thermodynamics and the closed system, the first law of thermodynamics, and open systems. The second law of thermodynamics, entropy, numeric value of entropy .
