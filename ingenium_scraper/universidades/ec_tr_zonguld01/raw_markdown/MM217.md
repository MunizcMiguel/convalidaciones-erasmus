### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is to teach the methods of finding analytical solutions to common first and second order ordinary differential equations.

### Course Content

Definition, types and classification of differential equations, first order differential equations, second order differential equations, operator method for constant coefficient equations, differential equation systems, laplace transformations.
