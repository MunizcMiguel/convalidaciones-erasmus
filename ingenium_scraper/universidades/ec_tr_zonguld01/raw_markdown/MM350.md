### Objectives of the Course

To teach the methods used in the manufacturing process and the technology used in these methods. To gain the ability to reveal how all input parameters affect output parameters in these methods used in the manufacturing process with an engineering approach. To gain the ability to design processes in industrial production from raw material input to product production.

### Course Content

Classification of Manufacturing Methods: (Comparison, Advantages and Limits)

Metal Cutting Theories and Components: (Cutting Force and Energy Requirement, Cutting parameters, Cutting tool properties, Machinability, Cutting Fluids etc.)

Machining methods: (Turning, Milling, Drilling, Grinding etc.)

Advanced Manufacturing Methods: (Mechanical energy, Heat energy, Electrical energy, Chemical energy and Electro chemical energy are used methods.)

Computer Aided Manufacturing Technology: (CNC Machines and Programming Fundamentals)
