### Objectives of the Course

This course is like the beginning to the fluid mechaniccs for engineering disciplines. Within the scope of the course, conservation laws which are the basis of fluid mechanics are taught. In additon, the use of the conservation laws for the development of methods for analyzing simple flowing systems are mentioned.

### Course Content

Introduction and Basic Concepts, Properties of Fluids, Fluid Statics and Applications, Fluid Kinematics and Reynolds Transport Teorem (RTT)
