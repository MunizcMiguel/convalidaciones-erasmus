### Objectives of the Course

Training students about applying second law to thermodynamics systems, analysing power and refrigerationn cycles and specifying thermodynamics properties and solving engineering problems.

### Course Content

Open systems and second law of thermodynamics, efficiency, analysis of thermodynamics processes. Gas power cycles, vapor cycles, refrigeration cycles, gas mixtures, chemical reacitons.
