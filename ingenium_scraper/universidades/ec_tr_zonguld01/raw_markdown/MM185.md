### Objectives of the Course

Especially the concept of computer hardware - software issues and programming, graphics applications, and introduce students to issues related to the algorithms

### Course Content

Introduction to Computer (definition, historical development, types of computers, computer hardware (motherboard, processor, memory, hard drive, video card, sound card, network card, modem, cd / dvd drive / writer drive, floppy drive, keyboard, mouse, monitor, speaker, microphone, printer, scanner, plotter), Operating systems, office programs and applications, word processors (Microsoft Word, and applications), data and graphics processors (Microsoft Excel and applications).
