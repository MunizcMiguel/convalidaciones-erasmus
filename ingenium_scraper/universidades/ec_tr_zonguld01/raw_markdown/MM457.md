### Objectives of the Course

Teaching students gas turbine cycles used in aircraft and other areas, principles and analysis.To inform the students about the main components of gas turbines. Present advantages and disadvantages of gas turbines compared to other energy producing machines.

### Course Content

Basic concepts, ideal cycle analysis, gas dynamics, one-dimensional steady and unsteady flow, the actual performance of gas turbine cycles, radial and axial compressors, combustion systems, axial flow turbines, gas turbine performance analysis.
