### Objectives of the Course

Learning the basic principles and features of the measurement process; It is suitable for all kinds of physical and electrical measurements that may be encountered. to be able to choose measuring instruments, to make appropriate connection forms; Ability to perform error-free measurements.

### Course Content

Basic principles of measurement. Measuring physical quantities. Expansion of measuring ranges of measuring instruments.
