### Objectives of the Course

While fossil energy resources are decreasing in the world, energy costs will increase much more. The aim of this course; to give the students what can be done to minimize these costs.

### Course Content

Energy economy in sanitary installation, energy economy in air conditioning systems, energy economy in heating systems and thermal insulation
