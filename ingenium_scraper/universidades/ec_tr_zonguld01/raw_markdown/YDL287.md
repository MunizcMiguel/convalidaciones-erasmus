### Objectives of the Course

The course will improve intellectual skills such as reading texts about their profession, guessing meanings of new words in texts by using “skimming” and “scanning” techniques, reading for gist and reading for specific information. This course will prepare ground for improving students’ vocabulary knowledge , coping with complex language structures in advanced translation studies which they will make in YDL286 course and making presentations.

### Course Content

This course includes reading skills and word and term knowledge mostly.
