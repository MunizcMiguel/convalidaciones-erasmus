### Department / Program

### Type of Program

Formal Education

### Type of Course Unit

Compulsory

### Course Delivery Method

Face To Face

### Objectives of the Course

To have students gained the background for electricity and magnetism necessary in their education

### Course Content

Coulomb’s force, the electric field, electric flux, Gauss’s law, electric potential, capacitors, current and resistivity, direct current circuits, Kirchhoff’s rules, magnetic field, Biot-Savart’s law, Ampere’s law, induction, Faraday’s law, Lenz’s law
