### Objectives of the Course

To create drawing of mechanical elements and assembling in CAD.

### Course Content

Making isometric perspective and 3D drawings and dimensions with Autocad, Introduction to Solidworks software. Relations, Dimension, adjustment, Drawing Templates. create of 3D solids modeling and section views. create of 2D views from 3D solids parts. create of 3D assembly . Illustration of surface finish and tolerance symbols.
