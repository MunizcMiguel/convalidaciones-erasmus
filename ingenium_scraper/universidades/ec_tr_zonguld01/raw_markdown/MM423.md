### Objectives of the Course

Aim of the course are the investigation of the thermal behaviour of the electronic devices and the systems; and also, providing the ability to design these systems under various constraints.

### Course Content

Introduction to thermal design in electronic devices and fundamental principles of thermal design. Thermal conduction in electronic systems. Steady state and transient thermal conduction in electronic devices. Thermal conduction in extended surfaces and heat sink design. Thermal convection and radiation heat transfer in electronic devices. Principles of natural convection and free convection in parallel plates. Phase change heat transfer in electronic systems. Conjugate heat transfer in electronic devices. Alternative electronics cooling methods. Heat transfer and fluid flow in microchannels. Computer aided electronic systems thermal design.
