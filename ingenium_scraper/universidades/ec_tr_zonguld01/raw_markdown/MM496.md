### Objectives of the Course

Basic knowledge of Computational Fluid Dynamics and the use of commercial CFD software

### Course Content

Introduction to CFD and CFD Procedure / Basic CFD Equations / Meshing and Boundary Conditions / Basic Numerical Methods and Techniques / CFD Solution Analysis and Turbulence Modeling / Commercial Software - Geometry Generation, Meshing, Analysis and Display of Results
