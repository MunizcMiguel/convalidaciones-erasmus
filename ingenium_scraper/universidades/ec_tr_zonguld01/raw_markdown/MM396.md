### Objectives of the Course

Application of vectors and tensors possesses main points to solve problems ranging from mechanics to general relativity.

### Course Content

1. Vectors

1.1 Cartesian unit vectors

1.2 Non-cartesian unit vectors

2. Vector operations

2.1 Del operator

2.2 Grad

2.3 Div

2.4 Curl

2.5 Laplacian

3. Vector applications

3.1 Curvilinear motion

3.2 Electric and magnetic fields

4. Variant components

4.1 Coordinate transformation

4.2 Non-orthogonal coordinate transformation

4.3 Covariant and contravariant vector components

5. Higher-rank tensors

5.1 Definitions

5.2 Tensor derivatives and Christoffel symbols

5.3 Covariant differentiation

6. Tensor applications

6.1 The inertia tensor

6.2 Tensor applications in solid mechanics

6.3 Tensor applications in fluid mechanics
