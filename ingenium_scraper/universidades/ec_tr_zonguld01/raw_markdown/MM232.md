### Objectives of the Course

To know the most important types of steel and cast iron alloys used in industry, properties and phase diagrams. To know the heat treatments applied in the industry and the time-temperature-transformation diagrams. To konow the heat treatment faults. To know the materials standards.

### Course Content

Steel, cast iron. Phase diagrams. Heat treatments. Standards.
