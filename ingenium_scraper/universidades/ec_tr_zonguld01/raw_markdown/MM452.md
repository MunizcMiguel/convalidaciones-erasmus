### Objectives of the Course

Applications using related softwares and computers in mechanical engineering design is introduced.

### Course Content

Modifying design and iterations, optimization, optimization using finite element method, optimization for dynamic systems, design of complicated systems, virtual prototyping, and template based design, solving non-linear problems with finite element model, solving forming process with finite element method.
