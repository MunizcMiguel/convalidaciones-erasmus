### Objectives of the Course

Mechanical power transmission elements (Gears, belts and belts drives, chains) order for the detailed design work of students

### Course Content

Gear terminology and basic concepts. Gear series. Spur, helical and bevel gears.Worm gears, planetary gear mechanisms, mechanisms of belt drives and chain mechanisms.
