### Objectives of the Course

This course is designed to provide information about analysis of electrical and electronic circuits and to create of the basic electrical and electronics engineering knowledge for Mechanical Engineering students.With the basic concept of current, voltage and power,the impact of capacity, inductors and resistors in constant current circuits are examined.

### Course Content

Basic Principles of Electricity. Circuit Analysis. AC Circuits. Phasors. Principle of Electromechanics. AC Power. Three Phase Systems. Transformers. Magnetic Circuits.
