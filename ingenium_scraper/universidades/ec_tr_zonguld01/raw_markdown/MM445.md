### Objectives of the Course

To describe the basic features of pumps and turbines for engineering applications and to introduce its importance. To teach and to apply the methods used in the analysis of engineering problems involving fluid.

### Course Content

Descriptions of power, power losses and efficiency. Pumps in series and parallel connections. Similarity, specific speed. Thoma number, cavitation effect and the reaction machines. Thoma number, cavitation and its effects on reaction machines. Pumps, centrifugal and axial pumps. Constructions, characteristics, operating information and calculations of centrifugal pumps. Constructions, characteristics, operating information and calculations of axial pumps. Water turbines, Pelton turbines, a special type of impulse and reaction turbines, Francis, Kaplan and propeller turbines.
