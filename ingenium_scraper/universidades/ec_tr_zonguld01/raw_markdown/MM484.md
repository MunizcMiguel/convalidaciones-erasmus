### Objectives of the Course

To explain the basic information of human engineering.To explain the development and goals of human engineering. To indicate the necessity of human factors on the design of equipments apart from technology. To explain the importance of worker health and safety.

### Course Content

Introduction to Human Factors. Research Methods. Design and Evaluation Methods. Cognition. Decision Making. Control. Biomechanics of Work. Work Physiology. Stress and Workload. Safety, Accidents, and Human Error. Human-Computer Interaction. Automation. and Human Factors Transportation. Selection and Training. Social Factors.
