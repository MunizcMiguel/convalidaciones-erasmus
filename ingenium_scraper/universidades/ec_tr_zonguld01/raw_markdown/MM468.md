### Objectives of the Course

The main objective of the lecture are to provide the students to have some general knowledge about ceramics and composites and their properties, applications, features, fabrication method and its processing conditions

### Course Content

Traditional and engineering ceramics, colloidal systems, rare earths materials, Consolidation and densification techniques of ceramics, Sintering, oxide non-oxide ceramics, applications, composite materials and their manufacturing.
