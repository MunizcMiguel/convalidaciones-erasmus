### Objectives of the Course

To teach compressible flows properties, the basic rules and principles which are using in flows analysis.

### Course Content

Steady one-dimensional compressible flows properties, sonic wave, isentropic flows in variable cross-section (nozzle, difuser). Frictionless and non-adibatic flows in constant cross-section channels. Normal shock waves, oblique shock waves. Reflection and convergence of oblique shock waves. Expansions waves in nozzles and difusers. General flows. Little perturbation flows. Introduction to characteristic method.
