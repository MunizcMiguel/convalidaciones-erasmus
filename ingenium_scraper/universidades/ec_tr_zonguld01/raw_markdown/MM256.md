### Objectives of the Course

Explanation of engineering mechanics theory and application(dynamics).Indicate the application of principle of mechanics into engineering problem's solution.

### Course Content

Kinematics and kinetics of particles. Kinematics and kinetics of sysyems of particles. Plane kinematics and kinetics of rigid bodies.Newton's second law. Work-energy and ımpulse-momentum methods.
