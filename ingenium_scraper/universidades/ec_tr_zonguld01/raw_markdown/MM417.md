### Objectives of the Course

It is aimed to teach about renewable energy sources and how to use the sources. Students will learn types of sources.

### Course Content

Definition and sources of energy, solar energy, wind energy, small scale hydraulic energy, geothermal energy, wave energy, ocean thermal energy conversion, biofuels.
