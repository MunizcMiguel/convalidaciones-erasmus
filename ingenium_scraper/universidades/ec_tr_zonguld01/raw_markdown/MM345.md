### Objectives of the Course

In this course, the relationships between motion and geometry in accordance with the engineering design goals are analysed. The basic concepts of mechanisms are introduced and taught. The fundamental laws and approaches that are used to analyse the motions of machines are presented. Moreover, the required information which is used to analyse and synthesize the motion of machines is illustrated.

### Course Content

Introduction to mechanisms, basic concepts of mechanisms, kinematic analyses and synthesis of mechanisms, linear mechanic systems, cam mechanisms.
