### Objectives of the Course

• To teach general information about Renewable Energy • Techniques to be applied during use • To learn the advantages and disadvantages of Renewable Energy sources compared to each other • To learn the engineering calculation methods • Importance of Renewable Energy for our country

### Course Content

The course is a required course for energy engineering degree. The course provides the basics of energy conversion processes and all renewable energy systems with emphasis on solar and bioenergy. The topics include mechanical heat engines, hydrogen energy, energy from the sun, and wind and water energy - with an introductory chapter of basic energy terms, fuel cells, electrolyzers and processes for hydrogen production to biomass.
