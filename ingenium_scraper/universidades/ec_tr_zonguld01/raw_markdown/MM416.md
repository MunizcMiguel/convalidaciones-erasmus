### Objectives of the Course

To enable the student to determine the relationship between machine tool, cutting tool and workpiece material in machining.

To teach students the effect of cutting tool geometry and cutting tool material properties on machinability. To ensure that the student can choose the right cutting tool and design the cutting tool with optimum features.

### Course Content

Chip removal mechanics and chip formation. Chipbreakers and chipbreaker designs in cutters. Cutting forces in machining, force measurement and calculation. Tool wear and measurement, tool wear mechanisms. Finishing surfaces, surface roughness and its measurement. Heat and temperature distribution, measurement and calculation of heat and temperature. Tool life and tool life models. Processing economics and processing cost. Tool geometry, tool selection and tool holder selection depending on machining characteristics. Machinability concept and machinability parameters. Material properties-machinability relationship. Factors affecting machinability. Special production methods and tooling design.
