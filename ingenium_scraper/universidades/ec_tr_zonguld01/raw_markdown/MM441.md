### Objectives of the Course

To have knowledge and experience about computer aided analysis, synthesis and design of the mechanisms. To achieve experience about solving an engineering problem with computer aid.

### Course Content

Analysis and synthesis of mechanism with the aid of computer programs. Kinematics and Kinetics Analysis, Synthesis. Kinematic Analysis and Computer Programs Elementary groups. Dynamic Analysis. Strength Analysis of General Planar Mechanisms. Computer Programs related to them. Programming Techniques of synthesis of the mechanisms.
