### Objectives of the Course

To learn; material types, structures, the effect on strength and properties, destructive and nondestructive tests, alloys, phase diagrams.

### Course Content

Types of materials. Atomic structure and atomic bonding. Crystal structures. Lattice structures. Directions, planes, Miller indices. Crystal structure defects. The effect on materials' mechanical behavior of crystal structure defects. Destructive testings. Tensile, compression, bending, torsion, hardness, impact, wear, fatigue, creep, corrosion tests. Non-destructive testings. Radiography, ultrasonic, magnetic partical, Eddy current, liquid penetrant tests. X-ray difractions. Spectral analysis. Properties of metals. Alloys. Phase diagrams.
