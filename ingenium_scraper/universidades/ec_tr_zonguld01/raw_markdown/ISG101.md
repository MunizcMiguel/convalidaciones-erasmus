### Objectives of the Course

The aim of the course is to increase the knowledge of occupational health

and safety legislation; occupational health and safety in the workplace;, to

have knowledge about the dangers and risks that may occur in the working

environment; to gain safe behavior skills to protect against occupational

accidents and occupational diseases during their work, and to create a

safety culture.

### Course Content

Occupational health and safety legislation and implementation principles

that employees should know within the scope of Occupational Health and

Safety Law No. 6331. General occupational health and safety information

needed by employees to create a safety culture. Workplace hazards, risks,

emergencies, collective protection measures, use of personal protective

equipment and changing employee behavior. Prevention of work accidents

and occupational diseases.

### Course Methods and Techniques

Face to face. Lectures. Video Views. Question answer. Individual study. Case

Study. Group Discussion.
