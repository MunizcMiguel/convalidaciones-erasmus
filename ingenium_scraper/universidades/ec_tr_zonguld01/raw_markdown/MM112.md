### Objectives of the Course

In this course, To introduce the engineer candidate to the algorithm logic and flow charts that are the basis of computer programming, to give the basis of programming, and to make them understand how to convert a problem into a language that the computer can understand and program it.

### Course Content

Introduction to Computer Programming, Algorithm preparation, drawing flowcharts. Introduction to the Fortran 90 programming language. Programming and applications with Fortran 90.
