### Objectives of the Course

In the engineering world, the fundamental course begins with Statics. Statics plays with the equilibrium equations between load and geometry in Newton's space.

### Course Content

Fundamental principals. Statics for a particle. Equilibrium forces. Statics for rigid bodies. Centroids. Structural analysis. Forces applied to beams. Shear and moment diagrams. Moment of inertia.
