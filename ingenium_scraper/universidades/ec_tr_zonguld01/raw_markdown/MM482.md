### Objectives of the Course

To provide information on basic nuclear reactor engineering applications. To provide information about thermal-hydraulic and heat transfer calculations in nuclear power cycles.

### Course Content

Basics of nuclear physics, nuclear reactors and nuclear power, nuclear reactor theory, heat transfer in nuclear reactors, radiation and radiation protection, reactor accidents.
