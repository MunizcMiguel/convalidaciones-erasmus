### Objectives of the Course

To be realised the importance of numerical methods according to developing computer and calculation methods. Application of numerical methods into the engineering problems.

### Course Content

Predictions and errors. Roots of equations. Algebraic systems of equations. Eigenvalue problems. Curve fitting.Interpolation. Least square method. Numerical differentiation and integration. Ordinary differential equations.
