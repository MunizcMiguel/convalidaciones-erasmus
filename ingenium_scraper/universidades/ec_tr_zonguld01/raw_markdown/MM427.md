### Objectives of the Course

The importance of thermal comfort and thermal insulation, introduction of heating systems like radiator etc., methods of calculations and necessary informations about ventilation technic.

### Course Content

The importance of heating and thermal comfort, heating systems, thermal insulation, safety systems, heat loss calculation, the calculations and selection of heater, radiator, boiler, pump, chimney, fuel tank etc., calculations and design of piping and ventilation systems, air duct calculation and sample project study.
