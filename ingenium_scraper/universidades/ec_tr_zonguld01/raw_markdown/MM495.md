### Objectives of the Course

The intended outcome of this course is to understand and apply basic economic principles, especially discounted cash flow calculations and internal rate of return.

### Course Content

The nature of engineering economics and basic concepts of cost calculation and analysis, revenue calculations, profit maximization and the break-even point analysis, cash flow, depreciation and amortization concept of depreciation, and calculations; time value of money, simple and compound interest calculations, evaluation of investment projects, profitability ratio, payback period, net present value analysis, internal rate of return, benefit cost analysis and purchasing-lease analysis.

### Course Methods and Techniques
