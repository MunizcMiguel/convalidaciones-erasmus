### Objectives of the Course

This course provides skills and application techniques of both energy methods and finite element methods to the field of mechanics.

### Course Content

Variational Calculus: Fundamental introduction. General variational methods: Colocation Method, Rayleigh-Ritz Method, Galerkin Method, Petrov-Galerkin Method. Examples for Galerkin Method. Introduction to Finite Element Method. Displacement Formulation: Derivations for bar extension and flexure problems. Examples.
