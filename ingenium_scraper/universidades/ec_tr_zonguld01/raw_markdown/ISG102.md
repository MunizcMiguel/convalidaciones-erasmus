### Objectives of the Course

The aim of the course is to enable students to identify hazards and risks in

a working environment, to have knowledge about risk assessment methods,

to learn occupational accident and occupational disease prevention

methods, to know possible emergencies and what to do in these situations

and to learn occupational safety and health legislation related to their field.

### Course Content

Legislation related to the field issued within the scope of the Occupational

Safety and Health Law and its implementation; Workplace hazards, risks,

emergencies, collective protection measures, use of personal protective

equipment and changing employee behaviour in one's own line of business.

Risk assessment methods and practices in preventing work accidents and

occupational diseases.

### Course Methods and Techniques

Lectures. Video Views. Question answer. Individual study. Case Study. Group Discussion.
