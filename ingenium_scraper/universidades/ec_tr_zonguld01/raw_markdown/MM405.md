### Objectives of the Course

The aim of this course is; The aim of this course is to give information about the techniques of transportation technique used in machinery industry, usage characteristics, selection of the elements forming the system, dimensioning and projecting.

### Course Content

Importance of transportation technique and its place in industry; elements used in the transport technique machines (hook, rope, chain, spool, drum, sprocket, brake and lock mechanisms etc.); calculation and selection of these elements; lifting machines (jacks, hoists, cranes, etc.) and their operation principles.
