### Objectives of the Course

To introduce systems of linear equations and Gauss elimination method. To introduce matrices and elementary row operations. To apply matrices to systems of linear equations. To introduce special type matrices and equivalent matrices. Calculate determinants of square matrices. Find the inverse of a matrix. Examine the concept of vectors, linear dependence, linear independence and vector space. To find eigenvalues and eigenvectors of matrices. Calculate inner products of vectors in real and complex vector spaces.

### Course Content

Matrices and elementary row operations, To apply matrices to Linear equation systems. Algebraic operations on Matrices, Special matrix types, Elementary matrices, Elementary Column operations and Equivalent matrices, Determinants and some properties, Inverse of a matrix, Solution of systems of linear equations with matrices. Vectors, linear dependence, linear independence. Eigenvalues and eigenvectors of matrices. Similarity. Inner product in real and complex vector spaces.
