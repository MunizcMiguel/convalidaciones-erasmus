### Objectives of the Course

To give an ability to write and read technical drawings. To give an ability to draw machine elements and assembly drawings.

### Course Content

Technical Drawing in Engineering, Meaning of Line Types, Fundamentals of dimensioning, Principles of Projection, Orthograpic Views, Section Views, Perspectives, Surface Finishing Symbols, Tolerances, Fasteners, Mechanical Assembly Drawing, İntroduction to an CAD Software.
