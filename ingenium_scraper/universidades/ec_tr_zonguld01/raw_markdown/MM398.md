### Objectives of the Course

To indroduce convection and radiation. To interpet ability calculate of thermal systems by using heat transfer principles. To teach forced and natural concection systems. and radiation heat transfer. To introduce types and analysis of heat exchangers. To teach radiation heat transfer.

### Course Content

Convection heat transfer. Conservation equations in velocity, momentum and thermal boundary layers. The convection heat transfer for external and internal flows. Heat transfer of natural convection. Types and analysis of heat exchangers. Fundamental concepts of radiation heat transfer. Blackbody and gray surface radiation. Radiation heat transfer between gray surfaces. Net radiation method. Radiation shields.
