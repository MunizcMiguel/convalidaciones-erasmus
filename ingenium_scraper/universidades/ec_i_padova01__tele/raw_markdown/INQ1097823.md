# PHYSICS 2 (INQ1097823)

## General information

- **Degree programme:** INFORMATION ENGINEERING (IN2801)
- **Curriculum:** PERCORSO COMUNE
- **Year of study:** 2
- **Academic year of delivery:** 2026/2027
- **Period:** First semester
- **ECTS:** 9
- **Hours:** Workshop: 8; Lesson: 64
- **Scientific sector (SSD):** FIS/03, FIS/01
- **Type of activity:** Basic
- **Area:** Fisica e chimica
- **Exam type:** Scritto
- **Grading:** Final grade
- **Attendance:** Not mandatory
- **Teaching methods:** Workshop, Lesson

## Prerequisites

Mathematics Prerequisites: Formulas for calculating the surface areas and volumes of the most common plane figures and solids; differentiation and integration of functions

Physics Prerequisites: Kinematics and dynamics of a material point; concepts of kinetic energy and potential energy

## Learning objectives

Properties of static and time-varying electric and magnetic fields.

Basic theory of electrical circuits.

Properties of electromagnetic waves.

Phenomena of wave optics.

Integration of vector functions in two and three dimensions.

## Innovative teaching methods: Teaching and learning strategies

- Group work

- Problem solving

- Case study

- Simulations

- Project work

- Problem based learning

- Use of technological tools (moodle and/or other educational tools, software, videos, quizzes, wooclap)

- Feedback

- Assessment activities during the course

## Examination methods

Students who have attended the laboratory sessions are eligible to take the exam.

The exam consists of an initial written test. A score of 18/30 or higher qualifies the student for the oral exam.

Classroom tests are administered throughout the year, and together they are equivalent to a written exam.

## Assessment methods

Ability to solve the written proposed problems correctly and precisely. Ability and completeness of the exposition of the physical items discussed during the oral part of exam.

## Contents

ELECTROSTATICS

The 3 fundamental laws of electric charge. - The Coulomb force and the torsion pendulum. - Introduction of the vacuum permittivity. - The definition of the electric field. - The dynamics of a point like charge. - The dynamics of a dipole - Calculation of the potential energy associated to the Coulomb force - Definition of the Electric Potential - Electric field generated by a uniformly charged straight line - Potential energy of a dipole in an external electric field - Electric field and potential of a uniformly charged disc - Integral and differential form of the conservation of the electric field - Definition of the flux of a vector field - Gauss’ Law for the electric field, integral form - Electric structure of matter - The concept of electric equilibrium - Coulomb’s theorem - Relation between surface charge density and curvature radius - The isolation property of a conductor: the Faraday’s cage - Capacitance and Capacitors - Capacitance of a sphere - Measurement unit of capacitance and new units for electric permittivity - Capacitance of a parallel plates’ capacitor - Capacitance of a coaxial cylinders’ capacitor - Capacitance of concentric spheres capacitor - Capacitors in parallel and series connection - Work done on charging a capacitor - Energy density of an electric field - Pressure on a charged conductor

ELECTRICITY IN MATTER

Polarization by electron orbit deformation and by orientation - Definition of polarization density vector and its relation with number density - Polarization effect in a non-uniform electric field - Definition of the Electric Induction - The polarization and the electric susceptibility - Relation between electric field inside the dielectric and that on vacuum

CURRENT AND CIRCUITS

Definition of electrical current - Intrinsic definition of current density - Expression of current density for discrete particles - Integral form of the continuity equation - Local form of the continuity equation - Firs Ohm’s Law - Second Ohm’s Law - Drude’s model of electrical conductivity in conductors - Joule effect - Resistances in series and in parallel - The dispersion relation and the phenomenon of dispersion - Kirchhoff’s Laws

MAGNETOSTATICS

The Lorentz force - Angular frequency and orbit radius of a particle under uniform magnetic field - The cyclotron - Force on a current - Definition of the magnetic dipole - Torque produced by a uniform magnetic field on a magnetic dipole - Work of the magnetic field on a moving charge and on a current loop - Potential energy of a magnetic dipole in a magnetic field - The Laplace’s Law: the magnetic field generated by an infinitesimal stretch - The magnetic permeability of vacuum - The magnetic field generated by a single charge in motion - The magnetic field along the axis of a coil - The magnetic field generated by an indefinite, thin, straight wire - The magnetic field along the axis of a finite length solenoid - The cases of symmetric and indefinite solenoid - Forces between parallel wires - Gauss’ Law of the magnetic field - The Ampere’s Law

MAGNETISM IN MATTER

The magnetic dipole associated to the electron orbital motion - Diamagnetism - Paramagnetism and Ferromagnetism - The magnetization density vector - The magnetization currents: definition of the current linear density - Definition of the Magnetic Field Strength

TIME VARYING FIELDS

Phenomena due to the relative motion between a magnet and a solenoid - The Faraday’s Law - The Lenz’s Law - Generation of the electric field by a varying magnetic field - Eddy currents - Definition of self-induction or inductance - The inductance of a solenoid - The inductance of a coaxial cable - Energy spent to create a current - The magnetic energy density - Self-induction similitude with the electric potential energy - Transient in a RLC circuit

THE ELECTROMAGNETIC WAVES

The Maxwell solution of Amper's paradox - Definition of the displacement current - From the integral to the differential form of Maxwell’s equations - The D’Alembert’s equation and its solution - From Maxwell’s equations to D’Alembert’s equation for electric and magnetic fields and solutions - Definition of vacuum impedance - The harmonic wave case: definition of wave number, wavelength, period - Expression of a plane harmonic wave that propagates in any direction - The energy, the intensity and the Pointing vector -
