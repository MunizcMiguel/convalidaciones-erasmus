## Prerequisites

None. Students are expected to have basic mathematical knowledge required to pass the entrance exam for the undergraduate program. No preliminary exams are required.

## Learning objectives

By the end of the course, students should have acquired familiarity with the concepts of vector space, linear function, matrices and their properties, scalar products, and symmetric bilinear forms. They are expected to develop solid skills in calculations involving vectors and matrices. Students should be able to apply these concepts to solve concrete problems in linear algebra and basic analytic geometry, especially regarding linear subvarieties in affine spaces.

## Contents

Vector spaces and subspaces. Bases. Dimension. Coordinates. Change of basis. Intersections, sums, and direct sums of subspaces. Linear functions. Matrices and matrix operations. Matrices associated with linear functions. Row echelon form. Invertible matrices. Systems of linear equations. Determinants. Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and eigenspaces of endomorphisms or matrices. Characteristic polynomial. Diagonalizable endomorphisms and matrices. Similar matrices. Scalar products in real vector spaces. Vector norms and angles. Orthogonality between vectors and subspaces. Gram-Schmidt process. Orthogonal matrices. Symmetric bilinear forms. Real symmetric matrices and diagonalization. Spectral theorem. Affine spaces. Points, lines, and planes in space. Linear subvarieties in affine spaces. Parallelism, incidence, orthogonality. Distance between linear subvarieties.
