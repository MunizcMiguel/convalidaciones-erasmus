## Prerequisites

Number sets. Prime numbers. Linear and quadratic equations/inequalities and simple systems. Polynomials and their factorization. Exponentials and logarithms. Trigonometric concepts and functions. Euclidean geometry (axioms, criteria for triangles) and analytic geometry (lines, circles, ellypses, parabolas and hyperbolas).

## Learning objectives

Learning mathematical language and basic techniques of one variable mathematical analysis, including limits of functions and sequences, convergence of series, differentiation and (Riemann and generalized) integration.

## Examination methods

The exam consists of a written test, which requires the performance of some exercises and the answer to some open theory questions, on the course program. The test is passed if both the exercises part and the theory part are sufficient. If not, the whole exam goes. A separate from the teacher, in some cases in certain ones (for example, when the part of exercises and / or theory is slightly insufficient, or if there is a large discrepancy between exercises and theory), the student can be summoned for a short supplementary test (theory or / and exercises). The supplementary test takes place a few days after the written test and the final grade of the exam will depend on the overall evaluation of the written test and the integration.

## Assessment methods

Correct and well justified calculations. Mastering definitions and some basic proofs.

## Contents

Sets and functions. Natural numbers and induction. Real numbers, infimum and supremum. Elementary functions and inequalities. Basic topology: neighborhood, open and closed sets. Limits and basic properties (with rigorous statements and proofs). Sequences. Landau's o, O, and ~. Classical limits. Number series and their convergence. Continuous functions and their basic properties. Derivative and their basic properties (with rigorous statements and proofs). Higher order derivatives and convexity of a function. Taylor formula (of any order). Graphing a function. Riemann integral and computations of real integrals. Generalized integrals and their convergence. Introduction to ordinary differential equations.
