## Contents

Computer-based Language Skills Test, for the certification of the knowledge of English language in particular for STEM disciplines (Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics), level B2 “receptive skills” (Listening and Reading) of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages. The activity does not include a course, but only a test which has to be taken at the University Language Center (CLA). For more information about the English Language Test, please visit the following webpage https://www.ingegneria.unipd.it/prove-inglese. Please do NOT contact the teacher in charge.
