## Contents

The characteristics of the final exam are defined by the Bachelor's Degree Regulations. The final exam is oral and involves the writing of a final paper. The material submitted by the student for the final exam will be evaluated by a preparatory committee, appointed by the Department Director and composed of at least two faculty members. The preparatory committee will formulate an opinion/assessment of the work done, and forward it to the Graduate Committee appointed by the Department Director, which will make the final judgment. The final exam must be written and discussed in English. Further information is available at https://stem.elearning.unipd.it/mod/book/view.php?id=234&chapterid=46
