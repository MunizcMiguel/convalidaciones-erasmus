## Prerequisites

The course assumes basic knowledge of mathematical analysis and linear algebra.

## Learning objectives

1. Knowledge and understanding Understand the basic properties of circuits with time-varying voltages and currents. Know the basic methods for the analysis of linear resistive circuits of two-terminal elements) and introduction to circuits with ideal controlled sources. Know the basic methods for phasor analysis of two-terminal elements circuits in sinusoidal steady state. 2. Ability to apply knowledge and understanding Apply knowledge and methods to solve introductory linear circuits. 3. Independent judgment Develop autonomous reasoning skills with reference to the introductory circuits indicated. 4. Communication skills Correctly use the technical terminology of the field. 5. Learning skills Develop independent study skills.

## Examination methods

The exam is in person, in written form. It consists of a section with exercises and another one for the theory. Should there be any interpretive doubts regarding what is reported by the candidate in the written test, the professor may require the candidate to take an oral exam.

## Assessment methods

The exercises are intended to verify the level of skill acquired by the student to solve a linear circuit in DC steady state and in sinusoidal steady state. The theory questions are intended to verify the level of knowledge acquired by the student on circuits.

## Contents

Introduction. Current and voltage. Time-varying voltages and currents. Kirchhoff’s laws. N-terminal elements, two-terminal elements. Power. Topology of two-terminal element networks. Adynamic two-terminal elements. Resistive two-terminal element circuits. Basic analysis methods and theorems. Introduction to linear resistive circuits with ideal controlled sources. Circuits with time-invariant voltages and currents: some first-order cases and introduction to second-order circuits. Two-terminal element circuits in sinusoidal steady state. Phasor method. Basic analysis methods and theorems. Series RLC circuit and parallel RLC circuit in sinusoidal steady state.
