# Computer simulation of systems and processes

- **Title**: Computer simulation of systems and processes

- **Hours**: 15 lectures + 30 laboratories per semester

- **Study period**: Summer/winter

- **ECTS**: 7

## Assessment methods

Evaluation of projects (codes with simulations done by students) + midterm and final exam

- **Language of instruction**: instruction: English

## Prerequisites

Basic course in higher mathematics, basic skills in programming

## Course content

 Basic definitions and methods used in modeling and simulations
o Kinds and constitution of models
o Solution methods
o Tools used in simulations,
 Equilibrium systems:
o Discrete and continuous mechanical systems,
o Systems in biology, chemistry and economy,
 Evolution of spatially homogeneous systems
o Biological systems,
o Chemical reactors,
 Transport in discrete systems
o Flow in networks,
o Energy exchange systems,
 Transport in continuous systems
o Advective, diffusive and dispersive transport,
o Transport of heat in barriers,
 Vibrations and wave propagation phenomena
o Mechanical, biological and chemical oscillations,
o Waves in continuous systems,
 Nonlinear systems
o Percolations, bifurcations and chaos,
o Fractals,
 Verification and validation of models.

## Learning outcomes

Knowledge on basic methods of modelining and simulations of systems
and processes described by algeabraic, differential and integral systems of
equations with examples from engineering, biology, chemistry, economy
etc.
