### Learning objectives

Conforming to the objectives of the Degree Program, the course provides the basis for the development of advanced systems for the analysis and interpretation of biological data. Such systems are used in all areas of information bioengineering in order to build systems to support diagnosis, data interpretation systems in a clinical study and clinical indicators. The course aims to introduce the student to the adoption of the most common computational methods and tools currently exploited in bioinformatics analysis and to the acquisition of notions on their functioning. At the end of the course, the student will have acquired the skills necessary to analyze and interpret biological data through the Python programming language, as well as having notions on the analysis and design of biological databases. In particular, the student will acquire the basic notions on: • algorithms for the analysis of biological data and their applications; • interpretation of biological data; • analysis and design of biological databases. Knowledge and understanding The student will know the theoretical and practical aspects of the principles relating to the processing of biological data, as well as notions relating to the analysis of biological databases. Applying knowledge and understanding The student will be able to design solutions to simple biological data processing problems. Making judgements The student will be able to analyze problems and will be aware of their own abilities, being able to solve a problem even in a different way from how the student learned in class. Communication skills The student will be able to describe, with a sufficient level of formalism and appropriate language, a solution to a specific problem. Learning skills Given a problem, the student will be able to evaluate the different solution strategies and choose the most suitable in specific circumstances, aware of the limitations and strengths of the strategy adopted.
