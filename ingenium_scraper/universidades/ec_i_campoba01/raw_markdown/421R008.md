### Learning objectives

Theteachingaims to providestudents with basic knowledge in the fields of human anatomy and occupationalhygiene, with an integratedapproachaimedatapplication in engineering. The medicalengineerswillneed tounderstand the biological and morphologicalbasis of the human body and the determinants of health in order to design, develop and evaluatetechnologies and devices to support medicine. Specifically, studentswill be able to: • Understand the morphologicalorganization of the human body atvariouslevels (cells, tissues, organs, systems). • Know the maindeterminants of health and environmental and occupational risks. • Analyse the mainprevention and safety strategies in the workplace. • Collaborate with healthcare figures in the design and evaluation of biomedicaltechnologicalsolutions. These objectives are in line with the educational profile of the degree course in Medical Engineering, whichintegratesmedical-biological knowledge with engineering skills.

### Prerequisites

There are no compulsory prerequisites. It is advisable to have acquired basic knowledge of biology and cell structure.
