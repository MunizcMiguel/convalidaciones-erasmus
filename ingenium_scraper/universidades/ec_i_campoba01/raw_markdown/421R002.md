## Learning objectives

The course aims to provide students with the tools to understand the principles of Geometry and Linear Algebra, as essential tools for the theoretical construction and practical resolution of typical engineering models. The course covers basic linear algebra and vector calculus, necessary for understanding other disciplines. Students will know the basic aspects of Linear Algebra and Geometry, will be able to analyze the important aspects of a function of an engineering problem and solve it using Linear Algebra. Students will be able to independently apply the theorems of Geometry and Algebra to the resolution of engineering problems. Students will be able to describe, with a sufficient level of formalism and appropriate language, a theorem or the resolution of a problem; they will be able, given a problem, to evaluate the different resolution strategies and choose the most suitable one in specific circumstances, aware of the limitations and strengths of the adopted strategy.

## Prerequisites

Since the course takes place during the first semester of the first year, the knowledge of high school mathematics is sufficient.

## Teaching methods

The course is taught in the first semester and consists of 48 hours (6 CFU). The lectures consist of a mix of theoretical parts and exercises.
