## Learning objectives

The student, in line with the objectives of the course, will develop skills related to programming languages. In particular, the student will learn: • the architecture of computers; • a programming language; • computer networks. Knowledge and understanding The student, at the end of the course, will know the theoretical and practical concepts about programming languages, computer architecture and computer networks. Applied knowledge and understanding The student will be able to design, through the use of a programming language, algorithmic solutions to simple problems also in biomedical field. Making judgments The students will be able to solve a problem in a different way than they already did during the lessons. Communication skills The student will be able to describe a solution to a specific problem with sufficient level of formalism and appropriate languages. Learning skills The student will be able to evaluate different strategies and choose the most suitable solution in specific situations, while being aware of the limitations and strengths of the selected solutions.

## Assessment methods

Written and oral examinations. • The written examination has a duration of 2 hours and consists of practical exercises that require the formalization of a solution to a specific problem, through the use of the C programming language. This test is designed to assess the knowledge and ability to understand and apply the student’s independent judgment. Students with a grade higher than 18/30 in the written test will have access to the oral examination. The solutions of the exercises and the results of the written tests are published on Moodle and, during the oral test, all students will be able to view their works. • The oral examination consists of the discussion of the exercises solved during the written test in order to evaluate both communication skills and their ability to learn. The final mark is the average of the marks obtained in the two tests.
