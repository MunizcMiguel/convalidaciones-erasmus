## Contents

Part 1 (3 ECTS) 1. Electrical circuits: generators (voltage/current, indipendent/controlled), resistor, capacitor, parallelel/serier connections, Kirchoff's laws, Thevenin's and Norton's theorems. Electric power. 1ects. 2. Semiconductor devices: junction diode. 3. Operational amplifiers: concept and applications. 1ects. Part 2 (6 ECTS) 4. MOS transistor (1ects) 5. Inverter: operating principle (ratioed, static, dynamic), figures of merit, power consumption. 1ects. 6. CMOS circuits: combinational and elements of sequential logic circuits. 2ects. 7. Semiconductor memories: operating principles of ROM, OTP, static and dynamic RAM and non volatile flash memories. 1ects. 8. Interconnects. 1ects.

## Learning objectives

The aim of the course is to describe the operation of those basic electrical and electronic components and circuits that enable the realization of digital electronic systems.

## Prerequisites

Mandatory Prerequisites: Analisi Matematica I. The student is strongly encouraged to acquire foundational knowledge in computer science (Boolean algebra) and to master the basic notions of Physics, with emphasis on electricity, resistors, capacitors, circuits.

## Teaching methods

The course is delivered in Italian, in presence. Attendance is not mandatory, but strongly recommended. Working students are invited to contact the lecturer. The course consists of front lectures and exercises, both for all topics. Exercises are grouped in short periods and will be more frequent close to exam dates. Homeworks are not scored. Lectures will be delivered in presence or streaming, according to the University guidelines and the official schedule. At the start of the course the teacher communicates students if recordings will be available and for how long.

## Other information

Knowledge and understanding: 1.1 Understand the basic concepts of electrical circuits. 1.2 Understand the basic concepts of semiconductor devices. 1.3 Understand the concept of amplification and main operational amplifier applications. 1.4 Understand the concept of logical inverter and its performance. 1.5 Understand the basic digital CMOS circuits. 1.6 Understand the different types of semiconductor memories. Applying knowledge and understanding: 2.1 Be able to analyze electrical circuits under DC and transient operation. 2.2 Be able to analyze basic diode circuits. 2.3 Be able to analyze circuits with operational amplifiers. 2.4 Be able to analyze basic CMOS digital circuits and the mutual constrains arising from their interconnections. Autonomy of judgment: 3.1 Be able to autonomously judge the adequacy of a particular component for digital circuits to satisfy assigned specifications expressed through the main and most common performance metrics for digital circuits. Communication skills: 4.1 Be able to communicate in Italian with third party experts in the sector regarding the functions and performance of digital circuits Learning skills: 5.1 Ability to learn how new digital circuits work and compare them with those learned in the course.

## Assessment methods

The final written exam including theoretical questions aiming to verify "knowledge and understanding" learning outcomes and simple exercises aiming to assess the "ability to apply knowledge and understanding" learning outcomes. The exam is organized in two parts (one for basic circuit analog and one for digital electronics topics 1 hour and 4-7 questions/exercises each). The maximum no. of points for each exercise is specified. Total maximum = 33 points. The evaluation takes into account of the gravity of the errors made and of the average performance of the class.
