## Contents

The Internship 9 CFU corresponds to a stage in the company for a period of three months (and at least 360 hours). Internships are managed by the Internship Office (located on the second floor of the Faculty). More information can be found on the specific page: http://www.ingmo.unimore.it/site/home/servizi-studenti/ufficio-stage.html The presentation and discussion of a report on the activities carried out during the internship is the final test. At the end of the Internship, the student must hand in the company and to tutor a report which will be presented and discussed during the final test; the form in which it must be drafted and delivered the report on the Internship will be agreed with the tutor / company mentor (and should not be delivered to the student secretariat).
