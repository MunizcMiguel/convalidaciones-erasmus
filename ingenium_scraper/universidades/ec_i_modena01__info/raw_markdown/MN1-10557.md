## Contents

The final exam (3 CFU) requires 75 hours of work and can consist in the presentation to the examining commission for the final exam of: a) a report on the activity carried out during the internship; b) a paper on a topic, assigned by a tutor. In case a) at the end of the Internship, the student will have to deliver a report to the company and to the tutor; while in case b) the student will have to ask a tutor a topic on which to prepare the paper. The form in which the report must be drawn up and delivered will be agreed with the tutor: in both cases, however, the report must not be delivered to the student secretariat. The score (always a whole number) that can be assigned to a student in the final exam can vary from 0/110 to 3/110 and takes into account the quality of the work performed and the demonstrated exhibition capacity. Once the final exam has been passed, the student must participate in the first public proclamation for obtaining the degree and the proclamation of a Doctor of Computer Engineering, in which the Degree Commission: a) will take note of the weighted average of the marks obtained in the exams, rounded to the nearest whole, b) will take note of the score obtained in the final exam, c) will assign a 'bonus' of 2 points to the students who complete the study cycle within three academic years from the first enrollment, d) calculate the sum of the scores referred to in items a), b), c), e) may unanimously assign a "cum laude" if the sum referred to in point d) is equal to or greater than 110.
