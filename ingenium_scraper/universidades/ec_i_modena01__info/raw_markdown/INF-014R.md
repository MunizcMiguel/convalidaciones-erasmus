## Contents

Introduction to Operations Research. Linear Programming 3CFU Naive solution of simple LPs The Tableau and Pivot operations The Simplex Algorithm (basics) Handling unbounded problems Geometry of Simplex Initializing Simplex Degeneracy Cycling and the Use of Bland's rule Matrix notation Duality: dual variables and dual linear program Strong duality theorem Complementary Slackness Understanding the dual problem: shadow costs Sensitivity analysis Integer Linear Programming 2CFU Integer vs. Real-valued variables Cutting Plane Method (generalities and Gomory's cuts) Branch and Bound Method(Generalities, searching strategies, B&B for knapsack problem, standard B&B for PLI) Dynamic Programming (generalities, DP for knapsack 01, weight version and cost version, Bellman-Ford algorithm for Shortest Path Problem) Computational complexity 0.5CFU Generalities Graph Theory 0.5CFU Generalities Shortest Path Problem (Dijkstra algorithm)

## Assessment methods

Unseen Written Examination It consists of three parts 1 Some theory questions content: question regarding the theoretical part of the course (definitions, theorems, etc) duration 25' no text or note is allowed no calculator is allowed 2 Mathematical model content: design of a linear programming model for a given problem implementation with GLPK texts and notes are allowed calculator is allowed 3 Linear programming and graph theory exercises content: solution of numerical exercises on linear and integer programming and graph theory. texts and notes are allowed calculator is allowed Parts 2-3 have an overall duration of 1:30-2:00 hours Grading: part 1 : from -5 to 10, (minimum score 3) part 2 and 3: up to 27 After a written examination The teacher may require an oral questioning to assess the final grade The student may require an oral questioning to assess the final grade The oral determines the final grade which can be higher or lower of the grade of the written examination, usually by 4 points at most
