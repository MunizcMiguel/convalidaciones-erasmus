## Contents

## Learning objectives

The course aims to understand the functioning of companies both from an organizational point of view and from the accounting representation of their results, up to assessing the aspects of strategic management. Starting from its definition and the elements characterizing the company in relation to the environment, the accounting tools to support the decision-making process are introduced. After illustrating the processes for detecting aspects of business management, we move on to an introduction to the business strategy and the role of innovation.

## Other information

Knowledge and understanding. Through lessons and discussions of concrete sector and business examples, the student learns: - the concepts of business and competitive environment, with specific reference to the sectoral structure, the definition of business, competitive dynamics and the basic outlines of business strategies; - the differences and characteristics of competitive and growth strategies, methods of implementation; - the criteria of strategic coherence and the basic models of organizational design; The student is able to understand both the functioning of a company and the environmental context in which it operates. Applied knowledge and understanding. Real company and industry examples allow the student to apply the concepts learned to: - analyze competitive sectors and systems, - identify the most appropriate competitive strategies and implementation methods, - experiment with an integrated reading of company functions and their use for competitive purposes. Autonomy of judgement. The student is able to formalize judgments on the different conditions of attractiveness of a sector, and to evaluate the most appropriate strategies and the interests that converge in the company and principles of governance. Communication skills. The student is able to communicate the knowledge acquired clearly and concisely; correctly and appropriately use language, concepts and models acquired to effectively discuss the possible managerial solutions adopted and adoptable by companies. Learning ability. The student is able to independently update knowledge relating to business management and the use of strategy in light of the ongoing evolutions and changes that will occur in the extended and competitive environment in which a company operates.
