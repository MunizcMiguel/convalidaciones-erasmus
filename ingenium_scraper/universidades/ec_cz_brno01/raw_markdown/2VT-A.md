# Manufacturing Technology I

FSI-2VT-A | Acad. year: 2026/2027 | Winter semester

## Course summary

The course familiarizes students with the fundamentals of engineering technology and enables them to test practically individually selected operations.
1. Technology of presswork of sheet metal with practical application of operations of shearing, punching and bending on given components.
2. Technology of bulk forming with a view to free forging of material under heat using the operations of hot drawing, flattening and bending on given components.
3. Technology of gas welding, inclusive of mastering the fundamentals and manipulation with welding torch.
4. Technology of casting oriented to the fabrication of elementary mould and complicated mould with complicated parting plane.

Attendance in exercises is compulsory.
The attendance to the seminar is regularly checked and the participation in the lesson is recorded.

## Aims

The subject Production Technology I would enable the students to try practice some operations of single technologies on the base of which they would continue theoretical knowlege during theier next bachelor of general type of study.

Students are acquainted with presented technologies and they acquire manual skills when doing single operations, which they use during their further studies when preparing experiments.

### Lecture

26 hours, optionally

### Syllabus

- Crystallization and solidification of castings.
- Riseringst, stress, running system.
- The production of casting moulds and models, the ways of moulding.
- Machine moulding, permanent moulds, melting of metals.
- The laws of plastic deformation in the forming process.
- The production of metallurgical raw product – sheet metals, tubes, profiles, wires etc.
- Sheet metal forming – technology of cutting, deep drawing, bending.
- Bulk forming – extrusion, upsetting, forging.
- Welding – fusion welding, the basic methods of flame and electricity welding.
- Resistance welding, spot welding, seam welding, special ways.
- Pressure welding, brazing.
- The parting of metals-the methods’principles.
- Review of introduce technologies, questions to the exame.

### Laboratory exercise

22 hours, compulsory

### Syllabus

Weeks 2 to 12 – Stamping shop, forging shop, welding, foundry – manufacturing of products according to the technological process.

1. Stamping shop:
a) Basic information about metal sheet processing technology by cutting and bending.
b) Familiarization with the hand tools and machines that will be used in the work.
2. Forging shop:
a) Basic information about hot metal forming technology by forging.
b) Familiarization with forging tools, machines for free forging and operation of forge.
3. WELDING:
a) Demonstration preparation and demonstration of burner ignition, types of flame, demonstration of welding sheet metal parts.
b) Description of a given task, individual training of material melting and fusion weld with filler material.
4. FOUNDRY:
a) Introduction to melting equipment used in foundries.
b)Mold casting and casting evaluation.
