## Learning objectives

THE COURSE PROVIDES THE FUNDAMENTAL CONCEPTS RELATED TO SPECIAL AND UNCONVENTIONAL PROCESSING TECHNOLOGIES, INNOVATIVE AND CONVENTIONAL PERMANENT JOINING TECHNIQUES AND NON-DESTRUCTIVE TESTING; EMPHASIZING THE INTEGRATION OF SUCH TECHNOLOGIES WITH ROBOTICS AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEMS. KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING. AT THE END OF THE COURSE THE STUDENT KNOWS: - UNCONVENTIONAL PROCESSING TECHNOLOGIES; - FUSION AND SOLID-STATE WELDING METHODS; - NON-DESTRUCTIVE TESTING METHODS; - THE APPLICATION OF SYSTEMS BASED ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE; - ROBOTIC PROCESS AUTOMATION. APPLIED KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING AT THE END OF THE COURSE THE STUDENT IS ABLE TO: IDENTIFY THE MOST SUITABLE WELDING TECHNIQUES FOR THE PERMANENT JOINING OF MECHANICAL ELEMENTS; - DEFINE NON-DESTRUCTIVE TESTS SUITABLE FOR THE QUALIFICATION OF A MECHANICAL COMPONENT OR STRUCTURE; - DEFINE SYSTEMS BASED ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FOR PROCESS MONITORING; - IDENTIFY THE MOST SUITABLE ROBOTIC SYSTEMS TO BE USED TO INTEGRATE THE DIFFERENT TECHNOLOGIES, DEVELOP THE PROCESSING PROGRAMS. - EVALUATE THE ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT AND SUSTAINABILITY OF THE PROCESSES COVERED BY THE COURSE. AUTONOMY OF JUDGMENT BE ABLE TO IDENTIFY THE MOST APPROPRIATE MACHINING AND INSPECTION STRATEGIES TO CREATE OR VERIFY COMPONENTS THAT MEET THE SPECIFICATIONS IMPOSED BY THE DRAWING; KNOW HOW TO IDENTIFY SUITABLE SYSTEMS FOR THE AUTOMATION OF MACHINING PROCESSES THROUGH ROBOTICS AND THE BEST APPROACH TO THE PROGRAMMING OF PROCESSES; KNOW HOW TO CHOOSE AN AI-BASED SYSTEM FOR PROCESS MONITORING. COMMUNICATION SKILLS BE ABLE TO DESCRIBE, IN WRITTEN FORM, IN A CLEAR AND CONCISE WAY AND ORALLY EXPOSE WITH LANGUAGE PROPERTIES THE OBJECTIVES, THE PROCEDURE AND THE RESULTS OF THE ELABORATIONS CARRIED OUT. LEARNING SKILLS TO BE ABLE TO APPLY THE KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED TO CONTEXTS DIFFERENT FROM THOSE PRESENTED DURING THE COURSE, AND TO DEEPEN THE TOPICS COVERED USING MATERIALS OTHER THAN THOSE PROPOSED.

## Prerequisites

KNOWLEDGE OF THE BASICS OF MATERIALS SCIENCE AND CONVENTIONAL PROCESSING TECHNOLOGIES AND COMPONENTS AND PROGRAMMING OF CNC MACHINES IS REQUIRED.

## Teaching methods

THE COURSE IS DEVELOPED IN THEORETICAL LESSONS AND CLASSROOM EXERCISES INTEGRATED WITH LABORATORY VISITS AND PRACTICAL DEMONSTRATIONS. IN THE THEORETICAL LESSONS, JOINING PROCESSES, NON-DESTRUCTIVE TESTING TECHNIQUES, ROBOTIC APPLICATIONS, THE USE OF AI FOR MONITORING THE PROCESSES COVERED BY THE COURSE ARE ILLUSTRATED. THE EXERCISES WILL FOCUS ON THE APPLICATION OF AI MODELS TO THE PROCESSES COVERED BY THE COURSE, ON THE PROGRAMMING OF INDUSTRIAL ROBOTS AND ON THE INTERPRETATION OF THE RESULTS OF NON-DESTRUCTIVE TESTING. THE LABORATORY DEMONSTRATIONS ARE AIMED AT PROVIDING PRACTICAL FEEDBACK ON THE THEORETICAL NOTIONS PROVIDED AND APPLYING THE KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED.

## Assessment methods

THE EVALUATION OF THE ACHIEVEMENT OF THE SET OBJECTIVES WILL TAKE PLACE THROUGH AN ORAL INTERVIEW WHERE THE DEGREE OF KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED AND THE ABILITY TO EXPOSE THE TOPICS DISCUSSED WILL BE ASSESSED. THE OVERALL EVALUATION IS EXPRESSED IN THIRTIETHS. THE EVALUATION TAKES INTO ACCOUNT THE ABILITY TO IDENTIFY THE MOST APPROPRIATE MACHINING, TREATMENT AND CONTROL STRATEGIES TO CREATE MECHANICAL COMPONENTS, THE ABILITY TO CLEARLY AND CONCISELY EXPLAIN THE OBJECTIVES, THE PROCEDURE AND THE RESULTS OF THE ELABORATIONS CARRIED OUT, AS WELL AS THE ABILITY TO DEEPEN THE TOPICS COVERED USING MATERIALS OTHER THAN THOSE PROPOSED. THE MINIMUM EVALUATION LEVEL (18/30) IS AWARDED WHEN THE STUDENT DEMONSTRATES A REDUCED UNDERSTANDING OF THE TOPICS COVERED, HAS A POOR EXPOSITORY ABILITY AND LIMITED AUTONOMY AND JUDGMENT SKILLS. THE MAXIMUM LEVEL (30/30) IS AWARDED WHEN THE STUDENT DEMONSTRATES A COMPLETE AND IN-DEPTH KNOWLEDGE OF THE TOOLS AND METHODS ANALYZED, IS ABLE TO SOLVE THE PROPOSED PROBLEMS AND SHOWS A REMARKABLE ABILITY IN DEFINING PROCESSING AND CONTROL PROCEDURES. THE FINAL GRADE IS EXPRESSED IN THIRTIETHS WITH POSSIBLE HONORS. HONOURS ARE AWARDED WHEN THE CANDIDATE DEMONSTRATES SIGNIFICANT MASTERY OF THE THEORETICAL AND OPERATIONAL CONTENTS AND SHOWS THAT HE/SHE IS ABLE TO PRESENT THE TOPICS WITH CONSIDERABLE LANGUAGE PROPRIETY AND AUTONOMOUS PROCESSING SKILLS EVEN IN CONTEXTS OTHER THAN THOSE PROPOSED BY THE TEACHER.
