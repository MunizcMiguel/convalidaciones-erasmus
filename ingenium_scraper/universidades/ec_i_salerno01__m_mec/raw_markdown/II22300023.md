## Learning objectives

KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING THE COURSE AIMS TO PROVIDE AN IN-DEPTH UNDERSTANDING OF HEAT TRANSFER MECHANISMS (CONDUCTION, CONVECTION, AND RADIATION) AND THEIR APPLICATIONS IN ENGINEERING CONTEXTS. APPLYING KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING STUDENTS WILL BE ABLE TO APPLY THE ACQUIRED KNOWLEDGE TO ANALYZE AND DESIGN THERMAL SYSTEMS, EVALUATE ENERGY PERFORMANCE, AND OPTIMIZE SOLUTIONS BASED ON SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS. MAKING JUDGEMENTS DEVELOP THE ABILITY TO INTERPRET THERMAL DATA AND MAKE INFORMED DESIGN DECISIONS, TAKING INTO ACCOUNT TECHNICAL, ECONOMIC, AND ENVIRONMENTAL ASPECTS. COMMUNICATION SKILLS BE ABLE TO EFFECTIVELY COMMUNICATE THE RESULTS OF THERMAL ANALYSES AND DESIGN SOLUTIONS, BOTH IN WRITTEN AND ORAL FORM, USING APPROPRIATE TECHNICAL TERMINOLOGY. LEARNING SKILLS ACQUIRE THE NECESSARY SKILLS TO INDEPENDENTLY DEEPEN ADVANCED TOPICS IN THE FIELD OF HEAT TRANSFER AND TO PURSUE FURTHER STUDIES OR PROFESSIONAL ACTIVITIES IN THE ENERGY SECTOR.

## Prerequisites

## Assessment methods

THE ASSESSMENT OF THE ACHIEVEMENT OF THE INTENDED LEARNING OBJECTIVES WILL BE BASED ON THE EVALUATION OF A TEAM-DEVELOPED PROJECT REPORT AND AN ORAL EXAMINATION. THE PROJECT REPORT MUST BE SUBMITTED TO THE INSTRUCTOR AT LEAST FIVE DAYS PRIOR TO THE ORAL EXAM TO ALLOW TIME FOR CORRECTION. THE PROJECT WILL BE EVALUATED BASED ON THE FOLLOWING CRITERIA: PROJECT CONCEPT (0–5 POINTS) ACCURACY OF THE APPLIED DESIGN METHODS (0–15 POINTS) CRITICAL THINKING IN DESIGN CHOICES (0–5 POINTS) COMPLIANCE OF WRITTEN DOCUMENTATION AND GRAPHICAL DRAWINGS (0–5 POINTS) THE SCORE FOR EACH ELEMENT WILL BE ASSIGNED AS FOLLOWS: MAXIMUM SCORE IF NO ERRORS ARE FOUND; UP TO 80% DEDUCTION OF THE MAXIMUM SCORE IN PROPORTION TO THE NUMBER OF CONCEPTUAL ERRORS FOUND; ZERO POINTS IF THE EXPECTED ELEMENT IS MISSING. THE MINIMUM SCORE REQUIRED FOR THE PROJECT EVALUATION IN ORDER TO ACCESS THE ORAL EXAM IS 20 POINTS. AFTER THE INSTRUCTOR PRESENTS THE OUTCOME OF THE PROJECT ASSESSMENT, EACH STUDENT WILL TAKE THE ORAL EXAM INDIVIDUALLY, WHICH CONSISTS OF TWO THEORETICAL QUESTIONS ON TOPICS COVERED DURING LECTURES. THE AVERAGE DURATION OF THE ORAL EXAM RANGES FROM ABOUT TWENTY MINUTES (IN CASE OF QUICK AND CORRECT ANSWERS) TO UP TO ONE HOUR IF THE CANDIDATE HESITATES AND NEEDS TIME TO REASON. A PASSING LEVEL CORRESPONDS TO DEMONSTRATING THE ABILITY TO IDENTIFY APPROPRIATE METHODOLOGICAL TOOLS, PROPERLY SET UP THE PROBLEM FROM A PHYSICAL AND ENGINEERING STANDPOINT, AND INDICATE VIABLE APPROACHES TO SOLVING IT. AN EXCELLENT LEVEL IS ACHIEVED WHEN THE STUDENT SUCCESSFULLY TACKLES ASPECTS OF THE PROBLEMS NOT EXPLICITLY COVERED DURING LECTURES. THE ORAL EXAM WILL BE ASSESSED USING THE FOLLOWING CRITERIA: NO ANSWER (0 POINTS) ACCURACY OF THE ANSWER (1–13 POINTS) OVERALL METHOD AND USE OF APPROPRIATE TECHNICAL LANGUAGE (0–4 POINTS) THE MINIMUM PASSING SCORE FOR THE ORAL EXAM IS 14 POINTS, WHICH IS OBTAINED BY DEMONSTRATING AN UNDERSTANDING OF THE COURSE CONTENT AND THE ABILITY TO APPLY IT TO REAL-WORLD SCENARIOS. TO DETERMINE THE FINAL GRADE, THE PROJECT EVALUATION WILL ACCOUNT FOR 70% AND THE ORAL EXAM FOR 30% OF THE FINAL SCORE.
