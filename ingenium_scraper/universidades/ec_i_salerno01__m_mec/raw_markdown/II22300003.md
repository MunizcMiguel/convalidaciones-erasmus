### Learning objectives

- KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING PROVIDING METHODOLOGICAL KNOWLEDGE AS TO PLANNING NEW PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT AFTER A MARKET-DRIVEN PERSPECTIVE, BYALSO STIMULATING CREATIVITY AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP CULTURE IN THE STUDENTS. THE COURSE IS MULTIDISCIPLINARY, IN ORDERTO STIMULATE STUDENTS TO APPROACH TO THE SAME PROBLEMI AFTER DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVES. -APPLYING KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING THE STUDENT WILL BE ABLE TO PLAN, DESIGN, IMPLEMENT AND MANAGE THE DEVELOPMENT OD NEW PRODUCTS AND PROCESSES. THE CONTEXTUAL DESIGN AFTER A MARKET AND A TECHNICAL PERSPECTIVE WILL MAKE THE STUDENT APPROACH REAL BUSINESS CONTEXTS. - MAKING JUDGMENTS STUDENTS IN GROUPS HAVE TO DESIGN A NEW PRODUCT BY THEIR OWN, THUS DEVELOPING THEIR CAPABILITYOF MAKING AUTONOMOUS DECISIONS - COMMUNICATION SKILLS TECHNICAL LANGUAGE ON THE CONTENTS OF THE COURSE WILL BE DEVELOPED. FURTHERMORE, TIME MANAGEMENT WILL BE DEVELOPED SINCE STUDENTS ARE ASKED TO PRESENT THEIR PROJECTS IN 3 MINUTES TO AN EXTERNAL EXPERTS JURY OF ENTREPRENEURS AND MANAGERS. - LEARNING SKILLS THE STUDENT WILL ACQUIRE KNOWLEDGE FOR APPROACHING TO THE WORK IN COMPETITIVE CONTEXTS WHERE NEW PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT IS AN ISSUE. TEAM WORKING IS ALSO DEVELOPED.

THERE ARE NO PREREQUISITES FOR STUDENTS FROM THE MECHANICAL ENGINEERING OR MANAGEMENT ENGINEERING DEGREES. STUDENTS NOT ENROLLED IN THE MECHANICAL ENGINEERING OR MANAGEMENT ENGINEERING DEGREE PROGRAMS CAN PROFITABLY TAKE: 1) FONDAMENTI E METODI DELLA PROGETTAZIONE INDUSTRIALE PROVIDED THEY HAVE PRIOR KNOWLEDGE OF MECHANICAL DRAWING; 2) MARKETING E SVILUPPO PRODOTTO PROVIDED THEY HAVE PRIOR KNOWLEDGE OF BUSINESS MANAGEMENT.

### Assessment methods

FOR BOTH COURSES, THE EXAM INCLUDES AN INDIVIDUAL WRITTEN TEST, THE DRAFTING OF A GROUP PAPER, AND ITS PRESENTATION. FURTHERMORE, FOR MARKETING E SVILUPPO PRODOTTO, THE DRAFTING OF A MARKETING PLAN IS REQUIRED. GRADES ARE DETERMINED BASED ON ANALYTICAL QUALITY, STRATEGIC COHERENCE, OPERATIONAL FEASIBILITY, CORRECT USE OF MARKETING TOOLS, CRITICAL AND CREATIVE ABILITY, AND CLARITYOF PRESENTATION. 18-21: SIMPLE BUT COHERENT PLAN, BASIC KNOWLEDGE; 22-24: GOOD STRUCTURE WITH SOME CRITICAL ISSUES; 25-27: CLEAR AND WELL-STRUCTURED STRATEGY; 28-29: IN-DEPTH ANALYSIS AND CONVINCING PLAN; 30: COMPLETE, COHERENT, AND PROFESSIONAL WORK; 30 WITH HONORS: EXCELLENCE, ORIGINALITY, AND STRONG CRITICAL THINKING. THE WRITTEN EXAM CONSISTS OF A PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT QUESTION. GRADES ARE DETERMINED BASED ON THE STUDENT’S ABILITY TO UNDERSTAND THE THEORETICAL CONCEPTS AND APPLY THEM TO REAL-WORLD CONTEXTS. 18-21: BASIC UNDERSTANDING, SIMPLE AND PARTIAL APPLICATION TO REAL-WORLD CONTEXTS; 22-24: THEORETICAL UNDERSTANDING, CORRECT BUT NOT IN-DEPTH APPLICATION; 25-27: ABILITY TO CONNECT THEORY AND PRACTICE CLEARLYAND COHERENTLY; 28-29: CRITICAL AND WELL-ARGUED APPLICATION OF CONCEPTS TO REALISTIC SITUATIONS; 30: COMPLETE MASTERY OF CONTENT, HIGH APPLICATION SKILLS, AND EXCELLENT CLARITY OF PRESENTATION; 30 CUM LAUDE: ORIGINAL, CRITICAL, AND PARTICULARLY EFFECTIVE IN ANALYZING AND PROPOSING REALISTIC SOLUTIONS. THE PROJECT PLAN COMPLETED BY THE GROUPS WILL BE PRESENTED IN APPROXIMATELY 20 MINUTES. GRADES ARE DETERMINED BY THE GROUP’S ABILITY TO COMPLETELY ANALYZE THE PROBLEM, APPLY CONSISTENT METHODOLOGIES, AND DEVELOP EFFICIENT SOLUTIONS WITH AN EFFECTIVE MARKET PLACEMENT. 18-21: ESSENTIAL ANALYSIS OF THE PROBLEM AND ONLY PARTIALLY DEVELOPED SOLUTIONS; SUFFICIENT COLLABORATION; 22-24: GOOD METHODOLOGICAL APPROACH AND REASONABLE INTEGRATION BETWEEN TECHNICAL AND MANAGEMENT ASPECTS; 25-27: COHERENT, WELL-STRUCTURED PROJECT WITH APPLICABLE SOLUTIONS; 28-29: IN-DEPTH ANALYSIS, EFFECTIVE INTERDISCIPLINARY APPROACH, AND GOOD TEAMWORK SKILLS; 30: COMPLETE, INNOVATIVE, AND WELL-CONTEXTUALIZED PROJECT IN THE MARKET, WITH EXCELLENT TEAM COORDINATION; 30 CUM LAUDE: PROJECT OF AN ALMOST PROFESSIONAL STANDARD, WITH HIGH CRITICAL THINKING, STRONG MULTIDISCIPLINARY INTEGRATION, AND PARTICULARLY INNOVATIVE AND EFFECTIVE SOLUTIONS. THE OVERALL GRADE FOR MARKETING E SVILUPPO PRODOTTO IS THE AVERAGE OF: 1) THE MARKETING PLAN GRADE, 2) THE WRITTEN EXAM GRADE, 3) THE PROJECT GRADE. THE OVERALL GRADE FOR FONDAMENTI E METODI DELLA PROGETTAZIONE IS THE AVERAGE OF: 1) THE WRITTEN EXAM GRADE, 2) THE PROJECT GRADE. IN ADDITION, GROUPS ARE REQUIRED TO PRESENT A 3-MINUTE ELEVATOR PITCH NOT ONLY TO THE TEACHERS, BUT ALSO TO REPRESENTATIVES FROM THE CORPORATE WORLD: THIS PART, HOWEVER, WILL NOT BE EVALUATED WITH A GRADE, AND IS RATHER AIMED AT OBTAINING PRIZES FOR THE GROUPS PROVIDED BYTHE COMPANIES.
