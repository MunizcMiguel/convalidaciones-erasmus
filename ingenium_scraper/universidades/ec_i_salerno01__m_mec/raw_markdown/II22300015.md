## Contents

MODULE 1: INTRODUCTION TO ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING (AM) (5 HOURS): - DEFINITION, HISTORY, AND PRINCIPLES OF ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING - PRINCIPLES OF LAYER-BY-LAYER MACHINING. ADVANTAGES AND LIMITATIONS OF ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING. GENERALIZED AM PROCESS CHAIN. - INDUSTRIAL MATERIALS AND APPLICATIONS: RAPID PROTOTYPING, RAPID PRINTING, DIRECT DIGITAL PRODUCTION. - OVERVIEW OF THE TRANSITIONS BETWEEN GREEN AND DIGITAL AND THE ROLE OF AM - CASE STUDIES SHOWING THE IMPACT OF AM IN DIFFERENT INDUSTRIES. MODULE 2: ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING TECHNOLOGIES FOR POLYMERS (15 ORE): - OVERVIEW OF AM PROCESSES ON POLYMERS (E.G., STEREOLITHOGRAPHY, FUSED FILAMENT FABRICATION, TANK PHOTOPOLYMERIZATION, POLYJET PROCESSES, MATERIAL JETTING, BINDER JETTING). - ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING MATERIALS: LIQUID-BASED MATERIALS, POWDER-BASED MATERIALS, SOLID-BASED MATERIALS, METAL POWDER CHARACTERISTICS, PROPERTIES, AND APPLICATIONS. - PROCESS PARAMETERS, PROCESS DESIGN, CONTROL AND OPTIMIZATION. - AM-SPECIFIC DESIGN GUIDELINES AND CHALLENGES FOR POLYMERS, ENGINEERING PLASTICS, AND COMPOSITES. MODULE 3: ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING TECHNOLOGIES FOR METALS (15 ORE): - ENERGY SOURCES: LASER BEAM AND ELECTRON BEAM TECHNOLOGY, ELECTRIC ARC AND PLASMA TECHNOLOGIES. - ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING PROCESSES OF METALS (E.G., POWDER BED FUSION, DIRECTED ENERGY DEPOSITION, WIRE ARC ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING, SELECTIVE LASER MELTING, SELECTIVE LASER SINTERING, ELECTRON BEAM MELTING, BINDER JETTING, THICK FILM DEPOSITION) - SELECTION, PROPERTIES AND APPLICATIONS OF THE METAL MATERIAL. - PROCESS PARAMETERS, PROCESS DESIGN, PROCESS CONTROL AND OPTIMIZATION. - DESIGN PRINCIPLES AND CONSTRAINTS IN THE USE OF METAL-BASED AM. MODULE 4: POST-PROCESSING AND QUALIFICATION (5 ORE): - AM PRODUCT VERIFICATION. - DIMENSIONAL AND GEOMETRIC METROLOGY FOR AM - VOLUME-BASED MEASUREMENT SYSTEMS: 3D X-RAY COMPUTED TOMOGRAPHY. - IN-SITU AND IN-LINE QUALITY MONITORING: SOLUTIONS AND PERSPECTIVES. - HEAT TREATMENTS AND FINISHES - THERMO-MECHANICAL PERFORMANCE OF COMPONENTS MADE USING ADDITIVE TECHNIQUES. PRACTICE - USING COMMERCIAL CAD/CAM SOFTWARE (FUSION 360) (4 HOURS). - 3D PRINTING: CAD MODEL, CONSTRUCTION OF STL FILES AND OBJECTS (6 HOURS). - PRACTICAL ACTIVITIES IN THE LABORATORY (10 HOURS).

## Learning objectives

1. KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING: THE STUDENT WILL ACQUIRE AN IN-DEPTH KNOWLEDGE OF THE PRINCIPLES AND EVOLUTION OF ADDITIVE TECHNOLOGIES, UNDERSTANDING THE AM PROCESS CHAIN AND THE MAIN PHYSICAL MECHANISMS OF THE DIFFERENT PROCESSES (MATERIAL EXTRUSION, POWDER BED FUSION, BINDER JETTING, ETC.). THE CONCEPTS OF DESIGN FOR ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING (DFAM) AND THE PARADIGMS OF SHAPE, HIERARCHICAL, FUNCTIONAL AND MATERIAL COMPLEXITY, AS WELL AS THE FUNDAMENTALS OF ADVANCED PROTOTYPING AND DIGITAL MANUFACTURING, WILL ALSO BE INTRODUCED. 2. APPLYING KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING THE STUDENT WILL BE ABLE TO: - IDENTIFY AND SELECT THE MOST SUITABLE ADDITIVE PROCESS ACCORDING TO THE MATERIAL, GEOMETRY AND APPLICATION OF THE COMPONENT; - DESIGN AND OPTIMIZE A COMPONENT ACCORDING TO THE RULES OF DESIGN FOR ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING, USING CAD/CAE TOOLS AND TOPOLOGICAL OPTIMIZATION AND GENERATIVE DESIGN SOFTWARE; - DEVELOP AND VALIDATE AN EXPERIMENTAL OR SIMULATIVE DESIGN ON A COMPONENT PRODUCED USING ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING, EVALUATING THE MECHANICAL, THERMAL OR FUNCTIONAL PERFORMANCE OF THE COMPONENT. 3. MAKING JUDGEMENTS: THE STUDENT WILL DEVELOP THE ABILITY TO CRITICALLY EVALUATE THE TECHNOLOGICAL AND DESIGN SOLUTIONS RELATED TO ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING, ANALYZING THE ADVANTAGES AND LIMITATIONS COMPARED TO TRADITIONAL SUBTRACTIVE PROCESSES. THEY WILL BE ABLE TO CONSIDER THE ASPECTS OF SUSTAINABILITY, EFFICIENCY, COST AND INNOVATION IN THEIR DESIGN AND PROCESS CHOICES. 4. COMMUNICATION SKILLS: THE STUDENT WILL BE ABLE TO COMMUNICATE CLEARLY AND PROFESSIONALLY, USING THE TECHNICAL LANGUAGE OF ADDITIVE TECHNOLOGIES, BOTH IN WRITTEN FORM (TECHNICAL REPORTS, PROJECTS) AND ORAL (PRESENTATION AND DISCUSSION OF PAPERS). HE WILL ALSO BE ABLE TO INTERACT EFFECTIVELY WITH DESIGNERS, TECHNOLOGISTS AND RESEARCHERS IN THE INDUSTRIAL AND ACADEMIC CONTEXT. 5. LEARNING SKILLS: THE COURSE WILL PROVIDE THE METHODOLOGICAL BASES TO AUTONOMOUSLY DEEPEN THE ISSUES RELATED TO ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING, GENERATIVE DESIGN AND THE DIGITIZATION OF PRODUCTION PROCESSES, FAVORING THE ABILITY TO UPDATE WITH RESPECT TO TECHNOLOGICAL EVOLUTIONS AND INDUSTRIAL TRENDS OF INDUSTRY 4.0

## Assessment methods

THE FINAL EXAM IS AIMED AT ASSESSING THE ACHIEVEMENT OF THE KNOWLEDGE RELATED TO THE ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING TECHNIQUES FOR METALLIC AND POLYMERIC MATERIALS AND CONSISTS OF TWO PHASES: - THE FIRST INCLUDES AN ORAL TEST ON THE TOPICS COVERED IN THE LESSONS; - THE SECOND INVOLVES THE DISCUSSION AND EVALUATION OF THE TECHNICAL REPORT. THE ORAL EXAM IS AIMED AT ASSESSING THE STUDENT'S KNOWLEDGE OF ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING TECHNIQUES INTENDED FOR THE PRODUCTION OF COMPONENTS IN METALLIC AND POLYMERIC MATERIAL. THE TECHNICAL REPORT IS AIMED AT ASSESSING THE STUDENT'S ABILITY TO APPLY THE DESIGN FOR ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING TECHNIQUES TO METALLIC AND POLYMERIC COMPONENTS THROUGH INDIVDUAL OR GROUP WORK. THE PROJECT MAY COVER: THE APPLICATION OF THE DESIGN FOR ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING (DFAM) GUIDELINES FOR THE DESIGN AND THE PRODUCTION OF A NEW DESIGN OF A COMPONENT; THE EXPERIMENTAL ANALYSIS OF THE EFFECT OF THE OPERATING PARAMETERS OF AN AM MACHINE ON THE PRODUCTS. THE FINAL GRADE WILL BE DETERMINED BY THE SUM OF THE GRADE OF THE ORAL EXAM, UP TO A MAXIMUM OF 40%, AND THE GRADE OF THE TECHNICAL REPORT, UP TO A MAXIMUM OF 60% OF THE OVERALL GRADE. IN THE CASE OF A MAXIMUM GRADE (30 OUT OF 30), HONORS ARE AWARDED AT THE DISCRETION OF THE TEACHER TO THE STUDENT WHO SHOWS AUTONOMOUS PROCESSING SKILLS AND LOGICAL CONNECTION BETWEEN THE TOPICS OF THE COURSE.
