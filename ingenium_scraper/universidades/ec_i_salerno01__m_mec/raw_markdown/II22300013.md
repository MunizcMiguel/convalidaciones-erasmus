## Learning objectives

THE COURSE IS DIVIDED INTO TWO MODULES: GENERAL PLANT SERVICES AND INDUSTRIAL PROJECT MANAGEMENT REGARDING THE GENERAL PLANT SERVICES MODULE: THE COURSE AIMS TO PROVIDE STUDENTS WITH THE BASIC ELEMENTS RELATED TO GENERAL DESIGN PRINCIPLES, THE FUNDAMENTAL MANAGEMENT CRITERIA FOR MAJOR PLANT UTILITIES, INCLUDING A TECHNICAL-ECONOMIC COMPARISON OF VARIOUS ALTERNATIVES THE EXPECTED OUTCOMES OF THE COURSE INCLUDE THE ABILITY TO UNDERSTAND THE CONFIGURATION OF A UTILITY SYSTEM, KNOWLEDGE OF THE MAIN COMPONENTS AND THEIR FIELDS OF APPLICATION, THE ABILITY TO PERFORM SIZING AND MAKE THE OPTIMAL TECHNICAL-ECONOMIC CHOICE OF THE MAIN ELEMENTS PRESENT IN A SERVICE PLANT. INDEPENDENT JUDGEMENT BE ABLE TO IDENTIFY THE MOST SUITABLE ELEMENTS FOR CONFIGURING A UTILITY SYSTEM BASED ON THE SPECIFIED NEEDS OF THE USERS. BE ABLE TO EVALUATE THE TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY OF A SYSTEM DESIGN, AND BE ABLE TO COMPARE DIFFERENT SYSTEM ALTERNATIVES BASED ON ECONOMIC AND RELIABILITY PARAMETERS. COMMUNICATION SKILLS BE ABLE TO DESCRIBE, IN WRITING, IN A CLEAR AND CONCISE MANNER, AND TO PRESENT ORALLY WITH APPROPRIATE LANGUAGE THE OBJECTIVES, PROCEDURE, AND RESULTS OF THE ANALYSIS CONDUCTED. LEARNING ABILITY BE ABLE TO APPLY THE KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED TO CONTEXTS DIFFERENT FROM THOSE PRESENTED DURING THE COURSE, SUCH AS, FOR EXAMPLE, DIME SERVICE FACILITIES -------- REGARDING THE INDUSTRIAL PROJECT MANAGEMENT MODULE: THE COURSE AIMS TO PROVIDE STUDENTS WITH THE BASIC ELEMENTS RELATED TO METHODOLOGIES FOR IMPLEMENTING NEW INDUSTRIAL INITIATIVES, WITH PARTICULAR EMPHASIS ON THE TECHNICAL, ECONOMIC, AND FINANCIAL ASPECTS OF THE “BUSINESS PLAN” AND THE MAIN PROJECT MANAGEMENT TECHNIQUES. THE COURSE AIMS, IN PARTICULAR, TO PROVIDE THE TOOLS NECESSARY TO: DEVELOP THE MAIN COMPONENTS OF A PROJECT PLAN, ESTABLISH A CONTROL SYSTEM NECESSARY FOR THE EXECUTION PHASE OF ANY PROJECT; EFFECTIVELY ANALYZE AND MANAGE PROJECT RISKS; CORRECTLY EVALUATE THE TECHNICAL, ECONOMIC, AND FINANCIAL ASPECTS OF A NEW INDUSTRIAL INITIATIVE. THE SKILLS EXPECTED UPON COMPLETION OF THE COURSE INCLUDE THE ABILITY TO PREPARE A PROJECT PLAN, TO PLAN PROJECT ACTIVITIES WITH A FOCUS ON TIME AND COST SCHEDULING, AND TO PROPERLY MONITOR THEIR EXECUTION IN ACCORDANCE WITH EXISTING CONSTRAINTS. INDEPENDENT JUDGMENT ABILITY TO IDENTIFY A PROJECT’S CRITICAL ACTIVITIES, THE NECESSARY RESOURCES, AND THE CONSTRAINTS PRESENT DURING THE PLANNING PHASE. ABILITY TO ASSESS THE STATUS OF A PROJECT DURING THE EXECUTION PHASE. COMMUNICATION SKILLS BE ABLE TO DESCRIBE, IN WRITING, IN A CLEAR AND CONCISE MANNER, AND TO PRESENT ORALLY, USING APPROPRIATE LANGUAGE, THE OBJECTIVES, PROCEDURES, AND RESULTS OF THE WORK PERFORMED. LEARNING ABILITY BE ABLE TO APPLY THE KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED TO CONTEXTS DIFFERENT FROM THOSE PRESENTED DURING THE COURSE, SUCH AS PROJECTS OF DIFFERENT SCALES OR COMPLEXITY, OR RELATED TO INDUSTRIAL AND NON-INDUSTRIAL CASES.

FOR THE SUCCESSFUL ACHIEVEMENT OF THE GOALS SET, TECHNOLOGICAL, ECONOMIC AND IT KNOWLEDGE OF THE INDUSTRIAL ENGINEERING TRAINING AND ACQUIRED IN THE FIRST LEVEL DEGREE ARE REQUIRED.

THE COURSE INCLUDES THEORETICAL LESSONS AND CLASSROOM EXERCISES RELATED TO EACH TOPIC BEING DISCUSSED. THE LATTER ARE AIMED AT VERIFYING THE ABILITY TO PUT INTO PRACTICE, ON CONCRETE CASES, THE CONCEPTS LEARNED DURING THE THEORETICAL LESSONS.

## Assessment methods

LEARNING OUTCOMES FOR BOTH MODULES WILL BE ASSESSED THROUGH A WRITTEN EXAM AND AN ORAL EXAM. THE WRITTEN EXAM WILL INCLUDE EXERCISES SIMILAR TO THOSE CONDUCTED DURING PRACTICAL SESSIONS, COVERING, FOR THE INDUSTRIAL PROJECT MANAGEMENT MODULE, PROJECT ACTIVITY SCHEDULING, PROJECT CONTROL, AND PROJECT RISK MANAGEMENT; AND, FOR THE GENERAL PLANT SERVICES MODULE, THE SELECTION AND SIZING OF THE MAIN COMPONENTS OF A SERVICE PLANT. THE ORAL EXAM WILL DISCUSS THE WRITTEN ASSIGNMENT AND WILL ASSESS THE THEORETICAL KNOWLEDGE ACQUIRED AS WELL AS THE ABILITY TO APPLY IT IN PRACTICE, THROUGH QUESTIONS RELATING TO DESIGN PROBLEMS, SYSTEM SELECTION, PLANNING, AND CONTROL OF PROJECT ACTIVITIES. THE ORAL EXAMINATION IS GRADED ON A SCALE OF 30 FOR EACH QUESTION, AND THE SCORES ARE THEN AVERAGED ACROSS THE QUESTIONS. A SUFFICIENT MARK (18) IS GIVEN TO THE SINGLE QUESTION, WHEN THE STUDENT SHOWS ELEMENTARY BUT ADEQUATE KNOWLEDGE OF THE TOPIC; THE MAXIMUM SCORE IS ATTRIBUTED (30) IN THE PRESENCE OF THE ABILITY TO EXPLAIN THE SYSTEM ASPECTS CONNECTED TO THE INDIVIDUAL QUESTION WITH FULL AUTONOMY AND TECHNICAL LANGUAGE PROPERTIES. THE FINAL GRADE IS THE ARITHMETIC MEAN OF THE MARKS OBTAINED IN THE WRITTEN TEST AND THE ORAL EXAM. IN PARTICULAR, HONORS WILL BE AWARDED IN THE PRESENCE OF A MAXIMUM MARK AND ABILITY BY THE CANDIDATE TO DEMONSTRATE READINESS IN THE LINKS BETWEEN THE DIFFERENT TOPICS AND IN-DEPTH KNOWLEDGE OF THE SAME BEYOND WHAT IS EXPLAINED DURING THE LESSONS OF THE COURSE.
