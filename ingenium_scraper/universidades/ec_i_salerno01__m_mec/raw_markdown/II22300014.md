## Contents

EXECUTION AND ANALYSIS OF RESULTS (2H T + 4H E): CALCULATION OF THE NUMBER OF REPLICATIONS, TRANSIENT LENGTH, RUN DURATION, WELCH’S APPROACH, DUNCAN MULTIPLE RANGE TEST, AUTOCORRELATION AND BATCH METHOD, EXERCISES AND APPLICATIONS. ## Learning objectives

COURSE OBJECTIVES THE COURSE AIMS TO PROVIDE TOOLS TO SUPPORT THE MODELING AND OPTIMIZATION OF THE TECHNICAL AND ECONOMIC PERFORMANCE OF PRODUCTION AND LOGISTICS SYSTEMS THROUGH A REVIEW OF QUANTITATIVE METHODS TO SUPPORT THE DESIGN AND IMPROVEMENT OF SYSTEMS. REAL-WORLD CASE STUDIES WILL BE USED, EMPLOYING MULTIPLE MODELING TECHNIQUES IN CONJUNCTION TO ARRIVE AT THE SOLUTION TO THE PROBLEM AT HAND. THE PRIMARY APPLICATION CONTEXTS WILL BE LOGISTICS NETWORKS AND PRODUCTION-LOGISTICS SYSTEMS CHARACTERIZED BY FLEXIBILITY IN RESOURCE USE AND VARIABILITY IN ORDER MIX AND PROCESS REQUIREMENTS. MAIN SKILLS THE MAIN SKILLS WILL BE: KNOWING HOW TO USE THE CAUSE-AND-EFFECT DIAGRAM AND THE PARETO CHART FOR PROBLEM FORMULATION, SCHEMATIZING A PROCESS USING BLOCK DIAGRAMS, USING PROJECT MANAGEMENT (PM) SOFTWARE FOR SCHEDULING A SIMULATION PROJECT, USING IDEF0 AND FLOWCHARTS FOR THE FORMAL DEFINITION OF A MANUFACTURING SYSTEM, USING STATISTICAL TESTS TO IDENTIFY THE ORIGIN DISTRIBUTIONS OF SAMPLE DATA (PROCESSING TIMES, ORDER ARRIVALS, ETC.), USING A SIMULATION ENVIRONMENT TO IMPLEMENT A DISCRETE EVENT MODEL, CREATING ANIMATIONS TO VERIFY THE SIMULATOR’S OPERATION, USING STATISTICAL TESTS TO DETERMINE IF TWO SETS OF DATA ORIGINATE FROM THE SAME DISTRIBUTION, DESIGNING AN EXPERIMENTAL CAMPAIGN, DETERMINING THE DURATION OF RUNS AND TRANSIENTS, DETERMINING THE NUMBER OF REPLICATIONS TO ACHIEVE THE DESIRED PRECISION, ANALYZING RESULTS THROUGH THE USE OF PARAMETRIC AND NON-PARAMETRIC STATISTICAL TESTS, AND EVALUATING ALTERNATIVE MANAGEMENT STRATEGIES TO IMPLEMENT. JUDGEMENT AUTONOMY THE STUDENT ACQUIRES THE ABILITY TO INDEPENDENTLY FORMULATE THE OPPORTUNITY TO CONDUCT A SIMULATION STUDY AND TO CARRY OUT ALL PHASES OF ITS DEVELOPMENT. COMMUNICATION SKILLS THE STUDENT ACQUIRES THE ABILITY TO COMMUNICATE EFFECTIVELY ON TOPICS RELATED TO THE SIMULATION OF A PRODUCTION AND LOGISTICS PLANT AND ON THE TECHNIQUES FOR VALIDATION AND CONDUCTING EXPERIMENTS. LEARNING CAPABILITY THE STUDENT ACQUIRES THE ABILITY TO INDEPENDENTLY LEARN MORE IN-DEPTH AND SPECIFIC COMPETENCIES IN THE FIELD OF SIMULATION OF PRODUCTION AND LOGISTICS SYSTEMS.

## Assessment methods

THE ACHIEVEMENT OF THE LEARNING OBJECTIVES IS CERTIFIED THROUGH A WRITTEN EXAMINATION AND AN ORAL ASSESSMENT. WRITTEN EXAMINATION. THE WRITTEN EXAM LASTS APPROXIMATELY 2 HOURS. IT CONSISTS OF A PROBLEM DESCRIPTION FOLLOWED BY 4 OR 5 QUESTIONS DESIGNED TO VERIFY THE STUDENT'S ACQUISITION OF THE CORE SKILLS PROVIDED BY THE COURSE. SCORING: EACH QUESTION IS ASSIGNED A VARIABLE MAXIMUM SCORE BETWEEN 5 AND 15 POINTS. TOTAL SCORE: THE SUM OF THE MAXIMUM SCORES IS 32. PASSING GRADE: A MINIMUM SCORE OF 18 POINTS IS REQUIRED TO PASS THE EXAM. CONTENT AND STRUCTURE: THE ASSESSMENT IS STRUCTURED AS A WRITTEN REPORT AIMED AT VERIFYING THE UNDERSTANDING AND APPLICATION OF THE FOLLOWING: PROCESS SCHEMATIZATION TOOLS, STATISTICAL TESTS FOR IDENTIFYING THE UNDERLYING DISTRIBUTIONS OF SAMPLE DATA, ANOVA (ANALYSIS OF VARIANCE), THE SIMULATION ENVIRONMENT USED FOR MODEL IMPLEMENTATION, RUN LENGTH AND TRANSIENT PERIODS, NUMBER OF REPLICATIONS REQUIRED TO ACHIEVE THE DESIRED PRECISION, EVALUATION OF ALTERNATIVE MANAGEMENT STRATEGIES. ORAL EXAMINATION: THE ORAL EXAM COMPLEMENTS THE WRITTEN TEST AND LASTS APPROXIMATELY 15 MINUTES. IT FOCUSES ON THE STUDENT'S ABILITY TO CRITICALLY DISCUSS THE TOPICS ADDRESSED DURING THE COURSE AND CORRELATE THEM WITH INDUSTRIAL APPLICATIONS. FINAL GRADING: THE OVERALL GRADE IS CALCULATED AS A WEIGHTED AVERAGE OF THE TWO ASSESSMENTS: WRITTEN EXAMINATION: 80% (WEIGHT: 0.8), ORAL EXAMINATION: 20% (WEIGHT: 0.2) HONORS (LAUDE): ACADEMIC HONORS MAY BE AWARDED IF THE STUDENT ACHIEVES AT LEAST 30 POINTS ON THE WRITTEN EXAM AND DEMONSTRATES, DURING THE ORAL ASSESSMENT, EXCELLENT MASTERY OF THE SUBJECT MATTER AND THE ABILITY TO INTERRELATE THE VARIOUS TOPICS COVERED.
