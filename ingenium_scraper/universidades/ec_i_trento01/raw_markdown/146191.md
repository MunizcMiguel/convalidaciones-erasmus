# Organizations and innovation (146191)

## General information

- **Degree programme:** INDUSTRIAL ENGINEERING (0327G)

## Learning objectives

- Explore and analyse the contribution of individuals and their behaviours to organizational performance

- Enhance understanding and awareness of one’s own behaviours and group dynamics in workplace contexts

- Explore and analyse the nature of change within organizations and the main organizational issues

- Deepen knowledge of change management and innovation in organizations

## Prerequisites

Basic managerial concepts (e.g. business functions) are useful for this course.

## Contents

The main thematic areas of the course will be:

Part 1 – Organizational Behavior: focus on individual, group, and organizational behaviours and dynamics - within organizations (key topics include the history of organizational theory, personality, perception and motivation, team dynamics and leadership, organizational culture);

Part 2 – Organizational Innovation: focus on change within organizations, organizational models, and innovation management (key topics include change management, process management, project management, innovation management – with a particular focus on open innovation).

## Teaching methods

The course is part of the Blended Learning project of the University. So, there will be a mix of lectures, team work (including exercises and 'learning by doing' activities), online activities, discussions on case studies, discussion on articles, short videos, team presentations.

## Assessment methods

Assessment will be based on a comprehensive oral exam covering the entire course (regular exam sessions). As an additional option, mainly intended for students with a good level of attendance, it will also be possible to take intermediate assessments, consisting of a first written exam and a second oral exam (each on one of the two parts of the course), both of which must be passed.
