## Partition: ANALISI MATEMATICA 2 (Cognomi A-L)

### Prerequisites

Differential and integral calculus for functions of one real variable, linear algebra and analytic geometry in the plane and in the space.

### Teaching methods

Theory lessons: 72 hours.

### DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Evaluation Methods

The student will be assessed through the following tests: - a written test with exercises that will test the ability to solve problems by using the techniques learned during the course - an oral test in which the student will be asked to present some of the theoretical topics covered during the course, at the teacher's choice. For students with disabilities/invalidity or specific learning disorder (DSA), who have duly requested support to take the specific exam at the University Disability/DSA Info Point, the exam methods will be adapted in light of the University guidelines (https://www.univpm.it/Entra/Servizi_agli_studenti/Disabilita_e_DSA_Servizio_di_accoglienza)

### DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Evaluation Criteria

To successfully pass the exam, the student must prove, through the tests listed above, that he has understood the fundamental concept presented during the course; that he has acquired the techniques of differential and integral calculus for functions of several real variables and their applications, and that he is able to set problems and solve them by suitable application of the techniques and methods learned during the course. The maximum grade is given to students who, in the written test, demonstrate excellent ability and full autonomy in solving the proposed problems and, in the oral test, demonstrate a thorough knowledge of the concepts presented during the course, methodological rigor and appropriateness of scientific language.

### DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Measurement Criteria

A grade out of thirtieths is awarded, with possible honors.

### DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Final Mark Allocation Criteria

The student will be admitted to the oral test only if he passed the written test. The passing grade for both the written test and the oral test is 18/30. The overall rating comes from the comparative evaluation of the written and the oral test. The honour is bestowed upon those students who have performed the tests in a correct and exhaustive way and have shown a particular excellence and independence of thought.

### LEARNING OUTCOMES - Knowledge and Understanding

Through the study of the fundamentals of differential and integral calculus for functions of several variables, together with methods for solving ordinary differential equations, students will acquire the mathematical tools needed for engineering applications.

### LEARNING OUTCOMES - Capacity to apply Knowledge and Understanding

The many applications of the course topics in the applied sciences, for example in chemistry and in physics, will provide the student with the ability of modeling and solving practical engineering problems; they will also increase the ability of choosing independently the best solution techniques. The course will also provide the student with the ability to use mathematical laws in general scientific problems.

### LEARNING OUTCOMES - Transversal Skills

The expertise acquired in this course will be needed in order to study the material of later courses. Individual and collective problem-solving sessions will improve the ability to develop independent thought and learning capabilities. Oral presentations of the topics taught in the course, with the language proper of the basic disciplines of the degree course will help developing communication skills.

### Contents

Regular curves and elements of the differential geometry of curves in R^2 and R^3. Infinitesimal and differential calculus for functions of several variables: limits, continuity, partial derivatives, differentiability, Taylor’s theorem, and local maxima and minima. Implicit functions, the Implicit Function Theorem (Dini’s Theorem), and constrained optimization. Line integrals and multiple integrals. Regular surfaces and surface integrals. Conservative and irrotational vector fields. Work and flux of a vector field. Green’s theorem, the Divergence Theorem (Gauss Theorem), and Stokes’ theorem. Ordinary differential equations: existence and uniqueness theorems. Linear ordinary differential equations and selected classes of nonlinear ordinary differential equations.

### Reference texts

1. M. Bramanti, C. D. Pagani, S. Salsa, Analisi Matematica 2, Zanichelli, 2009. ISBN: 978-8808122810. 2. F. G. [docente], Analisi Matematica 2, Esculapio, 2020. ISBN: 978-8893851688. Additional teaching materials are available on the University’s Moodle platform at: https://learn.univpm.it/

### Contents

Linear ordinary differential equations and some ordinary nonlinear differential equations. Fourier series and their application to solving certain ordinary differential equations.
