## Partition: METALLURGIA (Cognomi M-Z)

### Prerequisites

Knowledge of the fundamentals of Chemistry is highly recommended.

### Teaching methods

38 hours face-to-face. 10 hours of classroom and laboratory training.

### E-LEARNING

NO

### DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Evaluation Methods

The exam consists of an oral exam with questions relating to all the topics covered in the course. For students with a disability or specific learning disability (SLD), who have made due requests for support to take the specific Profit Examination at the University Disability/SLD Info Point, the examination arrangements will be adapted in light of the provisions of the University Guidelines (https://www.univpm.it/Entra/Servizi_agli_studenti/Disabilita_e_DSA_Servizi).

### DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Evaluation Criteria

Students will be required to demonstrate that they are aware of the microstructures and/or phases present in the materials considered at the end of equilibrium and non-equilibrium cooling, and that they are able to identify steels and cast irons, as well as the typicalities of heat treatments. To pass the oral exam successfully, the student must demonstrate a comprehensive knowledge of the content, presented in a sufficiently correct manner with the use of adequate technical terminology. Topics such as CCT curves, Fe-C state diagram, remediation; tensile curves, are essential to overcome the Metallurgy oral. The maximum evaluation will be achieved by demonstrating in-depth knowledge of the content of the topics covered in the course.

### DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Measurement Criteria

Attribution of the final vote in thirtieths with possible laude.

### DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Final Mark Allocation Criteria

The oral exam will be divided into 3 questions, the first of which will always concern CCT curves, Fe-C state diagram, remediation treatment, or traction curves. Each question can be evaluated with a score ranging between 0 and 10 points. In the event that the student/student does not fully answer the first question, the exam is considered concluded and failed. Laude will be given to students who have demonstrated complete mastery of the subject.

### LEARNING OUTCOMES - Knowledge and Understanding

The teaching course enables male and female students to acquire basic knowledge about the nature, classification and properties of ferrous materials, including cast irons. These contents are part of the body of knowledge and operational capabilities in the specific fields of mechanical engineering, with regard to the choice of materials in the design process, and in the management of production technologies and industrial plants.

### LEARNING OUTCOMES - Capacity to apply Knowledge and Understanding

At the end of the course, students must be able to recognize the fundamental properties of the various classes of steel, cast iron, or aluminum alloy. This ability will be expressed through the following professionalizing skills: the ability to identify, starting from the structural demands that the design places on the material, the steel, cast iron or aluminum alloy most suitable for a given application, and the related supply status; the ability to analyze and manage the various phases of the component production process mechanical taking into account the properties of the metallic material involved.

### LEARNING OUTCOMES - Transversal Skills

Performing simple problem-solving exercises, conducted in a guided manner, in the classroom, and then individually through the Moodle platform, will be a tool to help students develop their independent judgment and learning ability.

### Contents

Theoretical lectures and classroom exercises (46 hours): Notes on the crystalline structure of metals; mechanical tests (tensile, hardness, Charpy test); state diagrams (complete miscibility, eutectic, peritectic) and their reading; solidification; primary metallurgy; recycling (secondary metallurgy); Fe-C diagram; austenite transformation structures; TTT and CCT curves; heat treatments of application interest; effect of alligants in iron; steel classification; general purpose steels; weldability of steels; steels in the civil-construction sector; automotive steels; hull and shipbuilding steels (nenni); special steels (reclamation, self-hardening, carburizing, nitriding, bearing; rope, surface hardening); carburizing process and nitriding process; tool steels (cold-working, hot-working, fast-working); stainless steels (austenitic, martensitic, ferritic, duplex); maraging steels; cast irons (grey, white, spheroidal). Exercises: Collegiate classroom exercises are planned throughout the year on the following topics: Status diagrams, CCT and TTT diagrams, steel recognition and estimation of their mechanical properties. Laboratory activities (2 hours remaining). Student groups for guided tours.

## Partition: METALLURGIA (Cognomi A-L)

### Prerequisites

There are no mandatory prerequisites for this course, although the knowledge of the fundamentals of Chemistry is highly recommended

### Teaching methods

Lectures: 36 hours Excercises and laboratory activity: 12 hrs

### E-LEARNING

no

### DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Evaluation Methods

The method for learning evaluation will consist in an oral interview. The interview will include exercises on phase diagrams, CCT and TTT curves.

### DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Evaluation Criteria

The candidate must be able to predict the microstructure and/or phases resulting from equilibrium (phase diagrams) and non equilibrium (TTT-CCT curves) transformations, and to properly identify steels and aluminum alloys by their designation according to UNI, AISI and IADS (International Alloy Designation System). During the oral examination, the candidate should also exhibit a proper knowledge of the properties, heat treatments and applications of the different classes of materials.The student must be able to coherently describe concepts.

### DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Measurement Criteria

Grading scheme is based on a scale of 30 points. Successful completion of the examination will lead to grades ranging from 18 to 30.

### DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Final Mark Allocation Criteria

The method for learning evaluation will consist in an oral interview. The interview will include exercises on phase diagrams, CCT and TTT curves.Maximum grade obtained in the oral examination will be 30 points (set of 4 questions, each evaluated up to 7.5 points).

### LEARNING OUTCOMES - Knowledge and Understanding

At the end of the course, the students will acquire the knowledge on the nature, classification and properties of ferrous metals, with hints on the same issues for aluminium alloys. These subjects constitute a fundamental part of the wealth of knowledge at the basis of mechanical engineering in the fields of mechanical design and materials processing.

### LEARNING OUTCOMES - Capacity to apply Knowledge and Understanding

At the end of the course students will be able to identify the main properties of the different groups of steels, cast irons and aluminium alloys. This knowledge will be reflected in the ability to use the following tools: 1. The capacity of selecting the best steel, cast iron or aluminium alloy, and the corresponding initial state, on the bases of the design functional requirements. 2. The ability of correctly analyzing and governing the production stages of mechanical components, taking into account the final properties of the used material.

### LEARNING OUTCOMES - Transversal Skills

Simple exercises will be carried out during the course; the students will have the possibility to individually carry out similar exercises in the moodle platform. These activities will enable students to improve their autonomy, and communication, learning and critical skills.

### Contents

Theory: 36 hours Principles of physical metallurgy. Deformation of metals and strengthening methods in metals. Phase diagrams. Steels: primary steel production (blast furnace, converter), scrap and recycling (electrical furnaces). Heat treatments of steels. Designation and properties of steels. Steels for general use, alloy steels, tool steels, stainless steels. Cast Irons. Light alloys (Aluminium) Exercises and laboratory activity: 12 hours Phase diagrams, TTT and CCT curves, Steel classification and calculation of their mechanical properties. Visits in the lab, sample preparation, optical microscopy, hardness.
