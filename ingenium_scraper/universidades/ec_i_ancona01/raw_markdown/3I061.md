- **Degree programme:** MECHANICAL ENGINEERING (IT25)
- **Curriculum:** STATUTARIO
- **Year of study:** 1
- **Academic year of delivery:** 2026/2027
- **Period:** Secondo Semestre
- **ECTS:** 9
- **Hours:** Lesson: 72
- **Scientific sector (SSD):** PHYS-03/A

- **Exam type:** Orale
- **Grading:** Final grade
- **Attendance:** Not mandatory
- **Teaching methods:** Lesson

## Prerequisites

Trigonometric, vector, and differential calculus.

## Teaching methods

Please refer to the course descriptions for the different partitions.

## E-LEARNING

The course is not offered via e-learning

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Evaluation Methods

Assessment is by means of a written and oral examination. In the written test, problems in classical mechanics and thermodynamics are to be solved using the methodologies and mathematical tools illustrated in the lectures. In the oral examination, the fundamental concepts of the discipline must be explained. For students with a disability/invalidity or specific learning disorder (DSA), who have duly requested support for the specific exam at the University's Disability/DSA Info Point, the exam arrangements will be adapted in the light of the University guidelines (https://www.univpm.it/Entra/Servizi_agli_studenti/Disabilita_e_DSA_Servizio_di_accoglienza)

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Evaluation Criteria

In the examinations, it must be demonstrated to have acquired the necessary autonomy to analyse and solve problems in classical mechanics and thermodynamics by correctly using the fundamental concepts, methodologies and tools presented in the lectures. The ability to schematise complex phenomena into their essential elements in the light of the knowledge acquired is also assessed. The maximum grade is awarded when full autonomy in setting up and solving problems and complete mastery of the methodologies, models and tools of classical mechanics and thermodynamics is demonstrated. The pass mark is awarded when sufficient knowledge of the methods, models and tools of classical mechanics and thermodynamics is demonstrated.

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Learning Measurement Criteria

Scores are in 30/30 scale, possibly with honors.

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXAMINATION - Final Mark Allocation Criteria

The overall mark will be positive if at least 18 points are awarded in both the written test and the possible oral test. The final mark is obtained by mathematically averaging the mark in the written test and the mark in the oral test.

## LEARNING OUTCOMES - Knowledge and Understanding

This course provides students with the fundamentals of the experimental method—common to all scientific disciplines—and the fundamental laws of classical mechanics and thermodynamics. It serves as an essential bridge between the knowledge acquired in high school and that of university-level education, and the knowledge it imparts enables students to acquire the necessary tools for a scientific approach to analyzing engineering problems.

## LEARNING OUTCOMES - Capacity to apply Knowledge and Understanding

The physics knowledge and methods acquired will enable students to understand, analyze, and model engineering problems. In particular, students will develop the ability to break down typically complex phenomena into their essential elements and apply the laws of classical physics to describe how they work. To this end, the exercises provided are often drawn from everyday experience. This knowledge and these methods are applicable to many of the courses students will take during their studies and, later on, to the challenges they will face in the workplace.

## LEARNING OUTCOMES - Transversal Skills

The methodological approach gained in this course and the exercises assigned during the course will help improve students’ overall ability to exercise independent judgment, as well as their capacity to learn and draw conclusions.
