## Actividades formativas

Como complemento a lo explicado en clase se visualizarán dos películas, de las cuáles habrá
que hacer un trabajo escrito, bien individual bien en grupo.
LEARNING ACTIVITIES
The course is structured so that each week students complete an assignment at home, as
indicated by the instructor. This assignment will serve as the material for the following class
session.
The topic of each session is specified in the course outline, and the assigned texts can be found
in the Content folder.
There are three main types of activities students will carry out:
1. Film Viewing: Students will watch selected films carefully at home. This will typically
require around two hours per week.
