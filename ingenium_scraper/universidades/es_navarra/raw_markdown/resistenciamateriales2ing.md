## Evaluación

Part I: Computer lab (CYPE) exam (10%) + Exam Chapters 1&2 (50%)
Part II: Group project (20%)** + Exam Chapters 3-4-5 (20%)
** The grade of the group project assigned to each participant will be affected by the performance
in the individual exam.
EXTRAORDINARY ASSESSMENT
The student will only have to repeat the failed parts. The mark of the passed part will be
maintained. In Part II, the extraordinary assessment will only take into account the exam, the
group project will not count.
Part I: Computer lab (CYPE) exam (10%) + Exam Chapters 1&2 (50%)
Part II: Exam Chapters 3-4-5 (40%)
